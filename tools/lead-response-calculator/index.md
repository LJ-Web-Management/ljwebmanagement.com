# What is slow lead follow-up costing you?

> Estimate how much revenue faster lead follow-up could add. Enter monthly leads, close rate, and customer value to see what a higher close rate is worth.

Faster, more consistent follow-up wins more of the leads you already pay for. Enter your numbers and an improvement you consider realistic to see what it is worth each year.

## How the calculation works

Revenue from leads is simple multiplication: **leads × close rate × customer value**. The calculator applies your expected improvement to the close rate and shows the difference.

The most useful number is often the last one: the yearly value of a single percentage point of close rate. If one point is worth tens of thousands of dollars a year, even a modest improvement justifies fixing follow-up.

## Why response speed matters

People who fill out a form or call a business are usually comparing options. The first business to respond clearly and helpfully has an advantage, and a lead that hears nothing for a day often books with someone else. Consistency matters as much as speed: many leads need several touches before they reply, and manual follow-up tends to stop after one or two.

## What an automated follow-up system does

- Replies to every new inquiry within a minute, by text and email, day or night.
- Routes the lead to the right person and alerts them instantly.
- Sends a follow-up sequence over the next days until the lead responds, then stops.
- Texts back missed callers so a phone lead is never lost to voicemail.
- Logs every touch in your CRM so nothing depends on memory.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a good lead response time?**

As fast as you can make it. For inbound leads, aim to acknowledge every inquiry within minutes during business hours. An automated reply can go out instantly around the clock, with a person following up as soon as they are available.

**How do I measure my current response time?**

Pull 20 recent leads and compare when each one arrived with when someone first replied. Most CRMs and form tools record both timestamps. The average, and the slowest few, tell you how much there is to gain.

**Does faster follow-up really increase sales?**

For inbound leads who are actively shopping, faster and more persistent follow-up generally means more conversations and more customers. How much depends on your market, price point, and sales process, so measure your close rate before and after the change.

**Will automated follow-up feel robotic?**

Not if it is written well. Short, specific messages in your own voice, sent at sensible times, read like a prompt reply from a busy team. The automation handles timing and persistence; people handle the conversation once the lead replies.
