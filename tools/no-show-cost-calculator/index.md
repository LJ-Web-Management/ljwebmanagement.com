# What are no-shows costing your business?

> Calculate the revenue lost to missed appointments each year and how much automated reminders and confirmations could recover. Free no-show cost calculator.

Every empty slot is time you paid for and revenue you did not earn. Enter your booking numbers to see what no-shows cost each year and what better reminders could recover.

## How the calculation works

- **Missed appointments per year** = appointments per week × 52 × no-show rate.
- **Revenue lost** = missed appointments × average revenue per appointment.
- **Revenue recovered** = revenue lost × the reduction you expect.
- **Reminder call hours** = appointments per year × minutes per call &divide; 60.

## What actually reduces no-shows

Most no-shows are not people who decided not to come. They forgot, mixed up the time, or could not reach you to reschedule. An automated system attacks each cause:

- A confirmation the moment the appointment is booked, with an add-to-calendar link
- A reminder a day or two before and another a few hours before, by text
- A one-tap way to confirm, cancel, or reschedule, so an empty slot becomes a rebooking
- A waitlist that offers freed slots to the next person automatically
- A friendly follow-up to anyone who misses, offering a new time

## Frequently asked questions

**What is a normal no-show rate?**

It varies widely by industry, patient or client type, and how far ahead appointments are booked. The useful benchmark is your own: track no-shows for a month before and after adding reminders, and compare.

**How many reminders should I send?**

Two is a good default: one a day or two before and one a few hours before. Add a confirmation at booking time. More than that tends to annoy people without improving attendance.

**Should reminders be texts or emails?**

Texts are read quickly and suit short reminders; email works for details such as preparation instructions or forms. Many businesses send both, with texts for timing and email for information. Get consent for texting and include opt-out language.

**Can automation fill cancelled slots?**

Yes. When someone cancels through the reminder link, the system can text the people on your waitlist who fit that time, in order, and book the first one who accepts.
