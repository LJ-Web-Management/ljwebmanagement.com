    AI prompts for business automation

    Copy-and-paste prompts for the work small teams do every day: email, sales, marketing, support, operations, finance, and planning your next automation. Replace the [bracketed] parts with your details.

    32 prompts
    Email & messages

    Turn rough notes into a clear email

    Paste bullet points or a voice-to-text ramble and get a ready-to-send email.

    You are helping me write a professional email.

Recipient: [who they are and our relationship]
Goal: [what I want them to know or do]
Tone: [friendly / formal / direct]

My rough notes:
[paste notes]

Write the email in under 150 words with a clear subject line. Put the request or key point in the first two sentences. End with one specific next step. Do not invent facts that are not in my notes.
    Email & messages

    Reply to a frustrated customer

    Draft a calm, accountable reply to a complaint for a person to review before sending.

    Draft a reply to this customer message for me to review.

Customer message:
[paste message]

What actually happened on our side: [facts]
What we can offer: [refund / redo / discount / call / nothing beyond an apology]

Rules: acknowledge the specific problem in their words, take responsibility where we are at fault, do not make promises beyond what I listed, keep it under 120 words, and end with a clear next step. Avoid generic phrases like "we value your business."

    Summarize a long email thread

    Get the decisions, open questions, and owners from a messy thread in seconds.

Return:
1. A two-sentence summary of what the thread is about.
2. Decisions made (bullet list).
3. Open questions (bullet list).
4. Action items with the person responsible and any deadline mentioned.

Only use information in the thread. If an owner or deadline is unclear, say "unclear".

    Classify incoming emails for routing

    A prompt to use inside an automation that sorts inbound email by type and urgency.

    Classify the email below. Respond with JSON only, using exactly these keys:
{"category": one of ["sales_inquiry", "support_request", "billing", "scheduling", "vendor", "spam", "other"], "urgency": one of ["high", "normal", "low"], "summary": a sentence under 20 words, "needs_human": true or false}

Mark urgency high only if the sender reports something broken, a payment problem, or a same-day deadline. Set needs_human to true for complaints, legal or medical questions, and anything you are unsure about.

    Automation planning

    Write test cases for an automation

    Realistic examples to test a workflow before it goes live.

Include 10 cases: 4 normal cases with realistic data, 3 edge cases (missing fields, duplicates, unusual formats), and 3 failure cases (an app is down, a value is invalid, a person needs to step in). For each, give the input, the expected result, and what should be logged or alerted.

    Choose between Zapier, Make, n8n, or custom

    A structured recommendation for which platform fits a workflow.

    How to get reliable results from these prompts

    • Give context. The bracketed parts matter most. The model only knows what you tell it about your business, customer, and goal.
    • Ask for a format. Word limits, bullet lists, tables, and JSON make output easier to use and to check.
    • Tell it what not to do. Rules such as "do not invent facts" and "say unclear if unsure" reduce confident mistakes.
    • Review before it reaches a customer. Treat AI output as a first draft, especially for anything involving money, health, legal matters, or commitments.
    • Protect sensitive data. Use business plans that do not train on your data, and leave out personal details a task does not need.

    From prompt to automation

    Prompts marked for use inside an automation (classifying email, extracting data) are written to return predictable output, such as JSON with fixed keys. That is what lets a workflow tool like Zapier, Make, or n8n read the answer and take the next step automatically. Learn how in our guide to AI workflow automation and lesson two of the free AI at Work 101 course.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which AI tool should I use these prompts with?

    Any capable AI assistant or model API works, including the chat assistants from the major AI providers. For automations, the same prompts can run through an API step in Zapier, Make, n8n, or custom code.

    What makes a good AI prompt for business?

    A clear role or task, the context the model needs, the exact output format, and rules about what to avoid. Short, specific prompts with an example of good output beat long, vague ones.

    Is it safe to paste customer data into AI tools?

    Only into tools whose business terms you have reviewed, and only the data the task needs. Many business and API plans do not train on your data by default, but check your provider's terms and any industry rules, such as HIPAA, that apply to you.
