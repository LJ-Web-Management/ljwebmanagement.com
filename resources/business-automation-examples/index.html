Business Automation Examples

50+ Business Automation Examples by Department & Industry

Real examples of business automation across every department, plus a look at how it plays out by industry.

Below are real-world business automation examples grouped by department, followed by a few by industry. Each one replaces a task someone was previously doing by hand, on a schedule, or from memory. For a definition of the term itself, see what business automation is.

Sales & CRM

  • Lead follow-up sequences: a new lead is automatically emailed and added to a follow-up schedule the moment they fill out a form, instead of waiting for a rep to notice.
  • Pipeline updates: deal stages update automatically based on activity (a signed contract, a missed call) instead of a rep updating the CRM by hand.
  • Quote and proposal generation: pulling pricing and terms into a ready-to-send document instead of building one from scratch each time.

Marketing

  • Email nurture campaigns triggered by what a contact does (downloads a guide, visits a pricing page).
  • Review and referral requests sent automatically after a purchase or completed job.
  • Content and social scheduling so posts go out on a plan instead of someone remembering each day.

Operations

  • Appointment scheduling and reminders that cut down on no-shows without a staff member calling every client.
  • Inventory and reorder alerts that flag low stock before it becomes a stockout.
  • Document collection that chases missing paperwork automatically instead of a staff member following up by phone or email.

Customer Support

  • Ticket routing and triage that sends a request to the right person based on what it's about.
  • AI website assistants that answer common questions and qualify a visitor before a human ever gets involved.
  • Status update notifications sent automatically when a ticket or order changes state.

Finance & Admin

  • Invoice generation and reminders for overdue balances.
  • Expense and time reconciliation that flags unbilled hours or mismatched receipts.
  • Reporting that pulls numbers from multiple tools into one dashboard instead of a manual weekly compile.

HR & Staffing

  • Onboarding checklists that assign paperwork, accounts, and training automatically for a new hire.
  • Shift and scheduling reminders sent without a manager texting each person.
  • Applicant screening and scheduling that moves a candidate to the next step without manual coordination.
