## Sales and CRM

- **Lead follow-up sequences**: a new lead is automatically texted, emailed, and added to a follow-up schedule the moment they fill out a form, instead of waiting for a rep to notice. See the [lead follow-up playbook](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/guides/automate-lead-follow-up/).
- **Missed-call text-back**: every unanswered call gets a text within seconds so the conversation continues. See [how it works](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/guides/missed-call-text-back/).
- **Pipeline updates**: deal stages update automatically based on activity (a booked meeting, a signed contract) instead of a rep updating the CRM by hand.
- **Quote and proposal generation**: pulling pricing and terms into a ready-to-send document instead of building one from scratch each time.
- **Stalled-deal alerts** that flag opportunities with no recent activity. More in the [CRM automation guide](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/guides/crm-automation/).

## Marketing

- **Email nurture campaigns** triggered by what a contact does (downloads a guide, visits a pricing page).
- **Review and referral requests** sent automatically after a purchase or completed job. See [how to automate review requests](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/guides/automate-review-requests/).
- **Win-back campaigns** for customers who have not purchased or visited in a while.
- **Content and social scheduling** so posts go out on a plan instead of someone remembering each day.

## Operations

- **Appointment scheduling and reminders** that cut down on no-shows without a staff member calling every client. See the [no-show guide](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/guides/reduce-no-shows-appointment-reminders/).
- **Inventory and reorder alerts** that flag low stock before it becomes a stockout.
- **Document collection** that chases missing paperwork automatically instead of a staff member following up by phone or email.
- **Vendor and crew confirmations** sent and tracked before a job or event.

## Customer support

- **Ticket routing and triage** that sends a request to the right person based on what it's about.
- **AI website assistants** that answer common questions and qualify a visitor before a human ever gets involved.
- **Status update notifications** sent automatically when a ticket, order, or job changes state.

## Finance and admin

- **Invoice generation and reminders** for overdue balances. See the [invoicing guide](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/guides/automate-invoicing-payment-reminders/).
- **Failed payment recovery** for subscriptions and memberships.
- **Expense and time reconciliation** that flags unbilled hours or mismatched receipts.
- **Reporting** that pulls numbers from multiple tools into one dashboard instead of a manual weekly compile.

## HR and staffing

- **Onboarding checklists** that assign paperwork, accounts, and training automatically for a new hire. See the [employee onboarding checklist](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/guides/employee-onboarding-automation/).
- **Shift and scheduling reminders** sent without a manager texting each person.
- **Applicant screening and interview scheduling** that moves a candidate to the next step without manual coordination.
- **Certification expiration alerts** before a license lapses.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is an example of business automation?**

A common example is a lead follow-up sequence: when someone fills out a contact form, an automated system emails them, adds them to a CRM, and schedules a follow-up reminder, all without a salesperson doing it by hand.

**Which business tasks are easiest to automate first?**

Repetitive, rules-based tasks that happen the same way every time are the easiest starting point, such as appointment reminders, follow-up emails, and document collection, before moving on to more judgment-heavy work.

**Can business automation examples be customized to a specific industry?**

Yes. While the underlying pattern (trigger, connected tools, automatic action) is similar across industries, the specific workflow is usually built around the tools and steps unique to that industry and role.
