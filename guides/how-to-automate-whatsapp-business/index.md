# How to Automate WhatsApp Business Messages

> How to automate WhatsApp for business: greeting and away messages, quick replies, the WhatsApp Business Platform for reminders and notifications, chatbots…

How to automate WhatsApp for business: greeting and away messages, quick replies, the WhatsApp Business Platform for reminders and notifications, chatbots, and the rules to follow.

LJ Web ManagementAutomation team Updated October 25, 2026 4 min read

Key takeaways

- The free WhatsApp Business app offers greeting messages, away messages, quick replies, and labels.
- Reminders, notifications, and integrations with your CRM require the WhatsApp Business Platform (API), usually through a provider.
- Business-initiated messages outside a customer's recent conversation use pre-approved templates and need opt-in.
- Automate confirmations, reminders, and first replies; keep real conversations with people.

**You can automate WhatsApp in two ways: the free WhatsApp Business app offers greeting messages, away messages, and quick replies, while the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API, usually through a provider) lets software send reminders and notifications, run chatbots, and connect WhatsApp to your CRM or booking system.** Business-initiated messages need customer opt-in and pre-approved templates.

In many countries, WhatsApp is where customers already talk to businesses. This guide explains what you can automate, which option you need, and how to stay within the rules. WhatsApp's features and policies change regularly, so confirm the details with WhatsApp's current business documentation before you build.

## Option 1: The WhatsApp Business app

The free app is ideal for small businesses handling conversations by hand, with a few helpful automations:

- **Greeting message:** an automatic welcome when a new customer messages you, or after a period of no contact.
- **Away message:** an automatic reply outside your business hours, with when you will respond and a link to book.
- **Quick replies:** saved answers to common questions, inserted with a shortcut.
- **Labels:** organize chats by stage, such as new lead, booked, or paid.
- **Catalog:** show products or services customers can browse in the chat.

The app does not connect to your other systems, so it cannot send reminders when an appointment is booked in your scheduler, for example.

## Option 2: The WhatsApp Business Platform (API)

For automation connected to your other tools, you need the Business Platform. Most small businesses access it through a provider (a messaging platform, CRM, or customer service tool with WhatsApp built in) rather than building on it directly. With it, you can:

| Automation | Trigger |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmations and reminders | Appointment booked in your scheduler |
| Order and delivery updates | Status change in your store or delivery system |
| Instant replies to new leads | Form submission or ad click-to-chat |
| Payment reminders with a link | Invoice due or overdue |
| FAQ chatbot with handoff | Customer sends a question |
| Feedback and review requests | Job or visit completed |

## The rules to know

- **Opt-in:** customers must agree to receive WhatsApp messages from your business before you message them first.
- **Templates:** messages your business starts, such as reminders and updates, generally use templates that WhatsApp has reviewed and approved.
- **Conversation window:** after a customer messages you, you can reply freely for a limited period; after that, you need a template again.
- **Pricing:** the Platform charges by message type and country, and providers may add fees.
- **Quality:** if many people block or report your messages, your sending ability can be limited. Only send messages people expect and value.

## Designing a WhatsApp chatbot

- Start with a menu of your top five reasons people message you
- Answer each from approved information, with links where useful
- Collect key details (name, service, preferred time) before handing off
- Offer "talk to a person" at every step, and route it to your team inbox
- If you add AI, ground it in your own content and tell it to hand off when unsure

## WhatsApp, SMS, or email?

Use the channel your customers prefer. In regions where WhatsApp dominates, it often gets faster responses than email. In the United States, SMS is still the default for reminders and quick replies. Many businesses use both: a text for the reminder, WhatsApp for customers who start conversations there. If you are on the free app, turn on greeting and away messages and create quick replies for your ten most common questions today. When you are ready to connect WhatsApp to bookings or your CRM, choose a provider that supports the Business Platform and start with confirmations and reminders. ## Frequently asked questions

**Can you automate WhatsApp Business messages?**

Yes. The WhatsApp Business app includes greeting messages, away messages, and quick replies. For automated reminders, order updates, chatbots, and integrations with your CRM or booking system, you need the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), typically accessed through an approved provider or an automation platform that connects to one.

**What is the difference between the WhatsApp Business app and the WhatsApp Business Platform?**

The app is free and made for small businesses managing chats on a phone or computer, with basic automation. The platform (API) is for sending messages from software at scale, connecting to other systems, and building chatbots, and it has usage-based pricing and messaging rules.

**Can I send automatic WhatsApp reminders?**

Yes, through the WhatsApp Business Platform. Reminders and other business-initiated messages generally use templates approved by WhatsApp, and customers need to have opted in to receive messages from you.

**How much does WhatsApp Business automation cost?**

The WhatsApp Business app is free. The Business Platform charges for messages based on their type and the recipient's country, and providers may add their own fees. Check current pricing with WhatsApp and your provider.
