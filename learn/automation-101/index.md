> A free, eight-lesson course on business automation: how workflows work, what to automate first, how apps connect, choosing a platform, testing, and measuring ROI.

Eight short lessons that take you from “we should automate something” to a clear plan for your first workflow. No technical background needed, no sign-up, and a quick knowledge check at the end of every lesson.

## About this course

**Is this automation course really free?**

Yes. Every lesson is free to read, with no sign-up. Your progress is saved in your browser so you can pick up where you left off.

**Do I need technical skills?**

No. The course explains every concept in plain language. Where a lesson touches on technical ideas such as APIs or webhooks, it focuses on what they do for you rather than how to code them.

**How long does the course take?**

About an hour in total. Each lesson takes around eight minutes and ends with a three-question knowledge check.
