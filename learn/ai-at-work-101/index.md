## About this course

**Is this AI course free?**

Yes. All six lessons are free with no sign-up, and your progress is saved in your browser.

**Do I need to know how to code?**

No. The course is written for business owners and teams. It explains how AI works at the level you need to use it well, and shows how no-code automation tools can run AI steps for you.

**Should I take Automation 101 first?**

It helps but is not required. [Automation 101](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/learn/automation-101/) explains triggers, actions, and how apps connect, which makes lesson three of this course easier to follow.

**Which AI tools does the course use?**

The lessons are tool-neutral. The techniques apply to the major AI assistants and model APIs, and to automation platforms such as Zapier, Make, n8n, and Power Automate that can call them.
