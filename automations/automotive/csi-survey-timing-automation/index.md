How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The manufacturer-required Customer Satisfaction Index survey is automatically sent after a purchase or service visit, timed to the specific window that historically produces the highest response rate and the fairest reflection of the actual experience, rather than firing off immediately or getting delayed until it's irrelevant.
2. Any response indicating a problem is automatically routed to the CSI coordinator for same-day resolution before the survey is finalized, since a dissatisfied customer who gets a fast, personal fix often ends up submitting a far better score than the one they would have given in the moment of frustration.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a csi coordinator is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation
