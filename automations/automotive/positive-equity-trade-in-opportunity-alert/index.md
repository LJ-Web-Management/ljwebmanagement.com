How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically identifies customers who now have positive equity in their current loan and are strong trade-in candidates, alerting the used car manager to reach out with a specific, well-timed offer instead of a genuinely good trade-in opportunity sitting invisible in loan data nobody's actively monitoring.
2. Customers who didn't realize they had equity to work with are often receptive to a trade-up conversation they weren't expecting, and this kind of proactive, data-driven outreach consistently surfaces deals that a purely inbound sales process would never have found on its own.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, an used car manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
