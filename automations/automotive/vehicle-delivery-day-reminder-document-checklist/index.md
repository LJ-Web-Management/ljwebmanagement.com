## What this automation does, step by step

1. The day before a scheduled vehicle delivery or pickup, the customer automatically receives a reminder along with a checklist of exactly what to bring, driver's license, proof of insurance, the trade-in title if applicable, so the delivery coordinator isn't scrambling to track down a missing document while the customer waits in the finance office.
2. Deliveries with a pre-sent checklist consistently close faster than deliveries where the paperwork gap is discovered on the spot, and the customer walks away with a smoother, more professional final impression of the exact moment that shapes most of their loyalty going forward.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a delivery coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
