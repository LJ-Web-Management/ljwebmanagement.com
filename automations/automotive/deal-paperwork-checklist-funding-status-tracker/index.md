How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a deal is desked, the system generates a complete checklist of every document required for that specific deal (based on the lender, product mix, and customer type) and sends it to the F&I manager's deal queue.
2. As documents are received and uploaded, the checklist updates in real time, and the F&I manager knows exactly what's missing before they sit down with the customer.
3. After signing, the system tracks funding status with each lender and alerts the accounting office the moment a deal is funded and ready to post.
