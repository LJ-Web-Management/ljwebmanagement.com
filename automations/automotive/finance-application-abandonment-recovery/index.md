How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A customer who starts but doesn't finish an online credit application automatically triggers a follow-up within the hour offering help completing it, either by text with a direct link back to the saved form or a call from a credit analyst, instead of the abandoned application simply vanishing into an analytics report nobody checks.
2. Abandonment points are tracked automatically, so if customers consistently stall on the same field, income verification, for example, that friction point gets flagged for the finance team to fix.
3. Recovering even a portion of these stalled applications turns a soft lead into a closed deal that would otherwise have gone nowhere.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a credit analyst has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
