How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For connected vehicles reporting live telematics data, the system uses actual mileage and driving patterns rather than just elapsed calendar time to trigger service reminders, catching a high-mileage commuter who needs an oil change sooner than the standard six-month mark and holding off on a reminder for a vehicle that's barely been driven.
2. The service scheduler sees a far more accurate due list than one built purely on time-based rules, and customers get reminders that actually match how they use their vehicle instead of a generic date that either arrives too early or, worse, too late.
