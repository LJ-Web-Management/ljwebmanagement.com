How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. From the moment a vehicle is dropped off, the customer receives automated status updates at each stage of the repair process (vehicle received, inspection complete with recommended services, repair in progress, and vehicle ready for pickup) all via text without the service advisor having to make a single phone call.
2. Vehicles that have been ready for more than four hours but haven't been picked up trigger a reminder text to the customer and a flag to the service advisor, keeping the service bay from becoming a parking lot.

- DMS (e.g., CDK Global or Reynolds & Reynolds)
