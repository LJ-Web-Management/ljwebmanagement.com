How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A review request goes out automatically a few hours after the repair order is marked picked up, timed to when the customer's experience with the service department is still fresh rather than a generic monthly blast sent to everyone regardless of when they were actually in.
2. Customers who indicate a poor experience are routed privately to the customer experience coordinator instead of straight to a public review, while satisfied customers are routed directly to a one-click Google review link, keeping the public rating climbing while problems get caught and resolved before they go public.

Tools it connects

- Shop management software (e.g., Tekmetric or Shop-Ware)
- Zapier or Make.com
- NiceJob or Podium (review routing)
- Google Business Profile API

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a customer experience coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
