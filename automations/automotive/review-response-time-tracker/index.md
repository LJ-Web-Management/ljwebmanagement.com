How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation tracks how quickly the dealership responds to online reviews and alerts management whenever response time falls behind the target, protecting the online reputation instead of reviews sitting unanswered for days simply because nobody was specifically watching the clock on response time.
2. A fast, thoughtful response to both positive and negative reviews is one of the strongest signals shoppers use to judge a dealership before ever walking in, and consistent response time, not just eventual response, is what separates dealerships that manage their reputation actively from ones that only react.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a reputation specialist remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
