How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers are automatically texted at each stage of their vehicle's visit, the moment the initial inspection is complete, whenever additional recommended work needs approval with a direct link to approve or decline, and again the instant the vehicle is ready for pickup.
2. Approvals captured by text flow straight back into the repair order, so the technician can start additional work within minutes instead of the car sitting untouched while a service advisor tries to reach the customer by phone.
3. The service drive's phone lines get noticeably quieter once customers stop needing to call in just to check status.
