How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a scheduled test drive is marked complete, the customer automatically receives a follow-up sequence over the next few days with details on the specific vehicle they drove, comparable inventory, and available financing options tailored to what a sales consultant learned during the drive.
2. Customers who engage with the follow-up but haven't returned to the lot within a week are automatically flagged for a personal check-in call, keeping momentum going during the exact window when a test drive's initial excitement is most likely to translate into an actual purchase decision rather than fading into "we'll think about it."

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a sales consultant remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
