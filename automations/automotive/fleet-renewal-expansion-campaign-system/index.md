How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Twelve months before the end of a fleet account's vehicle replacement cycle, the system automatically triggers an outreach sequence from the fleet rep, a summary of the account's current vehicles, a preview of upcoming model changes, and a link to schedule a renewal consultation.
2. Fleet managers who engage receive a custom proposal generated from their account data; those who don't respond are re-queued for a phone call.
3. Between cycles, the fleet rep receives alerts when a tracked account adds employees, expands locations, or posts about growth, signals that a fleet expansion conversation is timely.

Without automation, a fleet sales representative has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.
