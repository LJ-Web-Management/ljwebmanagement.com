How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A scheduled vehicle appraisal appointment automatically triggers a confirmation and reminder to the customer, including a clear list of documents to bring, the title, registration, and a spare key if available, so the used car manager doesn't lose a slot to a customer who shows up without what's needed to actually complete an appraisal.
2. No-shows on appraisal appointments drop once the reminder includes the practical checklist, and appraisals that do happen move faster since the paperwork is already in hand instead of requiring a second visit to finish.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an used car manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
