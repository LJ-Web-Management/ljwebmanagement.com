How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a repair estimate is prepared in the body shop, it's automatically sent to the customer for review and, at the same time, submitted directly to their insurance claims adjuster, cutting out the delay that used to come from a body shop manager manually faxing or emailing the same estimate to two separate parties on two separate timelines.
2. Adjuster approvals and any requested revisions flow back into the same estimate automatically, so a supplement doesn't sit waiting in an inbox, and the whole approval cycle that used to take several days routinely closes within one.
