    FAQ

    Automotive automation questions

    What automotive workflows can be automated?

    The 51 automotive automations in our catalog cover lead follow-up & sales, customer service & communication, and scheduling, reminders & dispatch, among other areas, for roles like Used Car Manager, Service Advisor, and Shop Foreman. Popular starting points include Lead Follow-Up & Test Drive Scheduling System, Repair Order Status & Vehicle Ready Notification System, Maintenance Reminder & Lapsed Customer Win-Back System.

    Do I need to replace my current automotive software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in automotive automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Slack, and ActiveCampaign, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does an automotive automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
