How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When the new model year of a vehicle a past customer already owns is unveiled, that customer automatically receives an announcement highlighting what's new, paired with a trade-in incentive designed to bring them back in before the current model year's residual value starts to slide.
2. The dealership sales manager gets a report of which past customers responded and booked a visit, sharpening which model refreshes are actually worth a dedicated campaign versus a generic mass email, and this single automated trigger consistently surfaces upgrade-ready customers who weren't actively shopping until the message reminded them the new version existed.
