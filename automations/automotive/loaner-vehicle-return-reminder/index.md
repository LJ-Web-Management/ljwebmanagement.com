How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers driving a loaner or rental vehicle automatically receive a reminder text as the scheduled return date approaches, and a second reminder if the vehicle hasn't been returned by the due date, instead of the loaner fleet slowly shrinking as vehicles quietly go overdue and untracked.
2. The rental coordinator gets a live dashboard of every loaner out, who has it, and when it's due back, replacing a paper log or spreadsheet that only ever reflected reality if someone remembered to update it, and overdue loaner days drop sharply once the reminder is automatic instead of dependent on someone noticing.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a rental coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
