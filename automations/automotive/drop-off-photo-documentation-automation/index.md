How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Photos taken of the vehicle at service drop-off are automatically attached to the repair order, creating documentation that protects both the shop and the customer from later disputes over pre-existing damage, instead of the shop foreman relying on a written note that's easy to dispute after the fact.
2. Every vehicle now has a timestamped visual record from the moment it arrived, and disagreements about whether a scratch or dent was already there before service dropped sharply once the photo, not a memory or a note, is what settles the question.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a shop foreman has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
