How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Leads who saved a specific vehicle search are automatically emailed and texted the moment a matching unit arrives on the lot, often before it's even fully listed on the website, giving serious shoppers first crack at a vehicle instead of finding out once it's already sold.
2. The inventory manager gets a report of which saved searches are generating the most matches, useful signal for what to prioritize on the next buy, and a meaningful share of these alerts convert into a same-week visit since the customer is contacted at the exact moment their specific vehicle becomes available.
