How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers who ordered a special-order part automatically receive a text the moment it arrives at the parts counter, along with a direct link to schedule the installation, instead of the part sitting on a shelf while the customer has no idea it's already in.
2. The parts manager gets a weekly report of parts that arrived but haven't been claimed or scheduled after a set number of days, catching stalled orders before they turn into wasted inventory sitting in the back room.
3. Customers consistently comment that being told the moment a part lands, rather than having to call and check, is one of the most noticeable service improvements.
