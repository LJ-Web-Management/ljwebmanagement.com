How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers automatically receive a milestone thank-you or offer when they reach an ownership anniversary with their vehicle, one year, three years since purchase, instead of the relationship going entirely quiet between the original sale and whenever the customer eventually comes back in for service or a trade.
2. The customer retention specialist gets a steady, low-effort touchpoint that keeps the dealership's name in front of owners at a meaningful moment, and anniversary outreach consistently generates service visits and trade-in conversations that wouldn't have happened without the prompt.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a customer retention specialist, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
