How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a trade-in or used vehicle in inventory becomes eligible for certified pre-owned status after passing the required inspection, the automation automatically updates the vehicle's listing to reflect CPO status and alerts sales staff to the added value and financing benefits now available on that unit.
2. Vehicles used to sit listed as standard used inventory for days after actually qualifying for CPO simply because no one manually rechecked eligibility after inspection results came back, and closing that gap means the vehicle is marketed at its true, higher value from the moment it's earned rather than an arbitrary point later.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an used car manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
