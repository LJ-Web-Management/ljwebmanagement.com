How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers approaching a repeat purchase milestone, buying their third vehicle from the dealership for example, are automatically identified from purchase history and sent a personalized loyalty offer recognizing their continued business, rather than being treated exactly like a first-time shopper walking in cold.
2. The customer retention specialist gets a running list of loyal customers likely to be back in market soon based on typical trade-in cycles, letting outreach happen proactively instead of waiting for the customer to show up on their own, and repeat buyers who feel recognized report noticeably higher satisfaction than those treated as a brand-new lead every time.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a customer retention specialist has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
