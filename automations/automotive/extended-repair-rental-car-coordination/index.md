How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a repair estimate indicates a turnaround long enough to require a rental, the automation automatically flags the job and coordinates a rental car arrangement with the customer ahead of drop-off, confirming pickup timing and coverage details before the customer arrives without a way home.
2. The rental coordinator no longer has to manually cross-reference every open repair order against expected completion time to catch which customers will need a rental, since the system flags it automatically the moment an estimate crosses the threshold, and customers show up already knowing a rental is arranged instead of being surprised by the news at the counter.
