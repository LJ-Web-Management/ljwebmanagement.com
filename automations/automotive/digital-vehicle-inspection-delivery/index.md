How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a technician completes a multi-point inspection using a tablet-based digital inspection tool, photos and short video clips of any flagged issues (worn brake pads, a cracked belt, low tread depth) are automatically compiled with the technician's written notes into a single report and texted to the customer while the vehicle is still on the lift.
2. Customers see exactly what the technician saw instead of taking a stranger's word for it over the phone, and they can approve or decline each recommended repair directly from the report.
3. Approval rates on recommended work climb because customers are looking at their own worn brake pads instead of a verbal description, and the service advisor spends less time explaining and more time closing.
