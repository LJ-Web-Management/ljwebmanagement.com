How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Any inbound call to sales or service that goes unanswered automatically triggers a text to the caller within seconds, apologizing for missing them and offering a direct scheduling link, so a service inquiry or sales lead doesn't simply hang up and call the next dealership on the list.
2. The BDC representative gets a same-day report of every missed call and whether it converted into a booked appointment, giving hard visibility into a category of lost business that used to disappear without a trace the moment the phone stopped ringing.
