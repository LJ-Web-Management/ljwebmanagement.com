How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Using current market data (auction results, regional listing prices, and the dealership's own acquisition needs), this automation runs a monthly scan of past customers' vehicles on file and identifies anyone whose trade-in value has risen meaningfully since their last visit or purchase.
2. Those customers receive a personalized text or email with an actual estimated trade-in offer for their specific vehicle, not a generic "we want your car" message, along with a link to schedule an appraisal.
3. Customers sitting on a vehicle worth more than they realize get a concrete reason to come back in, and the dealership fills its used inventory pipeline with qualified trade-in leads instead of relying only on walk-ins.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, an used car manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation
