How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on the mileage and service interval recorded at each visit, this automation calculates when each customer is due for their next oil change, tire rotation, or recommended service and sends a reminder text two weeks before that estimated date.
2. Customers who haven't returned in six months receive a win-back offer automatically, and any customer who leaves a negative review online is immediately flagged for a personal call from the owner.
3. The shop's relationship with every customer is actively managed even when the shop is buried in cars.
