How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a technician finishes the diagnostic scan, this automation texts the customer a link to a digital estimate broken down by recommended, required, and deferred repairs, letting them tap to approve or decline each line item from their phone instead of waiting on a callback from the service advisor.
2. Approved work is pushed straight back into the shop's work order automatically, and the moment the last approved item is signed off by the technician, the customer gets a ready-for-pickup text with the final invoice attached.
3. Estimate approval time drops from hours of phone tag to minutes, and the service advisor's desk phone stops ringing every ten minutes with "is it done yet" calls.
