How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers who saved a specific vehicle listing on the dealership website automatically get notified the moment its price is reduced, prompting a second look from a shopper who may have passed on it purely over budget the first time around.
2. The internet sales coordinator can see which price-dropped vehicles generate the most re-engagement, useful signal for pricing strategy on similar inventory going forward, and a vehicle that's been sitting on the lot longer than the average turn time often finds its buyer through exactly this kind of automatic second chance rather than a fresh round of paid advertising.

Manually, an internet sales coordinator is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.
