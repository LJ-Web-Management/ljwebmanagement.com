How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When multiple vehicles belonging to the same business fleet account are coming due for service around the same time, the system automatically batches them into a single scheduling outreach to the fleet's point of contact instead of sending a dozen separate reminders for a dozen separate vehicles.
2. The fleet sales representative can see at a glance which accounts have vehicles clustering toward service, letting the shop plan bay capacity around a fleet visit instead of being surprised by it, and fleet customers appreciate getting one coordinated message rather than a flood of individual vehicle alerts.
