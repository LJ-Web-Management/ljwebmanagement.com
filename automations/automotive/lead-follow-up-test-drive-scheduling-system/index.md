How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every new internet lead (from the website, third-party listing sites, or ad campaigns) is immediately acknowledged with a personalized text and email, entered into the CRM with a lead score, and assigned to the first available salesperson.
2. If the salesperson hasn't made contact within two hours, the manager receives an escalation alert.
3. Leads who engage are guided straight to a self-scheduling link for a test drive appointment, cutting the average time from inquiry to appointment and dramatically increasing the percentage of leads that ever reach the lot.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a dealership sales manager is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry

- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
