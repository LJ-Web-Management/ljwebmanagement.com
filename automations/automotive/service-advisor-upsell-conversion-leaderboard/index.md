How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically tracks each service advisor's upsell conversion rate and surfaces a weekly leaderboard-style report to the fixed ops director, identifying coaching opportunities instead of upsell performance living only anecdotally in whoever happens to be watching the floor that week.
2. Advisors can see how their own numbers stack up, and a director walks into a coaching conversation with actual data on which specific service, which specific advisor, is underperforming, turning a vague sense that "upsells feel low lately" into a targeted, effective coaching plan.
