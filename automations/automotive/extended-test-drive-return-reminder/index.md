How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers given an extended, overnight test drive vehicle automatically receive a return reminder before the loaner is due back at the dealership, instead of the sales consultant relying on the customer to remember an appointment time given verbally a full day earlier.
2. Extended test drives are a powerful sales tool precisely because customers get to live with a vehicle before deciding, and a simple automatic reminder keeps that valuable inventory cycling back on schedule instead of quietly running late and cutting into the next customer's chance to try the same vehicle.
