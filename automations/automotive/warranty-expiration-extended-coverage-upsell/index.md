How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a factory or extended warranty approaches its expiration date, the affected customer automatically receives a notice explaining exactly what coverage is ending and when, paired with an offer to purchase extended coverage before it lapses, instead of a warranty quietly expiring with the customer finding out the hard way at their next repair.
2. The warranty administrator gets a rolling 90-day list of upcoming expirations to prioritize for personal outreach on higher-value vehicles, and extended warranty attachment rates climb meaningfully once the offer reaches customers while the decision still feels timely instead of after coverage has already run out.
