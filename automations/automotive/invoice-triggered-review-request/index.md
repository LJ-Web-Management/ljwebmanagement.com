How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a repair invoice is marked paid in the shop management system, this automation waits roughly two hours (enough time for the customer to drive away and settle in, not so long the visit feels distant) before sending a text asking for a quick Google review, with a direct link that drops the customer straight into the review form.
2. Customers who report a low satisfaction score in the initial quick-rating question are routed privately to the shop owner instead of being pushed toward a public review, catching problems before they turn into a bad post.
3. Review volume grows on a predictable weekly cadence instead of depending on the rare customer who thinks to leave one unprompted, and the shop builds a public track record it can point to when competing for new customers.
