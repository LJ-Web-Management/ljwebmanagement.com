How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A website visitor who requests a trade-in value automatically receives their estimate within minutes, pulled from live market valuation data, along with a clear next step to schedule an in-person appraisal, instead of the lead sitting untouched until someone gets around to calling.
2. Leads who open the estimate but don't book an appointment automatically get a follow-up call task assigned to the used car manager two days later.
3. Trade leads handled this quickly convert to an actual appraisal appointment at a far higher rate than leads left to follow up on their own.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an used car manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
