How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A few weeks after taking delivery of a new vehicle, the customer automatically receives a follow-up offer for accessories or add-ons that complement what they bought, all-weather mats, a roof rack, remote start, timed to when the initial purchase excitement has settled into daily use and the customer has a clearer sense of what they'd actually want to add.
2. Accessory sales generated through this follow-up are tracked back to the original delivery, giving the sales team real data on which vehicle types and trims drive the most add-on revenue instead of accessories being sold purely on the spot at delivery or not at all.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a sales consultant is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
