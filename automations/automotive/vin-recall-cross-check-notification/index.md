How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a week, this automation runs every customer VIN in the shop's database against the NHTSA recall API and manufacturer recall feeds, flagging any vehicle with an open, unaddressed recall.
2. Affected customers are automatically sent a text and email explaining the specific recall in plain language, along with a link to book the repair, which for many recalls is performed at no cost to the customer.
3. What used to require someone manually checking VINs one at a time, or customers finding out about a recall only after an incident, now runs as a standing weekly check that keeps the shop ahead of safety issues and turns recall traffic into an opportunity to build trust instead of just processing a mandatory fix.
