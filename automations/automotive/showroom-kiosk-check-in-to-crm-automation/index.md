How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Visitors who check in at a showroom kiosk are automatically logged into the CRM with their visit reason, triggering an automatic follow-up if they leave without making a purchase, instead of a walk-in visitor's information staying trapped on a paper sign-in sheet with no connection to the sales process at all.
2. The internet sales coordinator gets a same-day follow-up task for every visitor who didn't buy, capturing showroom traffic with the same discipline the dealership already applies to online leads, and walk-in visits that used to just evaporate if they didn't convert on the spot now feed directly into an actual follow-up pipeline.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an internet sales coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
