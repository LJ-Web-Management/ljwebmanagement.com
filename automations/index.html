    Privacy and cookie settings

    Analytics are on unless you turn them off. You can change this at any time from Your Privacy Choices at the bottom of every page.

    Your browser sent a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, so optional analytics are off. You can still turn them on below if you want to.

    Strictly necessary

    Remember your privacy choices (a first-party cookie named lj_consent, kept for 12 months) and on-device preferences such as course progress and the blog newsletter pop-up (browser local storage). We use these only to apply your choices, never to identify or track you.

    Always on

    Google Analytics measures visits, pages, devices, and approximate location. Microsoft Clarity records clicks, scrolling, and page interactions to create heatmaps and session recordings. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off.

    Selling, sharing, and targeted advertising

    We do not sell or share personal information, use advertising cookies, or engage in targeted advertising. Turning analytics off stops all optional measurement.
