How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every week, this automation pulls billed hours by project and phase from the project accounting system, compares them against the contracted fee and the percent-complete estimate, and updates a live profitability dashboard the principal can review in minutes.
2. Projects where fee burn is outpacing percent complete by more than 10% are flagged for a project manager conversation before the budget is exhausted.
3. Projects approaching the contracted fee threshold trigger an automatic prompt to evaluate whether a supplemental services request is warranted, turning budget management from a monthly accounting exercise into a weekly operational decision.
