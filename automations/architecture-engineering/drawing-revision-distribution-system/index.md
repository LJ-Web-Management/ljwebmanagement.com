How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a drawing set is revised and marked final, this automation automatically distributes the updated version to every stakeholder on the project's distribution list, logs exactly who received which revision number and when, and flags anyone still confirmed to be working from an outdated set.
2. Field crews and consultants stop building or coordinating off drawings that were already superseded two revisions ago.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a design coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
