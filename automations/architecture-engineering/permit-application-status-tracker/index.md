How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks every submitted permit application against the issuing jurisdiction's typical review timeline and automatically checks for status updates, notifying the project manager the moment a permit is approved, rejected, or flagged for additional information.
2. Instead of someone manually logging into a municipal portal every few days to check, the status change reaches the project manager the same day it posts.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a project manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
