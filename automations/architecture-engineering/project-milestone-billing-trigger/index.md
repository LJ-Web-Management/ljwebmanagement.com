How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a project reaches each contractually defined milestone, schematic design complete, design development complete, construction documents issued, this automation automatically generates the corresponding invoice based on the fee schedule written into the contract and routes it for internal review before it's sent.
2. Billing keeps pace with actual progress instead of trailing weeks behind because someone forgot to generate the invoice when the milestone was hit.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a principal is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
