## Architecture & Engineering automation questions

**What architecture and engineering workflows can be automated?**

The 10 architecture and engineering automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, compliance, documents & deadlines, and reporting & analytics, among other areas, for roles like Project Architect, Project Manager, and Civil Engineer. Popular starting points include [Drawing Revision Control & Submittal Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/architecture-engineering/drawing-revision-control-submittal-tracker/), [Permit Application Status & Review Comment Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/architecture-engineering/permit-application-status-review-comment-tracker/), [Project Profitability & Budget Variance Dashboard](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/architecture-engineering/project-profitability-budget-variance-dashboard/).

**Do I need to replace my current architecture and engineering software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in architecture and engineering automations are Slack, Airtable, Google Sheets, and Google Calendar, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does an architecture and engineering automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
