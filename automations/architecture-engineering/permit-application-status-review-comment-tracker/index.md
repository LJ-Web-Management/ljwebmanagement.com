How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a permit application is submitted to a reviewing agency, this automation logs the submission date, expected review clock, and assigned contact at each agency, and begins sending weekly status check reminders to the project engineer to follow up.
2. When review comments are received, they are automatically logged with the date received, parsed into individual comment items, and assigned to the responsible discipline for response, with a deadline calculated from the agency's resubmittal window.
3. No comment goes untracked, no agency deadline gets missed because the project engineer was focused on another submission.
