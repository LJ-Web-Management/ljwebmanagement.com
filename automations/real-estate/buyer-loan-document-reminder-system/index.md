How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a financing deadline approaches, buyers automatically receive reminders about outstanding documents their lender still needs, pay stubs, bank statements, an explanation letter, keeping the loan process on schedule without the loan progress coordinator having to personally chase the buyer down for paperwork the lender is waiting on.
2. Buyers who fall behind on document delivery are the single most common cause of a closing date slipping, and catching that lag early with an automatic nudge, rather than discovering it during a panicked call from the lender a week before closing, keeps far more transactions closing exactly on the date everyone originally agreed to.
