How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a visitor submits an inquiry on the website, this automation fires an instant text and email confirmation within seconds, letting the lead know a real person is already on it.
2. Behind the scenes, the system reads the inquiry's property type and location, cross-references the team's coverage map, and routes the contact to the correct agent automatically, whether that's the listing specialist for a downtown condo or the buyer's agent who covers the suburbs.
3. The lead's name, phone, email, and source campaign are logged into the CRM in the same motion, with a task created for the assigned agent to call within the hour.
4. No inquiry sits in a shared inbox waiting for someone to notice it, and the brokerage's average first-touch time drops from hours to under two minutes.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an inside sales agent (isa) manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
