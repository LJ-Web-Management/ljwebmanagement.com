How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. On the exact anniversary of a client's home purchase, the automation automatically sends a personalized check-in email that includes a current market update for their specific neighborhood, an estimated change in their home's value since closing, and a genuine note thanking them for their business.
2. The client relations coordinator doesn't have to track a single date by hand; the system pulls closing dates directly from the transaction records and queues every anniversary email a year in advance.
3. Clients who reply or click through are flagged for a personal follow-up call rather than another automated touch.
4. A relationship that used to fade quietly after the closing gift now gets a warm, well-timed touchpoint every year without anyone remembering to schedule it.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a client relations coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
