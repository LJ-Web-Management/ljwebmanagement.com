How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Buyer leads who show ongoing activity, saved searches, showing requests, repeated site visits, but don't have a pre-approval letter on file are automatically flagged after a set number of days of engagement without one.
2. The mortgage coordinator's automation sends a friendly nudge explaining why pre-approval matters in a competitive market, paired with a direct referral to a preferred lender and a one-click link to start the application.
3. Buyers who don't respond to the first nudge get a second, softer reminder a week later, and any buyer who completes pre-approval is automatically updated in the CRM and moved into active showing outreach.
4. Buyers who used to lose out on a home because they weren't pre-approved when it was time to write an offer are now caught and redirected weeks earlier.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a mortgage coordinator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
