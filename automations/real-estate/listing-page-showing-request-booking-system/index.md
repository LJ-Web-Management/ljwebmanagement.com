How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A buyer browsing a listing page can request a showing directly from the site instead of calling or texting the agent.
2. The automation checks the assigned agent's real calendar for open slots, confirms the requested time against the seller's showing window, and books the appointment automatically, no manual back-and-forth required.
3. Both the buyer and agent receive an instant confirmation, and a reminder text goes out the morning of the showing to cut down on no-shows.
4. If the requested time is unavailable, the system immediately offers the next three open slots instead of leaving the buyer waiting on a callback.
5. What used to take three or four rounds of phone tag to lock in a fifteen-minute showing now takes under a minute of the buyer's time.
