How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Visitors scan a QR code at the door instead of signing a paper clipboard, entering their name, phone, email, and what first attracted them to the listing directly into the CRM.
2. Each visitor receives an automatic thank-you text within minutes of leaving, and a short feedback request goes out that same evening asking for their honest impression of the price and condition.
3. Visitors who mention financing questions or ask about "similar homes" are automatically flagged for a same-day call from the listing agent, while the seller receives a same-day summary of turnout, average time spent per visitor, and overall feedback themes, replacing the old habit of guessing how an open house went from a stack of illegible sign-in sheets.
