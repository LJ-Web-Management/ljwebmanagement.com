How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a buyer has several showings scheduled in one day, the automation automatically compiles the addresses into an optimized driving route and sends the finished itinerary straight to the buyer's phone, complete with lockbox codes and showing instructions for each stop, instead of the tour logistics coordinator manually mapping out the day's order and texting directions between appointments.
2. Buyers show up on time to every stop and agents stop losing minutes at each house looking up the next address, turning a six-house Saturday into a smooth, well-paced day instead of a scramble.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a tour logistics coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
