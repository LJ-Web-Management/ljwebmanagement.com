How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When public records show an updated tax assessment on a home, the automation automatically notifies the owner, often a past client, with the new figure and what it means for their equity, keeping the agent's name in front of them as a genuinely useful resource instead of another unread marketing email.
2. The farming and prospecting specialist gets a report of which homeowners engaged with the notification, a strong signal of who's thinking about their home's value and worth a personal check-in call.

- Public records API (e.g., county assessor data feed)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Mailchimp or ActiveCampaign](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/mailchimp/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a farming & prospecting specialist manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
