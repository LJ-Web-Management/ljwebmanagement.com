    How it works

    What this automation does, step by step

    1. After each showing wraps up, the buyer's agent or the buyer directly automatically receives a short feedback request text asking about their honest impression of price, condition, and overall interest, timed to arrive while the showing is still fresh.

    2. Responses flow automatically into a shared report, and the feedback coordinator's automation compiles every showing's feedback into a single weekly summary the seller can review, showing trends like recurring price objections or condition concerns across multiple showings instead of one-off comments.

    3. Sellers who used to only hear feedback when an agent remembered to relay it verbally now get a consistent, organized picture of how buyers are actually reacting to their home.

    4. Listings with feedback-driven price or staging adjustments made within the first two weeks tend to sell measurably faster than those where feedback trends go unnoticed.

    Tools it connects
    • Twilio (SMS feedback request)
    • Zapier or Make.com
    • Jotform (feedback survey)
    • Google Sheets or Airtable (feedback compilation)
    • Gmail or Outlook (seller report delivery)

    Using different software? We build around what you already have, so you never have to switch tools to automate.

    The manual way vs. the automated way

    Without automation

    Left unautomated, a feedback coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

    With this automation

    • Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
    • Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
    • Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
    • Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
