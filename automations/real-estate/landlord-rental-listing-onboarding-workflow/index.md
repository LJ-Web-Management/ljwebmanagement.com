How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a landlord signs up to list a rental, the automation automatically walks them through a guided workflow collecting property details, photos, and lease terms needed to build the listing, instead of the rental onboarding specialist chasing down the same information across several separate email threads.
2. Landlords complete the intake at their own pace on their own schedule, and a listing that used to take days to assemble from scattered information now goes live within hours of the landlord finishing the guided form, getting the unit in front of renters faster and starting the rent clock sooner.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a rental onboarding specialist has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
