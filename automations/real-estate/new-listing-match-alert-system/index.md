How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every buyer's saved search criteria, price range, beds and baths, neighborhood, must-have features, is stored against a live MLS feed, and the moment a matching property hits the market the buyer receives an email and text with photos, price, and a direct link to schedule a showing, often before it shows up in a generic Zillow alert.
2. Buyers who open three or more alerts for homes in the same subdivision are automatically flagged to their agent as "hot" for a personal follow-up call.
3. Serious buyers in competitive markets can lose a home to a same-day offer, so shrinking the alert delay from the typical 24-hour MLS sync down to under 10 minutes has turned "I saw it on Zillow first" into a rare complaint instead of a routine one.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a buyer's agent spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
