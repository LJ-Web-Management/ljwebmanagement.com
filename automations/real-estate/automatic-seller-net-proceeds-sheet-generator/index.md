How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a listing agreement is signed, the automation pulls the agreed list price, existing mortgage payoff estimate, and typical local closing costs: commission, transfer taxes, title fees, and prorated items, and automatically generates an estimated seller net proceeds sheet in the brokerage's branded format.
2. The listing coordinator receives the completed document within minutes instead of spending twenty or thirty minutes building it manually in a spreadsheet, and the seller gets an emailed copy the same day the agreement is signed, setting realistic expectations from day one.
3. If the list price changes during negotiations, the net sheet automatically regenerates with updated numbers.
4. What used to be a manual calculation prone to copy-paste errors is now a consistent, accurate document produced the moment it's needed.
