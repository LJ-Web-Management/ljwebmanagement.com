How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new agent signs their independent contractor agreement, this automation automatically triggers the brokerage's full onboarding sequence: CRM and MLS account setup requests, access to the transaction management platform, welcome materials and the agent handbook, and a scheduled orientation call, all assigned as tracked tasks instead of a scattered checklist someone works through when they get a chance.
2. The HR onboarding specialist gets a real-time dashboard showing exactly which onboarding steps are complete and which are still pending for every new agent in the pipeline.
3. The new agent receives a welcome email with logins and next steps as each system is provisioned, rather than waiting days for access to trickle in.
4. Onboarding that used to take a new agent's first week and a half to fully complete now wraps up in two to three business days.
