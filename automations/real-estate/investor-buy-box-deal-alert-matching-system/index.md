How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every investor client's saved buy-box: price range, target cap rate, property type, and preferred zip codes, is stored against a live feed of new listings, and the moment a property matches every criterion, the investor receives an instant email with the address, key numbers, and a rough cap rate estimate calculated automatically from the listing price and local rent data.
2. The investor relations manager no longer has to manually cross-reference a spreadsheet of buy-boxes against the daily hot sheet; the matching happens continuously in the background across every active investor at once.
3. Investors who reply expressing interest are automatically flagged in the CRM for same-day follow-up.
4. In a market where a good investment property can go under contract within hours, shrinking the alert delay to minutes instead of a next-day email digest has directly resulted in more won deals.
