How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. From the day a contract is ratified, buyers and sellers are enrolled in an automated milestone sequence tied to the actual dates in the contract, inspection deadline, appraisal ordered, appraisal received, loan commitment, final walkthrough, and closing day, with a text or email firing the moment each stage completes in the transaction management platform.
2. If a milestone is 48 hours from its deadline and still marked incomplete, the coordinator gets an internal alert instead of finding out at the closing table.
3. What used to require a phone call practically every other day just to ask "where are we" is now visible to both parties automatically, cutting the coordinator's status-update calls by roughly half.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a closing coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
