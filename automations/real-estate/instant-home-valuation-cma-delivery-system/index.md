How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a website visitor requests a home valuation, this automation pulls recent comparable sales within the property's radius, price per square foot, days on market, and condition adjustments, straight from the MLS and public records data feeds.
2. A formatted Comparative Market Analysis report is auto-generated using the brokerage's branded template and emailed to the visitor within minutes, complete with a calendar link to discuss the numbers with a listing specialist.
3. The visitor is simultaneously added to the CRM as a seller lead and tagged with their estimated value range for future follow-up.
4. What used to take an agent thirty to forty-five minutes of manual research and formatting now happens automatically before the visitor has even closed the browser tab.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a listing specialist has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
