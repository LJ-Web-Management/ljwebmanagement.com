How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. On the anniversary of every past client's closing date, the system automatically sends a personalized email with a current market snapshot for their neighborhood, an updated estimate of their home's value, and a warm, low-pressure ask for referrals to friends or family who might be buying or selling.
2. Clients who reply or click through are flagged for a personal follow-up call rather than another automated touch, keeping the relationship human where it matters.
3. Running quietly year after year without anyone remembering a single date manually, this one sequence has turned what used to be an occasional forgotten "thinking of you" card into the agent's most reliable source of repeat and referral business.
