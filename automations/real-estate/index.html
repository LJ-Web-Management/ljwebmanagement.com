    FAQ

    Real Estate automation questions

    What real estate workflows can be automated?

    The 53 real estate automations in our catalog cover lead follow-up & sales, marketing & customer retention, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Farming & Prospecting Specialist, Real Estate Agent / Broker, and Property Manager. Popular starting points include Buyer Pipeline Nurture & Showing Follow-Up System, Lease Renewal Campaign & Vacancy Launch System, Commission Disbursement & Post-Close File Archiver.

    Do I need to replace my current real estate software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in real estate automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, and Outlook, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a real estate automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
