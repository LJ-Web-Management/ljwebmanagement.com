How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A weekly automated report compiles each agent's pipeline activity, new leads, showings, closings, into a single dashboard delivered straight to the broker, removing the need for the brokerage operations manager to manually pull numbers from the CRM, the transaction platform, and a lead source tracker separately every Monday morning.
2. Agents whose activity is trending down show up clearly against the team average, letting a broker have a coaching conversation grounded in real numbers instead of a gut feeling, and the entire report that used to take half a day to assemble by hand now lands in an inbox before the office even opens.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a brokerage operations manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
