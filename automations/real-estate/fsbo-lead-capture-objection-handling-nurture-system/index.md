How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Leads captured from for-sale-by-owner ad campaigns, whether from a landing page, a Facebook ad, or a direct mail response, are automatically tagged as FSBO and routed into a specialized nurture sequence built specifically to address the objections FSBO sellers raise most often: commission concerns, confidence in pricing their own home, and worries about losing control of the process.
2. Rather than dropping into the same general buyer or seller pipeline where messaging doesn't quite fit, these leads get a dedicated email and text sequence from the FSBO outreach specialist that walks through real numbers on what agent representation nets sellers on average, paired with a soft invitation to a no-obligation listing consultation.
3. Leads that used to get generic follow-up and quietly go cold now receive messaging built for exactly the objections they're already thinking about, meaningfully improving conversion from this notoriously tough lead source.
