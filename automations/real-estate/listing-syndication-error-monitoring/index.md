How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation continuously checks that a listing's photos, price, and status are displaying correctly across every syndicated portal, Zillow, Realtor.com, the brokerage site, and immediately alerts the digital operations coordinator if any site shows outdated or missing data, a stale price that undersells a home or a status still marked active on a property that's already under contract.
2. Catching a syndication error within the hour instead of whenever an agent happens to notice a confused buyer's email prevents the kind of listing embarrassment that quietly costs credibility with prospects browsing online.
