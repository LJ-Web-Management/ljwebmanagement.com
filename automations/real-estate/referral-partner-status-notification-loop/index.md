How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new lead's source is tagged as a referral partner, a lender, home inspector, or past client, the automation automatically notifies that partner by email the moment the lead's status changes in the CRM, whether it's a first showing booked, an offer submitted, or a closed deal.
2. The referral partner manager no longer has to remember to call every lender or inspector with an update, and partners get real visibility into how their referrals are progressing without ever picking up the phone.
3. Each notification includes a short summary and a thank-you note that reinforces the relationship.
4. Partners who see consistent, timely updates send more referrals, and the brokerage keeps every one of those relationships warm automatically instead of only when someone remembers to follow up.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a referral partner manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
