How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Website visitors can subscribe to ongoing updates for a specific neighborhood directly from the site, and each month the automation automatically pulls current listing counts, median sale price, average days on market, and recent closed sales for that exact area, drops the numbers into a branded report template, and emails it to every subscriber for that neighborhood.
2. The content marketing manager no longer has to manually research and design a dozen different area reports every month; the system generates and sends every neighborhood's report on the same automated schedule.
3. Subscribers who click through to view active listings in their area are flagged as warm leads and routed to the appropriate agent.
4. A single evergreen sign-up form now produces a steady monthly stream of genuinely useful content that keeps hundreds of past visitors engaged long after their first site visit.
