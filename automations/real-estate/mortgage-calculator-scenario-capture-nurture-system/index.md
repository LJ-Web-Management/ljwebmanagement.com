How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a visitor runs numbers through the website's mortgage calculator, entering a home price, down payment, and interest rate, the automation instantly emails them a formatted breakdown of their estimated monthly payment so they never lose the scenario they just built.
2. Their contact information and calculated results are logged into the CRM as a new buyer lead, and they're automatically enrolled in a nurture sequence that includes market tips, financing basics, and a referral offer to a preferred lender partner.
3. Leads who return to the calculator a second time within a week are flagged as high-intent and routed to an agent for a personal outreach call.
4. A tool visitors used to play with once and forget now becomes a steady, trackable source of financing-ready buyer leads.
