How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a property under an HOA goes under contract, the automation automatically identifies the governing association from the property record and sends a document request to the HOA management company for the resale certificate, governing documents, financial statements, and any pending litigation disclosures required for closing.
2. The HOA liaison is copied on the request and receives a reminder if the management company hasn't responded within the association's standard turnaround window, letting them escalate before it threatens the closing timeline.
3. Once documents arrive, they're automatically uploaded to the transaction file and the buyer's agent is notified.
4. A step that used to get forgotten until someone asked whether the HOA docs had arrived a week before closing now starts itself the same day the contract is signed.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a hoa liaison, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
