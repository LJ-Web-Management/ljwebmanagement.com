How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new listing goes live, the automation automatically generates the finished virtual tour and video walkthrough link from the media vendor's platform and pushes it out everywhere it needs to be seen: the listing portals, the brokerage's email list, and every social channel, in one coordinated pass instead of a multimedia marketing coordinator manually uploading the same file to five different places.
2. Each platform receives a properly formatted version: a short vertical clip for Instagram and TikTok, a full walkthrough for YouTube and the MLS listing itself, and a preview embed for the email blast.
3. Listings with a virtual tour attached within the first hour of going live see meaningfully higher early engagement than those where the tour trickles out over a day or two.
4. What used to take up to a full afternoon of manual uploading now finishes in minutes.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a multimedia marketing coordinator has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
