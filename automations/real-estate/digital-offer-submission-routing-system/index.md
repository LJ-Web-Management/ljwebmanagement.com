How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an offer is submitted electronically through the transaction platform, this automation instantly routes the complete offer packet to the listing agent and seller and sends an automatic acknowledgment receipt back to the buyer's agent confirming it was received and logged.
2. Every offer is automatically organized into a single tracking sheet showing price, terms, contingencies, and close date side by side, so the seller can compare multiple offers at a glance instead of digging through separate email attachments.
3. The offer management specialist is notified the moment a new offer lands, and a countdown reminder fires as the seller's response deadline approaches.
4. Offers that used to get buried in an inbox or delayed by a slow forward now reach every party within minutes of submission.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, an offer management specialist has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
