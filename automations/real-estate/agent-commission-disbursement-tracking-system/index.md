How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a deal's status changes to closed in the transaction platform, the automation automatically pulls the final sale price and the agent's commission agreement on file, calculates the exact split between agent, broker, and any referral fees owed, and generates a disbursement request that routes directly to the accounting manager for approval.
2. What used to be a manual paperwork step, someone pulling the contract and doing the math by hand, now happens within minutes of the closing being recorded, with the calculation automatically double-checked against the brokerage's standard commission schedule for accuracy.
3. Approved disbursements are logged automatically for monthly reconciliation and year-end reporting.
4. Agents get paid faster, and the accounting manager spends far less time chasing down closed-deal paperwork.
