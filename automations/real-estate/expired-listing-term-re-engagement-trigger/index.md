How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a listing's term expires without a sale, the automation automatically detects the status change and triggers an outreach sequence to the seller within 24 hours, opening with a low-pressure email offering a free updated market re-evaluation rather than a hard sales pitch.
2. If the seller doesn't respond within three days, a follow-up text goes out from the listing renewal specialist inviting a short call to talk through what happened and what could change for a relist.
3. Sellers who click into the market re-evaluation link are automatically flagged as warm and moved to the top of the outreach queue.
4. Instead of a seller feeling abandoned the moment their listing quietly expires, they hear from the brokerage the same day with a real plan for next steps.
