How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Incoming leads are automatically checked against existing CRM contacts, and duplicates are merged into a single record, preventing the same person from being contacted by two different agents by mistake, an awkward and unprofessional mix-up that used to happen regularly whenever a lead inquired through more than one channel.
2. The CRM data administrator no longer has to periodically run a manual deduplication pass across the whole database, and lead assignment stays clean and fair between agents since the system, not a coincidence of who checked the inbox first, decides who owns a given contact.

Tools it connects

- CRM (e.g., Follow Up Boss or LionDesk)
- Zapier or Make.com
