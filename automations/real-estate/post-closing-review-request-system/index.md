How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Three days after a transaction records, once the excitement of moving has settled but the experience is still fresh, the client receives a personalized text with a direct link to leave a Google or Zillow review, timed to a window when satisfaction and response rates both peak.
2. Clients who click through but don't complete a review within 48 hours get one friendly reminder, and any client who responds to the initial check-in with a complaint instead of praise is automatically routed to the broker for a private resolution call instead of a public review.
3. The result is a steady stream of five-star reviews collected at the moment clients are happiest, instead of the handful of reviews agents used to get only when they remembered to ask.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a client success manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
