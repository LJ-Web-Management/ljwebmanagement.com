How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a new listing goes live in the MLS feed, this automation automatically pulls the photos, price, bed and bath count, and key highlights, drops them into branded post templates, and schedules a coordinated rollout across the brokerage's Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn accounts.
2. Captions are drafted automatically using the listing description with a consistent brand voice, and the marketing director gets a preview link to approve or tweak before anything goes live.
3. Posts are staggered over the first week instead of dumped all at once, keeping the listing visible in feeds for longer.
4. What used to require thirty to sixty minutes of manual design and scheduling work per listing now happens automatically the moment the listing hits active status.

Tools it connects

- Zapier or Make.com
- MLS/IDX feed (e.g., Spark API)
- Canva (post templates)
- Buffer or Later (social scheduling)
- OpenAI API (caption drafting)
