How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Instead of buyers and agents trading four or five texts to find a time that works, this system publishes each agent's live availability to a public booking page linked from every listing and marketing email.
2. Buyers pick an open slot, the request is checked against the seller's showing instructions (lockbox code, pet warning, showing window), and a confirmation with driving directions goes out immediately by text and email.
3. The agent's calendar updates in real time across every device, and a reminder fires two hours before the appointment to cut down on no-shows.
4. What used to take a dozen back-and-forth messages per showing now takes under 90 seconds of the buyer's time.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a showing coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
