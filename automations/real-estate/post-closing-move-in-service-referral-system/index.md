How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a transaction closes, the buyer automatically receives a welcome email curated by the move concierge automation, listing recommended movers, cleaning services, and utility set-up providers for their specific new area, along with any partner discount codes the brokerage has arranged.
2. The email arrives within a day of closing, right when the buyer is actively searching for exactly these services, instead of a generic postcard weeks later that misses the window entirely.
3. Buyers who click through to a partner link are logged in the CRM, letting the brokerage track which service referrals actually get used and strengthening those partnerships over time.
4. What used to be a nice-to-have an agent occasionally remembered to send now reaches every closed buyer automatically, adding real value at the exact moment it matters most.
