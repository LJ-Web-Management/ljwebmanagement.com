How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Instead of routing new leads through a fixed round-robin, this automation continuously tracks each team agent's current open lead count and recent average response time, then assigns every incoming lead to whichever agent can actually respond the fastest right now.
2. An agent who's slammed with showings and has been slow to respond over the last few days automatically receives fewer new leads until their response time recovers, while a fast-responding agent with bandwidth gets a heavier share.
3. The team lead dispatcher gets a weekly summary showing distribution and response metrics per agent, making coaching conversations data-driven instead of anecdotal.
4. Leads that used to sit with an overloaded agent for hours now land with someone who can call back in minutes, meaningfully lifting the team's overall conversion rate.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a team lead dispatcher has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
