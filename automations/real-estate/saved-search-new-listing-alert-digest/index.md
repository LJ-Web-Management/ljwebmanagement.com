How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a buyer saves a search on the website, specifying price range, bedroom count, and preferred area, this automation quietly watches the live MLS feed on their behalf.
2. The instant a new listing matches every saved criterion, the buyer receives an automatic email with photos, price, and a direct link to view it; no agent has to remember to re-run the search or manually forward anything.
3. Buyers who click into three or more alerts in the same week are flagged in the CRM as actively house hunting, prompting a personal check-in call instead of another automated touch.
4. What used to require an agent setting a recurring reminder to search listings by hand now runs continuously in the background, keeping dozens of buyers engaged at once without adding a minute to anyone's day.
