How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a student books a lesson, this automation automatically matches them with an available instructor based on location and vehicle type needed, and confirms the booking on both the student's and instructor's calendars.
2. Matching stops depending on a coordinator manually cross-checking two calendars for every single lesson request.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a scheduling coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
