## Driving Schools automation questions

**What driving school workflows can be automated?**

The 5 driving school automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, operations, inventory & vendors, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Scheduling Coordinator, Student Services Coordinator, and Fleet Manager. Popular starting points include [Lesson Scheduling & Instructor Matching](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/driving-schools/lesson-scheduling-instructor-matching/), [Permit/License Test Date Reminder](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/driving-schools/permit-license-test-date-reminder/), [Vehicle Maintenance & Inspection Scheduling](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/driving-schools/vehicle-maintenance-inspection-scheduling/).

**Do I need to replace my current driving school software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in driving school automations are Google Calendar, Twilio, Mailchimp, and QuickBooks, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a driving school automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
