## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on each student's enrollment date and state-required practice hour minimums, this automation automatically tracks their progress and reminds them when they're eligible to schedule their permit or road test.
2. The student never has to independently track state requirements themselves to figure out when they're actually ready.
