## What this automation does, step by step

1. For the fleet of training vehicles, this automation automatically schedules routine maintenance and required inspections based on mileage and calendar intervals.
2. Vehicles go off the road for scheduled service on a predictable calendar rather than a surprise breakdown interrupting a lesson mid-session.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a fleet manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
