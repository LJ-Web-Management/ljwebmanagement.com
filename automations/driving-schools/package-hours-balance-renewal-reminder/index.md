How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a student uses lesson hours from a purchased package, this automation automatically tracks their remaining balance and sends a renewal reminder before the hours run out.
2. The booking pipeline stays full instead of a student's lessons quietly stalling out because no one flagged that their package was almost spent.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a billing coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
