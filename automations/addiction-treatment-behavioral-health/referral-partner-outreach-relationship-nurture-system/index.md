How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The behavioral health facility's referral relationships (hospital social workers, primary care physicians, employee assistance programs, criminal justice partners, and school counselors) are actively managed through a CRM that tracks every interaction and flags referral sources who haven't sent a client in 90 days for a re-engagement visit or call.
2. New referral partners who send their first client receive a personalized thank-you within 24 hours and are enrolled in an educational content sequence about the facility's clinical model, outcomes data, and admission process.
3. Monthly, a referral activity report is automatically generated and sent to leadership showing which referral channels are producing admissions and which are receiving outreach without converting, directing the BD rep's time toward the highest-yield relationships.
