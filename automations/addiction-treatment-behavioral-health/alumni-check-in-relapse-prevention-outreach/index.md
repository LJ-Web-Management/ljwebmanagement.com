How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At set intervals after discharge, 30, 90, and 180 days, this automation automatically reaches out to alumni with a check-in message and connects them to alumni programming or support resources.
2. The ongoing contact this maintains is strongly tied to long-term recovery outcomes, and it happens on a schedule instead of depending on staff remembering to follow up months later.

Tools it connects

- [Twilio or email](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/twilio/)
- Zapier or Make.com
- A CRM or alumni database

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means an alumni coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
