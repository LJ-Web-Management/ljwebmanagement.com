How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As beds open up and admissions are scheduled, this automation automatically updates a live availability count and notifies the admissions team and referral partners in real time.
2. The manual phone calls typically needed to check current capacity, which cost referral partners time and sometimes cost the facility the referral entirely, are replaced by an always-current number.
