    FAQ

    Addiction Treatment & Behavioral Health automation questions

    What behavioral health workflows can be automated?

    The 10 behavioral health automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, compliance, documents & deadlines, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Case Manager, Alumni Coordinator, and Admissions Coordinator. Popular starting points include Inquiry-to-Intake Conversion & Insurance Verification System, Treatment Plan Review & Clinical Documentation Compliance Tracker, Discharge Planning & Aftercare Referral Coordination System.

    Do I need to replace my current behavioral health software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in behavioral health automations are Twilio, Jotform, Slack, and Airtable, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a behavioral health automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
