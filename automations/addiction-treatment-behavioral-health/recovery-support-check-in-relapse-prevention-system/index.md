How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After discharge, alumni receive a structured check-in sequence (a 30-day, 60-day, 90-day, 6-month, and 1-year message) celebrating their recovery milestones and connecting them to alumni meetings, peer support resources, and crisis line information.
2. Alumni who respond to a check-in indicating they are struggling are immediately routed to the alumni coordinator for a personal follow-up call, with a prompt to assess whether a higher level of care is appropriate.
3. Alumni who have been strong in their recovery receive invitations to become peer mentors, creating a self-sustaining support community where the people furthest along in their recovery are giving back to those earlier in theirs.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an alumni coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
