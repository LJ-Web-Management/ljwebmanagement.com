How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically builds and maintains a running history log for every service address, past jobs, installed equipment, technician notes, so any technician arriving on-site has full context instantly instead of starting from scratch or calling the office to ask what was done last time.
2. A technician pulling up to an unfamiliar address can immediately see the exact HVAC model installed three years ago or a note about a tricky access point, and the field technician manager sees fewer callbacks caused simply by a tech not knowing what a previous visit already established.
