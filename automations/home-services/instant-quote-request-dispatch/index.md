How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a homeowner submits a quote request through the website form, this automation immediately parses the job details, service type, and property address, then texts the on-call estimator a summary with a one-tap callback link.
2. If the estimator hasn't acknowledged the lead within five minutes, the alert automatically escalates to a backup estimator and the sales manager on Slack, so no request sits unanswered in an inbox overnight.
3. The customer receives an automatic confirmation text letting them know a specialist is reviewing their request and will call shortly, which keeps them from filling out three more competitors' forms while they wait.
4. Estimators who used to call back hours later, once someone finally checked the contact form inbox, now respond within minutes, and the company converts noticeably more of the leads it's already paying to generate.
