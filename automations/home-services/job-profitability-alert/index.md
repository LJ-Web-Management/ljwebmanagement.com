How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As labor hours and material costs get logged against a job in real time through the field service app, this automation continuously compares actual spend to the original estimate and automatically flags the job the moment costs exceed a set threshold, typically ten or fifteen percent over budget, while the crew is still on site rather than after the invoice is closed out.
2. The financial controller gets an immediate alert with a cost breakdown, giving management a chance to investigate scope creep or a pricing error and make a call (absorb the cost, or start a change-order conversation with the customer) before the job wraps and the loss is locked in.
3. Margin erosion that used to get discovered weeks later during a monthly profitability review now gets caught and addressed in the moment, protecting margin on every job instead of just the ones someone happened to double-check.
