How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an existing customer refers a new client, whether through a shared link or by simply giving out their name, this automation logs the referral in the CRM and ties it to the original customer's record automatically.
2. Once the referred customer's first job is completed and the invoice is paid in full, the system automatically triggers the referring customer's reward, a discount code, account credit, or gift card, without the customer loyalty coordinator having to remember who referred whom or manually process a single payout.
3. A running leaderboard shows which customers are driving the most new business each quarter, making it easy to recognize top referrers with an extra thank-you.
4. Referral tracking that used to live in someone's memory or a sticky note now runs itself with a complete, auditable trail.
