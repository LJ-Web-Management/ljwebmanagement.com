How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For customers on a recurring service plan, such as quarterly pest control, lawn care, or seasonal HVAC maintenance, this automation automatically schedules the next visit as soon as the current one is marked complete, based on the plan's set frequency, and sends the customer a confirmation with the option to adjust the date in two taps.
2. If a proposed date conflicts with the customer's calendar, they can reply to reschedule and the change syncs back into the field service system automatically without a phone call in either direction.
3. The recurring services coordinator no longer spends hours each week manually rebooking hundreds of standing appointments, and customers stay on their plan because the next visit is already locked in before they even think to wonder when it's due.
