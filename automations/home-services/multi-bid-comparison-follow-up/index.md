How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a large job's estimate is flagged as one of several bids the customer is weighing, this automation starts a week-long follow-up sequence built specifically to differentiate the company rather than just chase a signature: an email highlighting workmanship warranty length and financing options, a text with links to recent reviews and before-and-after photos from similar jobs, and a personal call task assigned to the senior estimator on day five if the bid is still open.
2. Every touch is designed to answer the questions a customer is actually weighing between competing bids instead of a generic "just checking in" nudge.
3. Estimates that historically got lost in a stack of competing quotes now get a structured case made for why this company should win the job, and close rates on multi-bid jobs improve measurably.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a senior estimator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
