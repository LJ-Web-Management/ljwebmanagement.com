How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Thirty days before a customer's annual maintenance plan expires, this automation triggers a renewal sequence: a personalized email with the customer's service history and plan benefits, a follow-up text from the service manager, and (for customers who don't renew) a final "last chance" offer with a small discount.
2. Lapsed plan customers also receive a re-engagement sequence tied to seasonal tune-up reminders, turning one-time jobs into recurring revenue relationships.
