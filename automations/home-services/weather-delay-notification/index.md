How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For jobs scheduled outdoors, a roof replacement, a gutter install, an exterior electrical run, this automation checks the forecast automatically each morning against the day's job list and flags any appointment at risk from rain, high wind, or extreme heat.
2. When a job is flagged, the customer automatically receives a text explaining the concern and offering to reschedule to the next available slot, well before a crew is loaded up and dispatched only to turn around at the curb.
3. The operations coordinator gets a daily summary of every affected job so the week's schedule can be rebalanced proactively instead of reactively.
4. Wasted truck rolls and idle crew hours drop noticeably once weather delays are caught the night before instead of discovered by a foreman standing in the rain.
