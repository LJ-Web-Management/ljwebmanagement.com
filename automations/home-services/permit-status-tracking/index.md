How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For jobs that require a municipal permit, such as a panel upgrade or a full re-roof, this automation logs the application the moment it's submitted and checks the local authority's portal on a set schedule to track its status automatically.
2. The permit coordinator and the customer both receive a notification the moment the permit is approved, and if the status hasn't changed after the jurisdiction's typical turnaround window, an internal alert flags it for a manual follow-up call to the permitting office before it becomes a scheduling problem.
3. Jobs that used to stall silently because nobody was checking a portal now move forward the moment a permit clears, and the office can tell any customer exactly where their permit stands without digging through email threads or calling the county themselves.

### Without automation

Left to a person, this means a permit coordinator re-enters the same data more than once, double-checks it by hand, and still occasionally misses something. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.
