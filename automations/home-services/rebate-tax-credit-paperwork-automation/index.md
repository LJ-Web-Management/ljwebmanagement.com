How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a qualifying install, such as a high-efficiency HVAC system, a solar array, or a heat pump water heater, this automation automatically pulls the equipment model, efficiency rating, and install date from the work order and generates the required rebate or tax credit paperwork, pre-filled and ready for the customer's signature within a day of job completion.
2. The completed forms are sent for e-signature and, once signed, automatically submitted to the utility or program administrator on the customer's behalf, with the rebate program coordinator only stepping in for programs that require manual review.
3. Customers who used to leave real rebate money on the table because the paperwork felt like a hassle now receive it as a normal part of the closeout process, and rebate program participation rises significantly as a result.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a rebate program coordinator has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
