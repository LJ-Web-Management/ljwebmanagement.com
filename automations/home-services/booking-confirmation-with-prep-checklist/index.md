How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a customer books a job online, this automation sends an immediate confirmation with the appointment window, technician name, and a short prep checklist tailored to the job type: clearing a path to the electrical panel, securing pets, moving vehicles away from the driveway, so the technician isn't delayed the second they arrive.
2. A reminder text goes out again the morning of the appointment with the same checklist as a quick refresher.
3. Jobs that used to run late because a technician arrived to find the work area blocked or a dog loose in the yard now start on time far more consistently, and the scheduling coordinator fields noticeably fewer same-day rescheduling calls caused by access issues that a five-minute heads-up would have prevented entirely.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a scheduling coordinator remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
