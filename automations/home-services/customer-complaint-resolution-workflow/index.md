How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a complaint is logged, whether through a phone call, a text reply, or a low rating on a post-job survey, this automation automatically opens a resolution ticket, notifies the customer success manager immediately, and sets a 24-hour follow-up deadline that shows up on the manager's task list until it's marked resolved.
2. The customer receives an automatic acknowledgment confirming the issue has been received and that someone will be in touch, so they're never left wondering whether their complaint disappeared into a void.
3. Overdue tickets automatically escalate to the owner if the deadline passes without resolution.
4. Complaints that used to get handled inconsistently, quickly if someone happened to notice, forgotten if they didn't, now follow the same tracked process every time, and customer retention after a service issue improves measurably.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a customer success manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
