How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer starts an online financing application for a larger job, such as a new HVAC system or roof replacement, but abandons it before submitting, this automation detects the incomplete application within the hour and sends a friendly text and email offering to help finish it, along with a direct link back to where they left off.
2. If there's still no response after 24 hours, the finance coordinator receives a task to call the customer directly and walk them through it live.
3. Financing partners like Wisetack or GreenSky flag approval status automatically back into the CRM, so the team always knows exactly where a big-ticket deal stands.
4. Jobs that used to stall out and go cold because a financing form felt like a hassle now get rescued and converted into booked, funded work.
