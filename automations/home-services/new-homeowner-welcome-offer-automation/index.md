How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Using public property records, the automation automatically identifies newly sold homes within the service area and sends the new owner a welcome offer introducing the company's services, reaching a household during the exact window when new homeowners are actively looking for a trusted HVAC, plumbing, or electrical provider they haven't chosen yet.
2. The growth marketing manager gets a steady, low-cost stream of genuinely warm leads generated entirely from public data instead of paying for broad advertising aimed at homeowners who already have a provider they're happy with.

Tools it connects

- Public records API (e.g., county recorder data feed)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Mailchimp or ActiveCampaign](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/mailchimp/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a growth marketing manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
