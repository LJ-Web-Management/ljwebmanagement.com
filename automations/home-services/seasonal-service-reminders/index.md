How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on each customer's equipment type and service history, this automation automatically segments the customer list and sends timed seasonal offers: an AC tune-up reminder six weeks before summer, a furnace safety check before the first cold snap, and a gutter or roof inspection nudge each fall.
2. Each message includes a direct booking link so the customer can grab a slot in two taps instead of calling during the office's busiest week of the year.
3. Customers who don't respond to the first message automatically receive one follow-up text ten days later before the sequence closes out.
4. The shop fills its calendar with planned, recurring work well ahead of peak season instead of scrambling to staff for a flood of emergency no-heat and no-cool calls once the weather actually turns.
