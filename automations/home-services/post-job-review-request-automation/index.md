How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The second a technician marks a job complete in the field-service app, the customer receives a text thanking them for their business with a direct link to leave a Google review, timed to land while the work is still fresh rather than days later when the memory has faded.
2. Customers who don't click within 48 hours get a single, friendly follow-up text; customers who give a low star rating are routed privately to the office instead of straight to a public review, giving the team a chance to resolve the issue first.
3. Review volume climbs steadily without anyone in the office having to remember to ask, and the shop builds a visible track record that new leads see before they ever call.
