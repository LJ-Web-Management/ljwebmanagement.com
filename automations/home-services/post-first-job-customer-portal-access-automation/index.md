How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a customer's first completed job, the automation automatically issues them access credentials to an online portal where they can view invoices, warranties, and their full service history, instead of a customer experience manager fielding calls asking for a copy of an old invoice or when a warranty actually expires.
2. Customers who have self-service access to their own records call the office noticeably less often for basic lookups, and the portal itself becomes a quiet, ongoing reminder of the company's brand every time a customer logs in to check something.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a customer experience manager, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
