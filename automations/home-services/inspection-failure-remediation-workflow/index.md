How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a completed job fails a required inspection, the automation automatically creates a remediation task and schedules the re-inspection, keeping the job from stalling out unnoticed in the gap between a failed inspection and someone actually being assigned to fix it.
2. The quality assurance coordinator gets an immediate alert with the specific inspection findings attached, and the technician responsible for the fix has clear next steps the same day instead of the failed inspection notice sitting in an inbox until a customer calls asking why the job still isn't finished.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a quality assurance coordinator has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
