How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every customer who completes a job is sent a unique referral link a few days after the invoice is paid, and any new customer who books through that link is automatically tagged in the CRM as a referral tied back to the original customer.
2. Once the referred customer's first job is completed and paid, the referring customer is automatically issued their reward (a discount code, a gift card, or an account credit) without anyone in the office having to remember who referred whom or manually process a reward.
3. Referral volume becomes visible on a live leaderboard instead of anecdotal, and the business can finally see which customers are actually driving new work.
