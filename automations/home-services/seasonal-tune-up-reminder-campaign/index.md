How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Six weeks before the start of cooling season and again before heating season, this automation pulls every customer whose equipment is due for a seasonal check (HVAC tune-up, gutter cleaning, sprinkler blowout) and runs a staged reminder sequence: an email with a booking link, a follow-up text five days later if there's no click, and a personal call task assigned to the office if there's still no response after two weeks.
2. Customers get ahead of breakdowns instead of calling in a panic on the hottest day of the year, and the shop fills its shoulder-season calendar with recurring revenue instead of scrambling for new leads.
3. Every touch is timestamped in the CRM so the team can see exactly who's been reached and who's overdue for a personal call.
