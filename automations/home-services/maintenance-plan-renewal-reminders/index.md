How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Thirty and again ten days before a customer's maintenance plan is set to renew, this automation sends a reminder email and text summarizing what the plan covers, when their next visit is due, and a one-click link to renew and pay instantly without a phone call.
2. Customers who click but don't complete payment receive a single follow-up nudge two days later; customers who let the renewal date pass entirely are flagged for the membership coordinator to call personally before the plan lapses for good.
3. Plans that used to quietly cancel because a renewal notice got buried in someone's inbox now renew automatically at a much higher rate, protecting a steady stream of recurring maintenance revenue that the business would otherwise have to replace with expensive new-customer acquisition every single month.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a membership coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
