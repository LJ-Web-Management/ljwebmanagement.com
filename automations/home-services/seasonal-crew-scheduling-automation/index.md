How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Six weeks ahead of the company's high season, this automation automatically sends every technician and crew member an availability request covering the upcoming peak weeks, with a simple form to confirm hours, note planned time off, and flag any scheduling constraints.
2. Responses populate directly into a staffing dashboard the workforce planning manager can scan in minutes instead of chasing down individual texts and voicemails from a dozen crew members.
3. Gaps in coverage are automatically highlighted well before the season starts, giving management time to bring on seasonal help or shift schedules rather than discovering a Tuesday with no one available once the phones are already ringing off the hook.
4. Staffing plans that used to come together in a last-minute scramble now get locked in weeks in advance.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a workforce planning manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Interview scheduling without email ping-pong
