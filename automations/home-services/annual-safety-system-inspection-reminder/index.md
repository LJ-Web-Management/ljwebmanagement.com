How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers with safety-critical installations, standby generators, sump pumps, gas line systems, are automatically flagged in the CRM with their required annual inspection date, and this automation sends a reminder email and text thirty days ahead of that date along with a one-click booking link.
2. Customers who don't book within a week receive a second reminder framed around the safety risk of a missed inspection, such as a generator failing to start during the next outage.
3. The safety compliance coordinator gets a monthly report of every customer still overdue so a personal call can close the gap before it becomes a liability issue.
4. Inspection compliance rates climb substantially once reminders happen automatically every year instead of depending on a customer's own memory of when they last had it checked.
