How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks mileage and elapsed time for every vehicle in the company's fleet using GPS telematics data, and automatically schedules routine maintenance, oil changes, tire rotations, inspections, before a vehicle hits its due threshold rather than after a dashboard warning light comes on mid-route.
2. The fleet manager receives a weekly summary of upcoming service needs and a booking confirmation once each appointment is set with the shop, and any vehicle flagged for urgent attention triggers an immediate alert rather than waiting for the weekly digest.
3. Unplanned breakdowns that used to sideline a technician for an entire day, and the job they were supposed to run, drop noticeably once maintenance happens on a proactive schedule instead of whenever someone remembers to check the odometer.
