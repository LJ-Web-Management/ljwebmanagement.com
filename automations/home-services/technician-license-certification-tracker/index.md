How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation maintains a running record of every technician's state license, EPA certification, and manufacturer credentials, with renewal deadlines tracked internally and reminders sent to both the technician and the HR and compliance manager sixty and fourteen days before each one expires.
2. Technicians can upload proof of renewal directly through a simple form, which automatically updates their record and clears the alert without anyone re-entering data by hand.
3. A dashboard shows the compliance manager every technician's status at a glance, color-coded by how close each credential is to expiring.
4. Missed renewals that could have meant a technician working uncertified in the field, or the company facing a compliance fine, get caught weeks in advance instead of being discovered during an audit or an inspection.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a hr & compliance manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation
