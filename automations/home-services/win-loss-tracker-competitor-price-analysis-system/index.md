How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every time a quote is marked won or lost in the CRM, the estimator is prompted to log the outcome with a reason code and (for lost quotes) the competitor's price if known.
2. The system aggregates win rates by job type, price range, and competitor over time, and delivers a monthly analysis showing exactly where the estimator is winning, where they're losing, and at what price points the market is moving.
3. Estimating decisions shift from gut feel to data, and pricing adjustments are made based on what the market is actually doing rather than what anyone assumes.
