How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers with equipment approaching its typical replacement age automatically receive a proactive outreach offering a replacement consultation before a breakdown forces the issue, instead of the sales operations coordinator relying on an emergency call as the only trigger for a replacement conversation.
2. Reaching a customer while their aging system is still limping along, rather than after it's already failed on the coldest night of the year, turns a stressful emergency sale into a calm, planned conversation, and customers consistently appreciate being warned ahead of a failure rather than surprised by one.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a sales operations coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
