How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a technician on-site identifies additional necessary work, a corroded line the original job didn't cover, for example, the automation automatically sends the customer a real-time approval request with clear, itemized pricing before the extra work begins, instead of a sales estimator or the technician trying to reach a customer by phone and talk through pricing verbally on the spot.
2. Customers approve the add-on with a single tap from wherever they are, and jobs move forward the same day instead of stalling out waiting for a callback that might not come until the technician has already left.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a sales estimator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
