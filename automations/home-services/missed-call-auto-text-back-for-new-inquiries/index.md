How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every call that rings through to the office and isn't picked up, whether it's during a lunch rush or after the last technician has clocked out, automatically triggers a text back within seconds confirming the business saw the missed call and inviting the caller to book online or reply with a convenient time.
2. The caller's number and timestamp are logged into the CRM automatically so the front desk can see exactly who called and whether they were reached.
3. Because most first-time callers who hit voicemail simply hang up and dial the next company on the search results page, this single text keeps those inquiries in the pipeline instead of losing them silently, recovering leads that would never have shown up in any report because no one ever knew the call happened in the first place.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a front desk coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
