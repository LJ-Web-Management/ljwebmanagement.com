How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each evening, this automation reviews the next day's job schedule across all technicians, flags any scheduling conflicts or double-bookings, calculates estimated drive time between jobs using mapping data, and delivers a pre-built morning briefing to each technician with their job list in priority order, including customer name, address, job type, and notes from previous visits.
2. The dispatcher starts the day with a clean, organized schedule instead of rebuilding it from scratch at 7 a.m. when the first technician calls to ask where they're going.

Tools it connects

- Field service software (e.g., ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro)
- Zapier or Make.com
- Google Maps API (drive time calculations)
- [Twilio (technician morning briefing text)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/twilio/)
- [Slack (dispatcher conflict alerts)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/slack/)
