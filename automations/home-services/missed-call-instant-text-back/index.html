    How it works

    What this automation does, step by step

    1. When a call to the shop goes unanswered, whether the phones are slammed during a storm or it's after 6 p.m. and the office has gone home, this automation fires a text back to that caller within 15 seconds: a friendly message confirming the business saw the missed call and a link to book online or reply with a good time to talk.

    2. A call-tracking webhook flags the missed call the moment it happens, and the text goes out before the caller has even set the phone down.

    3. Leads that used to dial the next name on the list because no one answered now get a response faster than most competitors' front desk could manage even with someone sitting there.

    4. Every text-back is logged against the caller's number in the CRM so the office can follow up personally the next morning.

    Tools it connects
    • CallRail
    • Twilio
    • Zapier or Make.com
    • ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro
    • Slack (missed-call office alert)

    Using different software? We build around what you already have, so you never have to switch tools to automate.

    Before and after

    The manual way vs. the automated way

    Without automation

    Done manually, a call intake specialist has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

    With this automation

    • Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
    • Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
    • Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
    • Clean CRM records without manual data entry
