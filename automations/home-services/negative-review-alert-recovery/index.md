How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The instant a new review posts online rating three stars or below, this automation sends an immediate internal alert to the customer experience manager with a link to the review and the customer's full job history pulled from the CRM.
2. A personalized outreach message goes out to the customer within the hour, acknowledging the issue and offering a direct line to resolve it, rather than leaving the complaint to sit publicly while the customer grows more frustrated.
3. Resolved issues are logged with the outcome, and in cases where the fix genuinely satisfies the customer, a polite follow-up asks if they'd consider updating or removing the review.
4. What used to be discovered by accident days later, if it was noticed at all, now gets a same-day response, protecting the business's online reputation before a bad experience spreads.
