How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a technician logs a completed installation in the field app, this automation automatically pulls the equipment's model and serial number from the work order, logs the warranty details internally, and submits the manufacturer registration form on the customer's behalf, something that historically got skipped entirely if the office was busy that week.
2. The customer receives a confirmation email with their registered warranty terms and a copy for their records, and the installation coordinator gets a running log of every unit registered that month for easy reference if a warranty claim ever comes up.
3. Registrations that used to fall through the cracks, leaving customers unknowingly uncovered, now happen automatically on every single install without adding a single extra step to the technician's day.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an installation coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
