How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an estimate is sent but sits unsigned for 48 hours, this automation kicks off a staged follow-up sequence: a check-in text asking if the customer has any questions, a value-focused email at day four highlighting financing options and warranty coverage, and a task assigned to the sales operations coordinator to place a personal call if the estimate is still open after a week.
2. Every touch is logged against the estimate so nothing gets followed up twice or missed entirely, and the sequence automatically stops the moment the customer signs or the estimate expires.
3. Estimates that used to go cold simply because no one had time to chase them now get consistent, gentle pressure to move forward, and the close rate on sent estimates climbs measurably as a result.
