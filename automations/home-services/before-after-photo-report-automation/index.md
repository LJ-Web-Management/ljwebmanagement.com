How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Photos a technician snaps on their phone before starting and after finishing a job automatically upload into the work order and compile into a simple side-by-side report, emailed to the customer within minutes of the job being marked complete.
2. The report shows exactly what was found, what was repaired or replaced, and the finished result, giving the customer visible proof of the value delivered instead of just a line-item invoice.
3. The quality assurance coordinator can also spot-check a sample of reports each week to catch any job that's missing photos before it goes out, keeping documentation consistent across every technician.
4. Customers who receive a clear before-and-after report dispute invoices far less often and are measurably more likely to leave a detailed, photo-referenced review afterward.
