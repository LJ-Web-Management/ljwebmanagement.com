    FAQ

    Home Services automation questions

    What home services workflows can be automated?

    The 53 home services automations in our catalog cover lead follow-up & sales, customer service & communication, and marketing & customer retention, among other areas, for roles like Sales Estimator, Field Technician Manager, and Membership Coordinator. Popular starting points include Seasonal Reactivation & Referral Campaign System, Multi-Technician Schedule Optimizer & Morning Briefing, Win/Loss Tracker & Competitor Price Analysis System.

    Do I need to replace my current home services software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in home services automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, Mailchimp, and Slack, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a home services automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
