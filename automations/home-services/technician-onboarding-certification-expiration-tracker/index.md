How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new technician is hired, this automation generates a 30-day onboarding checklist (safety training, equipment certifications, ride-along schedule, and tool issuance) with tasks assigned to both the manager and the new hire.
2. As each item is completed, it is logged with a timestamp.
3. Certifications that expire (EPA 608, NATE, manufacturer product certifications) are tracked with renewal reminders sent to both the technician and manager 60 and 14 days before expiration.
4. No technician goes into the field uncertified because a renewal date slipped past someone's awareness.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a field technician manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Interview scheduling without email ping-pong
