How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on each day's booked jobs, this automation automatically sequences the route to minimize drive time and dump run trips, and adjusts in real time if a job runs long or a same-day request comes in nearby.
2. A day's schedule stays efficient even as it changes throughout the morning instead of falling apart after the first delay.

- A field service platform (Housecall Pro)
- Google Maps API
- Zapier or Make.com
