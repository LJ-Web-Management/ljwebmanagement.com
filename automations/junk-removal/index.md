## Junk Removal automation questions

**What junk removal workflows can be automated?**

The 5 junk removal automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, lead follow-up & sales, and reporting & analytics, among other areas, for roles like Customer Service Rep, Dispatcher, and Crew Leader. Popular starting points include [Instant Quote from Photo Upload](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/junk-removal/instant-quote-from-photo-upload/), [Crew Route & Job Sequencing](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/junk-removal/crew-route-job-sequencing/), [Donation vs. Landfill Sorting Report](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/junk-removal/donation-vs-landfill-sorting-report/).

**Do I need to replace my current junk removal software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in junk removal automations are Google Sheets and Mailchimp, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a junk removal automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
