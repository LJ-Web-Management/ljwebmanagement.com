## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation monitors the day's remaining truck capacity and automatically closes off same-day booking on the website once trucks are full.
2. A crew that's already maxed out for the day never gets overbooked on top of a schedule it can't actually deliver.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, an operations manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
