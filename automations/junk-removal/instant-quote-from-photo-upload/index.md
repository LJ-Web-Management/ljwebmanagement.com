## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer uploads photos of the items they need removed through the website, this automation automatically generates a preliminary quote range based on volume and item type.
2. Customers get pricing without waiting for a callback, and the crew shows up with a realistic expectation of what the job involves instead of a blind guess.
