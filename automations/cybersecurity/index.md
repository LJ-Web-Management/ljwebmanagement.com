## Cybersecurity automation questions

**What cybersecurity workflows can be automated?**

The 10 cybersecurity automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, compliance, documents & deadlines, and hr, hiring & team management, among other areas, for roles like CISO, SOC Manager, and Compliance Officer. Popular starting points include [Security Awareness Training & Phishing Simulation Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/cybersecurity/security-awareness-training-phishing-simulation-tracker/), [Alert Triage, Enrichment & Incident Escalation System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/cybersecurity/alert-triage-enrichment-incident-escalation-system/), [Policy Review Cycle & Employee Attestation System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/cybersecurity/policy-review-cycle-employee-attestation-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current cybersecurity software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in cybersecurity automations are Slack, Airtable, Jotform, and DocuSign, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a cybersecurity automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
