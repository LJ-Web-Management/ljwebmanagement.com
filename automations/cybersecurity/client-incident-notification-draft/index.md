How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a security incident affecting client data is confirmed, this automation automatically drafts the notification communication using the pre-approved template and the timeline requirements for the relevant compliance framework.
2. The response team gets a head start with a compliant draft ready for review instead of writing one from scratch while already under pressure.

- Zapier or Make.com

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, an incident response lead has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
