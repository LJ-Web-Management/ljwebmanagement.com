How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Information security policies are tracked with annual review dates, and the assigned policy owner receives a review reminder 60 days before the policy expires along with the current version for markup.
2. Once the updated policy is approved, it is automatically distributed to all required attestors (employees, contractors, or role-specific groups) with a digital acknowledgment link and a completion deadline.
3. The compliance officer tracks attestation completion rates in real time, and any employee who has not attested by the deadline triggers an escalation to their manager.
4. The compliance program maintains a complete, auditable record of every policy version and every attestation without anyone maintaining a manual spreadsheet.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a compliance officer has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
