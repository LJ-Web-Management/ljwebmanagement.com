## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation continuously checks the expiration dates of SSL certificates and domain registrations across every client's environment and automatically alerts the team 30 days ahead of each expiration.
2. The entirely avoidable \\"certificate expired\\" outage, the kind that's embarrassing precisely because it was so preventable, stops happening.
