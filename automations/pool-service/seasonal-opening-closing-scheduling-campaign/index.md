## What this automation does, step by step

1. Ahead of each season, this automation automatically reaches out to customers to book their pool opening or closing service, spreading bookings out over the available scheduling window instead of everyone calling in during the same rushed week.
2. The office stops fielding a flood of calls the first warm weekend of spring.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an office manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
