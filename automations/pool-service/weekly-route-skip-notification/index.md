How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation automatically builds the weekly service route and, if a technician needs to skip a pool due to an access issue, a locked gate, a dog in the yard, automatically notifies both the office and the customer.
2. A skipped pool never just quietly doesn't get serviced with no one aware until the customer calls asking why their water looks off.
