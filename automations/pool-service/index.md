## Pool Service & Maintenance automation questions

**What pool service workflows can be automated?**

The 5 pool service automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, operations, inventory & vendors, and lead follow-up & sales, among other areas, for roles like Service Technician, Service Manager, and Route Manager. Popular starting points include [Chemical Reading Trend Alert](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/pool-service/chemical-reading-trend-alert/), [Weekly Route & Skip Notification](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/pool-service/weekly-route-skip-notification/), [Equipment Repair Quote-to-Approval Workflow](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/pool-service/equipment-repair-quote-to-approval-workflow/).

**Do I need to replace my current pool service software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in pool service automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, QuickBooks, and Stripe, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a pool service automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
