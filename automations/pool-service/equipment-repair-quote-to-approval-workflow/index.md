How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a technician identifies needed equipment repair during a routine visit, this automation automatically generates a quote with photos of the issue, sends it to the customer for approval, and schedules the repair automatically once approved.
2. No separate office callback is required to move from finding a problem to having the repair scheduled.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a service technician manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
