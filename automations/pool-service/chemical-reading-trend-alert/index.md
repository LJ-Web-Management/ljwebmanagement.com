How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As technicians log chemical readings during each visit, this automation automatically tracks the trend over time per pool and flags any pool showing a pattern suggesting an equipment issue, a failing chlorinator, a slow leak, rather than a normal one-off reading.
2. A developing equipment problem gets caught over a few visits instead of only when the pool turns green.
