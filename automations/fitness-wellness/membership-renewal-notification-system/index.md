How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Seven days before a membership auto-renews, the member receives a clear email showing the exact charge amount and date, along with an easy upgrade path to a higher tier if their usage over the past month suggests they'd get more value from it.
2. This single notification eliminates the "surprise charge" complaints and chargeback disputes that come from members who forgot they were on an annual plan, and it gives the gym a natural, low-pressure moment to offer an upgrade instead of waiting for the member to ask.
3. Members who see the charge coming and understand what it's for are far less likely to cancel out of frustration.
