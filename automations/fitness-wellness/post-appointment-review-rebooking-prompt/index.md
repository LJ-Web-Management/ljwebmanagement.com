How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a salon or spa appointment, the client automatically receives a review request along with a "book your next visit" link timed to the typical rebooking cycle for that specific service, instead of the spa manager relying on the client to remember to rebook on their own weeks later once the relaxation has worn off.
2. Clients who get a well-timed rebooking prompt convert to a repeat visit far more often than clients left to book whenever they happen to think of it, and the practice's calendar stays fuller with far less manual outreach.

Tools it connects

- Booking software (e.g., Jane App, Square Appointments, or Vagaro)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Twilio (post-session rebooking SMS)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/twilio/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a spa manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
