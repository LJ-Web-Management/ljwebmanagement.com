How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a member submits an online cancellation request, an automatic retention offer, a pause option or a discount, is presented before the cancellation is finalized, giving the cancellation specialist a real chance to save the membership instead of the request simply processing straight through with no opportunity to intervene.
2. A meaningful share of members canceling for temporary reasons, a busy season, a tight month, take the pause option once it's actually offered, and membership churn drops once every cancellation request runs through a genuine save attempt instead of a same-click confirmation.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a cancellation specialist handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
