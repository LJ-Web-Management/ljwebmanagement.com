How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a prospect tours the facility but doesn't sign up on the spot, they automatically receive a follow-up sequence over the following week addressing common hesitations, price, schedule flexibility, whether the classes are actually beginner-friendly, instead of the sales coordinator only following up once with a single generic email before giving up on the lead.
2. A tour that didn't convert immediately isn't necessarily a lost prospect, and a well-paced follow-up sequence consistently recovers a meaningful share of tours that would otherwise have simply gone cold.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a sales coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
