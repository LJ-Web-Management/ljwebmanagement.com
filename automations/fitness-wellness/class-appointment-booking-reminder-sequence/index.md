How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members automatically receive a reminder 24 hours and again 2 hours before a booked class or appointment, reducing the empty spots caused by simple forgetfulness instead of a front desk lead manually calling down a list of no-shows after the fact.
2. Reminders include a one-tap link to cancel or reschedule, so a genuine conflict frees the spot for someone on the waitlist instead of it just sitting empty, and class attendance rates climb noticeably once every booking gets a consistent two-step reminder instead of relying on members to remember on their own.

- Studio management software (e.g., Mindbody or Glofox)
- Zapier or Make.com

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a front desk lead, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
