How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a popular class nears full capacity, staff are automatically alerted to consider adding another session, and members on a related interest list are notified of the option, instead of the group fitness director only realizing a class needs a second time slot after weeks of members complaining they can never get a spot.
2. Popular classes stop turning away eager members simply because nobody was tracking capacity trends closely enough to catch it, and adding a second session becomes a proactive, data-backed decision instead of a reactive scramble after losing members to frustration.
