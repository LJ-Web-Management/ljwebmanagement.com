How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members are automatically notified before their renewal date with a one-click renew option, and staff are automatically flagged the moment a membership actually lapses so the membership coordinator can reach out personally before the member drifts away entirely.
2. Catching a lapse the same day it happens, rather than discovering it weeks later in a monthly report, gives a real chance to win the member back with a personal call instead of a generic reactivation email sent long after they've already found another gym.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a membership coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
