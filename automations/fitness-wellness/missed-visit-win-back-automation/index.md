How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A member who hasn't checked in for a defined number of days automatically receives a friendly re-engagement message, catching attrition before it becomes a cancellation instead of the retention specialist only noticing a disengaged member once they've already decided to quit and are calling to cancel.
2. Members who respond to an early check-in message return to their normal routine far more often than members left alone until the pattern is already entrenched, turning what used to be a purely reactive save attempt into a proactive one that actually works.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a retention specialist, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
