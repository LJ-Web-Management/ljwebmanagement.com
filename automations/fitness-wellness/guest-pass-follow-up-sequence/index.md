How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Someone who uses a guest pass automatically receives a follow-up offer a few days later inviting them to join as a full member, timed while their experience is still fresh, instead of the sales manager relying on the guest to walk back in on their own initiative.
2. A guest who had a genuinely good first visit is far more receptive to a join offer within the week than months later once the experience has faded, and guest pass conversion rates climb meaningfully once the follow-up happens automatically and on time every single time.
