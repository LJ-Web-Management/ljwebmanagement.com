How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members enrolled in a fitness challenge automatically receive periodic updates on their progress and where they stand on the leaderboard, keeping motivation and engagement high instead of a challenge quietly losing steam once the initial excitement of signing up fades.
2. The group fitness director no longer has to manually calculate and post leaderboard standings, and challenges that used to see participation drop off sharply after the first week hold engagement much better once members get regular, automatic visibility into how close they are to the top.
