How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each member gets a unique referral link they can text or share directly, and when a referred friend signs up and completes their first month, both the referring member and the new member automatically receive a credit toward their next billing cycle, no manual tracking on a spreadsheet, no front desk staff remembering who referred whom.
2. The referring member gets an instant confirmation text the moment their friend joins, which reinforces the behavior and tends to prompt more referrals from the same member.
3. Automating the reward removes the single biggest reason referral programs quietly die: rewards that get forgotten or delayed long enough that members stop bothering to refer.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a gym owner remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
