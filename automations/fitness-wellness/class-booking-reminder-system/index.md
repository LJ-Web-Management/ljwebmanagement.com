How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every member who books a class automatically receives a reminder text 24 hours before the session and a second reminder 2 hours out, each one including the studio's cancellation cutoff so members know exactly how much time they have to free up their spot for someone else.
2. Members who cancel after the cutoff are automatically flagged for the studio's late-cancel policy instead of the front desk having to track it manually.
3. Classes that used to run with 3 or 4 empty seats from last-minute no-shows fill back up because the waitlist gets notified the second a spot opens, keeping instructor-to-student ratios where they should be.
