How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a class reaches capacity, additional sign-ups automatically land on the waitlist and receive a confirmation with their position.
2. The moment a registered student cancels, the next person on the waitlist is immediately texted with a booking link that expires in 30 minutes, keeping classes full without the studio owner or front desk manually managing the list throughout the day.
3. Students who cancel after the deadline receive an automatic late-cancel fee notification per the studio policy, removing the awkward conversation from the staff's plate.
