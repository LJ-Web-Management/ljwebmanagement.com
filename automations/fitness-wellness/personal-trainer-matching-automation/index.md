How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. New members interested in personal training are automatically matched and introduced to a trainer based on their stated goals and schedule availability, instead of a manual front-desk handoff that often depends entirely on which trainer happens to be standing around when the interested member walks up.
2. Matches based on actual goal alignment and real schedule overlap lead to a stronger first session and a better shot at a long-term training relationship than a match made purely on who was available in the moment.
