How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically detects member milestones, a 100th check-in, a one-year anniversary, and sends a congratulatory message, sometimes paired with a small reward, instead of the member experience manager relying on staff to happen to notice and mention a milestone in passing.
2. Recognizing progress consistently, regardless of who's working the front desk that day, keeps members feeling genuinely seen at a gym or studio rather than treated as just another account, and milestone recognition consistently ranks as one of the most appreciated small touches members mention when asked what keeps them coming back.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a member experience manager is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
