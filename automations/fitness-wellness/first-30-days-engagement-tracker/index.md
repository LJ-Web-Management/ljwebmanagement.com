How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation tracks a new member's check-in frequency during their critical first 30 days and automatically triggers extra outreach if engagement is low, since early inactivity is one of the strongest predictors of an eventual cancellation.
2. The onboarding coordinator gets a live list of new members trending toward disengagement while there's still real time to intervene, and catching a slow start in week two instead of discovering it in month three's cancellation report saves memberships that would otherwise quietly lapse without anyone noticing until it's too late.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, an onboarding coordinator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
