How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. If an instructor or stylist's schedule changes, every client with a booking on that schedule is automatically notified with options to rebook, instead of a client finding out only when they arrive and discover their usual person isn't in.
2. The scheduling manager no longer has to manually cross-reference every affected booking against the schedule change and call each client individually, and clients consistently appreciate the heads-up even when the news itself, a swapped instructor, isn't what they were hoping for.

- Studio management software (e.g., Mindbody or Pike13)
- Zapier or Make.com

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a scheduling manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
