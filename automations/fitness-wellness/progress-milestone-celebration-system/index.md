How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As check-in data and workout logs come in, this automation watches for milestones, a 25th check-in, a 90-day attendance streak, a personal best on a tracked lift, and automatically sends a congratulations message the same day the milestone hits, along with a small reward like a branded shirt, a free session, or a shoutout in the studio's newsletter.
2. Recognizing progress in the moment it happens, rather than whenever someone gets around to noticing, keeps members emotionally invested in a program that can otherwise feel repetitive week to week.
3. Trainers get a running feed of member wins they can reference in sessions, making every "congratulations" feel personal instead of automated, even though the detection itself runs entirely on its own.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a personal trainer manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
