How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Clients nearing the end of a purchased personal training package automatically receive a renewal offer before their sessions run out, instead of the client success coordinator only realizing a package expired once the client stops showing up because they assumed training had simply ended.
2. Renewal offers timed to land a few sessions before the package runs dry catch the client while training is still an active part of their routine, converting far more renewals than an offer sent cold after weeks of no contact.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a client success coordinator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation
