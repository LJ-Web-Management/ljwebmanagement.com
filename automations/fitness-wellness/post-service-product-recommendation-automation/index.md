How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on the specific service a client received, they automatically get a follow-up email recommending relevant retail products to help maintain the results at home, instead of the retail manager relying on the service provider to remember to mention products during a rushed appointment.
2. Recommendations are tailored to the actual service performed, a specific shampoo for a color treatment or a particular recovery product for a deep tissue massage, and retail attach rates climb once the recommendation reaches the client at exactly the moment they're thinking about maintaining their results.
