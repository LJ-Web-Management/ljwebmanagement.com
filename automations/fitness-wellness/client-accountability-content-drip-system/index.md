How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. New coaching clients are automatically onboarded into a structured program: a welcome sequence with program expectations and the intake questionnaire, followed by weekly educational content drops tied to their program phase, daily habit tracking reminders, and a Friday accountability check-in form that routes their responses to the coach before their Monday call.
2. Clients who don't submit their check-in receive an automatic nudge, and coaches arrive at every call having already reviewed the week's data, making every session more productive from the first minute.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a health coach, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
