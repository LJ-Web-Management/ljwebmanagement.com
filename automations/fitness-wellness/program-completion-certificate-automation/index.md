How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a member finishes a multi-week program, such as a couch-to-5K series, they automatically receive a completion certificate along with a suggestion for their next program, instead of a member finishing a genuinely meaningful milestone with nothing but silence to mark it.
2. The member experience manager can see completion rates by program, and members who receive real recognition at the finish line, plus a clear next step to keep the momentum going, are far more likely to enroll in whatever comes next instead of quietly drifting away once the original goal is reached.

- Gym management software (e.g., Mindbody or Glofox)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a member experience manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
