How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members who haven't logged into the booking app in a while automatically receive a re-engagement message highlighting new classes or features they may have missed, catching a quietly disengaging member through their app behavior before it shows up as a missed check-in at the front door.
2. Reminding a member what's new, rather than a generic "we miss you" message, gives them an actual reason to open the app again, and this kind of early, specific nudge consistently recovers engagement that a later, blunter win-back message would have missed entirely.
