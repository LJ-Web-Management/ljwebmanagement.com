How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. New clients complete a digital health intake form as part of the online booking process, so the therapist has a complete picture of the client's concerns and contraindications before they walk in.
2. Immediately after each session, the client receives an automated follow-up text asking how they feel and including a one-tap rebooking link for their next appointment at their preferred interval.
3. Clients who haven't rebooked within 30 days receive a gentle reminder, keeping the appointment book full without the therapist making phone calls between sessions.

Tools it connects

- Booking software (e.g., Jane App, Square Appointments, or Vagaro)
