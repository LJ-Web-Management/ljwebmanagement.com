How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Separate from license renewal itself, the automation reminds trainers of required continuing education hours ahead of their recertification deadline, giving the HR and certification manager a second layer of tracking beyond just the certification expiration date.
2. Trainers who fall behind on CE hours get flagged with enough lead time to actually catch up before the deadline arrives, instead of a last-minute scramble to cram in required hours, and the studio avoids the operational disruption of a trainer suddenly unable to teach because their CE requirement quietly lapsed unnoticed.
