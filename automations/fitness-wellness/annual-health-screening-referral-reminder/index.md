How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members due for a routine annual health screening automatically receive a referral reminder pointing them to a partnered health provider, instead of the wellness director's outreach depending entirely on remembering which members are due and when.
2. Members appreciate a wellness-minded reminder that goes beyond just fitness, and a partnership referral program like this deepens the relationship between the studio and its health provider partners while genuinely helping members stay on top of care they might otherwise put off indefinitely.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a wellness director spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
