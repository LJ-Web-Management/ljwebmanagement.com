How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members who cancel automatically receive a short survey asking why, with the responses feeding directly into a report management uses to improve retention, instead of a cancellation simply closing out with no insight into what actually drove the decision.
2. The retention specialist can spot real patterns, a specific class getting cut, a pricing complaint that keeps coming up, that a handful of individual cancellations would never reveal on their own, and those patterns turn into concrete fixes instead of the same churn reasons quietly repeating month after month unnoticed.
