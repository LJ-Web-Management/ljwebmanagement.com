How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. If the regularly scheduled instructor for a class is swapped for a substitute, every member booked into that class automatically receives a heads-up notification ahead of time, instead of members finding out only when they arrive and discover someone unfamiliar teaching.
2. The instructor coordinator no longer has to manually track down and notify every affected booking by hand, and members consistently appreciate the advance notice, even when it doesn't change their decision to attend, simply because nobody likes an unexplained surprise at the front of the room.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an instructor coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
