How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Clients with a health or medical intake form on file are automatically reminded on an annual basis to review and update it before their next session, instead of the compliance coordinator relying on a client to remember to mention a new medication or injury on their own.
2. Keeping intake data current genuinely matters for client safety, especially in a training or spa setting where a new condition can change what's appropriate, and this simple annual reminder closes a gap that used to depend entirely on the client happening to think of it.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a compliance coordinator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
