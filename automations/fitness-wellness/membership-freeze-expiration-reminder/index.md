How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members who placed their membership on a temporary freeze automatically receive a reminder before the freeze period ends and billing is set to resume, instead of a member being surprised by a charge they'd forgotten was coming back.
2. The membership coordinator no longer has to manually track every active freeze and its end date, and members who get a clear heads-up before billing resumes are far less likely to call in confused or upset about a charge that was actually agreed to months earlier.
