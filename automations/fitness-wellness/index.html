    Fitness & Wellness Automations

    52 done-for-you automations built for the roles that keep this industry running. Book a consultation and we'll tailor any of these to your exact tools. Built for the owners implementing them and the staff who'll use them every day.

    Gym / Studio Owner

    Member Check-In & Retention Alert System

    Each day, this automation flags any active member who hasn't checked in for 14 days, adds them to a win-back sequence that starts with a "we miss you" text, escalates to a personal outreach from a staff member, and offers a complimentary session or class if they return within a set window. Members who hit streak milestones (30, 60, 90 days of consistent attendance) automatically receive a congratulations message and a referral incentive, turning the gym's most consistent members into its best recruiters.

    The manual approach means a gym / studio owner handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

    Tools Required
    Gym management software (e.g., Mindbody or Glofox)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (SMS re-engagement)Mailchimp or ActiveCampaignSlack (staff outreach prompts)
    Personal Trainer

    Client Progress Check-In & Workout Delivery System

    Each week, active training clients receive an automated check-in form asking about sleep, energy, soreness, and how last week's workouts felt, and their responses feed directly into a training log the trainer reviews before programming the next week's sessions. Completed workouts are logged via the client app, and any client who skips more than two workouts in a row triggers a personal outreach from the trainer. The trainer spends their time coaching, not chasing clients for updates or manually tracking adherence in a notebook.

    Done by hand, this depends entirely on a personal trainer remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips.

    Tools Required
    Training app (e.g., Trainerize or TrueCoach)Jotform or Typeform (weekly check-in)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (adherence check-in nudges)Google Sheets (progress tracking dashboard)
    Yoga Studio Owner

    Class Waitlist & Auto-Fill Booking System

    When a class reaches capacity, additional sign-ups automatically land on the waitlist and receive a confirmation with their position. The moment a registered student cancels, the next person on the waitlist is immediately texted with a booking link that expires in 30 minutes, keeping classes full without the studio owner or front desk manually managing the list throughout the day. Students who cancel after the deadline receive an automatic late-cancel fee notification per the studio policy, removing the awkward conversation from the staff's plate.

    Done by hand, this depends entirely on a yoga studio owner remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

    Tools Required
    Studio management software (e.g., Mindbody or Pike13)Zapier or Make.comTwilio (waitlist SMS notifications)Stripe (late cancel fee processing)
