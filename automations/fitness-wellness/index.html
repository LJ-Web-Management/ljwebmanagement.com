    FAQ

    Fitness & Wellness automation questions

    What fitness and wellness workflows can be automated?

    The 52 fitness and wellness automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, customer service & communication, and scheduling, reminders & dispatch, among other areas, for roles like Gym Owner, Retention Specialist, and Gym / Studio Owner. Popular starting points include Member Check-In & Retention Alert System, Client Progress Check-In & Workout Delivery System, Class Waitlist & Auto-Fill Booking System.

    Do I need to replace my current fitness and wellness software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in fitness and wellness automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, and Google Sheets, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a fitness and wellness automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
