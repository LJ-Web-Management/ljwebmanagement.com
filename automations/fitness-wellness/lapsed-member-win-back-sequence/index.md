How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an active member goes 2 full weeks without a check-in, this automation triggers a "come back" text with a genuinely useful incentive, a free guest pass, a complimentary session with a trainer, or a discounted month, rather than a generic guilt-trip reminder.
2. If the member still hasn't returned after 7 more days, a second message goes out with a stronger offer, and members who reactivate are automatically pulled out of the sequence.
3. Catching disengagement at 2 weeks instead of waiting for the eventual cancellation request means the gym is reaching members while they're still emotionally invested, not after they've already mentally checked out.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a gym / studio owner has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
