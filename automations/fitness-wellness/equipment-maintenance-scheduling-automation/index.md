How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Gym equipment is automatically flagged for scheduled maintenance based on usage hours or elapsed time since the last service, preventing breakdowns from being caught only after a member reports a machine that's already broken.
2. The facilities manager gets a proactive maintenance schedule instead of a purely reactive one, and equipment downtime drops noticeably once servicing happens on a predictable schedule tied to actual usage rather than whenever someone happens to notice something's making an odd noise.

Done manually, a facilities manager has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.
