How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a spot opens up in a fully booked class due to a cancellation, the next person on the waitlist is automatically notified and booked into the open slot, instead of the class scheduling coordinator manually working down a waitlist by phone every time a spot frees up.
2. The promotion happens within seconds of the cancellation, so a popular class never runs under capacity purely because nobody got around to filling the gap in time, and members on a waitlist trust it actually works instead of feeling like their name just disappears into a list nobody checks.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a class scheduling coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
