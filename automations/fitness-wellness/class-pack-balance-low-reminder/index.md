How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members using a punch card or class pack automatically get notified as their remaining balance runs low, prompting a timely repurchase before it hits zero, instead of the billing coordinator only learning a pack is empty when the member is turned away at check-in with no credits left.
2. Members who get an early low-balance warning repurchase at a far higher rate than members who discover they're out mid-checkout, and revenue from pack renewals becomes far more predictable once the reminder happens automatically at the same threshold every time.
