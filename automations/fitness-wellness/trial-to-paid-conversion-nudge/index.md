How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Three days before a free trial expires, the prospect automatically receives a limited-time join offer, priced and timed to create genuine urgency rather than a generic "don't forget to subscribe" reminder.
2. If they haven't converted by the last day of the trial, a final message goes out with a reminder of the offer's expiration and a direct signup link, and anyone who converts is instantly removed from the sequence.
3. Trials that end without any nudge convert at a noticeably lower rate than trials paired with a well-timed offer in the final days, since most people simply forget the trial is ending until it already has.
