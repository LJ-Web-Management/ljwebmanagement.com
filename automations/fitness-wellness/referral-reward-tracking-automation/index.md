How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a referred friend signs up, the system automatically detects the connection and triggers the promised reward, a free class or a discount, to the referring member without the loyalty coordinator manually tracking referrals in a spreadsheet and remembering who's owed what.
2. Members who get their reward promptly and without having to ask for it are far more likely to refer again, and referral volume climbs measurably once the reward is reliable and automatic instead of an informal perk that quietly stops working once follow-through gets inconsistent.

Tools it connects

- Referral platform (e.g., ReferralCandy) or Zapier custom build
- Gym management software (e.g., Mindbody or Glofox)
