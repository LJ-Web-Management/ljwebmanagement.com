How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation internally tracks instructor and trainer certification expiration dates and alerts management well ahead of each renewal deadline, instead of the HR and certification manager discovering a lapsed certification only when a liability question comes up or an insurance audit asks for proof.
2. Every instructor's certification status is visible on a single dashboard rather than scattered across individual personnel files, and the studio avoids the real liability exposure of an instructor unknowingly teaching on an expired credential.
