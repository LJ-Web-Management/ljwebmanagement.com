How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For businesses with a corporate wellness partnership, usage reports showing employee engagement are automatically compiled and sent to the partner company on a set schedule, instead of the corporate wellness manager manually pulling attendance data and building a report by hand every reporting period.
2. Partner companies get consistent, professional visibility into whether their wellness benefit is actually being used, which matters directly to whether they renew the partnership, and the studio's own team spends that time on member experience instead of spreadsheet work.
