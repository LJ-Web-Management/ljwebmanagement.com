How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a scheduled body composition scan, the results are automatically compiled into a simple, easy-to-read progress report and emailed to the member, showing change over time instead of the health coach manually building a comparison report for every scanned member.
2. Members can see their own trend line building visit after visit rather than a single disconnected number each time, and visible progress delivered automatically and consistently is one of the strongest drivers of continued engagement a program like this has.
