How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members who asked about an out-of-stock retail item, supplements, apparel, equipment, automatically receive a notification the moment it's back in stock, instead of the retail manager relying on the member to happen to check back on their own.
2. Interest captured at the point of the original ask converts into an actual sale once the item is available again, recovering revenue that would otherwise simply be lost the moment a member walked away from an empty shelf and never thought about it again.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a retail manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
