How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new patient's first appointment is booked, the owner automatically enters a welcome series that sends a pre-visit email covering what to bring, parking and check-in instructions, and a link to complete new patient paperwork online ahead of time.
2. After the first visit, the series continues with a message introducing the practice's wellness plan options, a summary of the clinic's after-hours and emergency policies, and an invitation to download the client portal app for records and scheduling.
3. The client care specialist no longer has to repeat the same onboarding explanation to every new client in person, and new owners arrive for their first visit already prepared, which measurably shortens check-in time and first-visit no-show rates.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a client care specialist has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
