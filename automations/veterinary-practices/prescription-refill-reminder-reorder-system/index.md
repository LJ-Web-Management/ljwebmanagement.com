How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every prescription filled through the practice is logged with its expected refill date based on dosing instructions, and 5 days before that date the pet owner automatically receives a text with a one-click reorder link that routes the request straight to the pharmacy coordinator's queue for approval.
2. Approved refills that require curbside or in-store pickup trigger an automatic ready-for-pickup text once filled, and medications tied to a required recheck exam prompt the owner to book that exam before the refill can be released.
3. The pharmacy coordinator no longer has to track refill dates manually across hundreds of active patients, and owners refill on time far more consistently once the reorder process takes one tap instead of a phone call during business hours.
