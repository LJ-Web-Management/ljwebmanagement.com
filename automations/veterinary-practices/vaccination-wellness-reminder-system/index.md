How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every patient in the practice management system is tracked against their vaccination schedule and wellness exam due dates, and automated reminders go out to the pet owner at 30 and 7 days before each due date, via text if a mobile number is on file, email if not.
2. Overdue reminders continue on a monthly basis until the appointment is scheduled, and the practice manager sees a live overdue list filtered by species, due service, and months overdue.
3. Preventive care that used to be left to the owner to initiate is now proactively driven by the practice, increasing compliance rates and the annual revenue per patient that comes with consistent preventive care visits.
