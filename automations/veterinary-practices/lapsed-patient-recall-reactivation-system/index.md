How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Patients who haven't been seen in 12, 18, or 24 months are automatically segmented into a lapsed-patient list pulled nightly from the practice management system, and each tier receives a different outreach: a friendly reminder email at 12 months, a text with a reactivation offer at 18 months, and a note added to the front desk's outbound call list at 24 months so a team member can personally check in.
2. The veterinary technician assigned to recall outreach sees a single dashboard of exactly which patients are overdue and by how much, rather than relying on staff to notice gaps during a busy day of appointments.
3. Practices running this system consistently bring back 10 to 15 percent of patients who would otherwise have quietly switched clinics or stopped seeking preventive care altogether.
