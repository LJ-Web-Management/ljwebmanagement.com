How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Appointment requests submitted via the practice website are automatically screened against the schedule for the correct provider, visit type, and patient species, and the pet owner receives an immediate confirmation with the scheduled date, time, and pre-visit instructions, including any fasting requirements, what to bring, and a link to complete the new patient form if it's the first visit.
2. No-shows receive an automatic follow-up the same day offering to reschedule, and clients who regularly no-show are flagged for the front desk to call rather than continue receiving automated booking access, protecting appointment slot availability for clients who reliably show up.

Left unautomated, a client service representative spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.
