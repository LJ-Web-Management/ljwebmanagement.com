    How it works

    What this automation does, step by step

    1. After every appointment, the veterinarian's discharge instructions are automatically formatted and texted to the pet owner as a clean, readable summary, including medications prescribed, dosing instructions, follow-up care reminders, and a direct link to call the clinic with questions.

    2. For surgical patients, a post-operative check-in message goes out automatically at 24 hours, 72 hours, and 7 days asking how the patient is recovering and flagging any owner-reported concerns for the veterinarian to review.

    3. Patients on long-term medications receive automatic refill reminders, and those on prescription diets receive check-in messages at 30 and 60 days to assess compliance and outcomes.

    Tools it connects
    • Veterinary practice management software
    • Zapier or Make.com
    • Twilio (discharge summary and post-op check-in SMS)
    • Jotform (post-op recovery check-in form)
    • Email (refill and diet compliance reminders)

    Using different software? We build around what you already have, so you never have to switch tools to automate.
