How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every confirmed appointment automatically triggers a reminder sequence: an email 72 hours out, a text 24 hours out, and a final text 2 hours before the visit, each including a one-tap link the owner can use to confirm or reschedule without calling the front desk.
2. Owners who reschedule are automatically slotted into the next available opening for that provider and visit type, and the vacated slot is immediately released back into the online booking calendar so it doesn't sit empty.
3. The front desk coordinator's no-show report updates automatically every week, and clinics running this reminder cadence typically cut no-shows by 20 to 30 percent within the first two months, recovering appointment slots that used to go unused.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a front desk coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
