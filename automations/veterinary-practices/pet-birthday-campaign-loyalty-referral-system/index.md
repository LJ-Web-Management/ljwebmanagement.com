How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every pet's birthday on file triggers a personalized birthday email from the practice, with the pet's name, a cute graphic, and a special birthday exam or grooming discount that expires within the birthday month.
2. Pet owners who haven't visited in 12 months receive their pet's birthday message with a particularly compelling win-back offer attached.
3. Clients who refer a new patient receive an automatic thank-you and a loyalty credit to their account, and the marketing coordinator sees a monthly referral leaderboard showing which clients are the practice's top ambassadors, so the team knows exactly who to recognize and appreciate.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

Manually, a marketing coordinator is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation
