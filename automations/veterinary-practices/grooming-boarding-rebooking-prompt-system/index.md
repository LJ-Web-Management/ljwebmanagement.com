How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each pet's grooming or boarding history is used to calculate its typical rebooking cadence, whether that's every 6 weeks for a groom or every few months for boarding around known travel patterns, and a rebooking reminder automatically goes out to the owner 5 to 7 days before that cadence is due to repeat.
2. Owners can rebook directly from the reminder text without calling in, and the grooming coordinator sees a forward-looking calendar showing how many rebooking slots are likely to fill each week based on historical response rates.
3. Grooming and boarding revenue, which used to depend entirely on owners remembering to call back in, becomes a predictable recurring stream once the rebooking prompt does the remembering for them.
