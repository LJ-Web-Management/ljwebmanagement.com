How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As medications are dispensed or administered, inventory counts update automatically in the practice management system, and any item that falls below its reorder threshold generates a purchase order suggestion for the inventory manager to approve with one click.
2. Controlled substances require a separate log entry per administration, and this automation prompts the administering team member to complete the DEA log entry at the time of use, with the inventory manager receiving a daily reconciliation report comparing administered quantities against log entries and flagging any discrepancy that requires investigation before it becomes a DEA compliance issue.
