## Veterinary Practices automation questions

**What veterinary practice workflows can be automated?**

The 10 veterinary practice automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, marketing & customer retention, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Practice Manager, Veterinarian, and Client Service Representative. Popular starting points include [Vaccination & Wellness Reminder System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/veterinary-practices/vaccination-wellness-reminder-system/), [Discharge Instructions & Post-Visit Follow-Up System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/veterinary-practices/discharge-instructions-post-visit-follow-up-system/), [Online Appointment Request & Confirmation System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/veterinary-practices/online-appointment-request-confirmation-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current veterinary practice software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in veterinary practice automations are Twilio, Jotform, Mailchimp, and Google Sheets, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a veterinary practice automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
