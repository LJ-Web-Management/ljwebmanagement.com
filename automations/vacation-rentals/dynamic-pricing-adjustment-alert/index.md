How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation monitors local occupancy trends, nearby events, and competitor pricing, and automatically flags nights where the current listed price is significantly under or over market.
2. The host adjusts before losing bookings to a price that's too high, or leaving money on the table with a price that's too low, instead of setting a rate once and forgetting about it.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a revenue manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
