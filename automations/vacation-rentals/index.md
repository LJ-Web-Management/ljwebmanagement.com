## Vacation Rentals & Short-Term Rentals automation questions

**What vacation rental workflows can be automated?**

The 5 vacation rental automations in our catalog cover customer service & communication, scheduling, reminders & dispatch, and reporting & analytics, among other areas, for roles like Property Manager, Operations Coordinator, and Revenue Manager. Popular starting points include [Pre-Arrival Check-In Instructions](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/vacation-rentals/pre-arrival-check-in-instructions/), [Cleaning Turnover Scheduling](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/vacation-rentals/cleaning-turnover-scheduling/), [Dynamic Pricing Adjustment Alert](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/vacation-rentals/dynamic-pricing-adjustment-alert/).

**Do I need to replace my current vacation rental software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in vacation rental automations are Twilio and Slack, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a vacation rental automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
