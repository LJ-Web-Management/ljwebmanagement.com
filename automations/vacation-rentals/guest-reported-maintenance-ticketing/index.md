## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a guest reports an issue during their stay, a broken appliance, a maintenance need, this automation automatically creates a maintenance ticket and routes it to the appropriate vendor or handyman, tracking it to resolution.
2. The issue stops getting lost three messages deep in a guest chat thread that no one revisits after checkout.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a property manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
