How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a guest checks out, this automation automatically notifies the cleaning team with the turnover deadline before the next guest arrives, and flags the property manager if a same-day turnover is at risk based on the cleaner's confirmed availability.
2. A cleaner double-booked or unavailable gets caught with hours to spare instead of discovered at check-in time.
