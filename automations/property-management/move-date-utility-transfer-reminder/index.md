How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Tenants automatically receive a reminder ahead of their move-in or move-out date to set up or transfer their utilities, reducing the service gaps and confused calls to the office that happen when a new resident arrives to a unit with no power on, or a departing tenant leaves utilities running in their name past move-out.
2. The reminder includes the local utility providers' contact information pre-filled for that specific property, turning what used to be a source of first-week frustration for new residents into a smooth, uneventful handoff.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a move coordinator, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
