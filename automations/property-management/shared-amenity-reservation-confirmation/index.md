How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a resident books a shared amenity, a clubhouse, a pool area, a rooftop deck, the reservation is automatically confirmed and added to a shared calendar visible to management, preventing the double-bookings that happen when two residents both think they've claimed the same Saturday afternoon.
2. The amenities coordinator no longer has to manually cross-check a paper sign-up sheet against a separate booking request inbox, and residents get instant confirmation instead of waiting to hear back about whether their requested date actually worked.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an amenities coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
