How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation continuously analyzes booking data across shared amenities and flags when something is consistently underused or overbooked, giving the portfolio analyst hard data to guide decisions instead of management going purely on impressions of which amenities "seem" popular.
2. An overbooked amenity might justify adding a second reservation slot or extended hours, while a chronically empty one might not be worth the maintenance cost at renewal, and both calls get made with actual usage numbers behind them rather than a guess based on who happened to complain loudest at the last resident meeting.
