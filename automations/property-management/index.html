    FAQ

    Property Management automation questions

    What property management workflows can be automated?

    The 40 property management automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, compliance, documents & deadlines, and marketing & customer retention, among other areas, for roles like Compliance Coordinator, Move Coordinator, and Resident Services Manager. Popular starting points include Maintenance Request Triage & Vendor Dispatch System, Rent Payment Reminder & Late Fee Notice System, 90/60/30-Day Lease Renewal Countdown Sequence.

    Do I need to replace my current property management software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in property management automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Airtable, and Google Sheets, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a property management automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
