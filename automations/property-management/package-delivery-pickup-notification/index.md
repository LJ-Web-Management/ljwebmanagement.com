How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a package arrives at the front desk or a smart locker, the resident automatically receives a notification letting them know it's ready for pickup, instead of packages piling up because nobody told the resident it had even arrived.
2. The front desk coordinator no longer fields a stream of "has my package come yet" calls, and packages that used to sit for days cluttering the lobby, sometimes going missing in the pile, get claimed within hours once residents know the moment something's waiting for them.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a front desk coordinator has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
