How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At move-out, the automation calculates deposit deductions directly from the move-out inspection data already on file and generates the itemized return letter within the legally required timeline, instead of the move coordinator manually itemizing damages against a paper move-in checklist that may or may not still be on file.
2. Every deduction is backed by a specific inspection note or photo, giving tenants a transparent, defensible breakdown rather than a vague lump-sum deduction, and disputes over deposit returns drop significantly once the calculation itself is standardized and documented.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a move coordinator, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
