How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A vendor assigned to a work order automatically receives the full job details by text and is asked to confirm availability, with an automatic follow-up if they haven't responded within a set number of hours, instead of the vendor relations coordinator calling around to confirm every single dispatched job by phone.
2. Vendors who don't confirm within the follow-up window are automatically flagged so the job can be reassigned before a tenant is left waiting on a no-show, and a documented confirmation trail exists for every job the moment it's assigned rather than only existing in someone's memory of a phone call.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a vendor relations coordinator has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
