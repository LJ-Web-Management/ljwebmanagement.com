How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Tenants identified as having an unregistered pet, whether flagged during an inspection or reported by a neighbor, are automatically sent a reminder to complete pet registration and pay any applicable pet fees, closing a revenue and liability gap that a compliance coordinator would otherwise only catch by chance.
2. Tenants who don't respond within a set window are automatically flagged for a formal notice referencing the lease terms, and unregistered pets that used to slip through unnoticed for months become a routine, low-friction paperwork item instead.
