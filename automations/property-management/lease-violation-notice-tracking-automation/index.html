    How it works

    What this automation does, step by step

    1. A documented lease violation automatically generates the legally compliant notice and creates the tracking record required before any further escalation can happen, instead of a compliance coordinator drafting a violation notice from scratch each time and hoping it holds up if the matter is ever contested.

    2. Every notice is pulled from an approved template matched to the specific violation type and jurisdiction, and the automatic tracking record means a pattern of repeated violations by the same tenant is visible immediately rather than requiring someone to remember and manually connect separate incidents months apart.

    Tools it connects
    • Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)
    • Zapier or Make.com
    • Mailchimp or email
    • Google Sheets or Airtable (deduction tracker)

    Using different software? We build around what you already have, so you never have to switch tools to automate.

    Before and after

    The manual way vs. the automated way

    Without automation

    The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a compliance coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

    With this automation

    • Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
    • Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
    • Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
    • An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
