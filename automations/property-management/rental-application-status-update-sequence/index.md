How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Prospective tenants automatically receive an update at each stage of the application process, received, screening in progress, approved, or denied, without the leasing agent having to personally call or email every applicant to relay the same news.
2. Applicants left wondering what's happening tend to keep looking elsewhere, so closing that communication gap measurably improves the rate of approved applicants actually signing the lease instead of taking a different unit that responded faster, and the leasing agent's day is freed from a steady stream of "any update" calls.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a leasing agent is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
