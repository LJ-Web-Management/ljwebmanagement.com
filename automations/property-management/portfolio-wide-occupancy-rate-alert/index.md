How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Management automatically receives an alert whenever portfolio-wide occupancy drops below a defined target threshold, prompting an immediate review of vacant units and marketing efforts rather than a slipping occupancy rate only becoming visible during the next scheduled monthly report.
2. The portfolio manager can drill into which specific properties are dragging the average down, letting leasing resources get redirected to the properties that need it most instead of every property receiving the same generic marketing push regardless of how it's actually performing.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a portfolio manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
