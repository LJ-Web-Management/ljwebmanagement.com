How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a smart water sensor detects usage patterns consistent with a leak, the automation immediately escalates an alert to the maintenance coordinator and the on-call vendor, often catching a slow leak behind a wall or under a sink well before it turns into major water damage a tenant would only notice once the ceiling below is already stained.
2. Response happens within minutes of the sensor triggering rather than whenever a tenant happens to notice a puddle, and the difference between catching a leak same-day versus a week later is routinely the difference between a small repair bill and a full unit remediation.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a maintenance coordinator has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
