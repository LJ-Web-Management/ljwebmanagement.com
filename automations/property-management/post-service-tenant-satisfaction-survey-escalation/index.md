How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a maintenance ticket is closed, the tenant automatically receives a short satisfaction survey asking how the repair went, and any low score is automatically flagged and routed to the resident services manager for a personal follow-up rather than sitting quietly in a report nobody reviews until the next resident satisfaction survey rolls around months later.
2. Catching a bad maintenance experience within a day of it happening, while there's still time to make it right, consistently prevents small frustrations from compounding into a lease that doesn't get renewed.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a resident services manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
