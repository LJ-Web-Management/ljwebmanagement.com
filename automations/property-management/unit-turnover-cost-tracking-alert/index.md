How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically tracks and totals turnover costs, cleaning, repairs, marketing, per unit, and flags any unit with unusually high turnover expense for review, giving the portfolio analyst hard numbers instead of a vague sense that a particular unit "always seems to cost more" between tenants.
2. A unit that consistently runs above-average turnover cost often points to a deeper issue, poor tenant screening, an aging unit needing a bigger investment, that only becomes visible once the cost is actually tracked and compared unit by unit rather than absorbed anonymously into the portfolio's overall expense line.
