How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Unit owners automatically receive a reminder ahead of HOA or condo assessment due dates, tracked and sent separately from any regular monthly rent reminder so the two obligations never get confused by an owner managing multiple payment types.
2. The association manager no longer has to manually cross-reference a separate assessment schedule against each owner's file, and late assessment payments, which can affect an entire association's budget and reserve funding, drop meaningfully once every owner gets a clear, timely reminder instead of relying on memory alone.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an association manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
