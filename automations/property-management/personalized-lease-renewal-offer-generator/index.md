How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a lease renewal approaches, the automation automatically generates a personalized renewal offer based on the tenant's actual payment history and length of tenancy, rewarding a long-tenured, always-on-time resident with a smaller increase than a flat rate applied to everyone regardless of track record.
2. The leasing manager can see exactly why each offer was calculated the way it was, and tenants who receive an offer that visibly reflects their good history renew at a noticeably higher rate than tenants handed the same generic increase as every other unit in the building.
