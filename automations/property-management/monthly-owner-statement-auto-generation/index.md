How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Monthly financial statements for each property owner are automatically compiled from rent collected and expenses paid, then delivered on a consistent schedule, instead of the portfolio accountant building each report by hand from raw transaction data every single month.
2. Owners with questions can see a clear, standardized statement rather than waiting on a manager's availability to explain a spreadsheet, and closing the books each month, which used to take days across a large portfolio, now happens automatically as transactions are recorded throughout the month.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a portfolio accountant has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
