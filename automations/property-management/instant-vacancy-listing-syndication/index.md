How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a unit is marked available in the property management platform, its listing, photos, price, and availability date included, is automatically pushed out to Zillow, Apartments.com, and every other connected rental portal simultaneously, instead of the marketing coordinator logging into each site one by one to post the same listing manually.
2. Listings that go live across every channel within minutes of a unit turning vacant get more views and more qualified inquiries in the first critical days than listings posted piecemeal over a week, directly shrinking the number of days a unit sits empty and earning nothing.

Tools it connects

- Property management platform (e.g., AppFolio or Buildium)
- Zapier or Make.com
- Zillow Rental Manager or listing syndication tool
