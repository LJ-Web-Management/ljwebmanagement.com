How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a unit is marked as upcoming-vacant, the automation automatically requests that updated photos or a video walkthrough be scheduled before the unit needs to go live on listing sites, instead of the marketing coordinator discovering on posting day that the only photos on file are years old and no longer represent the unit's actual condition.
2. The request is timed to land with enough lead time for photography to actually happen before the vacancy date, meaning the listing goes live with accurate, current media from day one rather than outdated photos that quietly cost the property qualified inquiries.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a marketing coordinator has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.
