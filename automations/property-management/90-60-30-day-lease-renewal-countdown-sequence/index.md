How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Both the tenant and the leasing manager are automatically notified on a fixed schedule, 90, 60, and 30 days out, before a lease expires, so a renewal offer or move-out plan is set in motion with real time to spare instead of the deadline sneaking up on everyone.
2. Tenants who haven't responded to the 60-day notice get a personal follow-up call flagged automatically, and the leasing manager's dashboard shows every unit's renewal status at a glance, replacing a spreadsheet that only ever reflected reality if someone remembered to update it that week.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a leasing manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
