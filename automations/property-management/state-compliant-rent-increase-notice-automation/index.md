How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. State-compliant rent increase notices are automatically generated and sent to tenants with the legally required advance notice period built directly into the timing, so a compliance coordinator never has to manually cross-reference which state's specific notice period applies to which property in a multi-state portfolio.
2. Every notice is generated from an approved template mapped to the correct jurisdiction, removing the risk of an increase notice being challenged or invalidated purely on a procedural technicality, a costly and entirely avoidable mistake that used to happen more often than management liked to admit.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a compliance coordinator, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
