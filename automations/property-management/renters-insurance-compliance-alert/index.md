How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Tenants whose renters insurance policy is expiring soon or missing entirely from their file are automatically flagged and sent a reminder to upload updated proof of coverage, closing a liability gap that a compliance coordinator would otherwise only catch during an occasional manual file audit.
2. Tenants who don't respond to the first reminder automatically receive a second, firmer notice referencing the lease requirement, and the property's overall coverage compliance rate becomes something management can see on a dashboard in real time instead of discovering a lapse only after an incident makes it urgent.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a compliance coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
