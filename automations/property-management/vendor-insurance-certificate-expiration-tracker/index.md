How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Vendors' certificates of insurance on file are automatically tracked for their expiration dates, and any vendor whose coverage has lapsed is automatically flagged or outright blocked from being assigned a new work order until an updated certificate is uploaded, closing a liability gap a vendor relations coordinator would otherwise only catch by chance.
2. Vendors get an automatic reminder as their coverage nears expiration, keeping most renewals current before they ever become a scheduling problem, and the portfolio never has an uninsured vendor working on a property without someone actually knowing it.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a vendor relations coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
