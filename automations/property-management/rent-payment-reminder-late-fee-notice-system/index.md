How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Tenants automatically receive a reminder a few days before rent is due, and if payment hasn't posted after the grace period, an automatic late notice with the calculated fee is generated and sent, without the accounts receivable coordinator manually running a delinquency report and drafting notices one at a time.
2. Every notice is logged with a timestamp, giving a clean record if the matter ever needs to escalate further, and on-time payment rates climb noticeably once tenants know a reminder, not silence, is what follows a missed due date.
