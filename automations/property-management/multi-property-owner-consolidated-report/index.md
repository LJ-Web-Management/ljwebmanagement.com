How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Owners with multiple properties automatically receive one consolidated performance report covering their entire portfolio instead of a separate report per property that they have to manually add together to see the full picture.
2. The portfolio accountant builds the reporting logic once, and it scales automatically as an owner adds properties, saving hours of manual report assembly every single reporting cycle, and owners consistently rate this single most valuable change when asked what improved about how the management company communicates with them.

Left unautomated, a portfolio accountant spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.

### With this automation
