How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on the move-in or move-out date already on file, tenants automatically receive a digital checklist covering exactly what's expected of them, along with a direct link to schedule the required inspection, instead of the move coordinator emailing each tenant individually and hoping they read it.
2. Inspections that aren't scheduled within a set window automatically trigger a reminder, and both move-ins and move-outs run through the same consistent, documented process every time, which matters most when a deposit dispute later depends on exactly what condition a unit was in on a specific day.

## The manual way vs. the automated way
