How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. New residents who haven't yet activated their online resident portal account automatically receive setup reminders with a direct activation link, increasing adoption of self-service tools like online rent payment and maintenance requests instead of a resident services manager continuing to field paper checks and phone-in maintenance calls indefinitely.
2. Higher portal adoption directly reduces manual work across the front office, since every resident who pays online and submits requests digitally is one fewer person calling in for something the portal already handles.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a resident services manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
