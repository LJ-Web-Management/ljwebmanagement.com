How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a lease is signed, the new resident automatically receives a digital welcome packet covering move-in logistics, community rules, and key contact information, so a resident services manager doesn't have to personally walk every new signee through the same set of basics on move-in day.
2. The packet includes direct links to set up the resident portal, register a vehicle, and submit a maintenance request, reducing the wave of basic "how do I" questions that otherwise floods the front office during the first week of every new tenancy.
