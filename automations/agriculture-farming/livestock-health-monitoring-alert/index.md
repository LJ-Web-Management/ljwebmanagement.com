How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on data from ear tags, automated feed intake logs, or manual health checks entered by staff on a mobile app, this automation automatically flags any animal showing a reading outside the normal range, reduced feed intake, unusual movement patterns, for the herd manager to check personally.
2. A minor issue gets caught and treated while it's still isolated to one animal instead of becoming a herd-wide problem discovered days later.

Tools it connects

- A livestock monitoring platform (Allflex or Cowlar)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a herd manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
