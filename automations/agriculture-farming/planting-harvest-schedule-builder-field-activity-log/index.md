How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on crop selection, field acreage, seed population rates, and the planned planting window, this automation generates a field-by-field planting schedule with equipment requirements and operator assignments.
2. As planting progresses, operators submit brief daily field logs via mobile form, and the farm manager sees a live map of completed versus remaining acreage in real time.
3. When harvest begins, the same system tracks field-by-field yield data as the combine reports it, compares actual yield against projected bushels, and flags underperforming fields for agronomic review, turning a spreadsheet that lives on one person's laptop into a shared, always-current operational picture.
