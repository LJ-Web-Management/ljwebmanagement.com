How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a crop scout submits a scouting report identifying pest pressure or disease above economic threshold (whether aphids, corn rootworm, white mold, or any flagged issue) this automation immediately notifies the farm manager and the designated agronomist, calculates the field acreage requiring treatment, generates a pre-filled pesticide application record, and schedules the application crew.
2. The entire chain from "I found a problem" to "the sprayer is scheduled" happens automatically, rather than through a series of calls and texts that eat hours out of a narrow spray window when every hour of delay matters.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a crop scout, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
