## Agriculture & Farming automation questions

**What farm workflows can be automated?**

The 10 farm automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, compliance, documents & deadlines, and lead follow-up & sales, among other areas, for roles like Farm Manager, Crop Scout, and Agricultural Sales Representative. Popular starting points include [Planting & Harvest Schedule Builder & Field Activity Log](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/agriculture-farming/planting-harvest-schedule-builder-field-activity-log/), [Pest & Disease Alert & Treatment Dispatch System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/agriculture-farming/pest-disease-alert-treatment-dispatch-system/), [Seed & Input Reorder Campaign & Pre-Season Booking System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/agriculture-farming/seed-input-reorder-campaign-pre-season-booking-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current farm software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in farm automations are Google Sheets, Twilio, Jotform, and Airtable, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a farm automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
