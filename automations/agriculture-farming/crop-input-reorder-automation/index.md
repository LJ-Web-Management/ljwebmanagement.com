How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As seed, fertilizer, and chemical inventory is drawn down and logged against each field's application, this automation tracks usage against what's actually needed for the remaining planned acreage for the season.
2. The moment projected usage suggests the farm will run short before the season's done, it automatically flags a reorder need to the operations manager, instead of the shortage surfacing mid-application when it's too late to source more in time.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a farm operations manager, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
