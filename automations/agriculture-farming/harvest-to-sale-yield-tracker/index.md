How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As harvest data is logged by field, this automation automatically compares actual yield against the season's projections, updates the available inventory figure for sale, and notifies the sales team of what's newly available to move.
2. The field connects directly to the sales pipeline the moment the combine finishes a pass, instead of yield data sitting in a yield monitor for days before anyone manually updates what's available to sell.
