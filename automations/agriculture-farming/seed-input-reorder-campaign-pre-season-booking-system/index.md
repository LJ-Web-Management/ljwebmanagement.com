How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each fall, this automation sends every grower in the rep's territory a personalized pre-season booking offer for seed, crop protection, and fertilizer, populated with last year's purchase history and any current early-pay incentives.
2. Growers who book early receive automatic order confirmations and are added to a delivery scheduling sequence for spring.
3. Growers who haven't responded by a set date receive a follow-up from the rep.
4. The result is a pre-season order book that is substantially locked in before the competition can make their pitch, rather than chasing individual orders in the chaos of spring planting.
