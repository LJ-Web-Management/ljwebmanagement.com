How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each animal in the herd is tracked by ID with a complete health record (vaccination dates, treatment history, weight gain, and breeding status) maintained automatically as events are logged via a mobile form in the field.
2. When an animal is due for a scheduled vaccination or health event based on age or last treatment date, the livestock manager receives a task alert with the animal IDs, the protocol, and the required product.
3. Withdrawal periods for treated animals are automatically flagged to prevent any medicated animal from being marketed before the withholding period has expired, a compliance issue with serious financial and legal consequences.
