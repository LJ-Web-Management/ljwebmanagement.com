How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation maintains a calendar of every crop insurance reporting deadline (acreage reporting, production reporting, prevented planting claims, loss notifications) for every policy and every covered field.
2. Reminders are sent to the farm accountant and the farm manager at 30 and 7 days before each deadline.
3. When a loss event occurs, the system generates a checklist of required documentation, assigns tasks for field verification and yield sampling, and tracks completion until the claim package is submitted, ensuring that a covered loss doesn't become an unpaid claim because a deadline was missed or a document was missing.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a farm accountant has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation
