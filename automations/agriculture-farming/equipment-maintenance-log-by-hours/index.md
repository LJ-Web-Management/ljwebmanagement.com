How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Tied to hour-meter readings on tractors and other equipment, whether logged manually or pulled from onboard telematics, this automation automatically schedules maintenance tasks at manufacturer-recommended intervals and orders the commonly needed parts ahead of the actual service date.
2. Equipment stops going down in the middle of a critical planting or harvest window because maintenance was scheduled around the calendar instead of around the machine's actual usage.

Tools it connects

- John Deere Operations Center or similar telematics
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Google Sheets (maintenance log)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/google-sheets/)
- Email or SMS

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an equipment manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
