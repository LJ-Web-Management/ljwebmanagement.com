How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Forty-eight hours before arrival, every guest receives a personalized pre-arrival message with their reservation summary, an early check-in upgrade offer, and add-on options tailored to their stay type, a romance package for anniversary bookings, a business kit for corporate travelers.
2. Responses flow directly into the PMS, updating the reservation and triggering any requested add-ons before the guest arrives.
3. On the morning of check-in, the front desk team receives a prepared briefing for every arriving guest (VIP status, special requests, pre-ordered upgrades, and any notes from previous stays) so every check-in feels personal rather than transactional.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a front desk manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
