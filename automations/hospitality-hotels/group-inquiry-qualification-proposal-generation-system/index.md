How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Group and event inquiries submitted through the hotel website are automatically screened for date availability against the function space calendar, guest room block requirements, and minimum F&B spend.
2. Qualified leads receive an automated preliminary availability confirmation within minutes and are booked for a discovery call with the catering manager.
3. Once event details are confirmed, the system assembles the proposal document (with personalized room block pricing, menu options, and AV packages pre-populated) and sends it to the client for e-signature.
4. The catering manager spends their time selling and closing, not reformatting the same proposal template for every inquiry.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a catering & events sales manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
