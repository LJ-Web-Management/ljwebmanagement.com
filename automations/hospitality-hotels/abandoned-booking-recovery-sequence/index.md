How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a website visitor starts a reservation, selecting dates and a room type, but leaves the booking engine before completing payment, the system automatically captures the session and sends a follow-up email within an hour with their selected dates pre-filled and a direct link back to checkout.
2. A second message goes out 24 hours later, and if the booking still hasn't been completed, a final message offers a small incentive, a complimentary breakfast or a room upgrade, to close the gap.
3. Visitors who abandon a booking during a rate spike are flagged separately so the revenue manager can see whether pricing, not indecision, is the real reason bookings are being lost.
4. Reservations that used to disappear the moment a guest closed the browser tab are now recovered at a meaningful rate every month.
