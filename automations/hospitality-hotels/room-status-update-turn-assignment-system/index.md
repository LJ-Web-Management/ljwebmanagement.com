How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As guests check out, room status updates automatically from "occupied" to "due out" in the housekeeping system, and turn assignments are pushed immediately to the assigned room attendant's mobile device.
2. When a room is cleaned and inspected, the attendant marks it complete via the app, triggering an instant update in the PMS so the front desk can assign it to an arriving guest or sell an early check-in.
3. Rooms that have been marked ready but not inspected within 30 minutes generate an alert to the housekeeping manager.
4. The front desk and housekeeping team operate from the same live picture rather than calling each other all day to check availability.
