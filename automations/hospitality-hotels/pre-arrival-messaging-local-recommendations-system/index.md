How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Three days before arrival, every incoming guest automatically receives a personalized message with check-in instructions, parking details, and a curated list of local restaurants and activities based on their length of stay and room type.
2. The same message includes one or two relevant upsell offers, an early check-in upgrade or a spa credit, that the guest can accept with a single tap, with the response flowing directly into the PMS to update the reservation.
3. Guests arriving after 9pm are automatically flagged for a separate late-arrival instructions message so the front desk isn't fielding the same "how do I get in" calls every night.
4. Guests show up already knowing what to expect, and the property captures upsell revenue that used to depend on a front desk agent remembering to ask at check-in.
