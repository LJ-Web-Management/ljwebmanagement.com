    FAQ

    Hospitality & Hotels automation questions

    What hotel workflows can be automated?

    The 10 hotel automations in our catalog cover lead follow-up & sales, reviews, feedback & reputation, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Hotel General Manager, Front Desk Manager, and Revenue Manager. Popular starting points include Guest Satisfaction Survey & Service Recovery System, Pre-Arrival Upsell & Check-In Preparation System, Rate Parity Alert & Competitor Pricing Monitor.

    Do I need to replace my current hotel software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in hotel automations are Twilio, Slack, Mailchimp, and Airtable, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a hotel automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
