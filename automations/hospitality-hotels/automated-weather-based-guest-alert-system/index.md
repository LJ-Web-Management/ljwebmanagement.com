How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The system checks the local forecast every morning and automatically triggers guest notifications whenever conditions cross a set threshold, heavy rain that would close the pool, high winds affecting a scheduled outdoor event, or a heat advisory that changes shuttle timing.
2. Affected guests receive a text explaining the change and, where relevant, an alternative activity or rescheduled itinerary before they've made plans around the original schedule.
3. The front office supervisor gets a copy of every alert sent, so staff at the front desk and pool are already briefed and aligned before a single guest asks a question.
4. Weather disruptions that used to generate a flood of confused guests at the front desk are now handled proactively, hours ahead of the first complaint.
