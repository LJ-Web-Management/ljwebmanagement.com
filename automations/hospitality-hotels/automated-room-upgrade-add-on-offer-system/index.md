How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on booking data, room type, length of stay, and days until arrival, guests are automatically segmented and sent targeted upsell offers: a suite upgrade for a milestone anniversary booking, a late checkout add-on for a guest with a late flight, a breakfast package for a family reservation.
2. Offers are presented through a simple, mobile-friendly page where the guest can accept and pay in two taps, and any accepted upgrade updates the room assignment and folio automatically without a phone call to the front desk.
3. Offers that go unopened after 48 hours are automatically resent once with a slightly different subject line before the window closes.
4. Upsell revenue that used to depend entirely on a front desk agent remembering to pitch at check-in now runs continuously in the background.
