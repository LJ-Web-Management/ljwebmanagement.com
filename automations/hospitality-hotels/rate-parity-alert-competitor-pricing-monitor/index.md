How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation checks the hotel's live rates across all OTAs and the direct booking channel every morning and alerts the revenue manager to any rate disparity, instances where an OTA is selling below the agreed rate or where the hotel's rate on one channel is significantly higher than another.
2. A competitor rate scan runs simultaneously, pulling the rates of the three to five designated comp set properties, and the revenue manager receives a daily summary showing exactly where the hotel is priced relative to the market.
3. Rate decisions are made with real competitive context rather than a gut feeling about where the market is moving.
