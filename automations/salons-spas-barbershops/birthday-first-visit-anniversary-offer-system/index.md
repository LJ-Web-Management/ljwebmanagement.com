How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Client birthdays and first-visit dates are pulled automatically from the booking platform each morning, and anyone with a birthday or first-visit anniversary in the coming week receives a personalized offer, such as a complimentary add-on or a percentage off their next service, valid for a 30-day window.
2. Redemptions are tracked automatically so the spa can see exactly how much return business the campaign drives, without anyone building a spreadsheet of dates by hand.
3. Clients feel remembered without staff having to keep a mental list of a few hundred birthdays.
