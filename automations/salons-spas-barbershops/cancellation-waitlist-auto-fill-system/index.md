How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a client cancels or reschedules, this automation immediately texts the next person on that time slot's waitlist with a 15-minute window to claim it before it moves to the next person in line.
2. Clients can join the waitlist for a specific stylist or service directly from their booking confirmation text, so the list builds itself without front desk staff keeping a notepad of who wanted in.
3. A slot that used to sit empty for the rest of the day is typically refilled within minutes of the cancellation coming in.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a front desk coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
