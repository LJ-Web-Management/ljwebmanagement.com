How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on the size of the product a client purchased and its average days-to-empty (a 2-ounce serum lasts about 60 days, a shampoo bottle about 45), this automation calculates the date the client is likely running low and sends a friendly text about a week beforehand with a one-click reorder or in-store pickup option.
2. Clients who reorder are automatically re-enrolled in the next cycle, and the system tracks which products drive the most repeat retail revenue.
3. Instead of retail sales depending on a stylist remembering to ask how the shampoo is working out, the reminder does the asking right when the client is about to need it.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a retail sales associate manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
