How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every morning, this automation scans each stylist's or provider's calendar for the next 48 hours and flags any gap longer than 45 minutes.
2. Those open slots are packaged into a same-day deal, typically 15-20% off, and pushed to a segmented list of clients who have booked that specific service before and live nearby, with a one-tap booking link.
3. The first client to respond claims the slot and everyone else's offer expires automatically, so there's no double-booking risk.
4. Chairs that used to sit empty on a slow Tuesday afternoon get filled without a manager scrambling to post on social media.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a booking coordinator is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation
