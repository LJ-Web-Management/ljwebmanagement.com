## Salons, Spas & Barbershops automation questions

**What salon and spa workflows can be automated?**

The 6 salon and spa automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch and marketing & customer retention, among other areas, for roles like Front Desk Coordinator, Salon Manager, and Spa Director. Popular starting points include [Appointment Confirmation & No-Show Reduction System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/salons-spas-barbershops/appointment-confirmation-no-show-reduction-system/), [Checkout Rebooking & Visit Cadence System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/salons-spas-barbershops/checkout-rebooking-visit-cadence-system/), [Birthday & First-Visit Anniversary Offer System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/salons-spas-barbershops/birthday-first-visit-anniversary-offer-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current salon and spa software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in salon and spa automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, Mailchimp, and Google Calendar, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a salon and spa automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
