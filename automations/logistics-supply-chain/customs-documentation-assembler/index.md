How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For every international shipment, this automation automatically pulls the commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin directly from the order management system and assembles them into the exact format required by customs for that destination country.
2. The completed packet is automatically sent to the freight forwarder as soon as the shipment is booked, instead of a coordinator manually gathering three separate documents from three separate systems under a shipping deadline.
3. Shipments move through customs faster because the paperwork is correct and complete before the truck even leaves the dock.
