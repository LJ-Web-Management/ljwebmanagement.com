How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every invoice that comes in from a freight carrier is automatically compared line by line against the rate that was originally quoted for that shipment.
2. Any invoice that comes in more than a set percentage over quote, commonly 5-10%, is automatically flagged and routed to the logistics manager with the specific discrepancy highlighted for review before it's approved for payment.
3. Overcharges that used to get buried in a stack of freight invoices and paid without a second look now get caught before the check goes out.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a logistics manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
