## Logistics & Supply Chain automation questions

**What logistics workflows can be automated?**

The 10 logistics automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors and compliance, documents & deadlines, among other areas, for roles like Logistics Manager, Warehouse Manager, and Procurement Manager. Popular starting points include [Shipment Tracking & Delay Alert System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/logistics-supply-chain/shipment-tracking-delay-alert-system/), [Receiving, Putaway & Pick-Pack-Ship Task Assignment](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/logistics-supply-chain/receiving-putaway-pick-pack-ship-task-assignment/), [Vendor Quote Collection & Purchase Order Generator](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/logistics-supply-chain/vendor-quote-collection-purchase-order-generator/).

**Do I need to replace my current logistics software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in logistics automations are Slack, Google Sheets, Airtable, and Twilio, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a logistics automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
