How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on each vehicle's mileage data synced from the telematics system, this automation calculates when the next oil change, inspection, DOT compliance check, or registration renewal is due and schedules it automatically with the designated service provider.
2. The driver and fleet manager both receive reminders 14 and 3 days in advance, and any vehicle that misses a scheduled service is flagged as out of compliance until the service record is uploaded, keeping the fleet legal and the maintenance log current without manual tracking.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a fleet manager is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out

- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
