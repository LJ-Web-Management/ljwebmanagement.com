How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a purchase order is created in the system, this automation automatically emails it to the vendor and then monitors the vendor's inbox for a confirmation reply.
2. If no confirmation arrives within 24 hours, it automatically sends a follow-up, and if 48 hours pass with still no response, it flags the buyer with a task to call the vendor directly.
3. Orders that used to sit silently unconfirmed for a week, discovered only when the expected ship date came and went, now get caught and chased down automatically within two days.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a procurement specialist has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
