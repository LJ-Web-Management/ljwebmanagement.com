How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As inventory levels update in the warehouse management system with each pick and receipt, this automation continuously watches every SKU against a reorder threshold calculated from that supplier's specific lead time.
2. The moment stock hits that point, it automatically generates a purchase order draft for the usual supplier at the usual order quantity and notifies the buyer to review and approve it.
3. The business stops finding out it's out of stock only after a customer order fails to fulfill, catching the gap while there's still time to reorder.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, an inventory planner has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
