How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Clients automatically receive a notification whenever their claim status changes, pulled directly from the carrier or claims system rather than a claims coordinator manually checking a portal and calling each policyholder with an update.
2. Status changes are written in plain language rather than raw claims jargon, so the client understands what actually happened without needing to call in and ask what a status code means, and the volume of "what's my status" phone calls into the office drops sharply once clients trust that they'll be told the moment anything changes.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a claims coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
