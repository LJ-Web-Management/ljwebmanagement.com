How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When multiple advisors serve the same household, a change made by one advisor, an updated address, a new account, a revised goal, automatically notifies the others, keeping every team member's records aligned instead of one advisor working from information the rest of the team never saw get updated.
2. The wealth management associate no longer has to manually cross-check household records across separate advisor files, and clients stop having the frustrating experience of updating their information with one advisor only to repeat the same conversation with another months later.
