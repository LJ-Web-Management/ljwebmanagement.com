How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Life events logged in the CRM, a marriage, a new child, a home purchase, automatically trigger a relevant outreach or service recommendation, such as an updated insurance review or a revised financial plan, rather than these moments passing quietly by until a client happens to mention it in an unrelated conversation months later.
2. The financial advisor gets a same-day notification with a suggested talking point tailored to the specific event, turning what used to be an easily missed opportunity to deepen the relationship into a consistent, timely touchpoint that clients genuinely appreciate hearing from their advisor about first.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a financial advisor spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
