How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a client refers new business to the firm, the automation automatically sends a thank-you note or small reward the same week, reinforcing the referral relationship without relying on someone remembering to do it during a busy stretch.
2. The referral partner manager can see exactly who's referring and how often, sharpening which relationships deserve extra attention, and consistently acknowledging referrals in a timely way keeps the behavior going, since clients and partners who feel genuinely appreciated for a referral are far more likely to send another one.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a referral partner manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork

- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
