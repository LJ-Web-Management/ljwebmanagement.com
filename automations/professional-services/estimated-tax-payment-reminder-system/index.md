How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Clients who make quarterly estimated tax payments automatically receive a reminder ahead of each deadline with their specific amount due and clear payment instructions, pulled straight from the current year's projections rather than a tax manager manually calculating and emailing every client's estimate by hand each quarter.
2. Clients whose income has changed meaningfully since the last projection are automatically flagged for a quick recalculation before the reminder goes out, avoiding the common problem of a client underpaying based on a stale estimate and facing an unexpected penalty months later at filing time.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a tax manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
