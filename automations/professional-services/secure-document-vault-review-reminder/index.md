How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Documents stored in a client's secure vault, wills, insurance policies, trust documents, are automatically flagged with review dates, prompting a reminder to revisit and update them periodically rather than sitting untouched for years after the initial upload while a client's actual life circumstances change around them.
2. The client relations coordinator gets a monthly list of vault items due for review, giving the firm a natural, low-pressure reason to reach out and check in, and clients consistently appreciate being reminded before an outdated document becomes a real problem instead of finding out after the fact.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a client relations coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
