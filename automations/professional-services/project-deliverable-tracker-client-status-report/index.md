How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every active engagement's deliverable schedule lives in a project management system that automatically alerts the project lead five days before each deliverable is due and escalates to the engagement manager if it's still open at two days out.
2. Once a deliverable is submitted, the client receives an automated update with a link to the document and a request for their review by a specified date.
3. A weekly project status report is generated automatically and emailed to the client contact on Friday afternoon, ensuring the client always feels informed even during weeks when the team is heads-down on the work.

Tools it connects

- Project management software (e.g., Asana, ClickUp, or Monday.com)
- Zapier or Make.com
- Google Drive or SharePoint (deliverable storage)
- [Slack (internal deadline alerts)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/slack/)
- OpenAI API (weekly status report narrative)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a management consultant, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
