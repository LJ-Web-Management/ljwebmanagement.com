How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once an engagement is signed, the client is automatically enrolled in a welcome sequence covering portal setup instructions, a document checklist, and a direct link to schedule the introductory call, so the first two weeks of a new relationship run on a consistent, professional rhythm instead of depending on whichever team member happens to remember what a new client needs first.
2. The client success manager can see exactly where each new client stands in the sequence, catching anyone who stalls at portal setup before it becomes a frustrating first impression that colors the entire engagement.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a client success manager is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
