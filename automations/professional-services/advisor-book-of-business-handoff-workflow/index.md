How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an advisor transitions roles or leaves the firm, the automation automatically triggers the client handoff communication and reassignment workflow to the new advisor, instead of the operations manager manually reassigning dozens or hundreds of client relationships one at a time while also drafting individual transition letters.
2. Every affected client automatically receives a warm, professional introduction to their new advisor at the same time the internal reassignment happens, and a transition that used to take weeks and risk client attrition along the way now happens smoothly within days.
