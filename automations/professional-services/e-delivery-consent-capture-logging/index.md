How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically captures and logs a client's consent to receive statements and required disclosures electronically the moment they opt in, keeping the firm compliant with delivery regulations without a compliance officer manually filing a paper consent form for every household.
2. Consent records are timestamped and stored alongside the client's file, ready to produce instantly if a regulator or auditor ever asks for proof, and clients who haven't yet given e-delivery consent are automatically identified so outreach can close the gap instead of the firm discovering it during an examination.
