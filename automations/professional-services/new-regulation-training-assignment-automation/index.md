How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new compliance regulation is identified, the automation automatically assigns the required training module to relevant staff with a tracked completion deadline, instead of the compliance officer manually identifying who needs the training and chasing down completion one person at a time.
2. Staff who haven't completed the assigned training as the deadline approaches are automatically flagged for a reminder, and the firm gains a clean, dated record proving every relevant employee was actually trained on a regulatory change, exactly the kind of documentation that matters most during an examination.
