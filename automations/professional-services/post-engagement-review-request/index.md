How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a filing, case, or policy is marked complete in the system, the client automatically receives a request to leave a review a few days later, timed to when the successful outcome is still top of mind rather than months afterward when the details have already faded.
2. Clients who indicate any dissatisfaction in the request are routed privately to the client relations coordinator instead of straight to a public platform, while satisfied clients get a direct one-click link to Google, letting the firm build its public reputation while quietly resolving friction before it ever becomes visible.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a client relations coordinator has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
