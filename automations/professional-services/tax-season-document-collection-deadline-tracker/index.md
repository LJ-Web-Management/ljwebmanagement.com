How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At the start of tax season, every client automatically receives a personalized document request list tailored to their entity type, with a secure upload portal link.
2. The system tracks what has been received versus what is outstanding and sends weekly nudge emails and texts to clients with missing items, escalating frequency as the filing deadline approaches.
3. The accountant sees a live dashboard showing which returns are ready to work, which are waiting on client documents, and which are at risk of missing the deadline, replacing the sticky-note system that usually passes for project management during tax season.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a cpa / accountant remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
