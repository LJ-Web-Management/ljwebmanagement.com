How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a client's investment portfolio drifts beyond its target allocation, whether from market movement or a large contribution or withdrawal, the automation automatically generates an internal alert flagging the account for advisor review and possible rebalancing, instead of drift being caught only whenever an investment analyst happens to pull that specific account for a manual check.
2. Every flagged account is logged with the exact percentage of drift and the date it crossed the threshold, giving the firm a clean audit trail showing portfolios are being actively monitored rather than reviewed only once a year on a fixed schedule regardless of what the market actually did.
