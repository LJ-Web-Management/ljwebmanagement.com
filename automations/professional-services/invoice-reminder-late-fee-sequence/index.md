How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks every unpaid invoice and automatically sends a friendly reminder a few days ahead of the due date, a firmer reminder the day it becomes overdue, and applies a configured late fee automatically once an invoice crosses a set threshold past due.
2. Firms recover the time they used to lose manually chasing down payment, and clients get consistent, predictable reminders instead of an occasional annoyed phone call.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a billing coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
