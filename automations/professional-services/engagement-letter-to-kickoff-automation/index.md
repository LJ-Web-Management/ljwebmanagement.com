## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a client signs their engagement letter, this automation automatically creates the client's folder structure, schedules the kickoff call, sends a welcome email outlining what to expect and by when, and adds every recurring deadline tied to the engagement, quarterly filings, review dates, to the shared team calendar.
2. New engagements stop starting with a manual setup checklist that varies depending on who's handling it that week.
