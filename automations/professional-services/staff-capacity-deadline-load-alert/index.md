How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation flags internally when a staff member's assigned deadlines exceed a safe workload threshold for a given week, prompting the operations manager to reassign work before a real crunch hits rather than discovering an overloaded team member only after a filing or deliverable is already late.
2. Workload is calculated automatically from every open engagement and its due date, giving a far more accurate picture than a manager's general sense of who seems busy, and reassignments that used to happen reactively in a panic now happen calmly, days ahead of the actual deadline crunch.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an operations manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
