How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically flags documents that have reached the end of their required retention period, routing them for archiving or destruction according to the firm's compliance policy instead of a records manager periodically combing through file storage trying to remember what's actually due for disposal.
2. Every flagged document goes through a review step before anything is destroyed, so nothing important is lost by mistake, and the firm gains a clean, dated log proving its retention policy is actually being followed rather than existing only as a written policy nobody consistently enforces.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a records manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
