How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A client who misses a scheduled consultation automatically receives a friendly follow-up message within the hour with an easy link to rebook, rather than falling silently out of the pipeline the way a missed appointment often does when the only next step depends on someone remembering to call.
2. The financial advisor gets a note on the client's file confirming the automatic outreach already went out, so no time is wasted duplicating the follow-up, and clients who missed a meeting for an ordinary reason, a scheduling conflict, a forgotten reminder, rebook at a high rate once the friction of re-engaging is removed.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a financial advisor has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
