How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Ahead of a scheduled client meeting, the automation automatically compiles a prep packet, an account summary, a suggested agenda, any open items from the last touchpoint, so the financial advisor walks in fully prepared instead of pulling account data and reconstructing open items from memory in the ten minutes before the meeting starts.
2. Every meeting starts from a consistent, complete picture regardless of how busy the week has been, and clients notice the difference when their advisor clearly already knows exactly where things stand instead of needing the client to catch them up first.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a financial advisor has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
