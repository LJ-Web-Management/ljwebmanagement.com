How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation tracks whether a client has actually downloaded or received their tax documents and automatically sends a reminder if they remain unopened as the filing deadline approaches, instead of the tax manager assuming a document was received simply because it was sent.
2. Clients who never noticed the original delivery email get a second chance to actually see it before the deadline creates real pressure, and the volume of last-minute "I never got my documents" calls the week before filing drops sharply once delivery confirmation, not just sending, is what the firm actually tracks.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a tax manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
