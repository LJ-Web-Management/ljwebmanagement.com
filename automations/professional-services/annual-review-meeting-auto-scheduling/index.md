How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a client's required annual review date approaches, they automatically receive a prompt with a direct link to schedule the meeting themselves, rather than staff having to reach out individually to every client on the book to coordinate a date.
2. Clients who don't respond within a set window automatically receive a second reminder, and any client still unscheduled two weeks out is flagged for a personal call, ensuring every required annual review actually happens on time instead of quietly slipping to whenever the client happens to remember or the advisor happens to notice the gap.
