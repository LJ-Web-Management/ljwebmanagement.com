How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. New coaching clients are automatically sent a welcome packet, a discovery questionnaire, a pre-work assignment, and a self-scheduling link for their first session, all within minutes of signing their agreement.
2. Before every session, both the coach and the client receive a prep reminder with a link to the shared session notes document and a brief agenda form for the client to fill out.
3. After each session, the client receives a summary of their commitments and a check-in question for 72 hours later, so accountability is built into the program structure rather than depending on the client to remember what they said they would do.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a business coach remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
