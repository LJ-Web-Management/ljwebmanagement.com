How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a major transaction such as a home purchase, a retirement, or an inheritance, the client automatically receives a check-in survey designed to surface additional service needs that a financial advisor might not otherwise hear about until months later in casual conversation.
2. A new inheritance often means a need for updated estate planning, a home purchase often means a need for updated insurance, and catching that need right after the life event happens, while it's genuinely top of mind for the client, turns a routine check-in into a natural, well-timed service conversation.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a financial advisor spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
