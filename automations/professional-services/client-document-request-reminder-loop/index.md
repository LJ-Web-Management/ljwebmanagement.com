How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At tax season or the start of any engagement requiring client documents, this automation automatically sends a checklist of exactly what's needed along with a secure upload portal link, and automatically reminds any client who hasn't uploaded everything on a set schedule, typically every 5-7 days.
2. The accountant sees a live dashboard of who's ready and who isn't instead of tracking document status in a spreadsheet that's out of date by the time anyone looks at it.

Tools it connects

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left to a person, this means a client relations coordinator re-enters the same data more than once, double-checks it by hand, and still occasionally misses something. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
