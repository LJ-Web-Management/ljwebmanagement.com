How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a firm updates its fee schedule, every affected client is automatically notified in advance with a clear explanation of what's changing and when it takes effect, satisfying the compliance requirement for advance disclosure without a billing coordinator manually identifying which clients fall under the updated schedule and drafting individual notices.
2. The notification is logged automatically for the compliance record, and clients consistently respond better to a clear, proactive heads-up about a fee change than they do to discovering it as a surprise line item on their next statement.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a billing coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
