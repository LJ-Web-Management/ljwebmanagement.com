How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation periodically reviews client accounts against service tier criteria, assets under management, engagement complexity, and flags candidates for a tier upgrade or a review conversation, instead of the practice manager relying on someone remembering to periodically audit the client list by hand.
2. Clients who've genuinely outgrown their current tier get identified automatically instead of quietly staying underserved or underpriced for years, and the firm captures revenue and service alignment opportunities that a purely manual review process would routinely miss.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a practice manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
