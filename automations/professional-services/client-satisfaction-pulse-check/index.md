How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After completing a major deliverable, a tax return, an audit, a financial model, this automation automatically sends the client a short satisfaction survey.
2. Any response below a set score triggers an immediate notification to the partner so a dissatisfied client gets a personal call before they've had time to consider taking their business elsewhere at the next engagement cycle.

Tools it connects

- Typeform or a survey tool
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Slack or email](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/slack/)
- A CRM

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a partner, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
