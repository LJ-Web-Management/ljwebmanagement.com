    FAQ

    Professional Services automation questions

    What professional services workflows can be automated?

    The 50 professional services automations in our catalog cover compliance, documents & deadlines, customer service & communication, and marketing & customer retention, among other areas, for roles like Financial Advisor, Compliance Officer, and Client Relations Coordinator. Popular starting points include Tax Season Document Collection & Deadline Tracker, Client Onboarding & Session Prep Automation, Project Deliverable Tracker & Client Status Report.

    Do I need to replace my current professional services software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in professional services automations are Mailchimp, Slack, Airtable, and Twilio, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a professional services automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
