How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation monitors relevant regulatory or tax law changes and automatically notifies affected clients with a brief, plain-language explanation of how the change actually impacts their specific situation, instead of a compliance officer trying to manually identify which clients out of hundreds are affected by a given rule change and reaching out one at a time.
2. Notifications are logged automatically for compliance recordkeeping, giving the firm a clear, dated record that clients were informed as required, and clients consistently cite this kind of proactive heads-up as a major reason they trust the firm is actually watching out for them.
