How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Engagement letters or forms sent for signature that remain incomplete automatically trigger reminders at set intervals, a gentle nudge after two days, a firmer one after five, until the client signs, instead of a practice manager manually checking a signature status page and remembering who still needs a follow-up call.
2. Documents that stay unsigned past a final threshold are automatically flagged for a personal outreach rather than another automated reminder, since at that point the holdup is usually a real question rather than simple forgetfulness, and reaching a live person resolves it far faster than a fourth email.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a practice manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
