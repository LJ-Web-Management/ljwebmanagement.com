How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A new client engagement automatically triggers scheduling of the required risk tolerance or needs assessment, ensuring it happens in the first week of the relationship instead of getting forgotten in the general rush of onboarding paperwork.
2. The client onboarding specialist gets confirmation once the assessment is complete and on file, and any new client who hasn't completed theirs within the expected window is automatically flagged, closing a compliance gap that used to depend entirely on someone remembering to circle back weeks into a busy new engagement.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a client onboarding specialist has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
