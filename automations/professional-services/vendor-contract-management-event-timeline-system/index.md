How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For every event, this automation builds a master timeline in the project management system based on the event date, working backward to populate due dates for each vendor contract, deposit, final headcount, and logistics confirmation.
2. Vendors receive automatic contract reminders and payment due notices, and the planner receives a daily briefing on what's overdue or due today across all active events.
3. The week before the event, every vendor gets a final confirmation email with load-in times, contacts, and venue instructions generated automatically from the event record.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on an event planner remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out

- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
