How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A logged client complaint automatically triggers a defined escalation workflow, notifying compliance immediately and setting a tracked resolution deadline, so nothing falls through the cracks the way an informally handled complaint sometimes does when it's addressed verbally and never actually documented anywhere.
2. Every complaint follows the same consistent process regardless of who first received it, and the compliance officer has a complete, timestamped record showing exactly how and when each complaint was resolved, precisely the kind of documentation regulators expect a firm to be able to produce on request.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a compliance officer remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
