How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Clients automatically receive a periodic reminder to review and update the beneficiary designations on their policies and accounts, catching an outdated election, an ex-spouse still listed, a deceased beneficiary never updated, before it causes a serious problem for the family it was meant to protect.
2. The paraplanner gets a report each quarter of which clients haven't confirmed their designations in over a year, letting the firm proactively close a gap that most clients never think to revisit on their own until an estate settlement forces the issue at the worst possible time.
