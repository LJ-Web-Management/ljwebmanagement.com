How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation internally tracks each advisor's required licensing and continuing education credits, alerting both the advisor and the HR and licensing coordinator well before any credential is at risk of lapsing, rather than a license expiration being discovered only when a routine audit or a client transaction gets blocked by an inactive registration.
2. Credit progress is tracked against each state and designation's specific requirement, and advisors get a running count of exactly how many hours are still needed before the deadline, turning what used to be a stressful last-minute scramble every renewal cycle into a routine, low-drama part of the calendar.
