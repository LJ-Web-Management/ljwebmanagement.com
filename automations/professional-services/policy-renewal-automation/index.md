How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Insurance clients automatically receive a renewal notice showing their current premium alongside a simple link to renew, sent well ahead of the policy's expiration date, replacing the old scramble of an insurance agent manually pulling an expiration report and calling down the list one policy at a time.
2. Clients who don't respond to the first notice automatically receive a second reminder as the date gets closer, and any policy still unrenewed inside the final week is flagged for a personal call, catching lapses in coverage before they happen instead of after a claim reveals the policy had already expired.
