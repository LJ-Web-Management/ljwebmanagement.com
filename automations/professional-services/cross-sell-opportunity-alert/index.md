How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When client data in the CRM suggests a relevant additional service, a new homeowner without a policy on file, a growing business without a retirement plan set up, staff are automatically alerted to reach out with a specific, timely offer instead of a cross-sell opportunity sitting unnoticed in a client's file indefinitely.
2. The wealth management associate gets a weekly digest of every newly triggered opportunity across the client base, turning what used to require someone periodically combing through client records by hand into a steady, low-effort stream of relevant conversations that clients experience as genuinely helpful rather than a sales pitch.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a wealth management associate has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
