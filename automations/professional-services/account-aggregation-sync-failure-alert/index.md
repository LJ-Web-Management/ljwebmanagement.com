## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically flags when a client's linked outside account data hasn't refreshed in a while, prompting a follow-up so the wealth management associate is always working from current numbers instead of unknowingly building a recommendation on stale balances that stopped syncing weeks earlier.
2. A silently broken account link used to only get caught when a client questioned a number that didn't match their own statement, and catching the sync failure proactively instead means every conversation is grounded in data that's actually current.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a wealth management associate has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation
