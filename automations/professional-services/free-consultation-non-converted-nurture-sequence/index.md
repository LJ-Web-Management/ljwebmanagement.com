How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Prospects who complete a free consultation but don't become a client automatically enter a long-term educational nurture sequence, useful articles, market updates, occasional check-ins, designed to keep the firm top of mind until they're actually ready rather than being written off as a dead lead the moment they don't sign on the spot.
2. The business development manager can see exactly which nurtured prospects are opening emails and clicking through months later, a strong signal they're warming back up, and a meaningful share of eventual new clients turn out to be prospects who said no the first time and simply needed more time.
