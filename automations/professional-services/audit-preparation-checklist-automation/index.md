How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As an audit or annual review date approaches, the client automatically receives a checklist of the specific materials and documents that will actually be needed, tailored to their engagement type rather than a generic one-size-fits-all list that leaves half the requested items irrelevant and the other half missing.
2. The audit manager can track completion against the checklist in real time, and clients who haven't started gathering materials with enough lead time are automatically flagged for a proactive check-in, preventing the all-too-common scramble in the final week before fieldwork is scheduled to begin.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on an audit manager, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
