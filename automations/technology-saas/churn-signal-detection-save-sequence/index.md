How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation continuously monitors usage data for signs a customer is disengaging: login frequency dropping below their normal baseline, key features going unused, or support tickets sitting unanswered.
2. The moment an account crosses the risk threshold, it automatically notifies the customer success manager with the specific warning signs that triggered the flag and fires off a \\"we noticed\\" outreach email to the customer.
3. Accounts that used to quietly let their subscription lapse get a save attempt while there's still time to turn it around.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a customer success manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
