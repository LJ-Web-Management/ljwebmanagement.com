How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a monitoring alert fires, this automation classifies the incident severity, pages the on-call engineer via the escalation policy, creates a war room channel in Slack, posts the current system status to the status page, and begins logging the timeline automatically so the postmortem document starts building itself from the first alert.
2. Once the incident is resolved, a postmortem template is auto-populated with the timeline, alerting chain, and initial impact data, turning the most stressful part of the job into a more structured, less chaotic response.

Tools it connects

- Monitoring platform (e.g., Datadog, PagerDuty, or OpsGenie)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Slack (war room channel creation)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/slack/)
- Statuspage.io (public status updates)
