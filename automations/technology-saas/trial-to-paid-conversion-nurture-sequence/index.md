How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new trial account is created, the user is enrolled in a behavior-driven nurture sequence: welcome email on day one, a setup checklist prompt on day two, a "have you tried this key feature?" email on day four, a use-case webinar invite on day seven, and a conversion offer on day twelve.
2. Users who complete the key activation event (the "aha moment") move to a fast-track conversion sequence, while users who haven't logged in within five days get a re-engagement push, all without the sales rep manually monitoring each trial account.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a saas sales representative remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
