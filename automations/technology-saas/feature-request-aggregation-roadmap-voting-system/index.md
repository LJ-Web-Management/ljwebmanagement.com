How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Feature requests submitted through in-app widgets, support tickets, sales calls, and NPS follow-ups are all routed into a single product feedback repository, where they are automatically deduplicated, tagged by theme, and linked to the customer accounts that submitted them.
2. When a request on the roadmap ships, every customer who submitted it automatically receives a "this feature is live" email, closing the loop that typically never gets closed, and turning product feedback into a customer retention tool.

Tools it connects

- Product feedback tool (e.g., Canny or ProductBoard)
- Zapier or Make.com
- CRM (for customer linking)
- Jira or Linear (roadmap)
- [Mailchimp or email (ship notifications)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/mailchimp/)
