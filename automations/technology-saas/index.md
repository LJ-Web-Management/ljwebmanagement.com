## Technology & SaaS automation questions

**What SaaS workflows can be automated?**

The 10 SaaS automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, reviews, feedback & reputation, and lead follow-up & sales, among other areas, for roles like Customer Success Manager, Product Manager, and SaaS Sales Representative. Popular starting points include [Churn Risk Detection & Intervention Playbook System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/technology-saas/churn-risk-detection-intervention-playbook-system/), [Feature Request Aggregation & Roadmap Voting System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/technology-saas/feature-request-aggregation-roadmap-voting-system/), [Trial-to-Paid Conversion Nurture Sequence](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/technology-saas/trial-to-paid-conversion-nurture-sequence/).

**Do I need to replace my current SaaS software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in SaaS automations are Slack, HubSpot, Mailchimp, and Stripe, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a SaaS automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
