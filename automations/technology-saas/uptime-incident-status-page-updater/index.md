How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a monitoring tool detects a service outage or degraded performance, this automation automatically updates the public status page with the incident details, posts an update to the company's status account on social media, and pages the on-call engineer.
2. Customers find out about an issue from the company's own channels within minutes instead of flooding support with \\"is this down\\" tickets while the team is still investigating.

Tools it connects

- Datadog or PagerDuty
- Statuspage or a similar status page tool
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Slack](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/slack/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a site reliability engineer spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
