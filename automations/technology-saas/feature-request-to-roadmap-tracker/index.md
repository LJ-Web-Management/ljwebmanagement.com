How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When customers submit feature requests through support tickets, in-app feedback widgets, or sales calls, this automation automatically extracts and tags the request by theme, adds it to a shared product roadmap board, and tallies how many distinct customers have asked for the same thing.
2. Product teams get real prioritization data, ranked by actual customer demand, instead of whoever complained loudest in the last team meeting driving the roadmap.

Tools it connects

- Productboard or Canny
- Zendesk or Intercom
- Zapier or Make.com
- OpenAI API (request tagging)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a product manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
