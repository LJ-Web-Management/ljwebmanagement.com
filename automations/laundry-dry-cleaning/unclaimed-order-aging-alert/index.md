How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks how long completed orders have sat unclaimed and automatically sends escalating reminders at set intervals, then flags orders approaching the store's abandonment policy cutoff so staff can follow the correct process before disposing of items.
2. Nothing gets disposed of, or forgotten indefinitely, without a documented trail of reminders sent.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a store manager has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
