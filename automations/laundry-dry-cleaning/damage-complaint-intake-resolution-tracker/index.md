How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer reports a damaged or missing item, this automation automatically opens a claim, requests any needed details or photos, and tracks the resolution through to a documented close.
2. A claim scribbled on a sticky note, easy to lose and impossible to audit later, gets replaced with a structured record.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a customer service manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
