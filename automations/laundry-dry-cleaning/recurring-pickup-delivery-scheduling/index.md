## What this automation does, step by step

1. For subscription or recurring pickup-and-delivery customers, this automation automatically schedules the next pickup based on their chosen frequency and sends a reminder the night before.
2. Customers stop having to remember to book each cycle manually, which keeps the recurring revenue base more predictable for the business too.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a route coordinator has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
