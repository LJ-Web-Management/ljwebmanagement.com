## Laundry & Dry Cleaning automation questions

**What laundry and dry cleaning workflows can be automated?**

The 5 laundry and dry cleaning automations in our catalog cover customer service & communication, scheduling, reminders & dispatch, and operations, inventory & vendors, among other areas, for roles like Store Manager, Route Coordinator, and Customer Service Manager. Popular starting points include [Order Ready Notification](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/laundry-dry-cleaning/order-ready-notification/), [Recurring Pickup/Delivery Scheduling](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/laundry-dry-cleaning/recurring-pickup-delivery-scheduling/), [Unclaimed Order Aging Alert](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/laundry-dry-cleaning/unclaimed-order-aging-alert/).

**Do I need to replace my current laundry and dry cleaning software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in laundry and dry cleaning automations are Twilio, Google Calendar, and Jotform, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a laundry and dry cleaning automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
