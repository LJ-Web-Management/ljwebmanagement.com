How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment an order is marked complete in the point-of-sale system, this automation automatically texts the customer that their order is ready for pickup.
2. Both premature pickup attempts and orders that sit forgotten on the rack for weeks drop sharply once the customer hears the moment it's actually ready.

Tools it connects

- A dry cleaning POS (Cleancloud or SPOT)
- [Twilio (SMS)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/twilio/)
- Zapier or Make.com

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a store manager is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
