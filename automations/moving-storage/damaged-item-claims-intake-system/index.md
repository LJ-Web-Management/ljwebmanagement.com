How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer reports a damaged or lost item after a move, this automation automatically opens a claim file, sends the customer a simple form requesting photos and a description of the item and the damage, and routes the completed submission to the claims adjuster with the original move's date, crew, and inventory list already attached.
2. Claims that used to start as a scattered email thread with photos attached three messages deep now arrive as one structured file, and the adjuster can start the assessment immediately instead of chasing down move details from dispatch first.
