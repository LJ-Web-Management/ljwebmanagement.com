How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a move is booked, this automation automatically assigns available crew members and a truck based on the job's estimated size and location, cross-checking crew certifications (piano moves, long-carry equipment) against what the job requires.
2. For days with multiple moves, it calculates the most efficient route between stops and sends the finalized schedule, including drive times and arrival windows, to the crew the night before.
3. What used to be an hour of manual scheduling and guesswork on a whiteboard now assembles itself as soon as a job is confirmed.
