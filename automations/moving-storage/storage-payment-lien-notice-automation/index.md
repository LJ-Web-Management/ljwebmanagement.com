How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For storage facilities, this automation automatically sends a payment reminder five days before each unit's due date, and if payment is missed, escalates through a state-compliant notice sequence, a late notice at 10 days past due, then a formal lien notice at the legally required interval for that state, each generated and mailed or emailed automatically with the correct legal language.
2. The facility manager gets a dashboard of every account currently in the notice pipeline instead of manually tracking dozens of accounts on dozens of different timelines in a spreadsheet, and the facility stays compliant with state self-storage lien laws without anyone having to memorize the deadlines.
