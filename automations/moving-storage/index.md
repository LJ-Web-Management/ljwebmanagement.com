## Moving & Storage automation questions

**What moving and storage workflows can be automated?**

The 5 moving and storage automations in our catalog cover customer service & communication, lead follow-up & sales, and scheduling, reminders & dispatch, among other areas, for roles like Sales Coordinator, Dispatcher, and Operations Manager. Popular starting points include [Instant Quote from Inventory List System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/moving-storage/instant-quote-from-inventory-list-system/), [Crew Assignment & Route Optimization System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/moving-storage/crew-assignment-route-optimization-system/), [Move-Day Status Update System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/moving-storage/move-day-status-update-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current moving and storage software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in moving and storage automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Google Sheets, and Stripe, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a moving and storage automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
