How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. On move day, this automation automatically sends the customer status texts at key points, crew en route, loading started, loading complete, en route to destination, and unloading started, pulled directly from the crew's check-ins on their mobile app.
2. Customers who used to call the office every hour asking where their truck is now get the answer before they think to ask.
3. The office phone stops ringing with status-check calls during the busiest part of the day, freeing dispatch to actually manage the day's moves.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on an operations manager, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
