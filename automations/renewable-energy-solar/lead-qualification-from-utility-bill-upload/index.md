## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a prospect uploads a copy of their utility bill during the quote request process, this automation automatically extracts their usage data to generate a preliminary system size and savings estimate.
2. The sales team gets a ready-made starting point instead of a blank lead that requires a full intake call before any real conversation can happen.
