## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation continuously compares each installed solar system's actual energy output against its modeled expected output and automatically alerts the service team when a system is underperforming beyond a normal range.
2. A failed panel or inverter often gets caught before the customer even notices their utility bill went up.
