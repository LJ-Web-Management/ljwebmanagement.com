## Renewable Energy & Solar automation questions

**What solar workflows can be automated?**

The 5 solar automations in our catalog cover customer service & communication, compliance, documents & deadlines, and billing, payments & finance, among other areas, for roles like Service Manager, Project Coordinator, and Finance Manager. Popular starting points include [System Performance Underperformance Alert](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/renewable-energy-solar/system-performance-underperformance-alert/), [Permit & Interconnection Application Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/renewable-energy-solar/permit-interconnection-application-tracker/), [Installation Milestone Payment Trigger](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/renewable-energy-solar/installation-milestone-payment-trigger/).

**Do I need to replace my current solar software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in solar automations are Slack, QuickBooks, and Mailchimp, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a solar automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
