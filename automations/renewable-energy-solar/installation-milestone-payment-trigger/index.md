How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a project passes each contractually defined milestone, site survey, permit approval, installation complete, activation, this automation automatically generates the corresponding invoice tied to the finance agreement.
2. Billing stays synced to actual progress instead of trailing behind because a milestone was reached but nobody triggered the invoice.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a finance manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
