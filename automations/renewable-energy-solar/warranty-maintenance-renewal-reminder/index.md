## What this automation does, step by step

1. As equipment warranties approach expiration, this automation automatically reaches out to the homeowner with maintenance plan or extended warranty options.
2. The offer lands before coverage lapses rather than after something breaks and the homeowner is left paying full price for a repair that could have been covered.
