How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a couple submits an inquiry through the website form, this automation checks the requested date against the studio's live calendar and immediately sends a warm response with available dates and a starter package overview, typically within minutes instead of the next business day.
2. If the couple confirms interest in a specific date, it pulls their guest count and selected services into a proposal template and generates a customized, branded quote automatically, ready for the planner to review and send.
3. What used to take a planner 45 minutes of manual quote-building between client calls now lands in the couple's inbox before they've even closed the tab.
