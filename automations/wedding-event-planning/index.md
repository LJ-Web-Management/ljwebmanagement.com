## Wedding & Event Planning automation questions

**What wedding and event planning workflows can be automated?**

The 5 wedding and event planning automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, lead follow-up & sales, and billing, payments & finance, among other areas, for roles like Lead Planner, Client Services Coordinator, and Studio Owner. Popular starting points include [Inquiry-to-Proposal Automation System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/wedding-event-planning/inquiry-to-proposal-automation-system/), [Vendor Confirmation Checklist Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/wedding-event-planning/vendor-confirmation-checklist-tracker/), [Payment Milestone Reminder Sequence](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/wedding-event-planning/payment-milestone-reminder-sequence/).

**Do I need to replace my current wedding and event planning software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in wedding and event planning automations are Mailchimp, Twilio, Google Calendar, and Google Sheets, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a wedding and event planning automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
