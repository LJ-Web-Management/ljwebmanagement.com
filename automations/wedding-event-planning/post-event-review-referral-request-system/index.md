How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. One week after the event, this automation automatically sends the couple a thank-you message along with a review request routed to Google and The Knot, plus a referral incentive, typically a discount for any friend they refer who books a consultation.
2. Couples who leave a review are automatically tagged in the CRM so the studio can track which reviews convert into referral leads over time.
3. Instead of relying on a happy couple to think to leave a review weeks after their wedding is a distant memory, the ask goes out while the excitement is still fresh.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a planner has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
