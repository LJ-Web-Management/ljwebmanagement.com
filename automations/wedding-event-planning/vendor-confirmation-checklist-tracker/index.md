How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For every booked event, this automation builds a checklist of every vendor involved, caterer, florist, DJ, rental company, and tracks each one's confirmation status against the event date.
2. Thirty days out, any vendor who hasn't confirmed final headcount, arrival time, and setup details automatically receives a follow-up email, and if a vendor is still unconfirmed 10 days before the event, the coordinator gets an internal alert to call them directly.
3. Instead of the planner manually re-reading a dozen email threads the week of the wedding, the checklist flags exactly who needs a nudge and when.
