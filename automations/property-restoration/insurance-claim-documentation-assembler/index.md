## What this automation does, step by step

1. As technicians log moisture readings, photos, and scope of work in the field, this automation automatically compiles it into the documentation package required by insurance adjusters.
2. The time between job completion and claim approval shrinks because the paperwork is already assembled instead of pieced together after the fact.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a claims coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
