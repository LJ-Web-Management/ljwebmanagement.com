How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new loss is reported, by phone, web form, or insurance referral, this automation automatically dispatches the on-call crew, opens a job file, and sends the customer a confirmation with the crew's estimated arrival time.
2. Response speed within the first hours affects both the extent of the damage and whether the job gets won over a competitor, and this automation removes the manual steps that eat into that window.
