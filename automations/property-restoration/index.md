## Property & Fire Restoration automation questions

**What restoration workflows can be automated?**

The 5 restoration automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, compliance, documents & deadlines, and reporting & analytics, among other areas, for roles like Dispatch Manager, Claims Coordinator, and Project Manager. Popular starting points include [First Notice of Loss Response Automation](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/property-restoration/first-notice-of-loss-response-automation/), [Insurance Claim Documentation Assembler](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/property-restoration/insurance-claim-documentation-assembler/), [Drying Equipment Monitoring Log](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/property-restoration/drying-equipment-monitoring-log/).

**Do I need to replace my current restoration software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in restoration automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, and QuickBooks, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a restoration automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
