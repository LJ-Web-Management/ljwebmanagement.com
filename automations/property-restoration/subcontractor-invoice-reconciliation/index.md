How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As subcontractor invoices come in for a restoration job, this automation automatically checks them against the original scope of work and flags any charges outside that scope for review before they're rolled into the final insurance billing.
2. Charges that don't belong in the claim get caught before they become a dispute with the insurer later.
