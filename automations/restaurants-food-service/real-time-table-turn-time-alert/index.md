How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The system continuously tracks how long each table stays occupied against the shift's historical average turn time, and the moment a table is running noticeably longer than normal during a busy service, the service manager gets an automatic alert on their phone rather than discovering the backup only when the waitlist has already grown out the door.
2. Patterns are logged over time by server, table, and daypart, giving management real data on where service is actually slowing down instead of relying on a gut feeling about "the corner section always running behind."

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a service manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
