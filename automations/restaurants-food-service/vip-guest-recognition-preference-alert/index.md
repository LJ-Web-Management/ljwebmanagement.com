How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a guest flagged as a VIP or frequent regular books a reservation, host stand staff are automatically notified with that guest's dining history, seating preference, and any known allergies or favorite dishes pulled straight from the CRM, so the table can be prepared and the server briefed before the guest ever walks through the door.
2. What used to depend entirely on a veteran host happening to recognize a face now happens consistently regardless of who's working the stand that night, turning every regular's visit into the kind of personal experience that used to only happen when the owner was on the floor.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a vip host remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips.

### With this automation
