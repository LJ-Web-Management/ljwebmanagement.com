How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a private event or banquet is booked, the client automatically receives a payment reminder as the deposit deadline approaches, with the exact amount due and a secure link to pay online rather than mailing a check or calling in a card number.
2. A second reminder goes out 48 hours before the cutoff, and the events coordinator is automatically flagged if the deposit still hasn't cleared by the deadline so the date can be released before another client is turned away for a slot that was never actually secured.
