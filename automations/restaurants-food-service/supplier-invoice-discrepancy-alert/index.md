How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every delivered supplier invoice is automatically checked against the original purchase order line by line, and any mismatch in quantity or price is instantly flagged to the accounts payable coordinator before the invoice is approved for payment, instead of a discrepancy only surfacing weeks later during a full audit of the books.
2. Suppliers with recurring discrepancies are automatically tracked over time, giving management hard numbers to bring to a vendor conversation instead of a vague sense that a supplier's pricing "feels off," and the restaurant stops quietly overpaying for cases that were never actually delivered at the quoted price.
