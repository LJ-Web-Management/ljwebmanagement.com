How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Catering inquiries submitted via the website are automatically qualified (guest count, date, event type, budget) and the system generates a preliminary quote and schedules a discovery call on the events manager's calendar.
2. Once the event is confirmed and details are finalized, the BEO document is automatically assembled from the collected information and sent to the client for review and signature, eliminating hours of back-and-forth and manual document formatting.
