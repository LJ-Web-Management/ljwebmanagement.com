How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Weekly inventory counts submitted by bar staff through a simple mobile form are automatically compared against par levels set for each product, and any item below threshold generates a purchase order to the appropriate distributor or supplier.
2. The bar manager gets a single consolidated order sheet rather than rebuilding it from scratch every week, and the system tracks cost-per-pour trends over time to flag when a product's margin is slipping before it shows up in the P&L.
