How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every online order automatically triggers a text the moment it's received, again when the kitchen marks it in preparation, and a final message the second it's ready for pickup or handed to a driver, pulled directly from the same status changes the kitchen is already making on the POS.
2. Customers stop calling the front counter to ask "is my order ready" because the answer is already sitting in their pocket, and pickup customers stop crowding the counter early since they know exactly when to walk in.
3. Front-of-house staff report a noticeable drop in status-check phone calls during the dinner rush within the first week of turning this on.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a digital ordering manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
