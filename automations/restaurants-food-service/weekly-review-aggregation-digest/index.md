How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A weekly summary pulling reviews from Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor into a single digest is automatically compiled and sent to management, replacing the need for the reputation specialist to log into three separate platforms individually just to see the week's full picture.
2. The digest highlights average rating trends and flags any standout complaint or compliment worth a closer look, and management walks into every Monday meeting already knowing exactly how the week's guest sentiment actually looked instead of guessing based on whichever review happened to be top of mind.

Tools it connects

- Google Business Profile and Yelp Fusion API (review links)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Google Sheets or Airtable (aggregated dashboard)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/google-sheets/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a reputation specialist manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
