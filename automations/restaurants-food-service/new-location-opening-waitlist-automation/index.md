How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Interest signups for an upcoming new restaurant location are automatically captured, and when the location actually opens, those signups automatically receive the launch announcement along with priority reservation access, instead of the multi-unit director building the pre-opening buzz list manually in a spreadsheet and hoping the announcement email doesn't get lost when it's finally time to send it.
2. Opening night reservations fill from a genuinely warm, already-interested list instead of relying entirely on cold local advertising, and the restaurant walks into its first week with tables already booked by people who were waiting for exactly this.
