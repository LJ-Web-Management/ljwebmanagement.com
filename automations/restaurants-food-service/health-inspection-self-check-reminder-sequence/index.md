How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Staff automatically receive recurring reminders to complete the restaurant's self-inspection checklist, temperature logs, sanitation checks, date labeling, on a set schedule, with an additional round of reminders in the days leading up to a scheduled or historically likely inspection window.
2. Completed checklists are automatically logged and time-stamped, giving the compliance manager a running digital record to show an inspector on demand instead of digging through a paper binder, and any item marked as failed is immediately routed to management for same-day correction rather than sitting unresolved until the next scheduled walkthrough.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a compliance manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
