How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A review request goes out by text or email a few hours after the actual meal, not the booking time, timed to when the food and service are still fresh in a guest's mind rather than buried under a day's worth of errands.
2. The message first asks a single one-tap satisfaction question; guests who respond positively are routed straight to a Google review link, while anyone who signals a problem is routed instead to a private feedback form that alerts the guest experience lead directly.
3. The result is a steady stream of public five-star reviews and a private channel for catching problems before they ever become a public complaint.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a guest experience lead remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
