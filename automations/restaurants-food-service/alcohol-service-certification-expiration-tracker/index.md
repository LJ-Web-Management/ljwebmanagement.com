How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically tracks each staff member's alcohol service certification expiration date and sends renewal reminders well ahead of the deadline, keeping the restaurant compliant without the compliance manager maintaining a separate certification spreadsheet and remembering to check it regularly.
2. Staff whose certification is about to lapse are automatically flagged and pulled from bar or serving shifts requiring it until renewed, and the restaurant avoids the real liability exposure of a server unknowingly working without a current, valid certification on file.
