    FAQ

    Restaurants & Food Service automation questions

    What restaurant workflows can be automated?

    The 53 restaurant automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, operations, inventory & vendors, and reviews, feedback & reputation, among other areas, for roles like Marketing Coordinator, Reservations Manager, and Host Stand Lead. Popular starting points include Online Review Response & Reputation Management System, Staff Scheduling & Open-Shift Fill System, Event Inquiry & Banquet Event Order (BEO) Generator.

    Do I need to replace my current restaurant software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in restaurant automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, Airtable, and Mailchimp, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a restaurant automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
