How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At the close of each shift, hours worked and role from the scheduling system are automatically matched against total tips collected in the POS, and the system calculates and allocates each employee's tip-out share according to the house's split rules, front-of-house, back-of-house, support staff, without a manager doing the math by hand at midnight.
2. A digital record of every calculation is stored automatically, giving the payroll administrator a clean audit trail if a dispute ever comes up, and closing out a shift now takes minutes instead of the error-prone spreadsheet math that used to keep managers at the restaurant long after the last table left.
