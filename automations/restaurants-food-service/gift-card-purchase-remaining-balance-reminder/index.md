How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. An online gift card purchase automatically triggers a delivery confirmation to the buyer along with a scheduled send date if it was purchased as a future gift, and months later, if the recipient still has an unused balance, they automatically receive a friendly reminder text that they still have money waiting to be spent.
2. Gift cards with a balance reminder attached get redeemed at a noticeably higher rate than cards left untouched, converting what would otherwise be a forgotten balance sitting on the books into an actual booked visit.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a gift card program administrator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
