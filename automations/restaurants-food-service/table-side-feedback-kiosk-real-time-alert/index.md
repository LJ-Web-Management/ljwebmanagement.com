How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A low rating entered on a table-side feedback kiosk automatically alerts the floor manager in real time, giving them a genuine chance to walk over and address the issue while the guest is still at the table instead of only finding out about a bad experience after they've already left and posted about it online.
2. Catching dissatisfaction in the moment, while there's still a chance to fix it with a complimentary dessert or a sincere apology, routinely turns what would have been a one-star public review into a guest who leaves feeling actually heard.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a floor manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
