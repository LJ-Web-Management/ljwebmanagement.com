How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Reviews left on DoorDash, UberEats, and Grubhub rarely surface anywhere the restaurant actually looks every day, so this system pulls new reviews from all three platforms into a single dashboard updated continuously throughout the day.
2. Any review below four stars triggers an immediate Slack alert to the operations manager with the guest's comment and order details, so a same-day response, a refund, an apology, or a corrected reorder, can go out before the guest's frustration hardens into a permanent bad impression.
3. Ratings across all three delivery platforms are tracked week over week in one place instead of three separate apps nobody has time to check, and negative trends tied to a specific item or daypart get caught within days instead of months.
