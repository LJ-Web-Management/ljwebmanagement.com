How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every time a new review is posted on Google, Yelp, or TripAdvisor, the system flags it, classifies the sentiment, and drafts a personalized response for the owner to approve and post, with negative reviews routed immediately to a priority inbox rather than sitting unnoticed for days.
2. Over time, the system tracks rating trends and identifies the most common complaint themes so the owner can address root causes rather than just managing one bad review at a time.

Tools it connects

- Zapier or Make.com
- Google Business Profile API
- Yelp Fusion API (monitoring)
- OpenAI API (draft responses)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a restaurant owner has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
