How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a guest with a credit-card-secured reservation fails to show, the automation automatically charges the agreed no-show fee and sends the guest a notification clearly explaining the charge, instead of the reservations manager manually pulling the card details and processing the fee by hand hours or days after the fact.
2. Guests know the policy upfront when they book, and consistent, automatic enforcement means the fee actually deters no-shows the way it's meant to, rather than being unevenly applied only when someone remembers to follow through on it.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a reservations manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.
