How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For dayparts that historically run quiet, an early lunch hour, a slow midweek happy hour, the system automatically sends a targeted promotion to nearby subscribers timed to land right before that window opens, driving traffic during the exact hours the restaurant most needs it rather than a generic discount blasted to everyone at the same time regardless of when they're likely to actually come in.
2. Redemption is tracked automatically by daypart, so the marketing coordinator can see over time which offers actually move covers during a specific window and retire the ones that don't, instead of running the same promotion indefinitely on a hunch.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a marketing coordinator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
