How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Corporate clients with a standing lunch order automatically have that order scheduled and confirmed on a recurring basis, without the client needing to place a new order every single time or the catering sales coordinator manually re-entering the same recurring order week after week.
2. Any change the client makes updates the standing order going forward automatically, and this kind of frictionless reliability is exactly what keeps a steady corporate account loyal instead of drifting to whichever competitor makes recurring ordering easier.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a catering sales coordinator has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
