How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As menu items sell throughout the day, the POS deducts ingredient usage against a recipe-mapped inventory count, and once any ingredient's on-hand quantity drops below its set par level, the system automatically emails a purchase order to the correct supplier with the standard order quantity pre-filled.
2. The kitchen manager gets a same-day notification of exactly what was ordered and from whom, and a weekly variance report flags ingredients being used faster than recipes predict, a signal of over-portioning or waste.
3. What used to be a Sunday-night ritual of counting walk-in shelves and phoning three different suppliers is now confirmed automatically before the manager finishes prep for the day.
