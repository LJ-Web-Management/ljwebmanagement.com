How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The system checks reservation volume for each upcoming night against a rolling average for that day of the week, and when bookings for, say, a Tuesday fall more than 30% below normal with less than 48 hours to go, it automatically triggers a promo, a discount code or a limited-time offer, pushed out as an email and text to the restaurant's guest list.
2. The offer is capped at a set number of redemptions so it doesn't cannibalize a night that ends up busy anyway, and results (redemptions, covers seated, revenue) are logged automatically so the coordinator can see which offers actually fill seats.
3. Slow Tuesdays that used to just be accepted as slow now regularly close within 10-15% of a normal night's covers.
