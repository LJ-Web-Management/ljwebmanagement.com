How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Members of the wine or beverage club automatically receive a shipment notification and renewal reminder ahead of each billing cycle, along with the specific bottles or selections included in that period's shipment, instead of a charge simply appearing on a statement with no context.
2. Members whose card fails at renewal are automatically prompted to update their payment method before the club is paused, and the beverage director gets a monthly report of upcoming renewals, giving enough lead time to reach out personally to any member showing signs of churn before they cancel instead of after.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a beverage director has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
