How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every staff member scheduled for an upcoming shift automatically receives a reminder text the afternoon before, and again a couple of hours ahead of clock-in, pulled directly from the live schedule so a swapped or dropped shift updates the reminder automatically instead of texting someone for a shift they no longer have.
2. No-call, no-shows that leave a shift short-staffed drop noticeably once reminders become routine, and the scheduling manager can see in the dashboard exactly who confirmed and who didn't respond, giving enough lead time to call a backup before service starts instead of finding out at the door.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a scheduling manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Interview scheduling without email ping-pong
- New hire paperwork and account setup before day one
- Certification and license expiration alerts
- Timesheet and time-off workflows that route themselves
