How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Marking a new hire as accepted in the scheduling system automatically triggers a sequence covering onboarding paperwork, a copy of the training schedule for their first two weeks, and a request to the kitchen or front-of-house lead for uniforms and system access to be ready before day one.
2. The HR onboarding coordinator no longer has to remember which of a dozen steps still needs doing for each new hire, since every task fires automatically off the same trigger, and new employees consistently show up to their first shift with a badge, a login, and a uniform waiting instead of standing around while someone scrambles to sort it out.

### With this automation
