How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation monitors ratings specifically on third-party delivery platforms and flags a drop in delivery-only scores separately from dine-in reviews, since the causes are usually completely different, food traveling poorly, a packaging problem, an inaccurate delivery estimate, rather than anything happening in the dining room.
2. The delivery operations manager can respond to the actual root cause instead of a blended rating masking a delivery-specific problem behind an otherwise strong overall reputation, and catching a delivery rating slide early prevents it from dragging down the restaurant's visibility in that platform's own search ranking.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a delivery operations manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
