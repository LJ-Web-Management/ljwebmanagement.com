How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The day a new seasonal menu goes live, the loyalty list and email subscribers automatically receive an announcement featuring the new dishes with photos, paired with a booking incentive to come try it in the next two weeks while the launch is fresh.
2. The same content is automatically formatted and queued for the restaurant's social channels so the announcement lands everywhere at once instead of the kitchen finishing a new menu that marketing gets around to promoting three weeks later, once the novelty and the reason to book a special visit has already faded.
