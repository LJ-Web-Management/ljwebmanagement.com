How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Guests who join the waitlist from a kiosk, the host stand tablet, or the restaurant's website are automatically texted the moment their table is ready, with no app download required.
2. The host stand's live queue shows an estimated wait time that updates as tables actually turn, and guests who don't reply within a set window are automatically texted a check-in before their table is offered to the next party.
3. Free from the crowd hovering by the door, guests can wait in their car or at the bar next door, and the host stand stops fielding the same "how much longer" question a dozen times a night.
