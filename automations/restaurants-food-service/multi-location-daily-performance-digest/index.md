How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every morning, sales, labor percentage, average check, and covers from every location automatically roll up into a single digest emailed to ownership before the first location even opens for lunch, replacing the old routine of a multi-unit director manually logging into a separate POS dashboard for each address.
2. Locations trending outside their normal range on any metric are automatically highlighted at the top of the report, so a slipping location gets a phone call that same morning instead of the trend only becoming visible weeks later during a monthly review.
