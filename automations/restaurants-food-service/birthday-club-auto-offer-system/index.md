How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Birthdays collected at checkout, on receipts, through a loyalty app, or via a simple sign-up form, feed automatically into a guest database, and on the first of each month every guest with a birthday that month receives an email with a free dessert or appetizer offer valid any time during their birthday month.
2. Guests who don't redeem within the first two weeks get a friendly reminder text with the offer attached again.
3. A free item redeemed almost always comes attached to a full paying party, so the birthday club consistently drives visits that wouldn't have happened that month otherwise, turning a single data field collected at checkout into a recurring, low-cost source of return traffic.
