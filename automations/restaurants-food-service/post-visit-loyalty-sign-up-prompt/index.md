How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Guests who pay their check and aren't already loyalty program members automatically receive a text or email invitation to join right after their visit, while the meal is still fresh in their mind and the incentive to sign up, a discount on their next visit, feels immediately relevant rather than arriving as an unrelated marketing message weeks later.
2. Enrollment through this moment converts at a far higher rate than enrollment prompted through a generic in-store sign or a QR code on a table tent, since it's timed to the exact moment a guest has just decided they enjoyed the experience enough to want a reason to come back.
