How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A catering inquiry submitted through the website form, guest count, event date, menu preferences, is instantly parsed and matched against a standing per-head pricing sheet, generating a starter quote with two or three available date options sent back to the prospect within minutes instead of the next business day.
2. The coordinator is notified immediately with the full inquiry details and a draft quote already attached, ready to refine rather than build from scratch.
3. Catering leads often request quotes from three competitors at once, so being the first to respond with real numbers, sometimes within five minutes of submission, has meaningfully increased the percentage of inquiries that turn into booked events.
