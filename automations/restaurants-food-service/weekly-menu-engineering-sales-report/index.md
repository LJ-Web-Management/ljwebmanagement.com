How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically compiles weekly sales-by-item data straight from the POS, highlighting the best and worst performing menu items so the menu development manager can make informed decisions about what to promote, reprice, or cut entirely.
2. What used to require someone manually exporting and sorting a sales report every week now lands automatically as a ranked list, and menu changes get made based on actual sales data instead of a hunch about which dish "seems" to be selling well.
