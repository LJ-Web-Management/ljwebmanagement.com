How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When the POS detects a closed check, the system waits two hours, then sends the guest a text or email asking one simple question: how was your visit?
2. Guests who respond positively are automatically routed to a direct link to leave a Google or Yelp review, while guests who indicate anything less than great are routed instead to a private feedback form that lands directly in the general manager's inbox rather than a public review page.
3. The manager can respond to a dissatisfied guest the same day, often before they've even thought about posting anywhere, and the restaurant's public rating climbs because only the genuinely happy visits get funneled toward public platforms.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a general manager, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
- Instant manager alerts when a customer reports a problem
- Alerts when a new review needs a response
- Survey and NPS results that reach the people who can act
