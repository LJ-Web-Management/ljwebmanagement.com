How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Any private dining or event inquiry that goes quiet without converting into a booked date automatically triggers a follow-up email about a week later, reopening the conversation with a fresh angle, a seasonal menu suggestion or an available date that just opened up, instead of the lead simply going cold in an inbox.
2. The private dining manager can see at a glance which stalled inquiries are due for a nudge and which have already been re-engaged, recovering a meaningful share of bookings that would otherwise have quietly gone to a competitor who happened to follow up first.
