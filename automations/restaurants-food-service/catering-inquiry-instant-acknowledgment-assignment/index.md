How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A catering inquiry submitted through the website is automatically acknowledged within seconds with a friendly confirmation that the request was received, while the full details, headcount, date, and budget, are simultaneously routed to the events coordinator's queue with an internal countdown timer flagging it if it isn't answered inside the target response window.
2. Leads that used to sit in a shared inbox until someone happened to check it now get a same-day reply as standard practice, which matters most for catering business, where the first company to respond usually wins the booking.
