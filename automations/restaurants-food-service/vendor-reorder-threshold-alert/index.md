How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a key ingredient tracked in the POS inventory module drops below its set reorder threshold, a purchase order is automatically generated with the standard quantity and sent directly to the supplier, instead of the purchasing manager relying on a walk-in count that only happens once or twice a week.
2. Order confirmations and delivery dates come back into the same tracker, so a missed or short delivery gets flagged before a Friday-night rush discovers a walk-in is empty, and month-end reconciliation against actual invoices takes minutes instead of a full afternoon of matching paper receipts.

Tools it connects

- POS system (e.g., Toast or Square) with inventory module
- Zapier or Make.com
- Email or supplier ordering portal
- [Google Sheets or Airtable (par level database)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/google-sheets/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a purchasing manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out

- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
