How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically compiles the day's specials, any 86'd items, and relevant service notes into a pre-shift briefing sheet, ready and printed or displayed before service starts, instead of the kitchen manager scrambling to verbally relay the same information to a rotating staff at the last minute.
2. Every server starts the shift with the identical, current information instead of whatever they happened to catch during a rushed pre-shift huddle, and guest-facing mistakes caused by a server not knowing about an 86'd item drop noticeably once the briefing sheet is consistent and automatic.
