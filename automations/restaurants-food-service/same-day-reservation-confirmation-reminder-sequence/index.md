How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a reservation is booked online or by phone, the guest automatically receives a confirmation text and email, followed by a reminder the morning of their visit with a one-tap link to modify or cancel.
2. Guests who don't confirm by mid-afternoon get a second, shorter nudge, and any cancellation instantly reopens the slot on the host stand's live floor plan.
3. Restaurants running this sequence typically see same-night no-shows drop by half, since the reminder lands during the exact window when someone still has time to call and change plans instead of simply not showing up.
