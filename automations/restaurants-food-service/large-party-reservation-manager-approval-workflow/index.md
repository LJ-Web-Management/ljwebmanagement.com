How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Any reservation request above a defined party size is automatically pulled out of the normal booking flow and flagged for the floor manager's review before it's confirmed, triggering a same-day call to the guest to lock in seating preferences, menu needs, and any special requests.
2. Once approved, the party is automatically blocked off on the floor plan and the kitchen gets an advance heads-up on the headcount, so a fifteen-top doesn't quietly slip through the online booking widget and surprise the kitchen and a full dining room at the same time.
