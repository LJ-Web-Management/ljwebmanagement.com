## Human Resources automation questions

**What HR workflows can be automated?**

The 10 HR automations in our catalog cover hr, hiring & team management, among other areas, for roles like HR Manager, Recruiter, and Onboarding Specialist. Popular starting points include [Job Posting & Multi-Platform Applicant Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/human-resources/job-posting-multi-platform-applicant-tracker/), [Interview Scheduling & Candidate Communication System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/human-resources/interview-scheduling-candidate-communication-system/), [New Hire Document Collection & Orientation Workflow](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/human-resources/new-hire-document-collection-orientation-workflow/).

**Do I need to replace my current HR software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in HR automations are Slack, Outlook, DocuSign, and Jotform, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a HR automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
