How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A weekly reminder goes to every hourly employee on Monday morning to submit their timesheet, and a follow-up goes to anyone who hasn't submitted by the Wednesday deadline.
2. The payroll manager receives a consolidated exceptions report (missing timesheets, overtime flags, unusual punch patterns) rather than having to audit every submission manually.
3. Payroll processes on time every cycle because the data collection process is automated rather than dependent on employees and managers remembering on their own.

### Without automation

Manually, a payroll manager is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation
