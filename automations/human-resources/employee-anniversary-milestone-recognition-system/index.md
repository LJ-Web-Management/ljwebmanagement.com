How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every morning, this automation checks employee start dates against today's date, and the moment someone hits a work anniversary, it automatically notifies their manager and posts a personalized shoutout in the team's Slack channel, tagging the milestone (one year, five years, ten years) so the recognition feels specific rather than generic.
2. Anniversaries tied to a reward, an extra PTO day, a gift card, or a bonus, trigger that reward request automatically instead of relying on a manager to remember and submit it.
3. Milestones that used to get missed entirely or celebrated late with an apologetic "sorry this is a few weeks overdue" now get recognized on the exact day, every time, without HR maintaining a manual spreadsheet of dates.
