How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Ninety days before the annual review cycle, this automation sends managers a schedule of their direct reports' review dates with self-evaluation forms pre-attached.
2. Reminders escalate for overdue self-evaluations and manager reviews, and completed reviews are automatically compiled into a calibration-ready format for the HR business partner to use in talent planning sessions.
3. No one misses their window, and the HRBP doesn't spend two weeks chasing down forms from every department head.

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a hr business partner spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.

### With this automation
