How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new position is approved, the job description is automatically posted to LinkedIn, Indeed, and the company careers page simultaneously, and all incoming applications are routed into a single ATS pipeline regardless of which platform they came from.
2. Applicants receive an immediate confirmation email with a timeline, and any applicant who hasn't received an update in 14 days is flagged for the HR manager, so no candidate is left waiting in silence while the hiring team deliberates.

Tools it connects

- ATS (e.g., Greenhouse or Lever)
- Zapier or Make.com
- LinkedIn and Indeed APIs or job board integrations
- [Gmail or Outlook (automated applicant communications)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/outlook/)
