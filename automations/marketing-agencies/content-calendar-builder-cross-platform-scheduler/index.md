How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A monthly content calendar built in Airtable or Notion feeds directly into a scheduling tool, automatically posting to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X at the optimal engagement times for each platform, with platform-specific caption formatting applied automatically.
2. When a piece of content performs unusually well, the system flags it for the social media manager to consider boosting as a paid post, turning organic performance data into a paid media signal without any manual monitoring.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a social media manager has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
