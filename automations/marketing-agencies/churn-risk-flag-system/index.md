How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every month, this automation compares each client's current email open rates, meeting attendance, and average response time against their trailing three-month baseline, looking for the kind of sudden pullback that usually precedes a cancellation notice.
2. Any account showing a meaningful drop across two or more of these signals gets automatically flagged as "at risk" in the CRM, and the retention specialist receives a notification listing the specific data points that triggered it, not just a vague warning.
3. Instead of finding out a client is unhappy when they submit a 30-day cancellation notice, the team now gets a two-to-three week head start to schedule a save call and address whatever's actually going wrong.
