How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every few hours, this automation checks each client's live ad spend in Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager against their monthly budget, and calculates the pace at which they're on track to finish the month.
2. The moment a campaign is projected to exceed its budget by more than 10%, the media buyer gets an immediate Slack message with the exact campaign, the overage amount, and a one-click pause link, and if no one responds within a set window, the campaign pauses itself automatically.
3. Agencies that used to discover a blown budget at the end of the month, when it was too late to do anything but apologize, now catch the overage while there's still time to course-correct and protect the agency's margin on that account.
