How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Throughout each day, this automation monitors ad spend pacing against monthly budgets, CPC against targets, and conversion rates against benchmarks, and sends an immediate Slack alert if any campaign crosses a threshold in either direction.
2. Overspending on a poor performer is caught before it burns the month's budget, and a campaign that suddenly starts converting unusually well triggers a budget increase prompt so the ads manager can capitalize on momentum rather than capping it by accident.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a paid ads manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
