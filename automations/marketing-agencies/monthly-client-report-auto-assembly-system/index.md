How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. On the first business day of each month, this automation pulls performance data directly from Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, the client's Google Analytics account, and their email marketing platform, then drops every metric into a pre-built, branded report template without anyone opening a single dashboard.
2. The finished PDF is emailed to the client by 9 a.m., and a Slack notification confirms delivery to the account manager so nothing slips through on a busy Monday.
3. What used to take a marketing operations manager four to six hours per client across a portfolio of twenty accounts now takes zero manual hours, and every client gets their report on the same predictable date every month instead of whenever someone finds the time.
