How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every client account is automatically scored monthly based on usage, results, and engagement, and accounts that meet the "ready for upsell" criteria trigger a task for the account manager to reach out with a specific service expansion proposal.
2. Clients who score low on the health index receive a proactive check-in call prompt before they reach the point of canceling.
3. The account manager's week begins with a prioritized list of exactly which clients need attention and why, not a gut feeling about who to call.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, an account manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
