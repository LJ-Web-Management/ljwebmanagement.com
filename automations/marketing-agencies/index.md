## Marketing Agencies automation questions

**What marketing agency workflows can be automated?**

The 10 marketing agency automations in our catalog cover reporting & analytics, scheduling, reminders & dispatch, and lead follow-up & sales, among other areas, for roles like Agency Owner, Social Media Manager, and SEO Specialist. Popular starting points include [Client Reporting & Performance Dashboard System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/marketing-agencies/client-reporting-performance-dashboard-system/), [Content Calendar Builder & Cross-Platform Scheduler](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/marketing-agencies/content-calendar-builder-cross-platform-scheduler/), [Rank Tracking & Monthly SEO Report Generator](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/marketing-agencies/rank-tracking-monthly-seo-report-generator/).

**Do I need to replace my current marketing agency software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in marketing agency automations are Slack, Google Sheets, HubSpot, and Airtable, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a marketing agency automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
