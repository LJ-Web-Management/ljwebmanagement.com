How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every piece of content submitted for client approval, whether it's a blog post, an ad creative, or a social caption, gets a timestamp the moment it's sent, and this automation tracks how long it's been sitting unanswered.
2. After 48 hours of silence, the client automatically receives a friendly, non-pushy nudge reminding them the piece is waiting on their sign-off, and after 5 days with still no response, the creative director gets flagged directly so they can decide whether to call the client.
3. Content that used to sit in client inboxes for two or three weeks, quietly stalling the production calendar, now gets approved or escalated within days, keeping the whole content pipeline moving instead of backing up behind one unanswered email.
