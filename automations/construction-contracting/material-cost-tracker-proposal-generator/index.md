How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an estimator finalizes a takeoff, the system checks current supplier pricing via API or a regularly updated price sheet, calculates material costs automatically, applies the configured labor and overhead markup, and generates a formatted proposal document ready to send.
2. Any material that has spiked significantly in price since the last update is flagged in red so the estimator knows before a proposal goes out the door at the wrong number.

Tools it connects

- Estimating software (e.g., Buildxact or ProEst)
- Zapier or Make.com

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a construction estimator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
