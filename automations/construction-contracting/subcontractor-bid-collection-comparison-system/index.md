How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new project goes out for bids, this automation sends a formatted bid request to the right subcontractors from a categorized vendor database, tracks who has and hasn't responded, follows up with a reminder at the deadline, and compiles all received bids into a side-by-side comparison sheet for the GC to review.
2. What used to be a week of email chasing becomes an organized, documented bid process that runs mostly on its own.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a general contractor spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
