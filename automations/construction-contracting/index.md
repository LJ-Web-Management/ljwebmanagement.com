## Construction & Contracting automation questions

**What construction workflows can be automated?**

The 10 construction automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, compliance, documents & deadlines, and lead follow-up & sales, among other areas, for roles like General Contractor, Project Manager, and Construction Estimator. Popular starting points include [Subcontractor Bid Collection & Comparison System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/construction-contracting/subcontractor-bid-collection-comparison-system/), [Daily Site Progress Report & Punch List System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/construction-contracting/daily-site-progress-report-punch-list-system/), [Material Cost Tracker & Proposal Generator](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/construction-contracting/material-cost-tracker-proposal-generator/).

**Do I need to replace my current construction software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in construction automations are Airtable, Google Sheets, Jotform, and Slack, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a construction automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
