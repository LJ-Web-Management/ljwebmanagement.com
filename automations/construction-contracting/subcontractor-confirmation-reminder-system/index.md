How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a subcontractor's start date is confirmed for a job, this automation sends them an immediate text confirming the date, the site address, and the scope expected on-site, and logs the confirmation in the project schedule.
2. A reminder text goes out automatically 48 hours before the confirmed date, and a second same-day reminder goes out the morning of, cutting down on the no-shows and last-minute reschedules that throw off an entire week's sequencing.
3. The field superintendent receives a daily digest showing exactly which subs are confirmed for the next three days and which haven't yet responded, so schedule gaps get caught and fixed days before they become a delay instead of hours before an empty job site.

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a field superintendent, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.
