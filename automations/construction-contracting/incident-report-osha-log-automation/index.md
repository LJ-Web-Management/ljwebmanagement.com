How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a field supervisor submits an incident report from the job site, this automation timestamps and logs it into the OSHA 300 log in the correct format, notifies the safety manager immediately, schedules a 24-hour follow-up for a root cause investigation form, and (if the severity meets the threshold) drafts the OSHA 301 form pre-filled with the available information.
2. Recordkeeping that used to lag by days is now complete within minutes of an incident occurring.
