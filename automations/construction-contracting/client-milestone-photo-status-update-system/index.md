How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At each major project milestone (foundation poured, framing complete, drywall up, final walkthrough scheduled) the field team snaps a few photos and marks the milestone complete in the project management system, and the client automatically receives a text with those photos and a short status update within minutes.
2. The message includes the percentage of the project now complete and the next milestone to expect, so clients aren't left guessing about progress between site visits or biweekly calls.
3. The client relations coordinator can see exactly which milestone messages have gone out for every active job from a single dashboard, and callback volume from anxious clients checking on progress drops noticeably once photo updates start arriving on their own.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a client relations coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
