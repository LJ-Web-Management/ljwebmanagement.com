How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every open permit across every active job is tracked against its expiration date and required inspection windows in one central log, and the compliance coordinator receives an automatic alert 14 days, 7 days, and 2 days before any permit is set to lapse or any inspection deadline is about to pass.
2. If a required inspection hasn't been scheduled within the alert window, the system automatically emails the responsible superintendent and copies the office so the gap doesn't sit unnoticed inside a single person's inbox.
3. Permits that do lapse trigger an immediate escalation to leadership rather than being discovered weeks later when a closing inspector rejects the paperwork, and chasing down expired permits after the fact is replaced by a compliance calendar the whole office can see at a glance.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a compliance coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
