How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every completed project is logged with its warranty start date, and as each project approaches the end of its one-year (or manufacturer-specified) warranty window, the client automatically receives an outreach email offering a complimentary inspection before coverage runs out.
2. Clients who book the inspection are automatically scheduled, and those who don't respond receive a second reminder 30 days before expiration along with information on paid renewal or maintenance service plans available after the warranty ends.
3. The customer care manager sees a rolling 90-day view of every warranty expiring across the client base, turning what used to be a forgotten obligation into a reliable source of repeat inspection revenue and referral goodwill.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a customer care manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
