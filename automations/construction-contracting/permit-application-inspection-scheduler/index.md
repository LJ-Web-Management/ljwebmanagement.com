How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a project hits the permitting stage, this automation generates the correct permit application forms pre-filled with project details, sends reminders to the responsible party to submit them by the target date, and (once permits are in hand) schedules required inspections based on the project timeline.
2. The office manager gets an alert any time an inspection is coming up in the next 72 hours, so nothing gets missed in the middle of a busy job calendar.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a construction office manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
