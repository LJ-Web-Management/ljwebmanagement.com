How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a donation is processed, whether through the website, a mail-in check entered manually, or a matching gift, the donor automatically receives a tax-compliant receipt by email within minutes, formatted with the organization's EIN, the exact gift amount, and the required non-goods-or-services language.
2. Receipts for gifts over a set threshold are automatically copied to the development associate so major gifts get a personal follow-up on top of the automated receipt.
3. At year-end, every donor automatically receives a consolidated giving statement summarizing all contributions made during the year, eliminating the days finance staff used to spend compiling statements by hand every January.
4. Donors get instant confirmation their gift went through, and the organization stays audit-ready without anyone tracking receipts in a spreadsheet.

Handled by hand, a development associate spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.
