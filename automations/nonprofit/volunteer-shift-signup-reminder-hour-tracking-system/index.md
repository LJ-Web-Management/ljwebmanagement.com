How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Open volunteer shifts are automatically posted to the volunteer portal and distributed via email and text to the relevant volunteer pool, with a one-click signup link.
2. Confirmed volunteers receive a reminder 48 hours before their shift with the location, parking instructions, and a contact for questions.
3. After the shift, volunteers receive a thank-you message and their hours are automatically logged, giving the coordinator a real-time count of total volunteer hours without maintaining a separate spreadsheet.
