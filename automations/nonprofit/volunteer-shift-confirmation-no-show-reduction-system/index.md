How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a volunteer signs up for a shift, they immediately receive an automatic confirmation text with the date, time, and location, followed by a reminder 48 hours out and a second reminder the morning of the shift with a one-tap link to cancel or reschedule if something comes up.
2. Volunteers who cancel within 24 hours of a shift automatically trigger a notification to the community engagement coordinator so a replacement can be found before the shift is short-staffed.
3. Anyone who no-shows twice in a rolling 90-day window is automatically flagged for a check-in conversation rather than quietly disappearing from the schedule.
4. No-show rates have dropped noticeably since reminders started going out automatically instead of depending on volunteers checking their own calendars.
