How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new client enrolls in a program, their intake form data automatically creates a client record in the case management system, assigns a case worker, generates an initial service plan template, and schedules the first check-in appointment.
2. As clients progress through the program, milestone outcomes are logged via a simple mobile form by the case worker and compiled automatically into a program-level outcomes report for funders and leadership, replacing the manual data entry and spreadsheet compilation that typically takes weeks at the end of a reporting period.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a program manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
