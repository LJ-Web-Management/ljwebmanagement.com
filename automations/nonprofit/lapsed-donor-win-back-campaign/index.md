How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every donor record is monitored continuously for giving activity, and any donor who hasn't made a gift in 12 months or more is automatically flagged and moved into a dedicated win-back sequence rather than staying lumped in with the general mailing list.
2. The first message is a personal, low-pressure check-in referencing their last gift and what it supported, followed two weeks later by a specific ask tied to a current program need.
3. Donors who don't respond after the sequence completes are moved to a lighter-touch quarterly newsletter track instead of being emailed weekly forever.
4. The annual fund manager gets a running list of exactly who lapsed and when, so re-engagement happens on a consistent 12-month trigger instead of once a year during a single mass appeal.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means an annual fund manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
