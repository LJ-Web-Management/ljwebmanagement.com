How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Event registration is handled through a branded page that captures guest information, meal preference, table assignments, and donation amount, and feeds it all into the event CRM automatically.
2. Registered guests receive a confirmation immediately, a logistics email one week before the event, and a donation receipt after the event.
3. Guests who registered but didn't show receive a "we missed you" email with a giving link, and post-event sponsors receive a customized impact report, all generated and delivered without any manual follow-up from the event team.

Tools it connects

- Event registration tool (e.g., Eventbrite or Qgiv)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a nonprofit event manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
