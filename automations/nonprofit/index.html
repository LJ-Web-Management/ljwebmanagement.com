    FAQ

    Nonprofit automation questions

    What nonprofit workflows can be automated?

    The 10 nonprofit automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, billing, payments & finance, and hr, hiring & team management, among other areas, for roles like Executive Director, Development Manager, and Volunteer Coordinator. Popular starting points include Grant Deadline Tracker & Application Status System, Donor Acknowledgment & Stewardship Drip System, Volunteer Shift Signup, Reminder & Hour-Tracking System.

    Do I need to replace my current nonprofit software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in nonprofit automations are Mailchimp, Google Sheets, Twilio, and ActiveCampaign, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a nonprofit automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
