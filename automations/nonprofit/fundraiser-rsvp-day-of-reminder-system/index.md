How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Guests who RSVP to a fundraiser or community event through the registration page are automatically added to the guest list, sent a calendar invite, and placed on a communication track that includes a reminder one week out and a final reminder the morning of the event with parking and check-in details.
2. Events that hit capacity automatically switch to a waitlist, and any guest who cancels frees up a spot that's offered instantly to the next person in line rather than staff manually managing an overflow list by hand.
3. No-shows are tracked automatically across events, giving the special events coordinator a real attendance-rate benchmark to plan capacity for the next event instead of guessing.
4. Every event runs the same reliable RSVP and reminder cadence without staff rebuilding the process from scratch each time.
