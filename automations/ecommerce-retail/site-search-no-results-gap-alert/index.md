How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When customers repeatedly search for a term that returns no results, the automation automatically flags it internally, surfacing either a real product gap worth sourcing or a fixable search and SEO issue, a common misspelling or a product that exists under different terminology than customers actually search for.
2. The site merchandiser gets a ranked list of the most frequent no-result searches instead of that valuable signal disappearing silently into site analytics nobody has time to dig through, and closing even a handful of these gaps routinely recovers sales that were quietly walking away empty-handed.
