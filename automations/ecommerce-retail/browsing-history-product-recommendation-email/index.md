How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Using the browsing and purchase data collected in Segment or directly inside Klaviyo, this automation builds a "you may also like" email a few days after a customer's most recent purchase, pulling from what they actually viewed and bought rather than a static, store-wide bestseller list.
2. A product recommendation engine like Rebuy or Nosto scores the catalog against that customer's behavior and feeds the top matches into the email template automatically through Zapier or Make.com, so every send is uniquely built per customer without the merchandising team hand-picking products.
3. Emails built this way consistently pull a meaningfully higher click-through rate than generic promotional blasts, because the products shown are ones the customer has already shown real interest in, turning a routine marketing email into a quiet, ongoing repeat-purchase driver.

- Shopify or WooCommerce
- Klaviyo
- Rebuy or Nosto (recommendation engine)
