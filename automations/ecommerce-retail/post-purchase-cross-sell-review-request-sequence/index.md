How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Seven days after a customer's first order is delivered, they automatically receive a product-specific email recommending complementary items based on what they bought, a curated cross-sell rather than a generic "you might also like" blast.
2. Fourteen days after delivery, a review request goes out with a direct link to the product page, and customers who leave a review receive a thank-you discount code for their next order.
3. The entire post-purchase relationship is managed automatically, turning a one-time transaction into a repeat customer without the store owner building a single manual campaign.

Tools it connects

- Klaviyo (post-purchase flow)
- Zapier or Make.com

### Without automation

The manual approach means a store owner handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation
