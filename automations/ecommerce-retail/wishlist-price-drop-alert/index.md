How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer saves an item to their wishlist using Swym or a native wishlist app, this automation keeps watching that product's price in the background, and the instant it drops, whether from a scheduled sale or a manual markdown, the customer automatically receives a notification through Klaviyo showing the old price crossed out next to the new one.
2. Zapier or Make.com checks price changes against the full wishlist database daily so no drop goes unnoticed, even on obscure, low-traffic SKUs the pricing team might not think to promote directly.
3. Because the alert reaches a customer who already decided they wanted the item and was only waiting on price, wishlist price-drop emails convert at a rate few other campaigns can match, turning routine markdowns into an active sales channel instead of a passive price change nobody notices.
