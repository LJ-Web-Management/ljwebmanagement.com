How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As orders come in, this automation continuously totals each customer's lifetime spend inside Shopify or WooCommerce and, the moment a customer crosses a defined threshold, automatically applies a VIP tag to their profile and syncs that segment into Klaviyo through Zapier or Make.com.
2. The newly tagged VIP is immediately enrolled in a dedicated perks sequence: early access to new drops, a higher loyalty point multiplier inside Smile.io, and a standing free-shipping code, all without anyone on the team manually reviewing spend reports to decide who qualifies.
3. Because the tag updates in real time rather than during a periodic manual review, VIP customers are recognized and rewarded the same week they earn the status instead of months later, keeping the top of the customer base engaged instead of quietly churning out unnoticed.
