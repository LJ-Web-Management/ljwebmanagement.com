How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a visitor views the same product page three or more times in a session without adding it to their cart, this automation treats that as high purchase intent and automatically triggers a follow-up email through Klaviyo highlighting that exact product, paired with supporting star ratings pulled from Judge.me or Yotpo and a size or color availability note.
2. Behavioral tracking is handled through Klaviyo's on-site tracking snippet, with Zapier or Make.com managing the trigger logic so only genuinely engaged browsers, not casual scrollers, receive the email.
3. Because the message targets shoppers who already showed real interest rather than a cold audience, browse abandonment emails typically convert at a noticeably higher rate than standard promotional sends, recovering purchase intent that would otherwise evaporate the moment the visitor closed the tab.
