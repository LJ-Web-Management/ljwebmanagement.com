How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers automatically receive a personalized discount code during their birthday month, pulled directly from their stored profile data, without the customer marketing manager building and sending a manual birthday list every single month.
2. The offer is timed to arrive at the start of the birthday month, giving customers plenty of time to actually use it, and birthday campaigns consistently convert at a noticeably higher rate than a generic promotional email since the offer feels personal rather than mass-blasted.
