How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As customers place orders, this automation tracks lifetime spend in real time and automatically moves them into the next loyalty tier the moment they cross a set threshold, no manual review, no waiting for a monthly batch update.
2. The newly upgraded customer immediately receives a congratulatory email laying out their new perks (free shipping, early access, a birthday reward) along with a welcome discount to use on their next order.
3. Customers notified the instant they hit VIP status engage with the perk far more than customers who find out about a tier change weeks later buried in a newsletter, making the loyalty program feel like a live relationship instead of a static point system.

Tools it connects

- Shopify or WooCommerce
- Loyalty platform (e.g., Smile.io or Yotpo Loyalty)
- Klaviyo (tier upgrade email)
- Zapier or Make.com
