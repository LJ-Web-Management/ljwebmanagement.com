How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As purchase orders are placed and received, this automation tracks the actual lead time for each supplier and SKU, not the lead time the supplier promised, but the lead time they delivered.
2. Over a rolling 90-day window, it calculates average and worst-case lead times per supplier and adjusts the recommended safety stock level accordingly.
3. When a supplier's reliability score drops below a set threshold, the inventory manager receives an alert to either adjust reorder points or begin qualifying an alternative supplier, before a reliability issue causes a stockout that was entirely predictable in hindsight.
