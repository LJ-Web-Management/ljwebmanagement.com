How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a free product is sent to an influencer, the automation automatically sends a follow-up request for the agreed content and tag within the negotiated timeframe, instead of the affiliate marketing manager manually tracking dozens of individual gifting agreements in a spreadsheet and remembering to follow up on each one.
2. Influencers who go quiet past the agreed window get a second, friendlier reminder automatically, and the brand actually collects on the content it paid to send out instead of a meaningful share of gifted product simply never resulting in a post.
