How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As an order moves from confirmed to packed to shipped to delivered, this automation pulls each fulfillment event from Shopify or WooCommerce and AfterShip's tracking feed and fires the matching update automatically: an order confirmation the moment payment clears, a shipped notice with the tracking number as soon as the label prints, and a delivered confirmation the day the carrier marks it complete.
2. Zapier or Make.com routes each event to Klaviyo for email and Twilio for SMS so customers get updates in whichever channel they prefer, without a support agent ever opening the order to check its status manually.
3. Since "where is my order" is consistently one of the top drivers of inbound support volume for online retailers, closing that information gap removes a large share of routine tickets before they're ever opened, freeing the support team to handle questions that actually need a human.

- Shopify or WooCommerce
- AfterShip or shipping carrier API

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a customer support lead is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
