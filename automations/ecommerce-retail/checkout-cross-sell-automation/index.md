How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on the specific items already sitting in a shopper's cart, this automation surfaces complementary product suggestions directly on the checkout page through Rebuy or a native Shopify checkout extension, a phone case shown next to a new phone, a matching accessory next to a dress, rather than a generic "frequently bought together" module unrelated to the actual cart contents.
2. The recommendation logic runs off real co-purchase data pulled from order history, refreshed weekly through Zapier or Make.com, so the suggestions stay accurate as the catalog and buying patterns shift.
3. Because the offer appears at the exact moment a customer is already committed to buying, checkout cross-sells convert at a far higher rate than post-purchase upsell emails, quietly lifting average order value on every transaction without the merchandising team touching a single product page.
