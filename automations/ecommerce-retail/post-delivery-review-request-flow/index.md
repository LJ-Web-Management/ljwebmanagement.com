How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Instead of asking for a review the day an order ships, this automation waits until 4 days after the estimated delivery date, giving the customer enough time to actually use the product before being asked to rate it.
2. The request includes a direct incentive for uploading a photo alongside the written review, since photo reviews convert future browsers at a noticeably higher rate than text-only reviews.
3. Customers who don't respond within a week get a single, friendly reminder before the request is closed out, keeping the review pipeline full without turning into spam.

Tools it connects

- Shopify or WooCommerce
- Product review app (e.g., Judge.me or Yotpo)
