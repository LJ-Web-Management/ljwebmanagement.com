How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Order data automatically enriches each customer's profile with category preferences and average order value, sharpening future segmentation and targeting without the customer data manager manually tagging customers based on what they've bought.
2. Every new order refines the profile a little further, so campaigns can target "customers who buy premium skincare and spend above average" with genuine precision instead of broad, guessed-at segments, and marketing spend goes toward messages that are actually relevant to the person receiving them.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a customer data manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
