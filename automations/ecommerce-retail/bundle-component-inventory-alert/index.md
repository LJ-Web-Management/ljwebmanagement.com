How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a bundled product sells in Shopify or WooCommerce, this automation automatically deducts each individual component from its own inventory count inside Cin7 or Skubana rather than only decrementing the bundle SKU itself, so the true stock picture for every component stays accurate even as it's sold both individually and inside bundles.
2. If any component's remaining stock would fall short of what's needed to fulfill an already-placed bundle order, the supply chain manager gets an immediate Slack alert through Zapier or Make.com before the order ships incomplete.
3. This catches the specific failure mode where a bundle looks purchasable on the storefront because the bundle SKU shows in stock, even though one component quietly ran out weeks earlier, preventing the kind of partial-shipment customer complaint that's expensive to fix after the fact and easy to prevent before it happens.
