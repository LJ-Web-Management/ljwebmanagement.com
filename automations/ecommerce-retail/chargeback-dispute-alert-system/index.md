How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a customer's bank initiates a chargeback through Stripe or PayPal, this automation immediately alerts the finance manager through Slack with the disputed order attached, including the original order details, tracking confirmation, and the full email and support ticket history with that customer pulled from Gorgias, everything needed to build a dispute response without hunting across four different systems under a deadline.
2. Zapier or Make.com assembles the evidence packet automatically and, for chargeback types with a clear paper trail (delivered and tracked, for example), can submit the initial response through Signifyd or the payment processor's dispute API without waiting on a person to start the process.
3. Responding to a chargeback within hours instead of days meaningfully improves the win rate on disputes, and having the evidence compiled automatically means a real order dispute is never missed simply because the alert got buried in a shared inbox.
