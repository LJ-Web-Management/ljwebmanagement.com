How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers who place a pre-order in Shopify or WooCommerce are automatically added to a dedicated launch-day segment inside Klaviyo, separate from the general customer list, so they can be messaged specifically about that product without cluttering the inbox of anyone who hasn't ordered it.
2. The moment inventory is received and the product flips to "in stock" or the fulfillment run begins shipping, Zapier or Make.com fires an immediate notification through email and, for customers who opted in, a Twilio SMS confirming their order is on its way, without anyone on the team having to remember which pre-orders are pending on which exact day.
3. Customers who pre-ordered and then hear nothing until a surprise shipping confirmation are far more likely to file a "where is my order" ticket than those kept informed in real time, so this alone measurably reduces launch-week support volume.
