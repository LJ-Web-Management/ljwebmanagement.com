How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Using order history pulled from Shopify or WooCommerce, this automation continuously recalculates each customer's lifetime spend and automatically sorts them into tiers, entry-level, core, and top-spender, inside Klaviyo, updating segment membership in real time as new orders come in rather than during an occasional manual export.
2. Each tier automatically receives different campaign treatment: entry-level customers get frequency-focused offers designed to earn a second order, while top-spender customers get early access and higher-value perks rather than the same blanket 10%-off code everyone else sees.
3. Zapier or Make.com keeps the tier logic synced between the store and the email platform so no customer is ever messaged based on stale data.
4. Treating customers differently based on actual value, instead of sending one campaign to the entire list, measurably lifts revenue per email sent and keeps top customers from feeling like just another name on the list.
