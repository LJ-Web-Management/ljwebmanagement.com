How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every sale that comes through an affiliate or influencer's unique link is automatically tracked back to its source using Refersion or ReferralCandy, attributing the order, commission rate, and payout amount without anyone cross-referencing UTM parameters by hand.
2. At the end of each pay period, Zapier or Make.com compiles a commission report per affiliate into Google Sheets, and approved payouts are pushed automatically through PayPal or Stripe rather than being calculated and issued manually one at a time.
3. Affiliates get a self-serve dashboard showing their real-time sales and pending commission, which keeps them motivated to keep posting without needing a status update from the marketing team.
4. Automating tracking and payout removes what used to be hours of manual reconciliation every month and keeps the affiliate program accurate enough that partners actually trust the numbers.
