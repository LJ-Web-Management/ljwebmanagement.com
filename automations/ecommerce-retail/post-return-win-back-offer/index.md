How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Rather than letting a returned order be the last interaction a customer has with the brand, this automation triggers the moment a return is completed in Shopify or WooCommerce and automatically sends a thoughtful follow-up through Klaviyo a few days later: an alternative product suggestion based on why the item was returned (a different size, a different style, a different scent) paired with a modest incentive to try again.
2. Returns flagged as quality issues are routed to a slightly more generous offer than returns flagged as "changed mind," since the brand is asking the customer to trust it again after a real product problem.
3. Zapier or Make.com manages the return-reason logic so the right offer reaches the right customer automatically.
4. Customers who receive this follow-up return to purchase again at a meaningfully higher rate than those simply refunded and left alone, turning a moment of disappointment into a second chance at the relationship.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a customer retention manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
