How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer submits a return request, this automation validates the order against return policy rules (window, product eligibility, condition), generates a pre-paid shipping label, sends it to the customer immediately, and creates a refund task that routes to the operations manager for approval once the tracking shows the item has been received.
2. What used to be a multi-day back-and-forth email chain becomes a self-service process that handles itself in hours.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on an e-commerce operations manager, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
