How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Email subscribers who haven't engaged in a long stretch automatically receive a re-permission campaign asking if they'd like to stay subscribed before being removed from the active list, protecting the sender's deliverability reputation instead of the lifecycle marketing manager letting a growing pool of disengaged addresses quietly drag down open rates and trigger spam filters.
2. Subscribers who confirm they still want the emails get reactivated as engaged contacts, while genuinely inactive addresses get cleanly removed, and the active list that's left behind performs measurably better across every campaign sent to it.
