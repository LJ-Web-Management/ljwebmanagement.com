How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer marks an order as a gift at checkout in Shopify or WooCommerce, this automation waits until a few days after the estimated delivery date and then sends the purchaser, not the recipient, a friendly follow-up through Klaviyo asking whether the gift landed well, paired with a discount code for their own next purchase as a thank-you for shopping the brand for someone else.
2. Zapier or Make.com filters specifically for orders tagged as gifts so regular customers never receive a message that wouldn't make sense for their own purchase.
3. Because the follow-up targets a moment of genuine emotional payoff, giving a gift that was well received, rather than a routine transactional touchpoint, it converts the purchaser into a repeat customer for themselves at a noticeably higher rate than a standard post-purchase email, extending one gift purchase into an ongoing relationship.
