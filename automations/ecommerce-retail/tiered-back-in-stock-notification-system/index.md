How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a product sells out, customers who click "notify me" on the product page are logged by SKU inside Swym or a Klaviyo back-in-stock form, building a live waitlist the merchandising team can see grow in real time.
2. The instant new inventory is received in Shopify or WooCommerce, Zapier or Make.com triggers a tiered release: VIP and past-purchaser segments get the restock email first, with the general waitlist following two hours later, so the highest-value customers don't lose out to a general announcement rush.
3. For limited-run products this staggered release turns restock day into a predictable, repeatable revenue spike instead of a chaotic first-come scramble, and it means merchandising never has to manually cross-reference who was waiting on which item.
