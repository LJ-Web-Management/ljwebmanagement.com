How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As orders come in from the website, Amazon, and Etsy simultaneously, this automation keeps stock counts synced across every channel in real time through a centralized platform like Extensiv or ChannelAdvisor, deducting inventory the instant a sale happens anywhere so the same unit can never be sold twice.
2. Zapier or Make.com handles the connection between the sync platform and each individual marketplace API, and if a sync fails or a channel drifts out of range of actual stock, the operations manager gets an immediate Slack alert rather than finding out from an angry customer email.
3. Before this kind of sync was in place, overselling on a fast-moving SKU across multiple channels was a routine, expensive problem; keeping every channel mirrored in real time eliminates it almost entirely and protects the business from the cancellations and account strikes that oversold marketplace orders cause.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, an operations manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
