How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a customer completes their very first order in Shopify or WooCommerce, this automation enrolls them in a dedicated welcome series through Klaviyo that's entirely separate from the store's general newsletter: the brand's origin story on day one, product care and usage tips on day three, tailored to whatever category they actually bought, and an invitation to the referral program on day seven with a friend discount code.
2. Zapier or Make.com confirms the order is genuinely a first purchase before enrollment, so returning customers never get a "welcome" email they've already seen.
3. New customers who go through a structured welcome series place a second order sooner and more often than those simply dropped into the general marketing list, making this one of the highest-leverage sequences a store can automate.

Tools it connects

- Shopify or WooCommerce
- Klaviyo
- Zapier or Make.com
- Referral platform (e.g., ReferralCandy or Smile.io)
