How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a shopper adds items to their cart and leaves the site without checking out, this automation starts a timed recovery sequence: a reminder email at 1 hour, a second email with a small incentive at 24 hours, and a final SMS nudge at 48 hours for shoppers who opted into texts.
2. Each message pulls in the actual product images and price from the abandoned cart so the shopper sees exactly what they left behind, not a generic "come back" message.
3. Carts recovered through this sequence convert at a meaningfully higher rate than carts left to sit untouched, turning a leaky point in the funnel into a reliable revenue source that runs without anyone monitoring it.
