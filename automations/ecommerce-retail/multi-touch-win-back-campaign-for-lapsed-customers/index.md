How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a customer's purchase history in Shopify or WooCommerce shows no order in 120 days, this automation pulls their past category and brand preferences into Klaviyo and builds a three-email win-back arc, starting soft with a "we miss you" note and escalating to a time-limited discount by the third touch.
2. Customers who open but don't convert are automatically synced to a retargeting audience through Zapier or Make.com, so the offer keeps following them off-site instead of dying in an inbox.
3. Anyone who places an order at any point in the sequence is instantly pulled out so they don't keep receiving win-back messaging after they've already come back.
4. Reactivating a lapsed customer this way costs a fraction of paid acquisition and consistently recovers revenue from a segment most retailers write off entirely.
