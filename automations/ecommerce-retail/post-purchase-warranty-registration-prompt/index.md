How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a purchase, customers are automatically prompted to register their product's warranty, building a clean database that makes future service or replacement claims far faster to process instead of a customer support manager trying to verify a purchase date from an old email receipt months or years later.
2. Registered customers get a smoother, faster experience the moment something actually goes wrong with a product, and the warranty database itself becomes a valuable source of customer contact data for future product announcements and recall notices.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a customer support manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
