How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As each order comes through checkout, this automation runs it against a risk model inside Signifyd or NoFraud, checking for mismatched billing and shipping addresses, an order value far above the customer's typical spend, or an account created minutes before the purchase.
2. Orders that score above the risk threshold are automatically held from fulfillment in Shopify or WooCommerce and routed to the risk manager through Slack with the specific red flags attached, rather than shipping on autopilot and hoping for the best.
3. Zapier or Make.com handles the hold-and-notify logic so nothing risky slips through during a busy sales period.
4. Catching high-risk orders before they ship prevents a meaningful share of chargebacks and fraudulent-order losses that would otherwise only surface weeks later as a dispute, protecting margin on exactly the orders that looked fine at a glance.
