How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customers already tagged as VIP based on lifetime spend or loyalty tier inside Smile.io are automatically added to a dedicated early-access segment in Klaviyo, and whenever a new product line is scheduled to launch, this automation sends that segment a private preview and early purchase link 24 to 48 hours before the line goes live to the general public.
2. Zapier or Make.com handles the timing so the early-access email fires from a single launch date entered once, rather than someone manually building a VIP-only send for every new release.
3. Because VIP customers get first pick before inventory is public, popular sizes and colors in a new line are far less likely to sell out on the VIP shoppers specifically, a subtle but real driver of loyalty.
4. Rewarding VIP status with genuine first access, not just a discount, keeps top customers feeling recognized rather than treated the same as everyone else.
