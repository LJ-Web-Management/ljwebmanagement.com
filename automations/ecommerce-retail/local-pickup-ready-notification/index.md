How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Orders placed for local pickup automatically notify the customer the moment the order is packed and waiting at the counter, instead of the customer guessing when to come by and either showing up too early or letting the order sit unclaimed for days.
2. The ecommerce manager sees pickup turnaround times improve once customers know exactly when to come, and staff stop fielding "is my order ready yet" calls that used to interrupt other work throughout the day for orders that weren't even close to done.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on an ecommerce manager, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
