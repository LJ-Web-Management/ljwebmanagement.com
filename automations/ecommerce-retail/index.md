## E-Commerce & Retail automation questions

**What e-commerce and retail workflows can be automated?**

The 53 e-commerce and retail automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, lead follow-up & sales, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Ecommerce Manager, Inventory Manager, and Loyalty Program Manager. Popular starting points include [Post-Purchase Cross-Sell & Review Request Sequence](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/ecommerce-retail/post-purchase-cross-sell-review-request-sequence/), [Proactive Order Delay Notification & Resolution System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/ecommerce-retail/proactive-order-delay-notification-resolution-system/), [Supplier Lead Time Tracker & Safety Stock Calculator](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/ecommerce-retail/supplier-lead-time-tracker-safety-stock-calculator/).

**Do I need to replace my current e-commerce and retail software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in e-commerce and retail automations are Google Sheets, Slack, Twilio, and Mailchimp, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does an e-commerce and retail automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
