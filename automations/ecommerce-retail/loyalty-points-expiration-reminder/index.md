How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As loyalty points inside Smile.io or LoyaltyLion approach their expiration date, this automation automatically identifies every customer with unused points expiring in the next 14 days and sends a reminder email through Klaviyo showing exactly how many points they have and what those points are worth toward their next order.
2. Customers with a meaningful balance, enough to cover a real discount, not just a few cents, get a slightly more urgent second reminder at the 3-day mark through Zapier or Make.com.
3. Because the message creates genuine urgency around something the customer already owns rather than asking them to act on a generic sale, expiration reminders convert to a purchase at a notably higher rate than standard promotional email, recovering revenue from points that would otherwise quietly expire unused and unnoticed.
