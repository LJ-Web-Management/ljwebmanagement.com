How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer uses the brand's hashtag, tags the brand in a post, or is identified as a micro-influencer in the product niche, this automation flags the content for the marketing manager to review, sends a templated DM requesting usage rights, and (once approved) adds the asset to a shared content library with tags for product, platform, and visual style.
2. Approved UGC is automatically scheduled into the social calendar to fill planned content slots, reducing the time spent hunting for authentic content to near zero while keeping the feed consistently fresh.

Tools it connects

- Social listening tool (e.g., Mention or a hashtag monitoring app)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Airtable or Google Sheets (UGC asset library)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/google-sheets/)
- Buffer or Later (content scheduling)
- Instagram and TikTok APIs (DM rights request)
