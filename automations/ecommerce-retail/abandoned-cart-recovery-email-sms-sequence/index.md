How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a shopper on Shopify or WooCommerce adds items to their cart and leaves without completing checkout, this automation splits the response by cart value: carts under $75 enter a two-email Klaviyo sequence spaced over 36 hours, while carts above $75 get an added SMS touch through Twilio within the first two hours, since higher-value carts respond better to a faster, more direct nudge.
2. Zapier or Make.com watches the cart abandonment event in real time and routes each shopper into the right track automatically, pulling in the actual product image and price so the message never feels generic.
3. Segmenting recovery effort by cart value instead of treating every abandoned cart the same lifts recovered revenue meaningfully compared to a single one-size-fits-all sequence, without anyone on the marketing team deciding case by case who gets the extra touch.
