How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a recurring subscription charge fails inside Recharge or Stripe Billing, this automation doesn't just retry the card once and give up: it schedules three retry attempts spaced over five days using card-updater logic, and after each failed attempt sends the customer an email through Klaviyo with a direct link to update their payment method before the subscription lapses.
2. If the third retry also fails, the customer gets a final SMS through Twilio warning that cancellation is imminent, giving them one last easy way to save the subscription.
3. Zapier or Make.com keeps the subscription status, the payment retries, and the customer messaging all in sync so nothing falls out of order.
4. Recovering failed payments this systematically saves a significant share of subscriptions that would otherwise churn silently over a simple expired card, protecting recurring revenue the business had already counted on.
