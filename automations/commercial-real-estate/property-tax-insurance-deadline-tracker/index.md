## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks payment and appeal deadlines for property taxes and insurance renewals across every property in a portfolio, automatically alerting the asset manager well ahead of each date.
2. Nothing gets paid late, and no appeal window closes unnoticed across a portfolio too large to track by memory.
