How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a tenant submits a maintenance request through the portal, this automation automatically routes it to the correct vendor based on the issue category, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, sends the tenant a confirmation with an expected response time, and follows up automatically if the issue isn't marked resolved within the vendor's SLA window.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a facilities coordinator remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out

- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
