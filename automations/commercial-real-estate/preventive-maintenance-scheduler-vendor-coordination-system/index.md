How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every piece of major building equipment (HVAC units, elevators, fire suppression systems, generators, and plumbing infrastructure) is tracked in a preventive maintenance calendar with manufacturer-recommended service intervals.
2. Service work orders are automatically generated and sent to the appropriate contracted vendor at the required interval, and the vendor confirms the scheduled date directly in the system.
3. After service is completed, the vendor submits a digital service report, which is automatically filed to the equipment record and triggers the next service window calculation.
4. The facilities manager receives a monthly maintenance compliance report showing every PM completed, every one overdue, and every vendor response rate, managing the building's equipment health proactively rather than reactively.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a facilities manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
