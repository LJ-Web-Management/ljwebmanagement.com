How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an analyst is asked to underwrite a potential acquisition or lease transaction, this automation pulls comparable sales and lease transactions from connected data sources, formats them into a clean comp grid, and feeds the data into a standardized investment memo template, pre-populating the property description, market context, and comparable analysis sections.
2. The analyst focuses their time on the qualitative judgment, market narrative, and financial modeling rather than the data aggregation and document formatting that typically consumes 60% of memo preparation time.
3. Final memos are sent to the client or investment committee via a branded delivery link automatically when the analyst marks the memo complete.
