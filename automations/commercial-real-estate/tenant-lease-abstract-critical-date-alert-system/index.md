How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every tenant lease in the portfolio is abstracted into a standardized database capturing rent commencement, rent escalations, option exercise windows, lease expiration, co-tenancy clauses, and renewal notice requirements.
2. Reminders are sent to the asset manager at 18, 12, 6, and 3 months before each critical date, because a renewal option that must be exercised in writing 12 months before expiration can be inadvertently waived if the notice date is missed, converting a renewing tenant into a lease-end negotiation.
3. When a tenant exercises a renewal option, the new lease terms are automatically updated in the database and the rent schedule is recalculated for the extended term.
