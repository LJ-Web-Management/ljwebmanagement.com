## Commercial Real Estate automation questions

**What commercial real estate workflows can be automated?**

The 10 commercial real estate automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, compliance, documents & deadlines, and operations, inventory & vendors, among other areas, for roles like Asset Manager, CRE Broker, and Property / Market Analyst. Popular starting points include [Deal Pipeline Tracker & LOI Follow-Up System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/commercial-real-estate/deal-pipeline-tracker-loi-follow-up-system/), [Comparable Sales Report & Investment Memo Generator](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/commercial-real-estate/comparable-sales-report-investment-memo-generator/), [Tenant Lease Abstract & Critical Date Alert System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/commercial-real-estate/tenant-lease-abstract-critical-date-alert-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current commercial real estate software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in commercial real estate automations are Google Sheets, Twilio, Airtable, and Google Calendar, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a commercial real estate automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
