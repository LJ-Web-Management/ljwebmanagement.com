## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a space is marked vacant or a notice to vacate is logged, this automation automatically pushes the listing to commercial listing platforms and notifies the leasing team's broker network.
2. The lag between a space going empty and it actually being marketed, which used to cost weeks of lost rent, shrinks to same-day.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a leasing agent has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
