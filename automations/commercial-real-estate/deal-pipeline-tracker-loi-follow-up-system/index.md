How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every active deal in the broker's pipeline (from prospecting through letter of intent, due diligence, and close) lives in a CRM that updates automatically as the deal progresses, and the broker receives a weekly pipeline summary showing deal count by stage, estimated commission value, and any deal that hasn't moved in 14 days.
2. When an LOI is sent, the system starts a response-clock and sends the broker a prompt to follow up if no response has been received in five business days.
3. Principals in a deal who go quiet during due diligence receive a structured check-in sequence from the broker, keeping communication open during the period when deals are most likely to quietly die without an obvious reason.
