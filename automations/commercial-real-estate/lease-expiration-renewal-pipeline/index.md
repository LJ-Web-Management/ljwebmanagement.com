How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks every tenant lease's expiration date across a portfolio and automatically triggers a renewal outreach sequence starting 180 days out, escalating in urgency as the date approaches and flagging the property manager directly if a tenant hasn't responded within 90 days of expiration.
2. Renewals stop being discovered at the last minute and become a managed pipeline months in advance.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a leasing manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
