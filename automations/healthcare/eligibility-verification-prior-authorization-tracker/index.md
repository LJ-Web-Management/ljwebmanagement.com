How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For every appointment scheduled more than 48 hours out, this automation automatically verifies the patient's insurance eligibility and pulls their current deductible and copay information, flagging any coverage discrepancies before the patient arrives.
2. Procedures that require prior authorization are identified from the appointment type, a pre-auth request is initiated automatically, and the status is tracked daily until approved, ensuring no one discovers a missing auth after a procedure has already been performed, which is when it becomes a write-off instead of a payment.
