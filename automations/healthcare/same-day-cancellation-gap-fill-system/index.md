How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a same-day cancellation opens a gap in the schedule, the automation automatically texts patients on a short-notice waitlist offering the newly open slot, filling time that would otherwise sit unused for the rest of the day instead of the scheduling coordinator working the phones trying to reach someone on short notice.
2. The first patient to confirm gets the slot automatically, and practices running this fill process consistently recover a meaningful share of same-day cancellations that used to represent pure lost revenue and idle provider time.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a scheduling coordinator, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation
