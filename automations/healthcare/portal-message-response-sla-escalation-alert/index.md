How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A patient portal message that hasn't received a staff response within the practice's target time window automatically escalates to a supervisor, preventing a question or concern from being missed entirely in a shared inbox that multiple staff members assume someone else is checking.
2. The patient portal administrator gets a daily count of messages that required escalation, useful signal for whether portal message volume has outgrown current staffing, and patients trust the portal as a real communication channel once messages reliably get answered within the promised window instead of sometimes disappearing for days.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a patient portal administrator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
