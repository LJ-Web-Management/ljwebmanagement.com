How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a telehealth visit is booked, this automation immediately generates a unique, secure video link through the practice's HIPAA-compliant platform and sends it to the patient by email along with a calendar invite.
2. A second reminder text goes out 15 minutes before the appointment with the same link and a one-line tech-check prompt, so patients aren't scrambling to find the email at the last minute.
3. Providers receive their own link automatically loaded into their daily schedule view, with no separate login step required.
4. No-shows on virtual visits, which tend to run higher than in-person appointments, typically drop by half once patients have a link in hand at exactly the moment they need it.

Tools it connects

- HIPAA-compliant telehealth platform (e.g., Doxy.me or Zoom for Healthcare)

- Zapier or Make.com

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a telehealth coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
