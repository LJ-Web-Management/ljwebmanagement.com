How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation internally tracks each provider's credentialing status and hospital privilege renewal dates, alerting administration well ahead of any expiration, instead of the credentialing coordinator discovering a lapse only when a claim gets rejected or a provider is blocked from admitting a patient.
2. Every credential and privilege is tracked against its specific renewal cycle, and the practice gains a clean, always-current view of exactly where every provider stands, turning what used to be an anxious annual scramble to renew everything on time into a routine, well-paced administrative process.
