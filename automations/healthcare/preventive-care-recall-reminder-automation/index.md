How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each night, this automation scans the patient roster against the recommended interval for annual physicals, cleanings, and standard screenings, and automatically queues an outreach message for anyone who has crossed their due date without a visit on the books.
2. A first reminder goes out by email, a follow-up text arrives a week later if the patient hasn't scheduled, and any patient still unbooked after 30 days is handed to the wellness program coordinator as a personal outreach task.
3. Every message includes a direct scheduling link so the patient can book in two taps rather than sitting on hold.
4. Practices that turn this recall system on typically bring back a meaningful share of patients who would otherwise have quietly drifted out of the preventive care cycle entirely.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a wellness program coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
