## Healthcare automation questions

**What healthcare workflows can be automated?**

The 53 healthcare automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, compliance, documents & deadlines, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Scheduling Coordinator, Revenue Cycle Manager, and Patient Portal Administrator. Popular starting points include [Front Desk Daily Briefing & Schedule Gap Alert](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/healthcare/front-desk-daily-briefing-schedule-gap-alert/), [Referral Tracking & Specialist Follow-Up System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/healthcare/referral-tracking-specialist-follow-up-system/), [Eligibility Verification & Prior Authorization Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/healthcare/eligibility-verification-prior-authorization-tracker/).

**Do I need to replace my current healthcare software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in healthcare automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, Slack, and Mailchimp, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a healthcare automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
