How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a new patient books their first appointment, this automation sends a secure digital intake packet 48 hours ahead of the visit, covering demographics, insurance details, medical history, and consent forms.
2. Completed forms are parsed automatically and written directly into the correct fields in the EHR, so the new patient coordinator opens the chart on visit day already populated rather than handing the patient a clipboard at the front desk.
3. Patients who haven't completed the packet by 24 hours before their visit receive an automatic reminder text with a direct link to finish it.
4. First-visit check-in time typically drops from 15 minutes of paperwork to under 3 minutes of verification, and the provider walks into the room with a complete history instead of a blank chart.
