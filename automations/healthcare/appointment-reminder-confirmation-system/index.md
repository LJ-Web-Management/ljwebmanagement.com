How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every scheduled appointment triggers an automated reminder sequence: a text and email 72 hours out, a second text 24 hours out, and a final same-day nudge two hours before arrival.
2. Each message includes a one-tap link that lets the patient confirm, reschedule, or cancel without ever calling the office, and every response updates the schedule in real time so the front desk coordinator sees the day's true status at a glance.
3. Patients who don't respond to the 24-hour reminder are automatically flagged for a personal call before the slot is written off.
4. Practices running this system typically cut no-show rates by 30 to 40 percent within the first two months, turning a schedule full of unconfirmed guesses into one the front desk can actually plan around.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a front desk coordinator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
