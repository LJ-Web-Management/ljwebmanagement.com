How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The instant a scheduled visit is marked as a no-show in the practice management system, this automation fires a re-engagement text within minutes, acknowledging the missed appointment without blame and offering a direct link to rebook the next available slot.
2. If the patient hasn't rebooked within 24 hours, a follow-up email goes out with two or three specific open times pulled live from the schedule, and patients who no-show twice in a rolling 12-month window are automatically flagged to the revenue cycle manager for a personal outreach call before a no-show fee policy kicks in.
3. Every recovered visit and every fee applied is logged automatically in a shared tracker so leadership can see the real dollar impact of missed appointments.
4. Practices running this typically recover 15 to 20 percent of no-show visits that would otherwise have simply vanished from the schedule.
