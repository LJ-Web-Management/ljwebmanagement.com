How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new patient completes intake, this automation automatically captures how they found the practice, whether it was a physician referral, an insurance directory listing, a Google ad, or a friend's recommendation, by pulling the response from the intake form directly into a centralized tracking sheet.
2. Each new patient record is automatically tagged with their source, and the data rolls up weekly into a report the marketing coordinator reviews to see which channels are actually converting into booked, paying patients rather than just generating clicks.
3. Referring physicians identified through the source data are automatically added to a thank-you outreach list.
4. Practices running this stop guessing at what's working and start shifting marketing budget toward the channels the data actually supports.
