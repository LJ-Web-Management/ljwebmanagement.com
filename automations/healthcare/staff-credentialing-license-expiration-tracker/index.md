How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every provider and licensed staff member's credentials (DEA license, state medical license, malpractice insurance, BLS certification, and board certifications) are tracked with expiration dates in a central system.
2. Renewal reminders are sent to the staff member at 120, 60, and 30 days before expiration, and the practice administrator receives a weekly compliance dashboard showing every credential status across the entire practice.
3. Any credential that lapses without a renewal on file triggers an immediate escalation, because an expired license is a compliance and liability issue that cannot wait until the next Friday report.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a practice administrator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
