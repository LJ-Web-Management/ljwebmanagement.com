How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically tracks each child's age-based well-visit schedule and sends reminders timed to each milestone visit, the two-week check, the twelve-month, the kindergarten physical, rather than relying entirely on parents to remember a schedule that shifts as their child grows.
2. The pediatric care coordinator gets a running list of children who've fallen behind their expected schedule, letting outreach happen proactively instead of only noticing a gap at the next visit, whenever that happens to be, and well-visit compliance improves noticeably once the reminder timing is handled automatically rather than left to parental memory alone.
