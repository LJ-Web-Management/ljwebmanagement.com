How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each morning before the office opens, the office manager and front desk team receive an automated briefing: today's confirmed appointments, patients who haven't confirmed, the day's unscheduled gaps, and any outstanding patient balances over a set threshold.
2. Unconfirmed appointments trigger an automated text asking the patient to confirm or reschedule.
3. Schedule gaps that appear during the day are automatically pushed to a waitlist outreach sequence so that cancellations are filled before the slot goes empty, and the front desk team starts every single day fully informed rather than discovering problems as they arrive.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a medical office manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
