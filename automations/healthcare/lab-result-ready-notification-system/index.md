How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment new lab results are uploaded and finalized in the system, this automation automatically sends the patient a portal notification and a text letting them know results are available, along with a brief note on whether they should expect a call from the office or can simply review the results themselves online.
2. Results flagged by the provider as needing discussion automatically route to the clinical support specialist's task queue for a callback, while routine normal results are released to the patient portal without staff intervention.
3. Patients who haven't logged in to view flagged results within 48 hours trigger an automatic reminder text.
4. This keeps patients informed the moment results land instead of waiting on a callback queue, while making sure nothing that needs a conversation slips through unnoticed.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a clinical support specialist has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
