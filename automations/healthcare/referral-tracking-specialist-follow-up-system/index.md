How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a provider refers a patient to a specialist, this automation logs the referral with the patient name, specialist, diagnosis code, and urgency level, sends the referral packet to the specialist office automatically, and texts the patient their specialist's contact information with instructions to schedule.
2. If confirmation that the appointment was booked hasn't been received within seven days, a follow-up task is created and the patient receives another reminder, closing the referral loop that typically gets lost the moment the patient walks out the door.

Tools it connects

- EHR with API access (e.g., athenahealth or eClinicalWorks)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Twilio (patient reminder texts)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/twilio/)
- [Airtable or Google Sheets (referral tracking log)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/google-sheets/)
