How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation scans the patient roster daily against each patient's last visit date and the recommended interval for their care type, whether that's an annual physical, a six-month cleaning, or a mammogram screening, and automatically queues a reminder message once a patient crosses that threshold.
2. A first message goes out by email at the 11-month mark, followed by a text at 12 months, and a final personal outreach task assigned to the recall coordinator if the patient hasn't booked by 13 months.
3. Every message includes a direct scheduling link so the patient can book in two taps instead of waiting on hold.
4. Practices that turn this on typically recover 20 to 25 percent of patients who would otherwise have quietly lapsed out of the practice.
