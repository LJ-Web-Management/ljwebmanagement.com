## Car Wash & Auto Detailing automation questions

**What car wash and detailing workflows can be automated?**

The 5 car wash and detailing automations in our catalog cover billing, payments & finance, scheduling, reminders & dispatch, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Membership Manager, Mobile Detailing Manager, and Location Manager. Popular starting points include [Membership Renewal & Failed Payment Recovery](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/car-wash-auto-detailing/membership-renewal-failed-payment-recovery/), [Mobile Detailing Route & Time-Slot Booking](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/car-wash-auto-detailing/mobile-detailing-route-time-slot-booking/), [Rain Check / Weather Guarantee Automation](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/car-wash-auto-detailing/rain-check-weather-guarantee-automation/).

**Do I need to replace my current car wash and detailing software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in car wash and detailing automations are Twilio, Stripe, and Google Sheets, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a car wash and detailing automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
