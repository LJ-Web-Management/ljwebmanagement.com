## What this automation does, step by step

1. For mobile detailing businesses, this automation lets customers book directly into open time slots based on their location and the technician's route for the day, automatically optimizing the day's schedule to minimize drive time between jobs.
2. A day's bookings assemble themselves into an efficient route instead of a manager rearranging a schedule by hand every evening.

Tools it connects

- A booking platform (Squire or Booksy)
- Google Maps API
- Zapier or Make.com
