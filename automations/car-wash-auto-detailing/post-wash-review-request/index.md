How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A few hours after a wash or detail is completed, this automation automatically sends a review request timed to when the customer's vehicle looks its best.
2. Timing the ask to the peak-satisfaction moment maximizes the odds of a positive review compared to a generic request sent days later.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

### With this automation
