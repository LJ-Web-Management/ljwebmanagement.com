## What this automation does, step by step

1. For commercial fleet accounts, this automation automatically compiles monthly usage and billing summaries by vehicle and sends them to the fleet manager.
2. A manual report pulled together at invoice time becomes an automatic one that's ready before the fleet manager even asks for it.

- A car wash POS with fleet billing

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a fleet accounts manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
