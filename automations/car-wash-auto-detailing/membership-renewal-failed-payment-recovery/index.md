How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For unlimited wash club members, this automation automatically retries a failed monthly charge, texts the member a payment update link, and only suspends access after a set number of failed attempts.
2. Memberships that would otherwise silently lapse the moment a card expires get recovered instead of quietly dropping off the books.

Handled by hand, a membership manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.
