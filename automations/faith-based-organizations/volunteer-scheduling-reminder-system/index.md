## What this automation does, step by step

1. For recurring volunteer roles, greeters, childcare, the tech team, this automation automatically fills the schedule based on each volunteer's stated availability, sends confirmation and reminder texts before every shift, and automatically reaches out to a backup list if someone can't make their assigned time.
2. Sunday mornings stop starting with a scramble to figure out who's actually showing up to serve.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a volunteer coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
