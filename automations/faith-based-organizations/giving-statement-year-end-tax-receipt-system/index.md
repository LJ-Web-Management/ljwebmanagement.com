How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Throughout the year, first-time givers receive an automatic thank-you acknowledgment with a summary of how their gift is being used.
2. At year end, every donor automatically receives their complete annual giving statement as a PDF by email (formatted to meet IRS requirements for charitable contribution acknowledgment) with a personal note from the senior pastor.
3. Donors who gave their largest gift in the current year are flagged for a personal call from the executive pastor or development team before year end, turning a compliance requirement into a meaningful touchpoint that deepens the donor's relationship with the organization before they decide their next year's giving.
