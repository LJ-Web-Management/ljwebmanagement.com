How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When someone expresses interest in joining a small group (through a weekend service card, the app, or the website) this automation captures their availability, neighborhood, life stage, and interest areas and matches them to up to three recommended groups.
2. The prospective member receives a personal introduction to each group leader, who is simultaneously notified of the new interested attendee with a prompt to reach out within 48 hours.
3. Groups that haven't logged attendance in two consecutive weeks receive a check-in from the small groups director, and groups that exceed their target attendance size are automatically prompted to consider launching a new group or adding a co-leader.

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a small groups director, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.
