How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When someone signs up for a small group through the church's website, this automation automatically matches them to a group based on their stated preferences, day, location, life stage, notifies the group leader of the new member, and sends the new member the group's meeting details.
2. A staff member no longer has to manually sort through sign-up forms trying to fit people into the right group by hand.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a small groups pastor has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
