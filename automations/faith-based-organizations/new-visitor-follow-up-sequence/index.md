How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a first-time visitor fills out a connect card, physical or digital, this automation automatically sends a warm welcome text and email within a day, and creates a personal follow-up task assigned to a staff member or volunteer for a call within the week.
2. No new visitor's first Sunday goes unfollowed simply because the connect card sat in a basket until Wednesday.

- Planning Center or Church Community Builder
