## What this automation does, step by step

1. At year-end, or whenever requested, this automation automatically compiles each donor's full giving history into a properly formatted tax statement and sends it directly to them.
2. What's often a manual, error-prone spreadsheet process handled by one overworked volunteer every January becomes an automatic, accurate process that runs itself.
