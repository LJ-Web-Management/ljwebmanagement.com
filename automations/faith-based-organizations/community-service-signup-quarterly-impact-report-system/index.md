How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Community service events are posted with a signup form that captures volunteer name, contact, and any applicable skills or background checks required.
2. As volunteers sign up, the outreach director sees a real-time capacity tracker and receives an alert when an event reaches its minimum viable volunteer count.
3. After each event, volunteers receive a thank-you message with a summary of what was accomplished (meals served, families helped, supplies distributed) and are invited to sign up for the next event.
4. Quarterly, a community impact report is auto-assembled from all logged service events and distributed to the congregation and community partners, giving the organization documented evidence of its community investment without anyone manually compiling statistics from a notebook.
