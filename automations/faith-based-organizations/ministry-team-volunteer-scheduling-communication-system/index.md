How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Ministry team leaders submit their volunteer needs for each weekend service through a simple form, and the system automatically matches open roles to volunteers based on their team, availability, and frequency limits, then sends each volunteer their assignment via text and email the week before.
2. Volunteers who don't confirm their assignment by Wednesday receive an automatic reminder.
3. Last-minute cancellations trigger an immediate text to the next qualified volunteer on the waitlist so every role is covered before Sunday morning.
4. The executive pastor sees a complete weekend readiness dashboard on Thursday rather than fielding a cascade of calls from team leaders on Saturday night.
