How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Community events, conferences, and weekend service registrations are handled through a branded registration form that captures attendance preference, meal choices, childcare needs, and volunteer interest.
2. Confirmed registrants receive an immediate confirmation with event details, a calendar invite, and a reminder 48 hours before the event.
3. Registrants who expressed volunteer interest are automatically routed to a volunteer coordinator for role assignment, and all assigned volunteers receive their instructions and check-in location automatically the day before.
4. Post-event, all attendees receive a digital follow-up with a recap, next steps for connection, and a giving opportunity, turning a one-time event into a relationship continuation.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, an events coordinator has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
