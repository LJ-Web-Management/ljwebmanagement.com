## Faith-Based Organizations automation questions

**What church and ministry workflows can be automated?**

The 10 church and ministry automations in our catalog cover billing, payments & finance, client onboarding & intake, and hr, hiring & team management, among other areas, for roles like Executive Pastor, Church Administrator, and Events Coordinator. Popular starting points include [Ministry Team Volunteer Scheduling & Communication System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/faith-based-organizations/ministry-team-volunteer-scheduling-communication-system/), [Giving Statement & Year-End Tax Receipt System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/faith-based-organizations/giving-statement-year-end-tax-receipt-system/), [Event Registration & Volunteer Assignment System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/faith-based-organizations/event-registration-volunteer-assignment-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current church and ministry software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in church and ministry automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Google Sheets, and Jotform, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a church and ministry automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
