How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a student withdraws or a spot otherwise opens in a full class or program, the waitlist automatically identifies the next eligible family in line and sends them an offer by text and email with a 24-hour window to claim the seat.
2. If the offer expires unclaimed, it automatically passes to the next family on the list rather than sitting with the enrollment coordinator until someone remembers to follow up.
3. Families further down the waitlist receive a periodic status update so they aren't left wondering whether they've been forgotten.
4. Seats that used to sit empty for a week or more while staff manually worked down a waitlist are now filled within a day or two.
