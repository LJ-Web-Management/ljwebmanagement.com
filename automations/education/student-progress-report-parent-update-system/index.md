How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After each tutoring session, tutors submit a brief session summary through a mobile-friendly form, and the system automatically formats it into a branded progress report and emails it to the parent, with a running log of sessions, topics covered, and improvement notes.
2. Parents who haven't re-booked a session in two weeks receive a gentle reminder, and the business owner gets a weekly rollup showing which students are on track and which may be at risk of dropping.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a tutoring business owner remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
