How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a teacher marks a student absent in the attendance system each morning, a notification goes out automatically to the parent or guardian within 15 minutes, by text and email, confirming the absence and asking for a reason if none was provided.
2. Students who reach three unexcused absences in a rolling 30-day window automatically flag the attendance coordinator for a follow-up call, rather than that pattern getting buried until a report card meeting.
3. Chronic absenteeism thresholds required for state reporting are tracked continuously in the background, so the compliance report that used to take a full day to assemble each semester is ready in minutes.
4. Parents get faster, more consistent communication, and the school catches attendance problems while they're still small.
