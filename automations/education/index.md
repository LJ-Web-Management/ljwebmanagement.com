## Education & Training automation questions

**What education and training workflows can be automated?**

The 11 education and training automations in our catalog cover customer service & communication, lead follow-up & sales, and hr, hiring & team management, among other areas, for roles like School Administrator, K-12 Teacher, and Tutoring Business Owner. Popular starting points include [Enrollment Inquiry & Waitlist Management System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/education/enrollment-inquiry-waitlist-management-system/), [Parent Communication & Grade Alert System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/education/parent-communication-grade-alert-system/), [Student Progress Report & Parent Update System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/education/student-progress-report-parent-update-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current education and training software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in education and training automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Google Sheets, and Jotform, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does an education and training automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
