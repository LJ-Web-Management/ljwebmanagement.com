How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At the midpoint of each grading period, the system automatically pulls current grades, missing assignment counts, and teacher comments from the gradebook for every student and compiles them into a branded, easy-to-read progress report.
2. Reports are emailed directly to parents on a set date each term rather than depending on individual teachers to remember and format them one by one, and any student whose average has dropped by 10 points or more since the last report is flagged for the academic dean to review.
3. Parents get a consistent, on-time update every single term, and teachers get roughly four hours back per grading period that used to go into manually building reports in the LMS.
