How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Ninety days before the current term ends, every enrolled family automatically receives a re-enrollment invitation with an early-bird tuition rate that expires after two weeks, encouraging families to commit before they start shopping around.
2. Families who haven't responded after the early-bird window receive a follow-up sequence at 60 and 30 days out, and any family that still hasn't re-enrolled by 14 days before term end is flagged to the director of admissions for a personal call.
3. Re-enrollment status for every family is visible on a single dashboard instead of living in the admissions director's memory, and the early-bird pricing model consistently locks in a large share of returning families well before the deadline pressure sets in.
