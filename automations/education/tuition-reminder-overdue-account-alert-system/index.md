How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Five days before each tuition installment is due, families are automatically sent a reminder email and text with the amount owed and a direct payment link, cutting down on the "when is this due again" calls to the front office.
2. Accounts that go seven days past due are automatically flagged to the business office manager with a payment history summary, and a second, more direct reminder goes out on day ten before any human has to get involved.
3. Families on a payment plan get their own tailored schedule of reminders rather than a generic notice that doesn't match their terms.
4. The business office spends its time on the handful of accounts that genuinely need a conversation instead of chasing every family every month.
