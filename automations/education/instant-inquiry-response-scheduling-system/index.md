How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a prospective family submits an inquiry form, whether from the school's website, a Facebook ad, or a directory listing, they receive an automated reply within two minutes containing program details, current tuition ranges, and a direct link to book a tour.
2. The inquiry is simultaneously logged in the CRM with source tracking, so admissions staff can see at a glance which channels are actually producing families who show up.
3. Inquiries that go three days without booking a tour trigger a personal follow-up nudge to the admissions coordinator, and families who do book are sent a reminder text the morning of their visit.
4. What used to depend on someone checking a shared inbox between meetings now happens automatically, so no inquiry sits untouched over a weekend.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, an admissions coordinator has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
