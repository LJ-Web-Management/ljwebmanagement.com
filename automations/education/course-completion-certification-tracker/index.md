How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As employees complete training modules, their completion status syncs automatically to an HR dashboard, and any employee who hasn't completed a required training by the deadline receives escalating reminders, first to them, then to their manager.
2. When a course is completed successfully, the employee's digital certificate is generated and emailed automatically, and their compliance record is updated without anyone in training or HR having to pull a report and send it manually.

Tools it connects

- LMS (e.g., TalentLMS or Docebo)
- Zapier or Make.com
- HRIS (e.g., BambooHR or ADP) for roster sync
- Email (certificate delivery and reminders)
