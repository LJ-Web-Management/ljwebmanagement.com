How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a new service installation is scheduled, this automation sends the customer an immediate confirmation with the date, the assigned technician's name, and what to have ready for the visit.
2. The day before the appointment, a reminder goes out with the technician's arrival window and a reschedule link, cutting down on missed appointments caused by a customer simply forgetting the date.
3. When the technician is dispatched and en route, the customer automatically receives an "on the way" text with a live ETA, the same experience customers now expect from other home service providers.
4. The customer care coordinator no longer manually calls every customer the day before installation, and no-show installation appointments have dropped because the reminder sequence catches conflicts early enough for the customer to reschedule instead of no-showing.
