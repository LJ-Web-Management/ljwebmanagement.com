How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a network monitoring alert fires indicating a service-affecting outage, this automation identifies which customer accounts are in the impacted area, sends proactive service disruption notifications via text and email before customers call in, updates the IVR message on the support line to acknowledge the outage, and posts a status update on the company's service status page, all within minutes of the alert firing.
2. As the restoration progresses, customers in the affected area receive staged updates, and a final "service restored" message goes out the moment the NOC confirms resolution.
3. Inbound call volume during outages drops significantly because customers are informed before they reach for the phone.
