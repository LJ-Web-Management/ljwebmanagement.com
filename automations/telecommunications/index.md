## Telecommunications automation questions

**What telecom workflows can be automated?**

The 10 telecom automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, customer service & communication, and scheduling, reminders & dispatch, among other areas, for roles like Customer Retention Manager, Field Technician Coordinator, and Network Operations Center Manager. Popular starting points include [Churn Prediction & Proactive Save Campaign System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/telecommunications/churn-prediction-proactive-save-campaign-system/), [Installation Appointment Booking & Technician Dispatch System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/telecommunications/installation-appointment-booking-technician-dispatch-system/), [Network Outage Alert & Proactive Customer Communication System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/telecommunications/network-outage-alert-proactive-customer-communication-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current telecom software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in telecom automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, Mailchimp, and ActiveCampaign, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a telecom automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
