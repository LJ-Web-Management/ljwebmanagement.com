How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Sixty days before a customer's contract term ends, this automation automatically flags the account and pulls the last six months of usage and payment history to score the account's renewal risk.
2. Accounts with declining usage, a recent support escalation, or a late payment in the last 90 days are marked at-risk and automatically triggered into a personalized retention offer sequence, a discounted upgrade or a loyalty credit delivered by text and email.
3. Accounts that show stable usage and clean payment history are routed into a simpler auto-renewal confirmation instead, so the retention budget isn't spent on customers who were never going to leave.
4. The renewals specialist works from a prioritized list of the 60-day window rather than discovering a churn risk the week the contract already lapsed, and every offer's redemption is tracked automatically back to the account.
