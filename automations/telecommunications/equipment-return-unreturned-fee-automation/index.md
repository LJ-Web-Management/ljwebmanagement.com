How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer cancels service, this automation immediately emails and texts equipment return instructions along with a prepaid shipping label generated through the carrier's API.
2. The system tracks the shipment using the label's tracking number and automatically updates the account the moment the equipment is scanned as received at the warehouse.
3. If the return window, typically 14 or 21 days, passes without a tracked return, the automation applies the unreturned equipment fee to the final bill and sends the customer a notice explaining the charge and how to dispute it if the equipment was already shipped.
4. The logistics coordinator sees a live dashboard of every pending return by days remaining, rather than manually chasing down modems and set-top boxes that were never sent back.
