How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a payment fails, this automation immediately attempts a retry after 24 hours, sends the customer a payment failure notice with an update link, and tries a second retry at 72 hours if the payment method hasn't been updated.
2. Accounts still unpaid after seven days move into a structured collections workflow: a service interruption warning at day 10, a courtesy hold at day 15, and escalation to the collections team with the full payment history on day 21.
3. The billing manager sees a live aging dashboard by delinquency bucket rather than working from a static report, and each stage of the collections workflow runs automatically without a billing agent manually managing each account through the sequence.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a billing manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
