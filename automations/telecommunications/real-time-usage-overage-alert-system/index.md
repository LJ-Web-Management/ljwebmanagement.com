How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation continuously monitors every customer's data or usage against their plan's allotment in near real time, checking usage totals against plan thresholds throughout the billing cycle.
2. The moment a customer crosses 80% of their allotment, an automatic text goes out letting them know how much they have left and how to add more before they run out.
3. If usage reaches 100%, a second warning fires immediately with clear options to upgrade the plan or purchase a data add-on, before the customer is hit with a surprise overage charge on their next bill.
4. Customers who upgrade or add data through the warning message have that change applied to their account automatically without a call to customer service.
5. The usage analytics manager sees overage warning volume and upgrade conversion by plan tier, turning what used to be a source of billing complaints into a self-service upsell opportunity.
