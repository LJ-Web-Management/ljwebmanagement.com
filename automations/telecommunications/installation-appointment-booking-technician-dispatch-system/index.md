How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new service order is created, this automation checks technician availability by geographic zone, books the installation appointment in the scheduling system, sends the customer a confirmation text with the technician's name and arrival window, and dispatches the job details to the assigned technician's mobile device.
2. The day before the appointment, both the customer and the technician receive a reminder.
3. When the technician is en route, the customer receives an automatic "on the way" update with a real-time ETA link.
4. Rescheduled appointments are automatically re-offered to the next available technician rather than falling into a manual rebooking queue.
