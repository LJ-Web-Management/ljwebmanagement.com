How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment the network monitoring platform flags an outage in a specific service area, this automation cross-references the affected node or circuit against the customer address database and instantly identifies every account inside the impacted radius.
2. Within minutes, affected customers receive a text and email notification acknowledging the outage and giving an estimated restoration time pulled directly from the NOC's ticket.
3. As field crews update the ticket with a revised estimate, every notified customer automatically receives an updated message rather than being left on the original estimate.
4. Once the ticket is closed and service is confirmed restored, a final all-clear message goes out to the same list.
5. The customer experience manager no longer fields hundreds of "when will this be fixed" calls during an outage, because customers already know before they pick up the phone.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a customer experience manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.
