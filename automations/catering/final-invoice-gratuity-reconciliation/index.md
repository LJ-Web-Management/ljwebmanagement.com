## What this automation does, step by step

1. After an event, this automation automatically reconciles the final headcount and any day-of add-ons against the original contract, generates the final invoice reflecting the true totals, and processes it.
2. A manual reconciliation that often happens days after the event, when details are half-forgotten, becomes immediate and accurate.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a billing coordinator remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
