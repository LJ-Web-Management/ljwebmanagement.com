## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an inquiry comes in with a guest count, date, and event type, this automation automatically generates a starter proposal with menu package options and pricing based on those details.
2. The prospect gets something concrete within minutes instead of waiting for a callback to even find out a ballpark price.

Tools it connects

- A catering CRM (Total Party Planner or Caterease)
- Zapier or Make.com
- Email

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a sales coordinator handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
