## Catering Companies automation questions

**What catering workflows can be automated?**

The 5 catering automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, lead follow-up & sales, and hr, hiring & team management, among other areas, for roles like Sales Coordinator, Event Coordinator, and Kitchen Manager. Popular starting points include [Event Inquiry-to-Proposal Automation](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/catering/event-inquiry-to-proposal-automation/), [Headcount & Dietary Restriction Finalization Reminder](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/catering/headcount-dietary-restriction-finalization-reminder/), [Ingredient Ordering by Event Calendar](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/catering/ingredient-ordering-by-event-calendar/).

**Do I need to replace my current catering software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in catering automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, and QuickBooks, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a catering automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
