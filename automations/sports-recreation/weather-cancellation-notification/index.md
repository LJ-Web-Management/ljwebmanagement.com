How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When weather conditions meet the facility's own cancellation criteria, checked automatically against a live forecast, this automation automatically notifies every affected team and family via text before anyone heads to the field.
2. Families stop finding out a game is cancelled only after they've already driven to an empty, rained-out field.
