How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a player registers for a league, this automation automatically collects their info and stated preferences, places them onto a team roster based on the league's own rules for age, skill level, and requested teammates, and sends the player their team assignment, schedule, and coach's contact info.
2. What used to be a manual draft-and-assign process handled over a long evening with spreadsheets now runs itself as registrations come in.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a league coordinator, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
