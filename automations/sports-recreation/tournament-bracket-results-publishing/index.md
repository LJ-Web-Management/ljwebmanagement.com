How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As game results are entered by staff or referees, this automation automatically updates the tournament bracket in real time and publishes the results to the facility's website and social channels.
2. Someone no longer has to manually update a printed bracket taped to a wall while parents crowd around asking who plays next.

Tools it connects

- A bracket management tool (Tourney Machine or similar)
- Zapier or Make.com
- A website CMS
- Buffer or social scheduling tool

Using different software? We build around what you already have, so you never have to switch tools to automate.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a tournament director remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
