How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When registration opens, the online form captures team information, roster details, division preference, and payment, and the system automatically places the team in the correct division based on age and skill level, sends a registration confirmation with what to expect next, and issues the registration invoice.
2. Once all registrations close, the schedule is built based on team counts and field availability, and each team's coach receives their complete season schedule via email with calendar links for every game.
3. Reminder texts go to coaches and registered contacts 48 hours before each game, and game cancellations due to weather trigger automatic rescheduling notifications to all affected teams simultaneously.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a youth sports league director has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
