How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After each practice or game, the coach submits a brief performance log for each athlete using a simple mobile form (effort, technical execution, areas to address) and the system compiles individual athlete reports that are automatically shared with parents on a weekly basis.
2. Parents see their child's progress over time in a format that is informative without being overwhelming, and the coach builds a documented performance record without spending hours writing individual parent emails.
3. Athletes who miss practices without communication receive an automatic check-in to the parent within 24 hours, and those who hit a milestone (a personal best, a skills certification, a leadership recognition) receive a personalized congratulations message from the coach automatically.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a sports coach has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
