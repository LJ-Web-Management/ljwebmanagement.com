How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When program registration opens (swim lessons, adult fitness classes, youth basketball) enrollment is captured through the online portal and capacity is tracked in real time.
2. When a class fills, additional sign-ups are added to the waitlist automatically and the registrant is notified of their waitlist position.
3. The moment a spot opens due to a cancellation or withdrawal, the first person on the waitlist is automatically texted with a 24-hour window to claim the spot before it moves to the next person.
4. The recreation center director sees current enrollment across all programs versus capacity targets in a single dashboard, making it easy to identify underpopulated programs that need a promotional push and overpopulated programs that need a second section.
