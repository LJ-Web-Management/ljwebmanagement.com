## What this automation does, step by step

1. As internal staff and outside groups request to book fields, courts, or rooms, this automation automatically checks each request against the master facility calendar and instantly confirms or rejects it based on real-time availability.
2. The double-booking that happens when scheduling is spread across multiple spreadsheets and a shared inbox stops happening entirely.
