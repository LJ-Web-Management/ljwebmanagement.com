How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Before each season, this automation sends every athlete and parent a checklist of required eligibility documents (physical form, concussion training completion, emergency contact form, and signed code of conduct) with a secure upload portal and a deadline keyed to the first day of tryouts.
2. The athletic director sees a real-time eligibility dashboard showing which athletes are cleared, which are missing specific documents, and which have not yet initiated their submission.
3. Any athlete attempting to participate with incomplete eligibility documentation triggers an automatic hold notification to the head coach before the athlete steps on the field, protecting the school's compliance and the athlete from participating with an undisclosed health risk.
