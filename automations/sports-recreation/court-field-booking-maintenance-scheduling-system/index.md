How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Teams and individual players book facility time through an online portal that shows real-time availability across all courts, fields, and training spaces, and confirmed bookings immediately block the calendar and send a confirmation with access codes or check-in instructions.
2. When a court is vacated, a quick-inspection prompt goes to the on-duty staff via text to confirm the space is clean and equipment is in place before the next booking.
3. Any facility damage or maintenance need reported by a user or staff member automatically creates a work order with priority level, assigned maintenance staff, and an estimated resolution timeline, keeping the facility's condition from degrading silently between scheduled maintenance cycles.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a sports facility manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
