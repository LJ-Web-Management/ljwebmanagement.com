How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Throughout the day, caregivers log short notes on each resident or child, meals eaten, activities joined, nap times, and any mood observations, directly into the care app as part of their normal routine.
2. At a set time each evening, this automation compiles that day's entries for each individual into a short, warm, personalized update and sends it automatically to the family's preferred contact, no staff member spending 20 minutes at the end of a shift writing up 30 separate messages.
3. Families consistently rank this kind of daily visibility as one of the top reasons they trust a facility, and it happens without adding a single task to the care team's plate.
4. Any day with no notable entries logged still generates a simple "quiet, comfortable day" message so families never wonder if they were forgotten.
