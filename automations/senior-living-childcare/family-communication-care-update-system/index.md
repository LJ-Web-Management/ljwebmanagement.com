How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Family members of residents receive an automated weekly update summarizing notable activities, wellness check-ins, and any care plan changes, keeping loved ones informed without requiring the care team to field individual phone calls.
2. When a significant health event occurs, the primary family contact is notified within the hour via text and email, with a follow-up call task assigned to the care coordinator.
3. Prospective residents and their families in the inquiry pipeline receive a nurture sequence with community highlights, pricing, and a tour invitation, filling the census pipeline while the admissions team focuses on tours and transitions.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a senior living administrator is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
