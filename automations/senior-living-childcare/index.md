**What senior living and childcare workflows can be automated?**

The 10 senior living and childcare automations in our catalog cover compliance, documents & deadlines, customer service & communication, and billing, payments & finance, among other areas, for roles like Senior Living Administrator, Childcare Center Director, and Home Care Agency Owner. Popular starting points include [Family Communication & Care Update System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/senior-living-childcare/family-communication-care-update-system/), [Parent Communication & Tuition Billing System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/senior-living-childcare/parent-communication-tuition-billing-system/), [Caregiver-Client Matching & Schedule Management System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/senior-living-childcare/caregiver-client-matching-schedule-management-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current senior living and childcare software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in senior living and childcare automations are Twilio, Google Sheets, Mailchimp, and Jotform, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a senior living and childcare automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
