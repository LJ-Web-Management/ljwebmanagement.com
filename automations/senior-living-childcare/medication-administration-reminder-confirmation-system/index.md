How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Tied directly to each resident's medication administration record, this automation sends the assigned caregiver a reminder at the exact time a dose is due, whether that's a 7 a.m. blood pressure medication or an as-needed pain management dose ordered by a physician.
2. The caregiver must tap a confirmation once the medication is administered, and if that confirmation doesn't come through within 15 minutes of the scheduled time, the director of nursing is alerted immediately so the miss can be caught and corrected before it becomes a pattern.
3. A daily medication compliance summary is compiled automatically for every shift, showing on-time administration rates by caregiver and by resident.
4. What used to rely entirely on a caregiver's memory during a busy shift now has a second set of eyes watching every dose.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a director of nursing spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out

- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
