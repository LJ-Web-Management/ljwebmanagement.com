How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Families who join a childcare or senior living waitlist are immediately enrolled in a periodic update sequence letting them know their approximate position and any changes to the expected timeline, keeping them warm instead of wondering if they've been forgotten for six months.
2. The moment a spot opens, whether from a graduation, a move, or a cancellation, this automation identifies the next eligible family on the list and sends them a notification with a 48-hour, time-limited link to confirm enrollment before the offer passes to the next family.
3. Confirmed families are automatically moved into the onboarding workflow, and the waitlist re-sorts itself without anyone manually updating a spreadsheet.
4. Facilities running this system typically fill an open spot within days instead of the weeks it used to take working the list by hand.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, an enrollment coordinator has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
