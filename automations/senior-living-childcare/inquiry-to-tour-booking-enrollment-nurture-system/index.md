How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Families who reach out via the website, Google, or social media are immediately enrolled in a nurture sequence, a warm welcome email with a center overview video, a text message from the director within the hour, and a self-scheduling link for a center tour.
2. Families who schedule a tour receive a preparation email with what to expect, and those who visit but don't enroll within two weeks receive a follow-up sequence highlighting availability, testimonials, and any current enrollment incentives.
3. The marketing manager tracks enrollment pipeline conversion rates on a live dashboard rather than piecing together data from three separate tools.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a childcare marketing manager, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry

- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
