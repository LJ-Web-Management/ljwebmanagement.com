How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new client is onboarded, this automation matches them to an available caregiver based on skills, certifications, availability, geographic proximity, and client preferences, presenting the agency owner with a ranked shortlist rather than a manual search through a roster spreadsheet.
2. The confirmed schedule is immediately communicated to both the client family and the caregiver via text, and the caregiver receives a shift reminder 2 hours before each visit.
3. Missed clock-ins trigger an immediate alert to the scheduling manager so no client is ever left unattended without the office knowing.
