How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Parents receive automated daily updates from teachers via the parent app, and the center director's weekly newsletter is drafted from a simple form the director fills out on Friday and sent automatically to all enrolled families.
2. Tuition invoices are generated and sent automatically on the first of each month, with late payment reminders sent at 7 and 14 days overdue.
3. When a spot opens on the waitlist, the next family is immediately notified with a 48-hour enrollment hold, filling vacancies without the director spending the morning on the phone.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a childcare center director has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
