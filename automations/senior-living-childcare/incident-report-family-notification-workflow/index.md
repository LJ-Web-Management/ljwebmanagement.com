How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a staff member logs any incident, a fall, a minor injury, a behavioral episode, this automation immediately alerts the affected resident or child's family with the relevant details and the specific next steps from the individual's care plan, so the family hears it from the facility within the hour rather than finding out secondhand.
2. A separate, parallel record is created for internal compliance purposes, timestamped, assigned an incident category, and routed to the compliance manager for review, entirely independent of the family-facing notification.
3. Incidents that meet a state reporting threshold automatically flag the compliance manager with the applicable regulatory deadline attached.
4. Families get faster, more consistent communication, and the facility gets a compliance trail that doesn't depend on someone remembering to file the paperwork later.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a compliance manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
