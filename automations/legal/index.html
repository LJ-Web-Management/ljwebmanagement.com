    FAQ

    Legal automation questions

    What law firm workflows can be automated?

    The 52 law firm automations in our catalog cover compliance, documents & deadlines, billing, payments & finance, and lead follow-up & sales, among other areas, for roles like Client Services Associate, Legal Marketing Manager, and Intake Coordinator. Popular starting points include Matter Profitability & Unbilled Time Alert System, Discovery Request Tracker & Response Deadline System, Vendor Invoice Approval & Payment Scheduling System.

    Do I need to replace my current law firm software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in law firm automations are Clio, Slack, Twilio, and Google Sheets, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a law firm automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
