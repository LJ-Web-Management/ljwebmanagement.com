How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every time a case's status field changes inside the practice management system, whether it moves from "filed" to "discovery" or "in negotiation" to "settled," the automation automatically translates the internal status code into a plain-language update and pushes it to the client through the Clio client portal and a follow-up email.
2. A Zapier integration watches for status-field changes in MyCase and fires the notification within minutes rather than waiting for a staff member to remember to update the client, and clients can opt into SMS delivery through Twilio for faster visibility on their phone.
3. Because clients can see progress in real time instead of wondering, the client services associate's inbound "what's happening with my case" calls typically drop by more than half, freeing several hours a week that used to go to status-check phone calls.
