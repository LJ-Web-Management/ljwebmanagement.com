How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation continuously monitors each client's trust or retainer balance inside the billing platform, and the moment a balance drops below a firm-defined threshold, it automatically notifies both the billing coordinator and the client simultaneously: the client through a plain-language email explaining the balance and a secure link to replenish it, and the billing coordinator through a Slack alert flagging the account for follow-up if the client hasn't responded within a few days.
2. Because the notification is automatic and consistent for every client, no one on staff has to initiate the sometimes-awkward conversation about needing more money, and clients get advance notice before their balance hits zero and work has to pause.
3. Firms running this see far fewer stalled matters caused by a depleted retainer, and billing staff report the low-balance conversation becoming a non-issue instead of a recurring friction point.
