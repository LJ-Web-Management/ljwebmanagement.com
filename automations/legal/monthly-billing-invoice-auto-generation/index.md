How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Throughout the month, as attorneys log billable time entries directly in Clio or TimeSolv, the automation automatically compiles every entry tied to a matter into a draft invoice on a set billing cycle rather than waiting for someone to manually pull time records at month end.
2. Draft invoices route automatically to the billing manager in QuickBooks Online for a final review, with any entry missing a description or exceeding a flagged hourly threshold highlighted for a second look before it goes out.
3. Once approved, invoices send automatically to clients by email with a secure payment link, and unpaid invoices past 15 days automatically trigger a polite follow-up reminder.
4. What used to take the billing manager a day and a half of manual compiling each month now takes under an hour of review, and invoices consistently go out on the same date every cycle instead of drifting later each month.
