How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a matter moves through its key stages, filing, discovery, mediation, settlement, or a trial date being set, this automation fires a plain-language status update text to the client the moment the matter's status field changes in the practice management system.
2. Each update is pulled from a pre-approved template library so the language stays consistent and nothing client-facing goes out without review.
3. Clients can reply with a quick question that gets routed straight to the client relations coordinator instead of leaving a voicemail that takes two days to return.
4. Firms running this typically see "any updates?" calls drop by more than half, because clients stop feeling like they have to call just to find out their case still exists.
