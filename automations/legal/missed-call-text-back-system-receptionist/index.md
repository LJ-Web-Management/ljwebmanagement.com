How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an inbound call to the firm goes unanswered because every line is busy or it's after hours, the phone system automatically fires a webhook that triggers a Zapier workflow to send the caller a text message within seconds through Twilio, apologizing for missing the call and offering a Calendly link to book a consultation directly.
2. The text also gives callers the option to reply with their legal issue in a few words, which automatically creates a lead record in Clio Grow tagged by practice area for the intake team to review first thing.
3. The receptionist gets a morning Slack digest of every missed call and how each one was handled overnight.
4. Firms running this recover leads that would otherwise call a competitor while waiting on hold, and it's common to convert 15 to 20 percent of missed calls into booked consultations that never would have called back.

Manually, a receptionist is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.
