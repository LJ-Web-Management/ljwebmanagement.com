How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every call that rings through to voicemail, whether the attorney is in court, in a deposition, or simply on another line, triggers an automatic text within 60 seconds thanking the caller and asking two quick questions: what kind of legal matter it's regarding and the best time to reach them back.
2. The caller's reply is captured automatically as a new lead card with their answers already filled in, so the attorney opens a ready-to-call list instead of a string of unlabeled voicemails at the end of the day.
3. For a solo or small practice without a receptionist covering every line, this alone recovers callers who would otherwise hang up and call the next firm on their search results.
4. Practices using this typically convert a meaningfully higher share of missed calls into booked consultations simply because someone, or something, answered within a minute.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a solo practice attorney has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
