How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Former clients whose matters closed more than six months earlier are automatically enrolled in a periodic check-in sequence in Mailchimp, receiving a brief, genuinely useful email every quarter: a plain-language update on a relevant law change, a seasonal legal checklist, or a simple "how's everything going" note that isn't a sales pitch.
2. A Zapier workflow pulls the closed-matter list from Clio automatically so the client retention specialist never has to manually build or maintain the list.
3. Clients who reply or click into content are automatically flagged in the CRM for a personal follow-up rather than staying in the automated sequence.
4. Staying visible in a past client's inbox for years after their case closes, instead of disappearing the moment the invoice is paid, measurably increases referral volume and repeat business, since most people simply forget a firm exists until reminded.
