How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Existing clients automatically receive information about a newly added practice area that's genuinely relevant to their profile, estate planning clients being told about a new business law offering, for example, instead of a firm's newest service expansion going unnoticed by the very client base most likely to need it.
2. The legal marketing manager can target the message by client type rather than blasting every client with every announcement, and a firm's most reliable source of new-practice-area business is consistently existing clients who simply didn't know the firm now handled that kind of matter.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Manually, a legal marketing manager is stuck doing repetitive follow-ups and data entry that could otherwise run quietly in the background. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
