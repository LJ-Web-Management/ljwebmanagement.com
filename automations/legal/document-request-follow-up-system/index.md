How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a client is asked for documents, whether it's financial records for a divorce, medical bills for a personal injury claim, or signed affidavits, this automation logs the outstanding request and starts a follow-up sequence: a reminder text at 72 hours, a second reminder with a direct secure upload link at 5 days, and an escalation to the litigation support specialist for a personal call at 8 days if nothing has arrived.
2. Every uploaded document is automatically matched to the open request and marked complete, so staff spend no time cross-checking what's been received against what's still owed.
3. Firms typically cut the average time to collect a full document set by several days simply by removing the guesswork of who's still waiting on what.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a litigation support specialist spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
