How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every missed call to the firm's main line triggers an automatic text back to the caller within two minutes, acknowledging the call and asking what they're calling about, converting a missed call into a text conversation the intake specialist can respond to when available.
2. Calls and web form submissions that come in after hours are logged, auto-acknowledged with a clear response time expectation, and added to the intake specialist's morning queue with full context.
3. Firms that adopt this system stop losing prospective clients to competitors who answered first, because the firm's first response now goes out in seconds regardless of the hour.
