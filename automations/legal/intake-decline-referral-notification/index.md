How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a prospective matter is declined during the conflict check or initial evaluation, the automation automatically sends a polite decline notice along with alternative referral suggestions to the prospect, instead of a declined inquiry simply going unanswered, which reflects poorly on the firm even when taking the case genuinely wasn't possible.
2. The intake coordinator no longer has to draft the same decline email from scratch for every rejected inquiry, and prospects who receive a helpful referral instead of silence often remember the firm favorably enough to refer someone else down the road.
