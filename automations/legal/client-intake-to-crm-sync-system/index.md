How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new client submits the firm's web intake form, this automation instantly creates a matching record in the CRM with their contact information, case type, referral source, and a preliminary conflict-check flag based on opposing party names entered on the form.
2. The new client coordinator receives a notification the moment a submission needs manual conflict review, while clean submissions move straight into the assigned attorney's queue with no re-typing required.
3. Every field maps automatically from the web form to the CRM, so what a prospective client types once is the same data the firm works from through the entire matter.
4. Firms using this eliminate the lag, and the transcription errors, that come from an intake specialist retyping form submissions into the CRM by hand.
