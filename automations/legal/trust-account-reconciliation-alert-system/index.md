How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation runs a daily reconciliation check comparing trust account activity in the firm's accounting software against the ledger balances tracked in Clio, and the moment a discrepancy appears, whether it's a mismatched deposit or a disbursement that doesn't tie back to a client ledger, it automatically flags the account and alerts the trust accounting manager through Slack rather than waiting for the scheduled monthly reconciliation to catch it.
2. Each alert includes the specific matter, the amount involved, and the date of the discrepancy so it can be investigated immediately instead of requiring a manual line-by-line audit.
3. Resolved discrepancies are logged automatically in a Google Sheets audit trail for compliance records.
4. Catching trust accounting errors within a day instead of a month materially reduces the firm's exposure to a bar compliance violation, one of the most serious risks a firm can carry.

### Without automation

Without this system, a trust accounting manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.
