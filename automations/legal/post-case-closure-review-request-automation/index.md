How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a matter is marked closed and successfully resolved in Clio, the automation automatically waits a set number of days, timed to when client satisfaction is highest, before sending a review request by email and text through Twilio.
2. The request links directly to the firm's Google or Avvo review page and avoids referencing any case-specific outcome details to stay within bar advertising guidelines.
3. Responses are automatically filtered: clients who indicate a positive experience get routed straight to the public review link, while anyone who signals frustration is routed privately to the marketing coordinator for a follow-up call instead of a public post.
4. Every request and outcome logs to a Google Sheets tracker so the firm can measure response rates over time.
5. Firms running this consistently see review volume roughly triple compared to relying on staff to remember to ask.
