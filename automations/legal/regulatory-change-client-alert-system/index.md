How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation monitors legal and regulatory updates relevant to the firm's practice areas through a legal research alert service, and when a meaningful change is identified, it automatically drafts a plain-language summary explaining what changed and flags it for the regulatory affairs attorney to review and approve before it goes out.
2. Once approved, the automation automatically identifies which clients in Clio are affected based on industry or matter type and sends each one a tailored email through Mailchimp explaining exactly how the change impacts their business, along with a suggested next step or a prompt to schedule a call.
3. Every alert and its recipient list logs automatically for compliance records.
4. Clients get advance notice of regulatory changes that affect them directly instead of finding out after the fact, positioning the firm as the source that caught it first.

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a regulatory affairs attorney, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.
