How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every email and call log with opposing counsel is automatically captured and timestamped directly into the case file, creating a reliable, contemporaneous record without the litigation paralegal manually copying emails over or reconstructing a call log from memory after the fact.
2. Having a complete, automatically maintained communication history becomes invaluable the moment a dispute arises over what was actually said or agreed to, and attorneys stop having to dig through their own inbox trying to piece together a timeline that the system already kept for them.
