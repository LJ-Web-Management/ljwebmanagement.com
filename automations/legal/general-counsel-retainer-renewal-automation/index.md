How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For clients on an ongoing general counsel retainer, the automation automatically tracks each retainer's term end date in Clio and, 60 days ahead of expiration, generates a renewal notice along with an updated fee agreement reflecting any rate changes for the general counsel services coordinator to review before it goes to the client.
2. The renewal notice sends automatically through DocuSign for e-signature, with the same escalating reminder chain used for engagement letters if it goes unsigned.
3. Clients who don't respond within 30 days of the term end automatically trigger a personal outreach task assigned to the managing attorney rather than letting the retainer silently lapse.
4. Because renewals surface well ahead of the deadline instead of being noticed only when a client stops paying, the firm protects recurring general counsel revenue and rarely loses a retainer relationship to simple oversight.
