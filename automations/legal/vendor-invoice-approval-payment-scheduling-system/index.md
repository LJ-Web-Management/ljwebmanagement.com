How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every vendor invoice received by the firm (court reporter, process server, expert witness, office supplies) is automatically routed to the appropriate matter or overhead code, sent to the office manager for one-click approval, and queued for payment on the next scheduled payment run.
2. Invoices that sit unapproved for more than five business days trigger an escalation.
3. At month end, an expense summary by matter and by vendor category is generated automatically, giving the office manager the data needed for budget reviews without exporting anything manually from any system.
