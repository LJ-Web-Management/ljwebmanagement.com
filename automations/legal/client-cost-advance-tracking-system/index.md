How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every cost the firm advances on a client's behalf, filing fees, expert witness retainers, medical record requests, is automatically logged against the matter the moment it's entered into the billing system in Clio or TimeSolv, tagged by category and date so nothing gets buried in a general expense account.
2. The automation maintains a running advanced-costs total per matter and automatically flags it for reimbursement the moment the matter is marked settled or resolved, notifying the costs and disbursements clerk through Slack rather than requiring someone to dig back through months of expense entries.
3. A monthly Google Sheets report shows total costs currently advanced across all open matters, giving the firm visibility into outstanding exposure.
4. Firms running this reliably recover advanced costs at settlement instead of writing off forgotten expenses, which on caseloads with dozens of active matters can add up to real money left on the table.
