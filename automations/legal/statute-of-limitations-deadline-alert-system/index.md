How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a key date, whether a statute of limitations, a filing deadline, or a response due date, is entered into the case management system for a matter, the automation automatically cross-references it against the firm's docketing calendar in Clio and schedules a chain of advance warnings at 30, 14, 7, and 1 day out.
2. Alerts go simultaneously to the assigned attorney and the docketing clerk through both email and a shared Google Calendar entry, and any deadline within 48 hours that hasn't been marked complete triggers an urgent Slack alert to the managing partner as a backstop.
3. Because the system never forgets a date the way an overloaded calendar sometimes does, firms running this build a second layer of protection against the single costliest mistake a firm can make: missing a filing deadline or letting a statute run.
