How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a consultation is booked on Calendly, a Zapier workflow automatically sends the client a secure intake questionnaire through Jotform along with a personalized checklist of required documents, from photo ID to insurance declarations pages, based on the practice area selected at booking.
2. Completed responses and uploaded files sync directly into the client's matter file in Clio, so the case manager can confirm everything is in before the first meeting instead of chasing paperwork afterward.
3. Clients who haven't completed the questionnaire 48 hours before their appointment automatically receive a reminder text through Twilio, and anything still outstanding the morning of triggers an internal Slack alert to the case manager.
4. Attorneys walk into first meetings with signed intake forms, ID copies, and supporting documents already reviewed, cutting roughly 20 minutes of paperwork chasing out of every new matter.
