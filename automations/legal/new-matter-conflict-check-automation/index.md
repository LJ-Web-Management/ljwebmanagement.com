How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The instant a new matter is entered into the intake system through a Jotform submission or directly in Clio Grow, the automation automatically cross-references every party name, opposing party, and related entity against the firm's existing and former client database before the matter can move forward to formal acceptance.
2. Any potential match automatically routes to conflicts counsel through a Slack alert with the matching records attached for manual review, and the new matter is held in a "pending conflict check" status in the practice management system until it's cleared.
3. Clean checks automatically clear the matter to proceed to engagement letter generation without any manual gatekeeping step.
4. Running a conflict check on every single matter automatically, instead of relying on someone to remember to run it before accepting a new client, closes off one of the more common and costly compliance gaps at growing firms.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a conflicts counsel, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
