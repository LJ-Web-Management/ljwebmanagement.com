How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a prospect completes a paid or free consultation in Clio Grow but doesn't sign an engagement letter within a set window, the automation automatically enrolls them in a long-term nurture sequence in ActiveCampaign built around their specific practice area rather than letting the lead go cold.
2. The sequence mixes educational content, relevant case results, and periodic soft check-in emails spaced out over several months, and any prospect who clicks back into pricing or scheduling content automatically gets flagged to the business development coordinator for a personal follow-up call.
3. Leads that re-engage are automatically re-tagged as active in the CRM so they don't fall back into the general nurture pool.
4. Firms running this convert a meaningful share of "not now" consultations into signed clients months later, capturing revenue that used to disappear the moment a prospect said they needed to think about it.
