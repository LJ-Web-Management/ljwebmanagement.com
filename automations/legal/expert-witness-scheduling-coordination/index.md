How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation automatically coordinates and confirms an expert witness's availability against upcoming case deadlines and the firm's own calendar, reducing the scheduling conflicts that used to surface only after a trial date was already locked in and an expert's calendar suddenly didn't cooperate.
2. The trial paralegal gets a single view showing every expert's confirmed availability across every active matter, instead of tracking each one separately by email, and depositions and trial testimony get scheduled with real confidence instead of a tentative date that falls apart two weeks before it matters.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a trial paralegal spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
