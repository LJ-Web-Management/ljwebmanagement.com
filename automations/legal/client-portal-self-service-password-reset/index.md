How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Clients who forget their portal password can automatically reset it themselves through a secure automated flow, cutting down on support calls that used to require a client services associate to manually verify identity and reset access every single time someone got locked out.
2. The self-service flow follows the same identity verification standard every time, keeping the process secure without adding staff workload, and clients get back into their portal in minutes instead of waiting for office hours and a callback just to view a document they need right now.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a client services associate spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
