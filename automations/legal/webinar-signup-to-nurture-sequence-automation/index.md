How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Anyone who registers for a free legal webinar or seminar through the firm's registration page in Jotform is automatically added to a topic-specific nurture sequence in ActiveCampaign the moment they sign up, no manual list-building required.
2. Attendees who actually show up receive a different follow-up track than no-shows, including a replay link and a related case-study email, while no-shows get a shorter re-engagement sequence with a lighter offer to book a consultation.
3. A Zapier workflow syncs every registrant into Clio Grow as a tagged lead so the events and marketing coordinator can see webinar-sourced leads separately from other channels.
4. Prospects who aren't ready to hire an attorney the day of the webinar stay warm through months of relevant content instead of falling off the radar, and firms typically see a meaningful share of webinar attendees convert to consultations weeks or months later.
