How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new matter is entered as a specific case type, the automation automatically applies a pre-built bundle of the relevant statutory deadlines directly to the case calendar, rather than the docketing clerk manually researching and entering each applicable deadline one at a time for every new matter that comes in.
2. Every case of a given type starts with the exact same complete, correct set of deadlines already calendared from day one, removing the single most dangerous failure point in litigation practice, a deadline that was simply never entered in the first place.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a docketing clerk, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
