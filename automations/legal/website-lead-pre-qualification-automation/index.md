How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a visitor submits the firm's website contact form in Jotform or Gravity Forms, the automation automatically walks them through a short series of qualifying questions, case type, rough timeline, whether they're already represented by another attorney, before the submission ever reaches a staff member, using a conversational form flow rather than a flat contact form.
2. Based on the answers, the lead is automatically scored and tagged by practice area and urgency in Clio Grow, and high-priority matters, like an approaching statute of limitations or an active emergency, route straight to the digital intake specialist with a Slack alert instead of sitting in a general queue.
3. Lower-priority or non-matching leads are automatically routed to the appropriate nurture sequence instead of consuming staff time.
4. Intake staff know the case type and urgency before they ever pick up the phone, and the firm spends less time on unqualified inquiries.
