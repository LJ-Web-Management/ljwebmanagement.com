How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a discovery request is received and logged, this automation calculates the response deadline based on the applicable rules (federal, state, or local) creates a series of internal preparation deadlines working backward from that date, and assigns tasks to the responsible attorney and paralegal with escalating reminders.
2. Any task that misses its internal deadline triggers an immediate flag to the supervising attorney.
3. Litigation is managed to a calendar, not to whoever remembered to check their inbox that morning.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a paralegal has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
