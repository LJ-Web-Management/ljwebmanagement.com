How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every time a new client lists a referral source during intake, the system logs the referring attorney, accountant, or business contact, updates their referral count in the CRM, and automatically triggers a personalized thank-you email from the firm.
2. Referral partners who haven't sent a case in 90 days receive a gentle check-in from the marketing manager, turning the referral network from a mental note into a systematically managed revenue channel.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a legal marketing manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
