How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The automation internally flags when a paralegal's assigned task load exceeds a healthy capacity threshold, prompting the case manager to reassign work before deadlines are put at risk, rather than an overloaded paralegal quietly falling behind until a missed task surfaces the problem for everyone at once.
2. Workload is calculated automatically from every open task and its due date across every active matter, giving a far more accurate picture than a manager's general impression of who seems swamped, and reassignments happen calmly with days of lead time instead of in a last-minute scramble.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a case manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation
