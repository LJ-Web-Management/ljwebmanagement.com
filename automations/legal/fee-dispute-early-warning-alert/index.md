How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Late or partially disputed invoice payments automatically flag the account for a proactive conversation with the client before the situation grows into a larger, harder-to-resolve fee dispute, instead of a billing manager only noticing a pattern of pushback after several invoices have already gone unpaid.
2. Catching the first sign of friction early, while it's still one confused line item rather than an entire relationship gone sour, resolves the vast majority of disputes with a short clarifying call instead of an awkward, drawn-out collections conversation months later.
