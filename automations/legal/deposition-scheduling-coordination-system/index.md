How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a deposition needs to be scheduled, the automation automatically checks availability across every party's calendar, opposing counsel, the witness, and the court reporter, using Calendly synced to Google Calendar, and proposes a shortlist of dates instead of the litigation paralegal trading emails back and forth with three or four different offices.
2. Once all parties confirm a date, the automation automatically sends formal confirmations, books the court reporter and any videographer needed, and reserves a conference room if the deposition is in-office.
3. Reminder texts through Twilio go out to the client and attorney the day before, and any last-minute reschedule automatically notifies every party and re-triggers the confirmation chain.
4. What used to take days of back-and-forth email now typically resolves within a single afternoon, and depositions rarely get rescheduled due to a calendar mix-up.
