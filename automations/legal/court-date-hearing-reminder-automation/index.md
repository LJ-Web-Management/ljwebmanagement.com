How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As soon as a hearing or court date is entered into the case management system, the automation automatically schedules a reminder sequence to both the client and the assigned trial paralegal at two weeks, three days, and the morning of the appearance, delivered by email and text through Twilio.
2. Each reminder automatically pulls in the specific logistics for that hearing, courtroom number, judge's name, parking instructions, and a note on what documents or evidence to bring, so nothing has to be typed manually every time.
3. If a court date changes in Clio, the entire reminder chain automatically reschedules itself rather than requiring staff to catch and manually update it.
4. Clients show up prepared and on time far more consistently, and the trial paralegal is freed from manually tracking and re-sending reminders for every hearing on the firm's docket each week.
