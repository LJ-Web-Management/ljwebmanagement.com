How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new client signs an engagement letter, the automation automatically tags their matter in Clio Grow with the originating marketing channel or referral source, whether that's a Google Ads campaign, a referring attorney, or a past client, pulled from the original intake form data captured in Jotform.
2. A Zapier workflow feeds every tagged signing into a live Google Sheets or Airtable dashboard that calculates signed-client volume and revenue by source automatically, updating in real time rather than requiring someone to compile numbers at month end.
3. The business development director receives a weekly Slack summary showing which referral sources and marketing channels are actually converting into paying clients versus which ones just generate consultations that go nowhere.
4. With real signed-revenue attribution instead of a gut feeling, firms typically redirect marketing spend within a quarter toward the two or three sources actually producing business.
