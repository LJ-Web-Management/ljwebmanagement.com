How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For portrait and family photographers, this automation automatically reaches out to past clients around the one-year mark from their last session with a friendly reminder that it might be time for updated family photos.
2. Repeat bookings get generated on a predictable schedule instead of relying entirely on a past client happening to think of the studio again on their own.
