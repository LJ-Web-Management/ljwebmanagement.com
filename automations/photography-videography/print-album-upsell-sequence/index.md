How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A set number of days after gallery delivery, typically three to five, this automation automatically sends clients an email showcasing print and album options built from their own actual photos, timed for when excitement about the images is still at its peak.
2. Passive gallery viewers who'd otherwise just download files and move on convert into print and album buyers because the offer arrives at the moment they're most emotionally invested.
