How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Photography inquiries submitted through the website, Instagram DM, or Facebook are automatically captured in the CRM, acknowledged within five minutes with a warm reply and a link to view pricing and availability, and enrolled in a nurture sequence if they don't book a consultation immediately.
2. Inquiries that don't book within 48 hours receive a follow-up with a specific availability offer and a limited-time booking incentive.
3. The marketing manager sees a lead source dashboard showing exactly which channels (SEO, Instagram, referral, Google Ads) are driving inquiries, consultations, and bookings, so the advertising budget is directed toward the channels producing actual revenue rather than just traffic.
