How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a rough cut is shared with the client, this automation starts a revision clock based on the contract's revision window and sends the client a feedback form that captures timecoded notes in a structured format, eliminating the "the music feels a bit off around 1:15" email thread that makes revision cycles take twice as long.
2. When revision requests are submitted, they are automatically logged and sent to the editor as a structured list.
3. When the final approved file is delivered, a watermarked preview and a password-protected full-resolution download link are automatically generated and sent, protecting the final deliverable while making it easy for the client to share for approval before distribution.

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a videographer, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation
