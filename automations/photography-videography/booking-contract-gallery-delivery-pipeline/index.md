How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a photography inquiry is submitted through the website, the prospect receives an immediate availability confirmation, a pricing guide, and a link to book a discovery call.
2. After the call, the system sends a booking agreement and retainer invoice simultaneously, the session is not held until both are received.
3. A week before the session, the client gets a preparation guide with wardrobe tips, location details, and what to expect.
4. After the shoot, a gallery delivery notification is sent automatically when the edited gallery is ready, with a download link that expires in 30 days, triggering an extension request prompt if the client doesn't download within that window.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a photographer, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
