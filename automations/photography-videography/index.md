## Photography & Videography automation questions

**What photography and video workflows can be automated?**

The 10 photography and video automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, scheduling, reminders & dispatch, and lead follow-up & sales, among other areas, for roles like Studio Owner, Photographer, and Video Production Manager. Popular starting points include [Booking, Contract & Gallery Delivery Pipeline](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/photography-videography/booking-contract-gallery-delivery-pipeline/), [Project Brief Collection & Asset Delivery Pipeline](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/photography-videography/project-brief-collection-asset-delivery-pipeline/), [Session Booking & Pre-Shoot Questionnaire System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/photography-videography/session-booking-pre-shoot-questionnaire-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current photography and video software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in photography and video automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Jotform, and Stripe, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a photography and video automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
