How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a client inquires and selects a package, this automation automatically sends the contract for e-signature along with a deposit invoice, and only confirms the date on the studio's calendar once both are completed.
2. A date stops being held indefinitely for a client who expresses interest but never actually finalizes the booking, freeing that slot for someone ready to commit.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a studio manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
