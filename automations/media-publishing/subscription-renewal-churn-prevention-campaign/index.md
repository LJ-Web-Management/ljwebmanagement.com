How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Thirty days before a subscriber's renewal date, this automation sends a personalized renewal confirmation with a summary of what the subscription includes and any new content added in the past year.
2. Subscribers who don't renew by the expiration date receive a three-part win-back sequence over 30 days, a reminder, a testimonial-driven value email, and a final limited-time discount offer.
3. Subscribers who have not opened any content in 90 days are identified as at-risk before their renewal and receive a re-engagement campaign highlighting the content most similar to what they engaged with historically, rather than a generic blast that lands in the same inbox they've been ignoring.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a digital publisher spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
