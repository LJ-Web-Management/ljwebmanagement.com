How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The editorial calendar lives in a shared project management tool, and every assigned story automatically generates a task for the contributor with the due date, word count, style guide link, and editor contact.
2. As deadlines approach, contributors receive automated reminders at one week, three days, and the morning of their submission deadline.
3. Stories that miss their deadline trigger an immediate alert to the managing editor along with the contributor's history of on-time performance, so the editor can decide in seconds whether to chase the story or assign a replacement, rather than discovering the gap on the day the issue needs to close.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, an editor-in-chief has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
