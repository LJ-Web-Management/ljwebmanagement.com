How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a piece of content is marked approved in the editorial calendar, this automation automatically formats and schedules it for publishing across the website, social channels, and the email newsletter, each at its own individually optimized time.
2. What used to require manually copy-pasting the same piece of content across five different platforms with five different formatting quirks now happens with one approval click.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a content manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
