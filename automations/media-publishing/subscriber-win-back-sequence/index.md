How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a subscriber's engagement, opens, clicks, site visits, drops to zero for a set period, this automation automatically enrolls them in a win-back email sequence highlighting recent standout content.
2. Subscribers who still don't re-engage after the sequence are automatically removed from the active list, protecting sender reputation instead of letting a growing pool of dead addresses drag down deliverability for everyone else.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a newsletter editor, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
