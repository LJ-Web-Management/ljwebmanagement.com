How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation continuously monitors ad placements across the site and automatically alerts the ad ops team the moment fill rate for any placement drops below a set threshold.
2. An underperforming placement gets caught and addressed within a day or two instead of an entire week going by with unsold, revenue-losing ad space that no one noticed because no one was checking the dashboard.
