## Media & Publishing automation questions

**What media and publishing workflows can be automated?**

The 10 media and publishing automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, operations, inventory & vendors, and reporting & analytics, among other areas, for roles like Editor-in-Chief, Digital Publisher, and Podcast Producer. Popular starting points include [Editorial Calendar & Contributor Deadline Management System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/media-publishing/editorial-calendar-contributor-deadline-management-system/), [Subscription Renewal & Churn Prevention Campaign](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/media-publishing/subscription-renewal-churn-prevention-campaign/), [Guest Booking & Episode Production Pipeline](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/media-publishing/guest-booking-episode-production-pipeline/).

**Do I need to replace my current media and publishing software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in media and publishing automations are Mailchimp, Slack, Google Sheets, and Airtable, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a media and publishing automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
