How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every 24 hours, this automation checks performance data across the content library (page views, time on page, backlinks acquired, and social shares) and flags any piece of content that has surpassed the top-performer threshold.
2. High-performing articles trigger a repurposing workflow: a prompt to the content team to adapt the piece into a LinkedIn carousel, a short-form video script, a newsletter feature, and a lead magnet PDF.
3. Content that was high-performing six months ago but has seen traffic drop is flagged for a refresh review, because a well-ranking article that gets updated and re-promoted often outperforms a brand-new piece of content at a fraction of the production cost.
