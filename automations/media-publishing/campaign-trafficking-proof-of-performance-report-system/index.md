How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an advertising insertion order is signed, this automation creates a campaign record with all flight dates, placements, and creative specifications, sends the client a creative asset upload request with a deadline, and alerts the ad operations team to traffic the campaign on the launch date.
2. Mid-campaign, a performance snapshot is automatically delivered to the client at the halfway point.
3. When the campaign ends, a complete proof of performance report (impressions, clicks, engagement, and delivery against contracted inventory) is generated automatically and sent within 24 hours of the campaign end date, rather than becoming a two-week manual data-pull project for the ad ops team.
