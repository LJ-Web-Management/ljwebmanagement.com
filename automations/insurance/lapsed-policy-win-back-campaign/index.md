How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a policy lapses due to non-payment or non-renewal, this automation automatically enrolls the former client in a 90-day win-back sequence that offers a fresh rate re-quote and highlights whatever coverage gap they now have without a policy in force.
2. Revenue that would otherwise simply disappear from the books gets a structured, timed attempt at recovery instead of being written off the moment the policy lapses.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a retention specialist has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
