How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each week, this automation pulls production data for every agent (new policies written, premium volume, retention rate, cross-sell ratio) and updates a live scorecard that the agency owner can see at a glance.
2. Agents who are tracking below their monthly goal receive a proactive coaching prompt from the owner's calendar, and top performers are automatically recognized in the team communication channel.
3. The agency owner manages the team by data rather than instinct, and every producer knows exactly where they stand at all times.
