How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. New claims submitted through the portal are automatically assigned to the correct adjuster based on coverage type and workload, and the claimant immediately receives a confirmation with their claim number, their adjuster's name, and a document upload link for photos, estimates, and supporting materials.
2. The adjuster receives a pre-built claim file with everything collected to date, and the claimant automatically receives a status update every 72 hours until the claim is closed, eliminating the "where is my claim?" calls that consume a disproportionate amount of an adjuster's day.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a claims adjuster manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
