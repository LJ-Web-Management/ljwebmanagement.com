How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation reviews every client's current policies against a library of common coverage gaps, a homeowner's policy with no umbrella coverage, an auto policy with no renters coverage on file for a renting driver, and automatically flags the cross-sell opportunity to the agent along with a suggested talking point for the next conversation.
2. The agent stops relying on memory to catch these gaps across hundreds of accounts.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a personal lines agent has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
