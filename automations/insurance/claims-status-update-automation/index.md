How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a claim moves through each stage, filed, under review, adjuster assigned, approved, paid, this automation automatically texts and emails the policyholder a plain-language update the moment the status changes in the claims system.
2. The volume of \\"what's happening with my claim\\" calls to the office drops sharply because the customer already knows before they think to call.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a claims coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
