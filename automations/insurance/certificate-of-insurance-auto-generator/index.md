How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a commercial client requests a certificate of insurance, a routine ask from vendors and landlords, this automation automatically pulls the current policy data from the agency management system, generates the certificate in the correct standard ACORD format, and emails it directly to whoever requested it.
2. What used to be a multi-day request sitting in an account manager's inbox turns into a same-day, often same-hour, turnaround.

- A certificate-generation tool (Certificates Now or similar)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a commercial lines account manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
