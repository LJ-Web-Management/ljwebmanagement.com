How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Thirty days before each policy anniversary, the account manager receives a prompt to reach out to the client with a coverage review, and the client receives an email highlighting the value of their current coverage and any recent claims-free discount they've earned.
2. Life events tracked in the CRM (a home purchase, a new vehicle, a business expansion) trigger an immediate cross-sell alert with a suggested product and a pre-written outreach message the account manager can personalize and send in minutes rather than drafting from scratch.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an insurance account manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
