How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Clients are invited to share upcoming milestones (a marriage, a new home purchase, a new baby, a teen driver) directly through a short update form linked in every renewal and service email.
2. The moment a client logs one of these life events, the automation matches it against a library of relevant coverage triggers (umbrella liability for a new home, an added driver for a teen, life insurance for a new baby) and immediately sends the client an educational message along with a prompt for the producer to schedule a coverage review call.
3. The producer's task list updates automatically with the specific gap to address, turning a client's good news into a timely, relevant conversation instead of a missed cross-sell opportunity that surfaces only at the next renewal, if at all.
