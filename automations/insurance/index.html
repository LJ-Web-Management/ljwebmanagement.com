    FAQ

    Insurance automation questions

    What insurance agency workflows can be automated?

    The 12 insurance agency automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, lead follow-up & sales, and customer service & communication, among other areas, for roles like Insurance Agent, Claims Adjuster, and Insurance Account Manager. Popular starting points include New Prospect Intake & Instant Quote Request System, Claim Assignment, Documentation Request & Status Update System, Policy Anniversary & Cross-Sell Opportunity System.

    Do I need to replace my current insurance agency software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in insurance agency automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Slack, and Jotform, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does an insurance agency automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
