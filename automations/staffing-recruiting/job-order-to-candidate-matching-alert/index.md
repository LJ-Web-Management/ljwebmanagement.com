How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new job order is opened, this automation automatically scans the existing candidate database for people matching the required skills, location, and availability, and surfaces a ranked shortlist to the recruiter within minutes.
2. What used to be a manual search through hundreds of resumes now starts with a pre-built shortlist the recruiter can start calling immediately.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a recruiting manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Interview scheduling without email ping-pong
- New hire paperwork and account setup before day one
- Certification and license expiration alerts
- Timesheet and time-off workflows that route themselves
