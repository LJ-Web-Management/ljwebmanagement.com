How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every Friday morning, all active contractors receive an automated timesheet submission reminder with a direct link to their timesheet form.
2. Timesheets submitted by the cutoff are automatically validated against the approved schedule and flagged for any overtime requiring client approval before they are processed.
3. Timesheets not submitted by the deadline trigger an escalating reminder sequence, and the payroll coordinator receives a list of missing timesheets with enough lead time to chase them before payroll locks.
4. Approved timesheets flow automatically into the payroll platform, eliminating manual data entry and the errors that come with it at the busiest moment of every week.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a staffing payroll coordinator remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Interview scheduling without email ping-pong
- New hire paperwork and account setup before day one
- Certification and license expiration alerts
- Timesheet and time-off workflows that route themselves
