How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new job order is received from a client (via email, phone, or the client portal) this automation creates the order record in the ATS, formats the intake information into a structured job brief, sends it to the recruiting team with a notification and a deadline for first submittals, and fires a confirmation back to the client that the search has officially begun with an expected first-submittal timeline.
2. When the first candidates are submitted, the client receives an automatic notification with a portal link to review profiles and provide feedback, eliminating the delay between "we have the job order" and "the recruiter knows about it and is working it."
