How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new contractor is placed, this automation initiates the I-9 process with a digital completion link sent to the worker before their first day, monitors completion status with escalating reminders, and triggers E-Verify submission automatically upon I-9 completion.
2. Any reverification requirement for a worker with a temporary work authorization is calendared and the compliance manager receives a reminder 90 days before expiration.
3. Industry-specific certifications (OSHA 10, forklift, food handler) are tracked per worker, with renewal alerts sent before expiration.
4. A single compliance dashboard gives the compliance manager a real-time view of every worker's documentation status across the entire contractor base.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a compliance manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
