How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every open job order in the agency is tracked in real time against its fill deadline and fee value, and the agency owner receives a Monday morning digest showing orders by urgency, recruiter assignment, candidate pipeline depth, and estimated close probability.
2. Any job order that has been open for more than 14 days without a qualified submittal triggers an escalation alert, and orders approaching their client deadline with no placement imminent receive a priority flag with a suggested action.
3. The agency owner manages the revenue pipeline by data rather than intuition, and every recruiter knows exactly where their book of business stands before the week begins.
