## Staffing & Recruiting Agencies automation questions

**What staffing and recruiting workflows can be automated?**

The 10 staffing and recruiting automations in our catalog cover hr, hiring & team management, reporting & analytics, and compliance, documents & deadlines, among other areas, for roles like Agency Owner, Account Executive, and Recruiter. Popular starting points include [Client Fill Rate & Revenue Pipeline Dashboard](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/staffing-recruiting/client-fill-rate-revenue-pipeline-dashboard/), [Job Order Intake & Rapid Candidate Submittal System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/staffing-recruiting/job-order-intake-rapid-candidate-submittal-system/), [Candidate Re-Engagement & Availability Check System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/staffing-recruiting/candidate-re-engagement-availability-check-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current staffing and recruiting software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in staffing and recruiting automations are Google Sheets, Twilio, Slack, and Jotform, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a staffing and recruiting automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
