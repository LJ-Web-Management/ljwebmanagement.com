How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every candidate in the ATS who was placed more than six months ago, or who applied and wasn't placed more than 90 days ago, receives an automated check-in message asking if they are currently open to opportunities, with a one-click "yes, contact me" or "not right now" response option.
2. Those who express interest are automatically moved to an active pipeline and the recruiter is notified immediately with the candidate's profile.
3. Passive candidates who respond with "not right now" are scheduled for another automated check-in 60 days later.
4. The recruiter's active pipeline grows continuously from the existing database rather than depending entirely on new sourcing every cycle.
