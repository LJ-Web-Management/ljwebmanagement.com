## Pest Control automation questions

**What pest control workflows can be automated?**

The 5 pest control automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, compliance, documents & deadlines, and operations, inventory & vendors, among other areas, for roles like Service Coordinator, Inspector, and Compliance Manager. Popular starting points include [Recurring Treatment Scheduling & Reminder](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/pest-control/recurring-treatment-scheduling-reminder/), [Termite Inspection Follow-Up](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/pest-control/termite-inspection-follow-up/), [Technician Route & Chemical Usage Log](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/pest-control/technician-route-chemical-usage-log/).

**Do I need to replace my current pest control software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in pest control automations are Twilio, Google Calendar, and Mailchimp, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a pest control automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
