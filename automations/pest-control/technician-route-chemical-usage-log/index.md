## What this automation does, step by step

1. As technicians complete stops, this automation automatically logs the chemicals and quantities applied at each property, both for internal inventory tracking and to maintain the application records required by state pesticide regulations.
2. A compliance audit stops being a scramble to reconstruct records from memory and paper tickets.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a compliance manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
