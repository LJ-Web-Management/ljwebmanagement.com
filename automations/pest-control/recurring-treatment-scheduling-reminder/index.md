How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For customers on a quarterly or monthly treatment plan, this automation automatically schedules the next visit based on the service interval, sends the customer a reminder with the technician's arrival window, and reschedules automatically if the customer requests a change.
2. The recurring revenue base runs on a predictable cadence instead of someone manually rebooking every account by hand.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a service coordinator remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
