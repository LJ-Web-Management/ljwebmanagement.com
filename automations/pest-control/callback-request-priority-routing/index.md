How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer reports the pest problem hasn't resolved after a treatment, this automation automatically flags it as a priority callback, routes it to the original technician if available, and schedules it ahead of standard new appointments.
2. A customer with a return infestation is the highest churn risk in the pipeline, and this automation treats it that way instead of queuing it behind routine bookings.

- PestPac or Briostack

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a dispatch manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems

- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
