How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After an annual termite inspection is completed and logged, this automation automatically generates and sends the required inspection report to the customer, and to their lender or title company if the inspection is tied to a real estate transaction.
2. The report turnaround time these transactions usually require gets met without anyone manually drafting the same document from scratch each time.

Tools it connects

- PestPac or Briostack
- Zapier or Make.com
- Email

Using different software? We build around what you already have, so you never have to switch tools to automate.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on an inspector, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
