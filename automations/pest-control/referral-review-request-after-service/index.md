How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A few days after a completed service, this automation automatically sends a review request and a referral offer to the customer, timed for when the pest issue has visibly improved and satisfaction is at its peak.
2. The ask lands at the moment the customer is most likely to say yes instead of arriving as a generic follow-up weeks later.
