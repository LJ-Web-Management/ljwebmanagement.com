How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a technician marks a job complete, this automation automatically attaches photos of the completed work, generates the invoice, and sends it to the customer.
2. Commercial clients get documentation for their own records alongside the bill instead of having to request photos separately after the fact.

Tools it connects

- Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan
- Zapier or Make.com
- [QuickBooks Online](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/quickbooks/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a field technician supervisor manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
