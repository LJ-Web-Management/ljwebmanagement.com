## What this automation does, step by step

1. For unaccepted security system installation quotes, this automation automatically follows up at set intervals, three days, seven days, fourteen days, with the prospect.
2. These are typically higher-consideration purchases that benefit from a few gentle nudges rather than a single quote and silence, and the follow-up happens without a salesperson having to remember every open quote.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a sales manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
