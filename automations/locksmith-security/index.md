## Locksmith & Security Services automation questions

**What locksmith and security workflows can be automated?**

The 5 locksmith and security automations in our catalog cover customer service & communication, scheduling, reminders & dispatch, and compliance, documents & deadlines, among other areas, for roles like Dispatcher, Account Manager, and Security Consultant. Popular starting points include [Emergency Dispatch & ETA Texting](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/locksmith-security/emergency-dispatch-eta-texting/), [Recurring Maintenance Contract Scheduling](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/locksmith-security/recurring-maintenance-contract-scheduling/), [Key/Access Credential Audit Reminder](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/locksmith-security/key-access-credential-audit-reminder/).

**Do I need to replace my current locksmith and security software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in locksmith and security automations are Twilio, Google Calendar, Slack, and QuickBooks, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a locksmith and security automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
