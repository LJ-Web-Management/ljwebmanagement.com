## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an emergency lockout call comes in, this automation automatically identifies the nearest available technician, dispatches the job, and texts the customer a live ETA.
2. Response speed is often the entire basis of the sale in this business, and this automation shaves the minutes that used to go to manually checking which tech was closest.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a dispatcher spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
