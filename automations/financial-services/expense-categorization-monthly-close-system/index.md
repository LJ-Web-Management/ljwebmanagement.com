How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Bank and credit card transactions sync automatically into the accounting system each night, and a categorization engine applies the correct chart of accounts code based on vendor name, amount, and memo, flagging only the exceptions that need a human decision.
2. At month end, the bookkeeper receives a pre-built close checklist with every reconciliation item already pre-populated, turning a two-day monthly close into a focused half-day review.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a bookkeeper has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
