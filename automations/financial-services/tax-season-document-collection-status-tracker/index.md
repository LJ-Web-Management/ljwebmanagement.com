How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At the start of tax season, every client on the roster automatically receives a personalized document request checklist via email, with a secure upload portal link.
2. The system tracks what's been received versus what's outstanding, sends weekly nudges to clients with missing items, and gives the preparer a live dashboard showing which returns are ready to work and which are still waiting on a W-2 or K-1, eliminating the spreadsheet of sticky notes that usually passes for a tracking system.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a tax preparer, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
