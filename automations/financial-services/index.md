## Financial Services automation questions

**What financial services workflows can be automated?**

The 8 financial services automations in our catalog cover compliance, documents & deadlines, marketing & customer retention, and client onboarding & intake, among other areas, for roles like Financial Advisor, Insurance Agent, and Bookkeeper. Popular starting points include [Birthday, Milestone & Life-Event Outreach System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/financial-services/birthday-milestone-life-event-outreach-system/), [New Client Welcome & Coverage Gap Analysis System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/financial-services/new-client-welcome-coverage-gap-analysis-system/), [Expense Categorization & Monthly Close System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/financial-services/expense-categorization-monthly-close-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current financial services software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in financial services automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Google Sheets, and ActiveCampaign, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a financial services automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
