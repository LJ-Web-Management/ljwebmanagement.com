How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new client's first policy is bound, this automation launches a welcome sequence that introduces the agency, explains how to file a claim, and delivers the client's policy summary document automatically.
2. Thirty days after binding, the client receives a coverage review questionnaire asking about any changes in their home, vehicles, business activities, or life situation.
3. The agent receives a summary of any gaps identified (an uninsured vehicle, a home-based business, an umbrella policy need) turning the post-sale relationship into an opportunity to ensure the client is properly protected rather than simply properly sold.
