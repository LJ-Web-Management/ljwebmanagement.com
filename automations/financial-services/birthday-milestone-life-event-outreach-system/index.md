How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every client's birthday, portfolio anniversary, and logged life event (a retirement date, a child's college start date, a planned home purchase) triggers an automatic personalized outreach from the advisor.
2. Birthday messages are warm and non-sales-oriented.
3. Anniversary messages include a brief note on the account's performance and an invitation to review.
4. Life event triggers launch a financial planning conversation about the specific milestone approaching.
5. The advisor stays top of mind for every client throughout the year with touchpoints that feel personal and timely rather than scheduled and generic.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a financial advisor manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
