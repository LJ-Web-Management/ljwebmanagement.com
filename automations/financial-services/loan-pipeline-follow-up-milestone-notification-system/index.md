How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a loan moves through the pipeline (application received, appraisal ordered, conditions cleared, clear to close) both the borrower and the referring real estate agent receive automatic milestone update texts and emails.
2. The loan officer never has to remember who to notify at each stage, borrowers feel informed throughout the process, and real estate agents get the proactive communication that turns one referral into many.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a mortgage loan officer has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
