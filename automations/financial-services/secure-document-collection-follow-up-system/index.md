How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an advisor needs a client's tax documents, statements, or a signed form, this automation sends a secure, trackable request through the firm's client portal rather than an email attachment that gets lost in an inbox.
2. The client receives a link, an upload deadline, and a checklist of exactly what's still outstanding, and the system sends a reminder every three days until every item is marked received.
3. The client services coordinator sees a live dashboard showing who has fully responded, who has partially responded, and who hasn't opened the request at all, and escalation texts go out to anyone still missing documents 48 hours before a filing or meeting deadline.
4. What used to be a manual chase through email threads becomes a fully tracked process where nothing is missed and nobody has to ask twice.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a client services coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
