How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every clinical site's enrollment progress is tracked against its committed recruitment target and the overall trial timeline, and the clinical trial manager receives a weekly enrollment dashboard showing which sites are on track, which are lagging, and which are at risk of requiring a rescue intervention.
2. When a protocol deviation is reported, the system immediately notifies the trial manager and the sponsor's safety team, initiates a CAPA workflow with assigned deadlines, and logs the deviation in the study's master tracking file in the format required for regulatory inspection, ensuring that every deviation is documented, responded to, and closed within the protocol's required window.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a clinical trial manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
