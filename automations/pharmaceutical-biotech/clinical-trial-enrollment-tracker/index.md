How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks enrollment numbers against target recruitment goals for each active trial site and automatically alerts the study coordinator when a site is falling behind pace.
2. Recruiting efforts can be adjusted while there's still time to hit enrollment targets, instead of a shortfall surfacing only at a scheduled monthly review.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a clinical trial coordinator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
