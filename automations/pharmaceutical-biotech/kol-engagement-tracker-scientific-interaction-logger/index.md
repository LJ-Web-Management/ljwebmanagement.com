How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After every interaction with a key opinion leader (a scientific exchange, an advisory board, a conference presentation debrief, or a medical education discussion) the MSL submits a brief interaction log via mobile form capturing the topics discussed, the KOL's questions and feedback, and any follow-up commitments made.
2. The system automatically logs the interaction in the CRM, sends the committed follow-up materials on the promised timeline, and alerts the MSL when a KOL they've engaged with hasn't had contact in more than 90 days.
3. Relationships are maintained systematically rather than depending on the MSL's personal memory of dozens of simultaneous KOL relationships.
