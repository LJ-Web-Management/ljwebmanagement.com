How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For temperature-sensitive shipments, this automation continuously monitors data from in-transit temperature loggers and immediately alerts the quality team the moment a shipment records a temperature excursion outside the approved range.
2. The issue gets caught in transit instead of being discovered only after delivery, when the product may already need to be destroyed.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a quality assurance manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time.

### With this automation
