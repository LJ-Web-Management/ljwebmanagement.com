How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a field employee submits an incident or near miss report via mobile form, the system timestamps it, notifies the HSE manager and site supervisor immediately, assigns a root cause investigation task with a 24-hour deadline, and (based on the incident type and severity) automatically determines whether OSHA or state regulatory notification is required and populates the appropriate form pre-filled with available data.
2. The HSE manager's monthly compliance report is generated automatically from all logged incidents, turning a reactive documentation process into a proactive, always-current compliance record.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a hse manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
