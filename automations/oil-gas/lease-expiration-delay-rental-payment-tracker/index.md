How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every active lease in the land department's database is tracked against its primary term expiration date, continuous development obligation, and delay rental due dates, with automated reminders sent to the responsible landman at 180, 90, 30, and 7 days before each critical date.
2. Missed delay rental payments can void a lease worth millions, this automation ensures that no payment deadline is discovered after it has already passed.
3. When a lease is approaching expiration without a drilling commitment in the queue, the land manager receives a decision-point alert to evaluate whether to pay, negotiate an extension, or release the acreage.
