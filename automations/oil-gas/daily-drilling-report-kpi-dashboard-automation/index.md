How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At the end of each tour, the driller submits a standardized digital daily drilling report (footage drilled, bit hours, mud weight, NPT events, and cost codes) and the system automatically compiles it into a formatted summary, calculates daily and cumulative KPIs against the AFE budget, and emails it to the drilling superintendent, company man, and finance team.
2. Cost overruns against the authorized AFE trigger an immediate flag before the well reaches the point where a budget conversation can no longer change the outcome.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a drilling supervisor remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
- Threshold alerts when a key number moves
- Dashboards fed directly from your systems
- Client and owner reports assembled without copy and paste
