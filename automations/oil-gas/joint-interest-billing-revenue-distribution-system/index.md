How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. At the close of each production month, this automation pulls lease operating expenses by cost code and working interest owner from the accounting system, calculates each partner's proportionate share of costs and revenue per the JOA, and generates formatted joint interest billing statements for each non-operator.
2. Partners who are past due on their JIB payment receive automated reminder notices on a set schedule, and the accountant sees a live aging dashboard showing which partners are current and which are in default, replacing the manual Excel process that typically occupies the first two weeks of every month.

Tools it connects

- Oil and gas accounting software (e.g., WolfePak, Enertia, or Quorum)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Google Sheets or Airtable (JIB aging dashboard)](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/google-sheets/)
- Email (automated JIB statements and past-due notices)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, an oil & gas accountant has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
