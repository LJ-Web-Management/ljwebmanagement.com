How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation continuously compares real-time production data from each well against its expected output curve and automatically alerts the field operator the moment a well's output drops outside the normal range.
2. Equipment issues or downhole problems get caught days earlier than a manual daily check would ever catch them.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a field operations supervisor has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
