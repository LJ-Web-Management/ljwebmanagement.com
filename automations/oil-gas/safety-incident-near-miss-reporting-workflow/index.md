How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a field incident or near-miss is reported through a mobile form, this automation immediately notifies the safety manager, starts the required documentation, and sets follow-up reminders for any regulatory reporting deadlines tied to the incident.
2. Nothing about the reporting process depends on someone remembering to file the paperwork after a stressful moment in the field.

Tools it connects

- A mobile safety reporting app (SafetyCulture)
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Email or Slack](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/slack/)

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a hse coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
