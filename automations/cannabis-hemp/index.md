**What cannabis workflows can be automated?**

The 10 cannabis automations in our catalog cover compliance, documents & deadlines, marketing & customer retention, and operations, inventory & vendors, among other areas, for roles like Compliance Manager, Dispensary Owner, and Cannabis Cultivator. **Do I need to replace my current cannabis software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in cannabis automations are Airtable, Slack, Jotform, and Google Sheets, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a cannabis automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
