How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation automatically syncs inventory movement between the point-of-sale system and the state's mandated seed-to-sale tracking system in real time.
2. The reporting gaps and manual data entry errors that lead directly to compliance violations, and potentially to a license being at risk, get eliminated at the source.

Tools it connects

- METRC or BioTrack
- A cannabis POS (Flowhub or Dutchie)
- Zapier or Make.com

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a compliance manager has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
