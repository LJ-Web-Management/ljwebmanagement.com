How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Sensors in each grow room continuously monitor temperature, humidity, CO2, and light schedules, and any reading outside the set parameters triggers an immediate alert to the head grower and cultivation manager via text, because a HVAC failure that goes unnoticed for six hours during a critical flowering stage can destroy an entire room's yield.
2. Throughout the grow cycle, the cultivation team logs daily observations via mobile form (plant height, leaf color, pest sightings, watering notes) and the system builds a complete grow log by room, strain, and batch that is available for compliance inspection and internal quality review without anyone compiling it from handwritten notes after the fact.
