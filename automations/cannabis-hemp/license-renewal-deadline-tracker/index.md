How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks every state and local license and permit renewal deadline tied to the operation and automatically alerts ownership well ahead of each date.
2. A lapsed cannabis license typically can't be operated through the way a lapsed license in most other industries can, so this alert carries more weight than a routine reminder.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means an operations director handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
