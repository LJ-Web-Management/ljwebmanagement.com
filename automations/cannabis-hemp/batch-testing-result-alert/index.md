How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When lab testing results come back for a product batch, this automation automatically checks the results against state potency and contaminant limits, and immediately flags any batch that fails so it's pulled from the sales floor before it can be sold.
2. A failed batch never has the chance to reach a customer because a result sat unread in an email.

Tools it connects

- METRC or BioTrack
- A lab results portal
- Zapier or Make.com
- [Email or Slack](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/slack/)
