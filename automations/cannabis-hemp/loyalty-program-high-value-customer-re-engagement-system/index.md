How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every purchase automatically updates the customer's loyalty point balance, and customers who hit a tier milestone receive an automatic notification with their reward offer and an expiration date that creates urgency without being pushy.
2. Customers who haven't purchased in 30 days enter a re-engagement sequence, a "we miss you" message with a limited offer on a product category they've purchased before.
3. VIP customers identified by spend threshold receive early access announcements for new drops and limited menu items, giving the dispensary a direct revenue lever that doesn't depend on paid advertising every time a new product arrives.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a dispensary owner has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
