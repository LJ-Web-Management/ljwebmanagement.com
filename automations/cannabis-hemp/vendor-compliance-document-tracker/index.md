## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation tracks required compliance documentation, lab results, licensing, manifest records, for every incoming product from every vendor, automatically flagging any incoming shipment missing required paperwork before it's accepted into inventory.
2. Product with incomplete documentation never quietly makes it onto the shelf.
