How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Each week, this automation checks every dispensary account's order history, identifies accounts that are typically on a weekly or bi-weekly reorder cycle and haven't placed a new order, and sends the account buyer a personalized reorder reminder with their standard product list and current pricing pre-populated in a simple order form.
2. When new products hit the menu or pricing is updated, all active accounts receive an automatic product sheet or price list update from their account rep, so the buyer is informed before they order from a competitor simply because they didn't know the product existed.
3. The sales rep spends time on relationships and new accounts rather than manually chasing reorders.
