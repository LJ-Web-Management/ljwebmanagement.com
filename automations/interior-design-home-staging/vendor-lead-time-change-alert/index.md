How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation monitors vendor-reported lead times for commonly ordered items, fabrics, furniture, fixtures, and automatically flags the design team the moment a vendor's lead time changes significantly.
2. Quotes given to future clients reflect current, real lead times instead of outdated assumptions that end up embarrassing the designer when a \\"12-week\\" item turns out to actually take 20.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a procurement coordinator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
