How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The week before an installation, this automation sends a coordination email to every trade (furniture delivery crew, art installer, electrician, window treatment installer) with their scheduled arrival window, site address, access instructions, and the project manager's mobile number.
2. On installation day, trades check in via a quick text when they arrive, giving the project manager a live arrival status without standing at the door monitoring every delivery.
3. After installation, a digital punch list is completed via mobile form on site, and each incomplete item is automatically assigned to the responsible trade with a resolution deadline, so the reveal happens on time and every punch list item has an owner and a due date.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a design project manager, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
