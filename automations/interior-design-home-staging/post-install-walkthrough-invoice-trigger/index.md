How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a project's install is marked complete, this automation automatically schedules the final client walkthrough, and once that walkthrough is confirmed as done, automatically generates and sends the final invoice.
2. Project completion connects directly to getting paid without a manual step waiting on someone to remember to bill it out.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a design director has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
