## What this automation does, step by step

1. For staging companies with a physical inventory of furniture and decor, this automation automatically tracks which pieces are currently deployed at which properties and which pieces are free, flagging what's genuinely available before a new project is scheduled.
2. Double-booking the same couch for two staging jobs on the same weekend, a surprisingly common and costly mistake, stops happening.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a staging operations manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
