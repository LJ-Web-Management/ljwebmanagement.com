How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a project is completed and the client has signed off, this automation sends a project photography consent form and (once approved) schedules a professional photographer through the firm's preferred vendor, adds the project to the content queue, and notifies the marketing manager that a new portfolio piece will be available on the scheduled photography date.
2. Simultaneously, the firm's top referral sources (interior architects, real estate agents, and luxury builders) receive a curated project highlight email showcasing completed work from the past quarter.
3. Referral partners who sent a client in the past 12 months receive a personal thank-you note from the principal automatically, keeping relationships warm between direct referrals.
