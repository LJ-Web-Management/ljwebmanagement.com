How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment an appointment is cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, this automation instantly texts a pre-built waitlist of patients who have opted into "quick fill" notifications, offering the newly open slot on a first-come, first-served basis.
2. Whoever responds first automatically claims the appointment, receives a confirmation text with the time and any prep instructions, and the rest of the waitlist is notified the slot has been filled so they aren't left waiting on a response.
3. If no one on the quick-fill list claims the slot within 30 minutes, the opening is automatically posted to the practice's broader recall list as well.
4. The scheduling manager no longer spends the morning calling down a list one patient at a time, and chair time that used to sit empty after a late cancellation is now filled within the hour most days.
