How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. For every patient scheduled more than 48 hours in advance, this automation runs an automated eligibility verification against the patient's listed insurance plan, pulls their remaining annual maximum, deductible status, and applicable waiting periods, and saves the results to the patient account.
2. Any coverage finding that differs from what's on file (a lapsed plan, a changed carrier, or a reduced benefit) is flagged for the billing specialist before the patient arrives.
3. Procedures that require pre-authorization are identified based on procedure codes in the scheduled treatment plan, the auth request is initiated automatically, and status is tracked until approved, ensuring the billing specialist never discovers a missing auth after the procedure is complete.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a dental billing specialist has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
