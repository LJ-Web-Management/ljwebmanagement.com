How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a patient receives a treatment plan but doesn't schedule before leaving the office, this automation sends a follow-up sequence over the next three weeks, a same-day summary of the treatment plan with the provider's notes, a three-day check-in offering to answer any questions, and a ten-day message introducing financing options with a pre-qualification link.
2. Patients who pre-qualify for financing are flagged as warm leads for the treatment coordinator to call personally with a scheduling offer.
3. Unscheduled treatment that would otherwise go cold is systematically followed up, recovering thousands of dollars in revenue per month without any manual tracking by the coordinator.
