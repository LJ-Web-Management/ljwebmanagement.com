How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a new patient books their first appointment, this automation sends an immediate welcome email from the dentist with a personal introduction, a link to complete intake forms before the visit, and instructions for the office location and parking.
2. Forty-eight hours before the appointment, a confirmation text goes out with a one-tap confirm or reschedule option.
3. After the first visit, the new patient receives a follow-up message asking about their experience, and those who respond positively receive an invitation to leave a Google review within 24 hours.
4. The patient's first impression of the practice is warm, organized, and professional from the moment they book, not just from the moment they walk in.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a practice owner, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Intake forms that sync straight into your CRM or practice software
- Document collection with automatic reminders
- Welcome sequences that set expectations from day one
- Kickoff and first appointments scheduled without back-and-forth
