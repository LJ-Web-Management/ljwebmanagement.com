How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a successful cosmetic or restorative case, the patient is automatically sent a satisfaction survey, and those who respond positively are presented with a consent form to share their case as an educational example, with the resulting before/after content automatically queued in the content calendar for Instagram, the practice website, and Google Business.
2. On the review side, happy patients who complete the satisfaction survey receive a text within 24 hours with a direct link to the practice's Google or Yelp profile, boosting the review count with the patients most likely to leave a five-star review rather than blasting the entire patient list indiscriminately.
