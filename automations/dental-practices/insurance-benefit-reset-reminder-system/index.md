How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Starting in early November, this automation scans every active patient's insurance benefit usage and remaining treatment needs for the calendar year and identifies anyone sitting on unused benefits or an incomplete treatment plan.
2. Those patients automatically receive a text and email reminder explaining that their insurance benefits reset on January 1st and any unused amount doesn't roll over, with a direct link to book before the end of the year.
3. Patients who don't respond to the first reminder get a second message in mid-December with the practice's remaining open appointment slots for the year.
4. The insurance coordinator sees exactly how many dollars in unused benefits are still on the table across the patient base, and December scheduling fills with patients using benefits they would have otherwise lost.
