How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Patients who are overdue for their 6-month cleaning receive an automated outreach sequence (a text at the first overdue month, an email at two months, and a personal-feeling message from the hygienist at three months) with a one-tap booking link in every message.
2. Patients who haven't been seen in more than 18 months enter a reactivation campaign with a special welcome-back offer and a brief survey asking what prevented them from returning.
3. The office manager sees a live recall dashboard showing how many patients are overdue and what percentage have rebooked, turning a passive recall system into an active revenue recovery tool that runs every day without manual list management.
