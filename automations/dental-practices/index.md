## Dental Practices automation questions

**What dental practice workflows can be automated?**

The 10 dental practice automations in our catalog cover marketing & customer retention, lead follow-up & sales, and client onboarding & intake, among other areas, for roles like Dental Office Manager, Treatment Coordinator, and Dental Billing Specialist. Popular starting points include [Recall & Reactivation Campaign System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/dental-practices/recall-reactivation-campaign-system/), [Treatment Plan Follow-Up & Financing Offer System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/dental-practices/treatment-plan-follow-up-financing-offer-system/), [Insurance Verification & Pre-Auth Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/dental-practices/insurance-verification-pre-auth-tracker/).

**Do I need to replace my current dental practice software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in dental practice automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, Google Sheets, and ActiveCampaign, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a dental practice automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
