How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new patient books their first appointment online, this automation automatically sends them a digital intake packet, health history form, and insurance information request to complete before they ever arrive.
2. A reminder goes out 48 hours before the appointment to anyone who hasn't finished the forms yet, and completed forms sync directly into the practice management system so the information is already in the patient's chart when they check in.
3. Front desk staff no longer hand a clipboard to a new patient in the waiting room or spend ten minutes manually typing handwritten answers into the system.
4. The front desk supervisor sees which new patients have completed intake ahead of time, and same-day check-in for new patients now takes under two minutes instead of fifteen.
