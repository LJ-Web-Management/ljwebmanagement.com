How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a patient leaves the office with an unscheduled treatment plan, whether it's a filling, a crown, or an extraction, this automation automatically enrolls them in a multi-week follow-up sequence rather than letting the plan sit untouched in the chart.
2. Over the following three to four weeks, the patient receives a series of texts and emails reminding them of the recommended treatment, why it matters, and a direct online booking link so scheduling takes one tap instead of a phone call.
3. Messages are spaced to feel like helpful check-ins rather than a sales pitch, and any patient who books during the sequence is automatically removed from the remaining messages.
4. The case acceptance coordinator sees exactly how much unscheduled treatment is in the pipeline and how much of it converts to booked appointments, recovering revenue that used to disappear the moment the patient walked out the door.
