How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation checks every patient's last cleaning date against the practice's recommended recall interval (typically 6 months) and automatically sends a recall reminder by text and email the moment a patient becomes due.
2. Patients who don't respond within the first week receive a second reminder with a direct online booking link, removing the need for the front desk to manually pull a recall report and start dialing.
3. If a patient still hasn't booked within two weeks of the first reminder, the system automatically creates a phone-call task assigned to the front desk with the patient's contact details and recall history attached.
4. The hygiene department lead sees a live dashboard of overdue patients by how many reminders have gone out, turning recall from a monthly scramble into a daily, self-running queue that keeps the hygiene schedule full.
