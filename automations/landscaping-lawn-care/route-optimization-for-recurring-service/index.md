How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on each customer's service day and location, this automation automatically builds the most efficient daily route for crews, and re-optimizes automatically the moment a customer is added, cancelled, or rescheduled.
2. A route sheet that used to be built by hand every morning, and that fell apart the moment one customer called to reschedule, now updates itself.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a route manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
