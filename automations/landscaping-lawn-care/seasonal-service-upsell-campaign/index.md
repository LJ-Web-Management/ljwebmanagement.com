How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation automatically reaches out to existing mowing customers ahead of each season with relevant add-on offers, aeration in fall, mulching in spring, a fertilization program going into summer, timed for when the service is actually most needed and most likely to convert.
2. Upsells stop depending on a crew member remembering to mention it on-site.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a sales coordinator has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Instant first response by text and email on every new inquiry
- Follow-up cadences that stop the moment a lead replies
- Stalled-deal alerts before a pipeline quietly goes cold
- Clean CRM records without manual data entry
