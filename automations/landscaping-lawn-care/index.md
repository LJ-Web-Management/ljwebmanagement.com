## Landscaping & Lawn Care automation questions

**What landscaping workflows can be automated?**

The 5 landscaping automations in our catalog cover scheduling, reminders & dispatch, lead follow-up & sales, and billing, payments & finance, among other areas, for roles like Route Manager, Operations Manager, and Sales Coordinator. Popular starting points include [Route Optimization for Recurring Service](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/landscaping-lawn-care/route-optimization-for-recurring-service/), [Weather-Based Service Rescheduling](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/landscaping-lawn-care/weather-based-service-rescheduling/), [Seasonal Service Upsell Campaign](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/landscaping-lawn-care/seasonal-service-upsell-campaign/).

**Do I need to replace my current landscaping software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in landscaping automations are Twilio, Mailchimp, QuickBooks, and Stripe, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a landscaping automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
