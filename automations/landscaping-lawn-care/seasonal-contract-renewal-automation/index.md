How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As a customer's seasonal service contract nears its end, this automation automatically sends a renewal offer with updated pricing, and flags the sales team to personally follow up with any customer who hasn't renewed within a set window before the season starts.
2. Renewals stop being a scramble every spring to reach everyone before the season is already underway.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an account manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
