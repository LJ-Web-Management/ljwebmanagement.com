How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a crew marks a job complete in the field app, this automation automatically attaches the before and after photos to the customer's record, generates the invoice, and sends both to the customer.
2. Visible proof of work goes out alongside the bill instead of the customer just receiving a charge with no context for what was done.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a crew leader remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
