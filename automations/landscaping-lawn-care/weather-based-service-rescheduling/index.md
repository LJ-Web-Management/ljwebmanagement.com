How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When rain or unsafe conditions are forecast for a scheduled service day, this automation automatically identifies every affected customer, reschedules their service to the next available slot, and sends them a notification explaining the change before the crew would have shown up.
2. Crews stop getting sent out only to turn around, and customers stop wondering why no one showed.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, an operations manager has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
