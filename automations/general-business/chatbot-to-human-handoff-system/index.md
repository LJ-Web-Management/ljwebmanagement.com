How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The website chatbot handles the repetitive questions on its own (hours, pricing ranges, appointment availability, return policy) and answers them instantly, day or night.
2. The moment a visitor asks something the bot can't confidently resolve, or explicitly asks for a person, the conversation is automatically handed off to a live team member along with the full chat transcript, so the customer never has to repeat themselves from scratch.
3. Conversations that come in after hours are queued and routed to the first available team member the next morning with the same context attached.
4. Support staff spend their time on the questions that actually need a human instead of typing out the same five answers all day.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a customer support lead has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
