How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an existing customer or partner submits a referral through a branded form built in JotForm, the record automatically creates a new lead in HubSpot tagged with the referring party's name and routes it to the appropriate sales rep within minutes.
2. Make.com tracks that lead through every pipeline stage, and the moment it closes as a won deal, the system calculates the referral payout based on the program's tiered percentage rules and generates a payout request that flows into QuickBooks Online for processing.
3. The referring partner gets an automatic status update by email at each major milestone so they're never left wondering what happened to a lead they sent over.
4. Partnerships teams that used to track referrals in a scattered spreadsheet now have a fully auditable pipeline, and partners who see fast, reliable payouts consistently refer more business.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a partnerships manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation
