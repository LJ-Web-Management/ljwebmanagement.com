How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Opportunities in Salesforce that haven't had any logged activity, no email, call, or meeting, in 30 days get automatically flagged by Make.com and dropped into a stale-deal report reviewed weekly by sales operations.
2. Deals stale past 45 days trigger an automatic Slack alert to the rep's manager, and past 60 days with still no activity, the system automatically reassigns the opportunity to a fresh rep or moves it to a nurture status in HubSpot rather than letting it inflate the pipeline forecast indefinitely.
3. A running dashboard in Google Sheets shows stale-deal volume by rep so managers can spot coaching opportunities before it becomes a habit.
4. Pipelines that used to carry months of dead weight, quietly making forecasts look healthier than reality, now stay clean automatically, giving leadership a forecast number they can actually trust.
