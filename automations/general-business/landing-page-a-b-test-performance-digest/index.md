How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every live landing page variant running through a testing tool like Unbounce reports conversion, bounce, and time-on-page data into Google Analytics, and each Monday morning a Zapier automation pulls that week's numbers into a formatted Google Sheets report, calculates statistical significance, and flags any test that has reached a confident winner.
2. The digital marketing manager gets a Slack summary highlighting which variant is winning, by how much, and whether it's safe to roll the winning version out to 100 percent of traffic.
3. Tests that have run more than three weeks without reaching significance are automatically flagged for review instead of running indefinitely and wasting traffic.
4. What used to take an afternoon of manually pulling reports from four different tools now takes five minutes to review, and pages get optimized weeks faster than before.
