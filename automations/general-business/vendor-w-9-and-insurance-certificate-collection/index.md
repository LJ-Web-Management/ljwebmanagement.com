How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Before any new vendor gets set up for payment in Bill.com, they automatically receive a PandaDoc request for a signed W-9 and a current certificate of insurance, and payment stays blocked in the system until both documents come back and are verified, closing a common gap where vendors get paid before compliance paperwork is on file.
2. Insurance certificates get logged with their expiration date in Airtable, and 30 days before any policy lapses, both the vendor and the procurement team automatically get a renewal reminder so coverage never quietly expires mid-contract.
3. At year end, 1099 generation pulls directly from the verified W-9 records instead of finance scrambling to track down missing tax IDs in January.
4. This closes off a real liability gap and makes tax season measurably less painful.
