How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment the company, a spokesperson, or a named product appears in a news article, podcast, or industry publication, a Google Alerts and Mention combination catches it and Zapier routes the hit into a shared Google Sheets media log along with the outlet's estimated reach and the journalist's contact details.
2. A Slack notification pings the PR and communications manager immediately for coverage from top-tier outlets so the team can amplify it across social channels the same day through Buffer while it's still timely.
3. Every logged mention automatically feeds a quarterly media report showing total coverage volume, estimated reach, and sentiment breakdown, saving hours of manually compiling clippings before board meetings.
4. Journalist contact details captured this way also seed a running relationship database in the CRM for future outreach.
5. Teams using this workflow cut their press-clipping and reporting time by roughly 80 percent while catching coverage they previously would have missed entirely.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a pr & communications manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Review requests timed to the moment the job is done
