How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Incoming help desk requests, whether submitted through email, a Slack form, or the self-service portal, are parsed automatically and routed to the right queue in Freshservice based on keywords and the requester's department instead of sitting in one shared inbox for someone to triage by hand.
2. Password and access issues route straight to the identity team, hardware requests route to the desktop support queue, and anything tagged urgent pages the on-call technician through a Slack alert immediately.
3. Tickets that sit untouched for more than four business hours automatically escalate to the IT support specialist's manager with the full ticket history attached, and a weekly digest of aging tickets lands in the team's inbox every Monday.
4. Average time to first response drops from hours to minutes, and nothing sits forgotten in a queue nobody is watching.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on an it support specialist, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
- Support requests routed to the right owner with full context
- Escalations when a response deadline is about to slip
- Fewer inbound status calls for your front desk
