How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Sixty days before a membership or subscription is set to expire, the CRM automatically flags the account and Zapier kicks off a renewal sequence through ActiveCampaign: an early-bird renewal incentive at 60 days, a benefits-reminder email highlighting what the member has used that year at 30 days, and a final countdown email with a one-click renewal link at 7 days.
2. Members who haven't engaged with any renewal touchpoint by the 3-day mark get a personal phone call task automatically created for the membership manager in the CRM instead of another automated email.
3. Anyone who lapses despite the sequence rolls into a separate win-back track 30 days later with a fresh incentive.
4. Every renewal, lapse, and reactivation outcome logs into Google Sheets for cohort tracking.
5. Organizations running structured renewal sequences typically lift on-time renewal rates by 15 to 25 percent compared to a single reminder email.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a membership manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
