How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an employee submits a time-off request through Gusto, the system automatically checks their remaining balance, routes the request to their direct manager via Slack with one-click approve or deny buttons, and cross-references the requested dates against a shared team calendar to flag scheduling conflicts before they happen.
2. Once approved, the time off syncs instantly to Google Calendar and the payroll record, and denied requests trigger an automatic explanatory note back to the employee so nothing sits unanswered in an inbox.
3. What used to take days of back-and-forth emails between employees, managers, and HR now resolves in minutes, and HR no longer has to manually reconcile calendars against payroll at the end of every pay period.
