How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Any call to the business line that goes unanswered after a set number of rings, whether the team is on another call, out of the office, or simply swamped, triggers an instant automated text back to the caller within seconds, letting them know the business saw the missed call and will follow up shortly, along with a link to book online if that's faster.
2. The missed call and the caller's number are logged automatically into the CRM as a new lead so no follow-up slips through the cracks by the end of the day.
3. Businesses that used to lose a chunk of inbound leads to voicemail black holes start capturing conversations that would otherwise have gone straight to a competitor who happened to pick up.
