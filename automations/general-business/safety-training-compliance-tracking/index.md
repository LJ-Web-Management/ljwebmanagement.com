How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every employee's required safety certifications, from OSHA basics to equipment-specific training, get tracked with expiration dates in an Airtable compliance database, and 30 days before any certification lapses, the employee and their manager automatically receive a Slack reminder along with a booking link to the next available training session.
2. Anyone whose certification actually expires is automatically flagged and, depending on the role, can be blocked from clocking in for safety-sensitive tasks until they're recertified.
3. A live compliance dashboard gives the safety officer a real-time view of exactly who's current and who's at risk, replacing what used to be a manually maintained spreadsheet nobody trusted.
4. This keeps the company consistently audit-ready and meaningfully reduces the odds of a compliance citation.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a compliance officer has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
