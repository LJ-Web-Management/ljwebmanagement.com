How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a new customer's account is provisioned, a tracked onboarding checklist (profile setup, first key action, integration connected, first result achieved) lives in the CRM, and Zapier checks progress daily against expected timelines.
2. A customer who hasn't completed their profile setup within 48 hours gets a friendly nudge email through Mailchimp with a short how-to video; one who hasn't taken their first key action within a week gets a personal outreach task assigned automatically to the onboarding specialist in Slack.
3. Customers who breeze through every step ahead of schedule get fast-tracked into an advanced-features email track instead of the standard pace, rewarding engagement rather than ignoring it.
4. Completion rates by step feed into a Google Sheets funnel report so the team can see exactly where new customers stall out most often.
5. Businesses running progress-based nudges instead of a flat email calendar cut time-to-first-value by a third or more.
