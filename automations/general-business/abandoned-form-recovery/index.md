How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a prospect starts filling out a multi-step quote request or demo signup form but exits before submitting, a partial-completion event captured through Typeform or the site's native form tool triggers Zapier to log whatever fields were completed, name and email most commonly, into the CRM as a warm lead.
2. Within 30 minutes, an automated email through ActiveCampaign follows up referencing the specific offer they were looking at, with a one-click link back to finish the form pre-filled with their saved answers.
3. If there's no response within 24 hours, a second, shorter nudge goes out, and after that the lead rolls into the standard nurture sequence instead of getting emailed indefinitely.
4. Every recovered form logs its outcome (completed, converted, or unresponsive) into Google Sheets for tracking.
5. Businesses running abandoned form recovery typically reclaim 10 to 15 percent of leads who would otherwise have vanished without a trace.

### Without automation

The manual approach means a demand generation manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.
