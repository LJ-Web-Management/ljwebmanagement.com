    FAQ

    General Business automation questions

    What everyday business workflows can be automated?

    The 103 everyday business automations in our catalog cover lead follow-up & sales, hr, hiring & team management, and marketing & customer retention, among other areas, for roles like HR Manager, People Operations Specialist, and Operations Manager. Popular starting points include Universal Missed-Call Text-Back System, Real-Time Review Monitoring & Alert System, Chatbot-to-Human Handoff System.

    Do I need to replace my current everyday business software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in everyday business automations are Slack, Google Sheets, HubSpot, and Airtable, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does an everyday business automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
