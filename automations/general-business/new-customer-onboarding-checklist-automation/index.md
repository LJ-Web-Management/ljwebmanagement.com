How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a deal closes in the CRM, Zapier spins up a dedicated onboarding checklist in Asana or Trello covering every step from account setup to first-use training to the 30-day check-in call, with due dates calculated automatically from the close date.
2. The customer receives a branded welcome email through Mailchimp with a self-serve resource hub link, while internally the assigned customer success manager gets a Slack notification with the full checklist and customer context pulled straight from the CRM.
3. Any step left incomplete 48 hours past its due date automatically escalates with a reminder to the assigned owner and their manager.
4. Once every step is checked off, the customer automatically rolls into the standard engagement cadence and off the onboarding board.
5. Companies running a structured, automated onboarding checklist see time-to-first-value drop significantly and reduce early-stage churn in the first 90 days by a meaningful margin.
