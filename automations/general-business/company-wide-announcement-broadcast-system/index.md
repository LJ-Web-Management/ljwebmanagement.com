How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When leadership needs to get an announcement in front of every employee at once, whether it's a policy change, a snow day closure, or a major company update, the workflow pushes it out across every channel simultaneously instead of relying on one email that half the company won't open until tomorrow.
2. A single message drafted by the chief operating officer in a shared Google Doc triggers a Slack post to the company-wide channel, an email through Microsoft 365 to every distribution list, and, for time-sensitive alerts like a building closure, a text blast to all employees through Twilio.
3. The workflow tracks open and read rates where available and flags any employee who hasn't acknowledged a required-read policy update within twenty-four hours.
4. Critical information reaches the entire company within minutes instead of trickling out over a full day.
