How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Badge scans and business card captures from the show floor, collected through a lead-retrieval app or a JotForm survey at the booth, sync automatically into HubSpot the same evening rather than waiting for someone to manually key in a stack of cards after flying home.
2. Each lead is tagged with the specific event and booth conversation notes, scored based on the interest level the rep recorded on the spot, and routed to the right account executive with a personalized follow-up email queued in Zapier to send within 24 hours while the conversation is still fresh.
3. A Slack channel dedicated to the event gives the whole team visibility into which leads are being worked.
4. Companies that used to let trade show leads sit for two or three weeks before the first follow-up now make contact within a day, and show ROI has become something they can actually measure.
