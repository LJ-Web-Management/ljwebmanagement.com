How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a campaign brief is added to Airtable with the offer, target audience, and key benefits, Zapier passes those details to the OpenAI API, which drafts three headline and landing page copy variants matched to the brand's tone guide in seconds instead of a half-day of blank-page staring.
2. Drafts land in Google Sheets for the conversion rate optimization manager to review, edit, and approve before they're ever pushed live.
3. Approved variants are automatically loaded into the testing tool (Unbounce or a similar platform) with even traffic splits and a defined sample-size threshold.
4. Once a test reaches significance, the workflow notifies the team through Slack with the winning variant highlighted, and the losing pages are automatically archived rather than left live and confusing future audits.
5. Teams using AI-assisted first drafts get testable copy variants live two to three days faster and run noticeably more tests per quarter than before.
