How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a customer invoice in Bill.com passes 30 days past due, the account is automatically flagged and a firmer follow-up email goes out referencing the original invoice and late fee policy; at 45 days the assigned account manager gets a Slack alert to make a personal call, and at 60 days the account is escalated to the controller with a full payment history pulled from QuickBooks Online attached automatically.
2. Every escalation step and customer response gets logged in a shared Airtable collections tracker so nothing relies on someone's memory of who was supposed to follow up.
3. Companies using staged escalation like this typically recover significantly more of their aging receivables before an account ever needs to go to collections.
