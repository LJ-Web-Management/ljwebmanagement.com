How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The instant an opportunity is marked Closed Won or Closed Lost in the CRM, a short structured survey link built in Typeform fires off to the rep asking for the primary reason behind the outcome, whether that's pricing, timing, a competitor, or missing features, and Make.com writes the response straight back onto the Salesforce record as a tagged field.
2. Lost deals citing a specific competitor automatically populate a running competitive-loss tracker in Google Sheets that sales leadership reviews monthly.
3. Reps get a friendly Slack reminder if they haven't filled in the reason within 24 hours of the deal closing, keeping the data clean and current.
4. Teams that previously had almost no reliable loss-reason data now have a searchable, tagged history behind every closed deal, which has directly shaped two rounds of pricing and packaging changes.
