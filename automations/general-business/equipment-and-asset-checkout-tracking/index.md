How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an employee checks out a company laptop, projector, or piece of field equipment using a simple Airtable form (scanning a QR code tag on the item itself), the asset's status, borrower, and expected return date are all logged automatically, and the facilities team gets a Slack notification confirming the checkout.
2. Three days before an item is due back, the borrower automatically gets a reminder, and anything that goes more than a week overdue escalates to their manager with the item's full history attached.
3. A live inventory dashboard shows exactly what's checked out, what's overdue, and what's sitting unused in storage, which used to require someone physically walking the office with a clipboard.
4. Companies using this recover significantly more equipment on time and cut annual replacement costs from items that simply went missing.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without a system in place, a facilities manager has to manually chase down updates, send reminders one by one, and hope nothing falls through the cracks. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
