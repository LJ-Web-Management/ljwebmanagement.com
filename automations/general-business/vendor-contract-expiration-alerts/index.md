How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The procurement team's full vendor roster, service agreements, pricing terms, and expiration dates all live in a shared Airtable base instead of scattered PDFs across different inboxes.
2. A scheduled check runs every morning, comparing each vendor's contract end date against today, and any vendor within sixty days of expiration triggers a Slack alert to the procurement manager along with an automatically drafted comparison of that vendor's current pricing against two logged alternatives pulled from past bids.
3. High-spend vendors (anything over a set dollar threshold) get an extra escalation to the finance manager so renewal negotiations start with real leverage instead of a rushed signature the week the contract lapses.
4. Procurement stops losing negotiating power to deadline pressure and starts every renewal conversation with data already in hand.
