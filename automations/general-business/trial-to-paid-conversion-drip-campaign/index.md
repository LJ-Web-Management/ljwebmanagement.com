How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. From the day a prospect starts a free trial, ActiveCampaign enrolls them in a lifecycle sequence tuned to their behavior instead of a flat calendar: an onboarding tips email on day one, a feature-highlight email triggered by which tools they haven't touched yet, a case study relevant to their industry on day seven, and an urgency-driven upgrade reminder three days before the trial expires.
2. Zapier watches product usage data and reroutes highly engaged trial users into a faster-moving sequence with a personal outreach touch from sales, while low-engagement users get an extra educational nudge instead of a hard sell.
3. Everyone who converts is instantly moved out of the trial sequence and into the customer onboarding flow so messaging never overlaps.
4. Conversion data feeds back into Google Sheets weekly for trend analysis.
5. Companies running behavior-based trial sequences instead of generic countdown emails typically lift trial-to-paid conversion by 10 to 20 percent.
