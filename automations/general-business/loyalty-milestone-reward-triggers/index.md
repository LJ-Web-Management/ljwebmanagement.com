How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every purchase, referral, or review a customer completes adds points to their loyalty account tracked in a platform like Smile.io, and once a customer crosses a milestone threshold (500 points, their tenth order, or their one-year membership mark), Zapier fires an automatic congratulations email through Klaviyo along with a redeemable reward code.
2. VIP tiers unlock automatically once lifetime spend crosses set thresholds, triggering a separate welcome-to-VIP sequence with early access perks.
3. All milestone activity logs into the CRM so the loyalty program manager can see tier movement and reward redemption at a glance without pulling manual reports.
4. A Slack alert flags any customer approaching a milestone within the next seven days so the team can occasionally add a personal touch.
5. Programs running automated milestone triggers see redemption rates rise significantly and repeat purchase frequency increase within the following quarter.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a loyalty program manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork

- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
