How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every time a customer's lifetime order value, booking frequency, or referral count crosses a defined threshold in the CRM, Zapier automatically tags them as VIP and notifies the VIP experience manager through Slack with a snapshot of their purchase history and preferences pulled from Airtable.
2. VIP customers are automatically routed to a priority support queue, added to an exclusive Klaviyo email segment with early access to new offerings, and flagged for a handwritten thank-you note or small gift on their next order.
3. Any support ticket from a VIP-tagged contact gets an SLA override so it's answered within the hour instead of standard queue time.
4. Quarterly, the workflow compiles a VIP activity report so the team can see churn risk within this highest-value segment before it happens.
5. Businesses that formalize VIP treatment this way typically see retention among their top-spending customers improve by double digits year over year.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a vip experience manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Status updates triggered by real milestones in your systems
