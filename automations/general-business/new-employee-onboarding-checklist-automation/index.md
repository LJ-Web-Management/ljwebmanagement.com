How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new hire's signed offer letter lands in BambooHR, a multi-stage checklist kicks off automatically: IT gets a Slack task to provision a laptop and email account, the hiring manager receives a reminder to prep a first-week agenda, and the new hire is sent a welcome packet with I-9 and W-4 forms through DocuSign to sign before day one.
2. Google Calendar invites for orientation, benefits overview, and a 30-day check-in get created without anyone touching a calendar by hand, and a shared Airtable tracker shows HR exactly which onboarding steps are complete versus outstanding.
3. Teams that used to lose the first week to scattered emails and missed setup tasks now have every new hire fully provisioned and oriented before their first Monday even starts.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, this falls entirely on a hr manager, who has to track every step by hand across emails, spreadsheets, and sticky notes. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Interview scheduling without email ping-pong
- New hire paperwork and account setup before day one
- Certification and license expiration alerts
- Timesheet and time-off workflows that route themselves
