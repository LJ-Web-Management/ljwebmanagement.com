How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a candidate is moved to the interview stage in Greenhouse, they automatically receive a Calendly link scoped to only the interview panel's open availability, so no one has to email back and forth trying to find a slot that works for four different calendars.
2. Once booked, Google Calendar invites go out to the candidate and every panelist with the job description and resume attached, and a Slack reminder pings the panel the morning of the interview with the candidate's background summary.
3. No-shows or reschedule requests automatically trigger a follow-up link rather than a manual re-coordination effort.
4. Recruiting teams using this cut average time-to-schedule from several days down to under 24 hours, which directly reduces the number of good candidates lost to a faster-moving competitor.
