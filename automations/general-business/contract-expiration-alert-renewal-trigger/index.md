How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every signed contract stored in DocuSign carries its term length and expiration date, and 120 days before that date arrives, Zapier automatically creates a renewal task on the account in Salesforce, assigns it to both the contracts administrator and the account's sales owner, and posts a heads-up in Slack so nobody discovers an expired contract by accident during a billing dispute.
2. Auto-renewing contracts get a separate reminder 60 days out in case the customer wants to renegotiate terms before the renewal locks in silently.
3. A master contract calendar synced to Google Sheets gives finance and legal visibility into everything expiring in a given quarter without anyone requesting a manual pull.
4. Organizations that used to discover expired contracts only when a customer stopped paying now catch every expiration months in advance, protecting revenue that used to quietly lapse.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a contracts administrator, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.
