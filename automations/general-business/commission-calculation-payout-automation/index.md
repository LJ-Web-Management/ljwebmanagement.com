How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Closed-won deals sync out of Salesforce the moment they're marked won, and Make.com runs each one against the rep's commission plan, factoring in deal size, product mix, and any accelerator tiers, to calculate the exact payout owed.
2. The result populates a running commission ledger in Google Sheets that every rep can see in real time, removing the mystery around what they actually earned that month.
3. Approved totals push automatically into QuickBooks Online for payroll processing, with a Slack summary sent to each rep and their manager once numbers are finalized.
4. Clawbacks from cancelled deals are flagged automatically rather than discovered months later during a manual audit.
5. Finance teams that used to spend two full days each month reconciling commissions by hand now close the process in under an hour with far fewer disputes.
