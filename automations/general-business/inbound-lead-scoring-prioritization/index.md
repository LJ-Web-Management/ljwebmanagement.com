How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every inbound lead, whether it arrives through a website form, a chatbot conversation, or a content download, gets enriched automatically with firmographic data from Clearbit and scored against a weighted model that factors in company size, industry fit, and page-visit behavior tracked in HubSpot.
2. Make.com sorts leads into hot, warm, and cold tiers the instant scoring completes, pushing hot leads straight to a rep's queue with an immediate Slack ping while warm leads enter a nurture sequence and cold leads route to a long-term email drip instead of clogging a rep's task list.
3. The lead generation specialist reviews a weekly scoring-accuracy report in Google Sheets, comparing predicted score against actual close rate, and adjusts the model's weighting as patterns emerge.
4. Reps who used to spend their morning sorting good leads from junk now work only pre-qualified opportunities, and close rates on rep-worked leads have climbed accordingly.
