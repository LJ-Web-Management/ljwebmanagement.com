How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. This automation checks Google, Yelp, and Facebook every 15 minutes for new reviews and instantly routes anything rated 3 stars or lower to a dedicated Slack or email alert tagged urgent, so a manager can respond publicly within the hour instead of finding out about a bad review weeks later during a routine check.
2. Positive reviews are logged automatically into a running library the marketing team can pull from for testimonials and social proof.
3. What used to be an occasional manual scroll through three different review sites becomes a live feed that catches reputation problems while they're still fixable.
