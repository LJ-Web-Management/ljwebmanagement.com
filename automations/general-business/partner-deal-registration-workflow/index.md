How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Partners submit new deal registrations through a branded form built in JotForm, which checks the account against existing records in Salesforce in real time to catch conflicts before they turn into a channel-conflict dispute later.
2. Make.com routes clean registrations straight into Salesforce as a partner-sourced opportunity with the registering partner tagged for margin and payout purposes, and sends an automatic approval or rejection email within minutes instead of the days it used to take a channel manager to manually cross-check every submission.
3. Approved registrations get a protection window logged automatically, and Slack alerts the channel sales manager whenever a registration is about to expire unclaimed.
4. Partners who used to wait a week for registration confirmation, sometimes losing the deal to internal reps in the meantime, now get same-day answers, which has measurably increased how much pipeline partners are willing to bring forward.
