How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Employees who forget a password no longer have to open a ticket and wait for a technician; they answer two identity verification prompts through Okta's self-service portal (a code sent by Twilio SMS and a security question set at onboarding) and reset the password themselves in under a minute.
2. Every reset is logged automatically to a Google Sheets audit trail with a timestamp and the employee's name, and any account that triggers three failed reset attempts in an hour gets flagged and locked, with an instant Slack alert sent to the help desk coordinator to check for a credential-stuffing attempt.
3. Tickets that previously ate up close to a quarter of the help desk's daily ticket volume disappear from the queue entirely, freeing the team to work on problems that actually need a human.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a help desk coordinator has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
