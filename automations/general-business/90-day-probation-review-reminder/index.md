How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Using each new hire's start date stored in BambooHR, the system automatically counts down to the 90-day probationary review and sends the manager a Slack reminder two weeks out with a simple structured evaluation form covering performance, culture fit, and any concerns raised so far.
2. If the manager hasn't submitted the evaluation by day 85, HR automatically gets looped in with a reminder, since a missed probation review can mean losing the legal window to part ways cleanly if things aren't working out.
3. Completed reviews are logged automatically in the employee's BambooHR file, and any review flagged "at risk" triggers a private Slack check-in between HR and the manager to plan next steps.
4. This single reminder has saved companies from accidentally missing their own probation deadlines more than once.
