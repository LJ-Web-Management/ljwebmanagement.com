How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A fresh list of prospects pulled from LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Apollo.io is enriched with verified emails and firmographic data, then loaded into Outreach.io as a multi-step sequence blending email, LinkedIn touches, and call tasks spaced across three weeks.
2. Make.com watches the mailbox for replies and automatically pauses the sequence the moment a prospect responds, tags the lead as "engaged" in the CRM, and creates a follow-up task for the BDR instead of letting an automated email land in someone's inbox after they've already replied.
3. Bounced or unsubscribed contacts are scrubbed from future sequences the same day to protect sender reputation.
4. BDR teams running this setup typically book 20 to 30 percent more qualified meetings per rep per month, simply because no prospect gets an awkward, tone-deaf automated email after they've already said yes.
