How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customer birthdates and signup anniversaries captured at checkout or during account creation feed into Klaviyo, which automatically triggers a personalized offer email (and a follow-up SMS through Twilio for opted-in contacts) five days before the date, giving the customer time to actually use the discount.
2. The offer code is unique per customer and tracked in the CRM so redemption rates and revenue lift are measurable, not just guessed at.
3. A second reminder goes out on the day itself for anyone who hasn't redeemed yet.
4. Segments are refreshed nightly through Zapier so newly added customers are never missed and inactive accounts are excluded from the spend.
5. Businesses running birthday and anniversary campaigns typically see redemption rates two to three times higher than standard promotional emails, along with a noticeable bump in repeat visits during otherwise slow calendar weeks.

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a brand manager remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.
