How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Employees submit expenses with a photo of the receipt straight from their phone, and the workflow reads the amount and category automatically, matches it against the company's spending policy, and routes it to the right approver based on the dollar amount instead of every expense landing in one manager's inbox regardless of size.
2. Anything under a preset threshold routes for a single manager approval in Slack with a one-click yes; anything above it also loops in the accounts payable manager before it's cleared for reimbursement.
3. Approved expenses sync automatically into QuickBooks for reimbursement processing, and any report that sits unapproved for more than three business days triggers an automatic reminder to the approver.
4. Reimbursement turnaround drops from a multi-week wait to a few business days, and policy violations get caught before payment instead of after.
