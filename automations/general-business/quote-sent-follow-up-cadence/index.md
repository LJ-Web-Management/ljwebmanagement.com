How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a quote is generated in PandaDoc and marked as sent, Zapier logs the timestamp against the opportunity in Salesforce and kicks off a scheduled follow-up cadence: a check-in email at day two, a value-reminder email at day five, and a Slack nudge to the account executive at day eight if the quote still shows an "unopened" or "viewed but not signed" status.
2. Document-open and time-on-page tracking from PandaDoc feeds back into Salesforce so reps can see exactly which pricing page a prospect lingered on before their next call.
3. Quotes that go stale past two weeks automatically flag the deal for manager review instead of quietly rotting in the pipeline.
4. Sales teams using this cadence report closing meaningfully more quotes before they expire, and reps stop having to remember which proposals still need a nudge.
