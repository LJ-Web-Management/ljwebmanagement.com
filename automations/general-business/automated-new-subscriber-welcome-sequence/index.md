How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The moment a new contact submits a signup form on the website or checkout page, they're added to Mailchimp with a source tag, and a five-part welcome sequence begins automatically: an instant thank-you email, a brand story message on day two, a best-sellers roundup on day four, a social proof email with testimonials on day seven, and a soft-ask offer on day ten.
2. Zapier watches the signup form (Typeform or the site's native form) and pushes the contact straight into the right Mailchimp automation, tagging them by interest so future campaigns stay relevant.
3. Engagement data flows back into the CRM through a nightly sync so sales can see who's opening and clicking.
4. Businesses running this sequence typically see open rates climb 30 to 40 percent above single-blast newsletters and convert a meaningfully higher share of new leads within the first two weeks.
