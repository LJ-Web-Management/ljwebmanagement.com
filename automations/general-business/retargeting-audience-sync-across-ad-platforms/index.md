How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Customer and website visitor segments built in the CRM (recent purchasers, cart abandoners, high-intent page visitors) sync automatically every six hours through Zapier into custom audiences on Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads, so retargeting campaigns are always working off current data instead of a list uploaded weeks ago.
2. Customers who complete a purchase are automatically suppressed from prospecting ads within minutes to avoid wasting spend showing them an offer they already redeemed.
3. New high-intent visitors, identified by pages viewed or time on site through Google Analytics, get added to a warm retargeting audience with a tailored ad sequence instead of the generic prospecting campaign.
4. Every sync and audience size change logs into a Google Sheets dashboard so the growth marketing manager can spot drop-offs immediately.
5. Keeping audiences this fresh typically lowers cost-per-acquisition by 15 to 25 percent compared to static, manually uploaded lists.
