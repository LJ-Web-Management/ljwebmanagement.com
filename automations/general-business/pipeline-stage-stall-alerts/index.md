How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every open opportunity in the CRM is monitored against expected time-in-stage benchmarks pulled from historical close data; the moment a deal sits in the same pipeline stage longer than its benchmark, whether that's "Proposal Sent" for nine days or "Negotiation" for two weeks, Make.com fires an alert to both the deal owner and their manager in Slack with a direct link to the record in HubSpot.
2. A weekly digest compiled in Google Sheets rolls up every stalled deal by rep and by stage so sales operations can spot bottlenecks across the whole team rather than chasing one deal at a time.
3. Deals that stall twice in a row get automatically flagged for a joint call between the rep and manager.
4. Pipelines that used to quietly rot now get caught early, and forecast accuracy improves because stalled deals stop masquerading as healthy ones.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, a sales operations coordinator spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation
