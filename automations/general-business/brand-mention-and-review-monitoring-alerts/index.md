How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. A monitoring tool like Mention or Brand24 continuously scans social media, news sites, forums, and review platforms for any mention of the company name, key products, or leadership team, and routes every hit through Zapier based on sentiment and source.
2. Negative mentions or reviews under three stars trigger an immediate Slack alert to the reputation manager with a direct link to respond, while positive mentions get logged into a Google Sheets highlight reel that marketing can repurpose as social proof.
3. Mentions from journalists or high-authority sites get flagged separately and routed to the PR team for a faster response window.
4. Weekly, the workflow compiles a summary report showing mention volume, sentiment trend, and top sources, delivered straight to the team's inbox.
5. Businesses using always-on brand monitoring catch and respond to reputation issues within hours instead of days, often before a small complaint has a chance to spread.
