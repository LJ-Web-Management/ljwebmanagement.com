How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Actual spending data syncs out of QuickBooks Online into a live budget-versus-actual model in Google Sheets every night, and any department that crosses a set variance threshold, say 10 percent over its monthly budget line, automatically triggers a Slack alert to both the department head and the finance team with the specific line items driving the overage.
2. Instead of discovering a runaway budget line during the quarterly review, managers get the alert the same week it happens and can course-correct immediately, while finance builds a running log of variance explanations in Airtable for audit purposes.
3. Companies running this catch budget problems on average two months earlier than they would have with a traditional quarterly review cycle.
