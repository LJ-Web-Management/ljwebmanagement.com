How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every Monday morning, Make.com pulls the current pipeline-weighted forecast out of Salesforce and compares it against the number each rep submitted manually the previous week, flagging any opportunity whose close date slipped, whose amount changed by more than 15 percent, or whose stage moved backward instead of forward.
2. A summary report lands automatically in the VP of Sales's inbox and a condensed version posts to a leadership Slack channel before the Monday forecast call even starts, so the meeting focuses on why numbers moved instead of everyone scrambling to pull the data live.
3. Deals that have slipped two forecast periods in a row get automatically flagged as high-risk.
4. Forecast calls that used to run ninety minutes because half the time went to data-gathering now wrap in thirty, and forecast accuracy has improved because sandbagging and happy-ears entries get caught the same week they happen.
