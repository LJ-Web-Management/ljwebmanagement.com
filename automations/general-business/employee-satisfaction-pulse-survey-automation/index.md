How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every other Friday, a short five-question pulse survey goes out automatically through Typeform to the full team, with results feeding directly into a live dashboard in Google Sheets that tracks sentiment trends by department over time.
2. Anyone who hasn't responded within 48 hours gets a single friendly Slack reminder, and if a team's average score drops below a set threshold for two consecutive surveys, the department head and HR automatically get an alert to check in before the dip turns into a resignation.
3. Individual comments stay anonymous, but trend data gets reviewed automatically in HR's monthly meeting.
4. Organizations running this catch morale problems weeks before they show up in exit interviews, when there's still time to actually fix them.
