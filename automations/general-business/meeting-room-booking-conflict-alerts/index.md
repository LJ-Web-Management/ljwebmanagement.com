How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When two calendar invites land on the same conference room within the same time block, the workflow catches the double-booking the moment the second invite is sent instead of letting both teams discover it walking into the room.
2. It checks the room resource calendar in Google Calendar or Outlook every few minutes, and the instant an overlap is detected it sends a Slack direct message to both meeting organizers and the executive assistant with the conflict details and a list of open rooms nearby that fit the headcount.
3. If no one resolves it within fifteen minutes, the system automatically suggests the next available room and asks for a one-click confirmation.
4. Awkward mid-meeting room swaps and standing outside a locked door with a client in tow become a thing of the past.
