How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Pulling birthdate and hire date directly from BambooHR, the system automatically posts a celebratory message in a dedicated Slack channel on each employee's birthday and work anniversary, tagging their teammates so the recognition feels personal rather than automated.
2. Milestone anniversaries (one year, five years, ten years) trigger an extra step: the manager gets a reminder two weeks out to arrange a small gift or bonus through the company's rewards platform, and a note is added to the employee's file in Airtable for HR's records.
3. Companies running this consistently report noticeably higher scores on the recognition and belonging questions in their engagement surveys, without anyone on the HR team having to remember a single date.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, an employee experience manager has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Interview scheduling without email ping-pong
- New hire paperwork and account setup before day one
- Certification and license expiration alerts
- Timesheet and time-off workflows that route themselves
