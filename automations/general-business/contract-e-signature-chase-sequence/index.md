How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a contract goes out for signature through DocuSign, the system tracks whether the recipient has opened it, and if it sits unsigned past 48 hours, Make.com automatically sends a polite reminder email, followed by a second nudge at day five and a task assigned to the rep in Salesforce at day seven asking them to place a personal call.
2. Signed contracts trigger an instant Slack notification to the deal team and automatically kick off onboarding in the background so there's no gap between "signed" and "kickoff."
3. Contracts that stall past ten days escalate to the sales enablement manager for a status check.
4. Companies running this sequence cut average time-to-signature by several days and recover a meaningful share of deals that previously died quietly in an inbox waiting on a signature nobody chased.
