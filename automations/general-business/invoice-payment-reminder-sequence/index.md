How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every invoice generated in QuickBooks Online is automatically enrolled in a staged reminder sequence: a polite email reminder goes out three days before the due date, a follow-up email fires the day payment is due, and if the balance is still open five days later, a text reminder goes out through Twilio with a direct link to pay online.
2. Once a customer pays, they're instantly removed from the sequence and a thank-you receipt is sent, so nobody gets an awkward reminder for an invoice they already settled.
3. Businesses running this sequence typically see days-sales-outstanding drop noticeably within the first quarter, simply because most late payments turn out to be forgetfulness rather than an actual dispute.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, an accounts receivable specialist has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
