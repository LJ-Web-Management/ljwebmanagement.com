How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every Friday afternoon, the system checks who has and hasn't submitted their weekly timesheet in Gusto, then automatically sends a Slack reminder to anyone still missing one, followed by a direct message from their manager if the timesheet still isn't in by Monday morning before payroll runs.
2. Hourly and contract staff whose hours are unusually high or low compared to their normal pattern get automatically flagged for a quick manager review before approval, catching data entry mistakes before they hit a paycheck.
3. Operations teams that once spent Monday mornings tracking down three or four missing timesheets now process payroll on the first try nearly every cycle, with far fewer off-cycle correction runs.
