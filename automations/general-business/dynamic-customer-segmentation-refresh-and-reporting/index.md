How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every Sunday night, a scheduled Zapier workflow pulls the latest purchase, engagement, and support data from the CRM and recalculates each customer's RFM (recency, frequency, monetary) segment, automatically moving contacts between tiers like "high-value," "at-risk," and "new" as their behavior changes rather than relying on segments set once and forgotten.
2. Each segment syncs directly into Klaviyo and Meta Ads Manager so campaigns and ad audiences always reflect current customer status instead of stale exports.
3. A Monday morning report lands in the data and insights manager's inbox through Google Sheets showing segment size changes, migration between tiers, and which segments drove the most revenue that week.
4. Marketing and customer experience teams pull from the same live segment definitions instead of maintaining separate spreadsheets that inevitably drift out of sync.
5. Businesses running dynamic segmentation typically see campaign relevance and conversion rates improve measurably within the first full sending cycle.

Without this system, a data & insights manager manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.
