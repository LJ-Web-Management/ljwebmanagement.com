How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. After a customer hits a positive milestone, a five-star support rating, a completed project, or a strong NPS score, Zapier automatically sends a testimonial request through Typeform, asking for a short written quote and, optionally, a quick video upload.
2. Approved submissions route into an Airtable library tagged by industry, use case, and product line, so the content marketing team can pull the right testimonial for a landing page or sales deck in seconds instead of hunting through old emails.
3. With the customer's permission captured directly in the form, testimonials automatically publish to the website's reviews section and get queued into Buffer for a social media spotlight post built in Canva.
4. A quarterly report shows how many testimonials were collected, published, and reused.
5. Businesses running this workflow build a steadily growing library of fresh social proof instead of relying on the same five quotes from three years ago.
