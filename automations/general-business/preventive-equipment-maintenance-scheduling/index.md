How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every piece of shared equipment, from the office HVAC system to production machinery, is logged in UpKeep with its maintenance interval, last service date, and assigned vendor.
2. When a piece of equipment approaches its scheduled maintenance window, the workflow automatically creates a work order, assigns it to the appropriate technician or outside vendor, and drops a task on the operations manager's Asana board to confirm it was completed and signed off.
3. Equipment that misses a scheduled service date without a logged reason escalates to a Slack alert flagging it as overdue, since a skipped maintenance cycle is usually how a routine service call turns into an expensive breakdown.
4. Reactive, "it just broke" repair calls that used to eat into the budget get replaced by predictable, scheduled maintenance that costs a fraction as much.
