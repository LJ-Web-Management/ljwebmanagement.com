How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. New leads landing in the CRM are matched against a territory map built on postal code, industry vertical, and account size, then automatically assigned to the correct rep the instant they're created rather than sitting in a shared queue for someone to eyeball and reassign by hand.
2. Zapier cross-references the lead's company data enriched by Clearbit against the territory rules stored in Airtable, then updates the owner field in HubSpot and notifies the receiving rep in Slack with full context attached.
3. Leads that fall outside every defined territory, like a new market or an edge-case account size, route to the territory manager for manual review instead of getting silently dropped.
4. Reassignments that used to take a manager twenty minutes of manual sorting each morning now happen instantly, and reps start their day already working the right accounts.
