How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Once a contact registers for an in-person event, trade show booth visit, or local workshop through Eventbrite, Zapier enrolls them in a countdown sequence: a confirmation email with venue and parking details immediately, a reminder with an agenda and what-to-bring list one week out, and a final SMS through Twilio the morning of with a map link and the on-site contact's number.
2. Attendees who haven't checked the confirmation link within 48 hours of the event get a quick "still coming?" text to gauge headcount for catering and materials.
3. After the event, a check-in scan through the event app automatically triggers a same-day thank-you and next-steps email through Mailchimp instead of the usual week-long delay.
4. Every registration, attendance, and follow-up outcome logs into the CRM.
5. Field teams running this sequence typically see show-up rates improve 15 to 20 percent over email-only reminders.
