How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every laptop, monitor, and piece of loaner equipment gets a QR code logged in an Airtable asset registry the day it's purchased, tracking its serial number, assigned employee, and warranty expiration.
2. When an employee checks out equipment, scanning the QR code with their phone updates the registry instantly and notifies the IT asset manager in Slack, and the same scan-to-return process closes the loop when it comes back.
3. A monthly reconciliation compares the registry against actual headcount and flags any device assigned to someone who no longer works there, or any warranty expiring within thirty days, as a task in Asana.
4. What used to be a spreadsheet nobody trusted and an annual scramble to find missing laptops becomes a real-time, accurate picture of every device the company owns and where it is.
