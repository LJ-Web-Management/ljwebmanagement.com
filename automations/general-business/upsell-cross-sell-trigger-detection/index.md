How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Product usage data and billing history sync nightly from Stripe and the product database into HubSpot, where a set of trigger rules watches for signals like a customer approaching a usage cap, adding new users, or hitting a feature limit repeatedly in one week.
2. The moment a trigger fires, Make.com creates a tagged upsell opportunity on the account, assigns it to the account manager, and drops a Slack alert summarizing exactly which signal fired and what the recommended next package would be.
3. A ready-to-send email template pulled from HubSpot's sequence library gives the account manager a starting point instead of a blank page.
4. Accounts that used to get upsold only when a customer proactively asked now get a timely, relevant conversation started by the vendor, and expansion revenue per account manager has grown as a direct result.

Handled by hand, an account manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.
