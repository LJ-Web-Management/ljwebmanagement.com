How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an employee submits a reimbursement request with a photo of the receipt through a simple Airtable form, the request is automatically checked against the expense policy for spending limits and category rules, then routed to the appropriate manager for a one-tap Slack approval.
2. Approved requests sync automatically into Expensify for reimbursement processing and post to the correct general ledger account in QuickBooks Online without any manual re-entry, while anything flagged as out-of-policy routes straight to the controller for a manual review instead of getting auto-approved by a busy manager who didn't read closely.
3. Finance teams running this catch policy violations before payment goes out instead of during a later audit, and employees get reimbursed noticeably faster than the old paper-form process ever allowed.
