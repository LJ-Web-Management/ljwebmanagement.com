How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every employee with a company card or reimbursable purchase gets tracked against a monthly expense submission deadline in Expensify, and anyone who hasn't submitted receipts by the cutoff automatically receives a friendly Slack nudge, followed by a second reminder emailed through Gmail two days later if the report is still missing.
2. Once a report is submitted, it's automatically routed to the right manager for approval based on department, and approved reports sync directly into QuickBooks Online for reimbursement without any manual data entry.
3. Finance teams that used to chase down a dozen stragglers every closing cycle now close the books on time, and stray unsubmitted expenses no longer pile up into a surprise six-figure backlog at year end.
