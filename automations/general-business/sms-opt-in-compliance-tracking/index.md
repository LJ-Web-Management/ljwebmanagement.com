How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every time a contact opts into SMS marketing, whether through a website form, a point-of-sale prompt, or a reply keyword, Twilio logs the exact timestamp, opt-in method, and consent language shown at the time, and Zapier writes that record into a compliance-ready Google Sheets log required for TCPA and carrier audits.
2. Opt-outs (STOP replies) are processed instantly and synced back to the CRM and Mailchimp within minutes so no suppressed contact accidentally receives a campaign text, which risks carrier penalties.
3. Consent records older than the required retention window get flagged for archive rather than deleted outright, keeping the audit trail intact.
4. A monthly compliance summary automatically goes to the compliance and marketing ops manager showing total opt-ins, opt-outs, and any contacts that need re-confirmation under updated carrier rules.
5. This automated paper trail has saved businesses from real fines and turns what used to be a manual spreadsheet nightmare into a five-minute monthly review.
