How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every software subscription the company pays for gets pulled automatically each month from the corporate card statement and cross-referenced against an Airtable inventory of who owns each tool and how many seats are actually active according to the SSO login history in Okta.
2. Any subscription with zero logins in the last sixty days, or any tool with more purchased seats than active users, triggers a Slack alert to the finance manager with the monthly cost of the waste spelled out in dollars.
3. A running total of confirmed savings from cancelled or downsized subscriptions gets logged automatically to a shared Google Sheets dashboard that finance reviews at the end of every quarter.
4. Software spend that used to creep upward unnoticed month after month finally gets a monthly gut check with real numbers attached.
