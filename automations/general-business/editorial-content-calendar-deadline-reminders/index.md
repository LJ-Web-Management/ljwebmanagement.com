How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every content piece scheduled in the Airtable editorial calendar, whether it's a blog post, case study, or email newsletter, carries a draft-due date, an edit-due date, and a publish date.
2. Make.com scans the calendar each morning and fires off Slack reminders to the assigned writer and editor 48 hours before each deadline, then again the morning of if the status field still reads "not started."
3. Overdue items automatically escalate with a tagged message to the content marketing coordinator so nothing quietly slips a week.
4. Once a piece is marked "published," the workflow logs the live URL back into Airtable and notifies the social media manager to queue promotion.
5. Teams using this system report cutting missed deadlines by more than half and finally getting a reliable, predictable publishing cadence instead of last-minute scrambles the night before launch.
