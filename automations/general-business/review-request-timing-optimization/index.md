How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Rather than blasting every customer with a review request the moment an order closes, this workflow waits for the ideal window, calculated from historical data on when customers are most likely to actually leave a review (typically 3 to 5 days after delivery for products, or same day for completed services), before Zapier triggers a review request text through Twilio and a follow-up email through Mailchimp with a direct link to Google Business Profile or a platform like Podium.
2. Customers who don't respond within a week get one polite reminder and then drop out of the sequence entirely to avoid annoying repeat customers.
3. Every review submitted gets pulled back into a Google Sheets tracker and a Slack alert notifies the team of any review under three stars so it can be addressed immediately.
4. This timing-based approach lifts review response rates by 25 to 35 percent compared to a same-day blanket request.
