How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a prospect fails to join a scheduled discovery or demo call booked through Calendly, the no-show status syncs into HubSpot within minutes, and Make.com immediately sends a friendly "sorry we missed you" email with a fresh scheduling link, followed by a text reminder through Twilio if a phone number is on file, since many no-shows simply forgot rather than lost interest.
2. The original meeting gets logged on the contact record so reps can see a pattern if the same prospect no-shows repeatedly, and after a second no-show the task automatically routes to the sales coordinator for a personal phone follow-up instead of another automated nudge.
3. Reps who used to lose the rest of their day untangling a canceled schedule now get calendars that self-heal, and a meaningful share of no-shows end up rebooked and held within 48 hours.
