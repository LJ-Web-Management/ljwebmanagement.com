How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every registrant who signs up through the Zoom or Eventbrite registration form is instantly added to a dedicated Mailchimp automation that sends a confirmation email, a calendar invite, and two reminder emails (24 hours and 1 hour before the event) to keep no-show rates down.
2. After the event, Zapier splits the list automatically into attendees and no-shows using the platform's attendance data: attendees get a thank-you email with the recording and a follow-up offer, while no-shows get the recording plus a rebooking link for the next session.
3. Every registrant's engagement, attended, clicked, watched the replay, logs into the CRM as a lead score update so sales knows who to prioritize.
4. Events run through this workflow typically see attendance rates improve by 20 percent or more and cut the manual post-event follow-up work from a full day down to essentially zero.
