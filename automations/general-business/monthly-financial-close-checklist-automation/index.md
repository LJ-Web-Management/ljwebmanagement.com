How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. On the first business day of every month, a standardized close checklist automatically spins up in Asana with every task assigned to the right team member: reconciling bank accounts in QuickBooks Online, reviewing accrued expenses, closing the accounts payable and receivable subledgers, and preparing management reports, each with its own deadline before the target close date.
2. As each task is completed it's checked off automatically based on status updates, and any task still open 24 hours before the deadline triggers a Slack escalation to the controller.
3. A finished close package, including the P&L and balance sheet exported from QuickBooks Online, gets compiled automatically into a shared Google Drive folder for leadership review.
4. Finance teams using this consistently shave two to three days off their close cycle every single month.
