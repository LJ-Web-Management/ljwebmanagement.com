How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every proposal generated in PandaDoc carries a built-in 14-day validity window, and as that expiration date approaches, Zapier automatically sends a reminder to the prospect at the seven-day mark and a second one at 48 hours, each with a one-click link to book a call through Calendly if they have questions before deciding.
2. If the window closes with no signature, the opportunity in HubSpot automatically updates to a "proposal expired" status and creates a task for the proposal manager to either extend pricing or check in personally rather than letting the deal silently disappear from anyone's radar.
3. A monthly report in Google Sheets tracks how many proposals expire unsigned by rep and by deal size, surfacing patterns worth coaching on.
4. Proposals that used to quietly go stale now get a structured nudge every time, and the team recovers deals that would have otherwise gone completely cold.
