How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every vendor and service contract gets logged into an Airtable database with its renewal date, auto-renewal terms, and contract owner, and 60 days before any contract renews or expires, the owner automatically gets a Slack reminder and calendar task to review pricing and decide whether to renew, renegotiate, or cancel.
2. If no action is taken within two weeks, the reminder automatically escalates to that person's manager so a contract never silently auto-renews at last year's price, or worse, lapses on a service the business actually depends on.
3. A rolling 90-day view of upcoming renewals gets shared with finance every Monday so contract spend is always visible ahead of budgeting conversations.
4. This alone has saved companies from multiple accidental multi-year auto-renewals they didn't mean to commit to.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, an operations coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
