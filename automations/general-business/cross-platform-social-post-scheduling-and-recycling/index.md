How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every piece of approved content, blog posts, product announcements, customer wins, gets drafted once in Canva, logged into an Airtable content calendar with a publish date and platform tags, and then Zapier picks it up and pushes it into Buffer for scheduled posting across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X at the optimal time for each audience.
2. Evergreen posts that performed well get automatically flagged for recycling ninety days later so the feed never runs dry during busy weeks.
3. A Slack notification pings the marketing channel whenever a post goes live or a scheduled post fails to publish, so nothing sits stuck in a queue unnoticed.
4. Teams that used to spend a morning a week manually posting across four platforms cut that down to under an hour of batch content creation, while maintaining a steady daily posting cadence instead of sporadic bursts.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done manually, a social media manager has to remember to follow up on every case individually, often relying on memory, phone calls, and whatever notes didn't get lost. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Win-back sequences triggered by inactivity, not guesswork
- Renewals and memberships that never lapse silently
- Referral and loyalty programs that track themselves
- Segmented campaigns built from actual purchase history
