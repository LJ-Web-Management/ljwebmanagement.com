## Warehousing & Distribution automation questions

**What warehouse workflows can be automated?**

The 10 warehouse automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, reporting & analytics, and scheduling, reminders & dispatch, among other areas, for roles like Inventory Control Manager, Warehouse Director, and Receiving Manager. Popular starting points include [Labor Productivity Tracker & Shift Performance Dashboard](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/warehousing-distribution/labor-productivity-tracker-shift-performance-dashboard/), [Inbound Shipment Pre-Alert & Dock Scheduling System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/warehousing-distribution/inbound-shipment-pre-alert-dock-scheduling-system/), [Order Wave Planning & Pick Priority Automation](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/warehousing-distribution/order-wave-planning-pick-priority-automation/).

**Do I need to replace my current warehouse software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in warehouse automations are Slack, Google Sheets, Twilio, and Google Calendar, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a warehouse automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
