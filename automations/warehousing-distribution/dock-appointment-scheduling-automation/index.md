How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Carriers arriving to pick up or drop off freight can self-schedule a dock appointment through an automated booking link that checks real-time dock availability and automatically confirms the slot or suggests the next open one.
2. The phone-tag that used to eat up hours of a dispatcher's day manually scheduling every truck disappears, and the dock schedule stays accurate without anyone updating a shared spreadsheet by hand.

Tools it connects

- A dock scheduling tool (Opendock or C3 Reservations)
- Zapier or Make.com
- Email or SMS confirmation
- [Google Calendar](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/tools/google-calendar/)

Using different software? We build around what you already have, so you never have to switch tools to automate.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a dock operations coordinator spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Multi-touch reminders that measurably reduce no-shows
- Waitlists that backfill cancellations automatically
- Self-scheduling with instant confirmations
- Dispatch and on-my-way updates sent to the right people
