How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As orders move through the fulfillment process, this automation continuously flags any order that's been sitting in the picked or packed stage longer than the normal time threshold for that order type.
2. The warehouse supervisor is automatically notified the moment an order crosses that threshold, giving enough time to resolve whatever's stuck before it misses its shipping cutoff instead of finding out at the end of the day that a batch of orders didn't ship.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual version of this puts the entire workload on a warehouse supervisor, who has to juggle phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets just to keep things moving. None of it is difficult, it's just slow, repetitive, and easy to fall behind on.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
