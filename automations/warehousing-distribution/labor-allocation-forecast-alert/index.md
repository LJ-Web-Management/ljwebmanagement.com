How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. By comparing tomorrow's expected order volume against current confirmed staffing levels, this automation automatically alerts the warehouse manager if the forecast suggests the shift is understaffed for the volume coming in.
2. That alert lands with enough lead time to call in extra help before the volume actually hits, instead of the shortfall only becoming obvious once orders are already backing up on the floor.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a warehouse manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Daily and weekly digests delivered automatically
