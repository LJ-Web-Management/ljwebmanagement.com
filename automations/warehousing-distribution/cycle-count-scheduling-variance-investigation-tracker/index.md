How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. The inventory control manager sets a cycle count schedule by location zone, and this automation assigns count tasks to the designated counters each morning, collects count results via mobile scan or a simple form, and immediately calculates variances against the system-on-hand quantity.
2. Any variance above the threshold triggers an investigation task assigned to the inventory control team, and the manager receives a daily variance summary showing total shrink, potential causes by zone, and the status of open investigations.
3. Inventory accuracy is monitored continuously rather than discovered annually during a physical count.
