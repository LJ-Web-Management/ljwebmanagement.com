How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a supplier ships an order, this automation receives the ASN (Advanced Shipment Notice), cross-references it against the open PO, flags any quantity or item discrepancies before the truck arrives, and schedules a dock door and receiving crew window on the facility's dock management calendar.
2. Drivers receive a confirmation text with their scheduled arrival window and dock assignment, eliminating the unplanned arrivals that stack trucks in the parking lot and blow up the receiving team's day.
3. Any shipment arriving without a pre-scheduled appointment is flagged automatically for the receiving manager to route or reschedule.
