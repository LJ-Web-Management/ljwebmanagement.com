How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Sensors or machine operator forms feed real-time equipment status into a central dashboard, and any unplanned downtime event immediately triggers an alert to the maintenance team and plant manager with the machine ID, location, and last known status.
2. Simultaneously, a maintenance ticket is opened and the on-call technician is assigned.
3. Scheduled preventive maintenance windows are calculated from run-hour data and pushed to the maintenance calendar automatically, so the plant never discovers that a machine was overdue for service after it has already gone down.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

The manual approach means a plant manager handles each case reactively, after someone has already noticed something went wrong. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
