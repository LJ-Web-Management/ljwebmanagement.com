How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Based on each customer's historical purchase cycle, this automation calculates when they are likely due to reorder and sends the sales rep a prompt five days before that window, with a pre-drafted outreach message referencing the customer's last order details.
2. Accounts that haven't ordered in longer than their typical cycle receive an escalation to the regional sales manager.
3. When a customer's order volume grows significantly year over year, the sales rep receives an alert to schedule an account review and explore expanded capacity or new product categories.
