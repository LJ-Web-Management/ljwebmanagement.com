How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Weekly safety inspection checklists are assigned automatically to the designated inspector, completed via a mobile form on the floor, and routed to the EHS manager for review the moment they are submitted.
2. Any failed item generates a corrective action task with an assigned owner and due date.
3. The system also tracks every compliance certificate, permit, and training requirement across the facility, sending renewal reminders 90 and 30 days before each expiration, ensuring the facility is never surprised by an expired permit or certification during an OSHA audit.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Handled by hand, an ehs (environment, health & safety) manager spends time each day re-checking the same information across disconnected tools instead of doing higher-value work. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
