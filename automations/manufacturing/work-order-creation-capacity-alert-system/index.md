How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. As sales orders are confirmed, this automation generates the corresponding work orders in the production schedule, checks available machine and labor capacity for each required operation, and flags any work order that cannot be completed by the promised ship date given current capacity.
2. The scheduler sees conflicts before they become missed deliveries, and production supervisors receive a pre-shift briefing each morning with today's work orders sorted by priority, starting the day aligned rather than discovering the fire at noon.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Done by hand, this depends entirely on a production scheduler remembering to check in, update records, and follow up, with no safety net if something slips. It's the kind of work that quietly eats hours every week.

### With this automation

- Low-stock and reorder alerts before you run out
- Vendor and subcontractor confirmations that chase themselves
- Preventive maintenance scheduled by usage or date
- Work orders and project milestones that update stakeholders
