## Manufacturing automation questions

**What manufacturing workflows can be automated?**

The 10 manufacturing automations in our catalog cover operations, inventory & vendors, compliance, documents & deadlines, and marketing & customer retention, among other areas, for roles like Plant Manager, Quality Control Manager, and Production Scheduler. Popular starting points include [Equipment Downtime Alert & Preventive Maintenance Log](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/manufacturing/equipment-downtime-alert-preventive-maintenance-log/), [Defect Report & Root Cause Investigation Tracker](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/manufacturing/defect-report-root-cause-investigation-tracker/), [Work Order Creation & Capacity Alert System](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/automations/manufacturing/work-order-creation-capacity-alert-system/).

**Do I need to replace my current manufacturing software?**

No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in manufacturing automations are Slack, Google Sheets, Jotform, and Airtable, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

**How much does a manufacturing automation cost?**

We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

**How long does it take to set one up?**

Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
