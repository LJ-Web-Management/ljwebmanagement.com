    FAQ

    Government & Public Sector automation questions

    What public sector workflows can be automated?

    The 10 public sector automations in our catalog cover compliance, documents & deadlines, customer service & communication, and billing, payments & finance, among other areas, for roles like City / County Administrator, Public Works Director, and Grant Administrator. Popular starting points include FOIA / Public Records Request Tracking & Response System, Infrastructure Work Order & Inspection Scheduling System, Grant Application Pipeline & Compliance Reporting System.

    Do I need to replace my current public sector software?

    No. Every automation is built around the tools you already use. The integrations that come up most often in public sector automations are Google Sheets, Jotform, Slack, and Twilio, but we connect whatever CRM, scheduling, accounting, or field software you run today.

    How much does a public sector automation cost?

    We don't publish a price list because every automation is scoped to your tools and workflow. After a free consultation you get a flat, all-in quote before any work starts, with no hourly billing and no hidden fees.

    How long does it take to set one up?

    Simple, single-workflow automations typically go live within days. Multi-system integrations take longer. Either way, you receive a clear scope and timeline before development begins, so there are no surprises.
