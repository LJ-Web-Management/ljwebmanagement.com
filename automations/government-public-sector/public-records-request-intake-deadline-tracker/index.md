How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When a public records request is submitted, this automation automatically logs it, calculates the legally required response deadline, routes it to the department that holds the relevant records, and escalates internally if the deadline is approaching without a response drafted.
2. A missed statutory deadline, which can carry real legal exposure, gets caught with days of lead time instead of discovered after the fact.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without automation, a records compliance officer has to notice problems after they've already happened, then scramble to fix them manually one at a time. Over a month, those small manual steps add up to a significant amount of lost time.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
