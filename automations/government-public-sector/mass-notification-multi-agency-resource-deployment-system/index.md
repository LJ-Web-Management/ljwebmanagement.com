How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. When an emergency event is declared, this automation triggers a tiered mass notification sequence, text and email alerts to affected residents based on geographic zone, notifications to mutual aid partners and regional emergency management contacts, and a resource request form distributed to department heads for staffing, equipment, and supply commitments.
2. As resources are committed, the system updates a shared operational dashboard in real time so the coordinator knows exactly what is deployed, where, and for how long without chasing confirmations by radio and phone during the most stressful hours of the event.
