How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Every active grant (federal, state, and foundation) lives in a single pipeline with key dates: application deadline, award notice, contract execution, reporting periods, and closeout.
2. The grant administrator receives reminders 60, 30, and 7 days before each deadline, and department program managers are notified automatically when their performance data is needed for an upcoming report.
3. Awarded grants trigger a reporting calendar that pushes deadlines to the shared calendar and assigns data collection tasks to the appropriate staff, so quarterly and annual reports are assembled from collected data rather than built under a last-minute deadline with incomplete records.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Without this system, a grant administrator manually pieces together information from different places, then has to relay it to everyone who needs it. It rarely feels urgent in the moment, but it adds up fast.

### With this automation

- Invoices generated the moment a job or milestone completes
- Polite, escalating payment reminders that run on schedule
- Failed payment recovery for subscriptions and memberships
- Expense, commission, and reconciliation work handled automatically
