How it works

## What this automation does, step by step

1. Citizen service requests submitted via the city website, 311, or in person are automatically categorized by type (pothole, streetlight, water main issue, sign damage) assigned to the appropriate crew, and logged in the work order system with a priority level and target completion date.
2. Requesters receive an automated acknowledgment with a service request number and expected response window.
3. Completed work orders trigger a close-out notification to the requester.
4. The public works director sees a real-time dashboard of open requests by type, aging, and crew workload, replacing the manual dispatch board that gives no visibility into whether requests are being resolved within the city's committed service levels.
