## What this automation does, step by step

1. For every grant a department has received, this automation tracks required reporting deadlines and spending milestones, automatically reminding the grant manager well ahead of each deadline.
2. Future funding eligibility, which can be jeopardized by a single missed report, stops being put at risk by a deadline that simply slipped someone's mind.

## The manual way vs. the automated way

### Without automation

Left unautomated, a grants manager spends hours on the parts of this job that don't require judgment: copying data, sending reminders, and tracking status by hand.

### With this automation

- Expiration and renewal alerts on a 90, 60, and 30 day countdown
- Signature and document chasers that escalate on their own
- Deadline tracking for filings, permits, and inspections
- An automatic audit trail of every notice sent
