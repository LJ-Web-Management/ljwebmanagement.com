    The full process

    Five steps from first call to live system

    Every project follows the same process, so you always know what's happening next and why.

    Discovery Call

    Who it's for

    The same process, two different outcomes

    How this process plays out depends on which side of the desk you're sitting on.

    For business owners & executives

    Steps 1 and 2 are where you implement AI without needing to hire for it: you tell us where the delay is, and we scope and build the system around your existing tools. Step 5 is where it keeps paying off, with ongoing support instead of a project that goes stale the day it launches.

    For employees

    Steps 3 and 4 are the ones that touch your own role: the repetitive part of your job gets built into the system and tested against real examples from your work, and what's left afterward is the judgment and relationship work AI can't do. That's the part of a job that's hardest to automate away, and the part worth getting good at now.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need to replace my current software?

    Not necessarily. We can usually build the automation around the tools you already use.

    Can a small business benefit from automation?

    Yes. Automating even one repetitive, time-consuming process can make a meaningful difference.

    Will my team stay in control?

    Yes. Automation handles the steps you choose, while your team keeps control of decisions, exceptions, and customer relationships.

    What processes can be automated?

    Most repetitive, rules-based processes. Tell us what you want handled, and we'll confirm what's possible. You can also browse the automation catalog.

    How long does implementation take?

    It depends on the tools involved and the complexity of your request. You'll get a clear project scope before development begins.

    Will you show us how it works?

    Yes. We'll walk your team through the finished system so everyone knows how to use it.
