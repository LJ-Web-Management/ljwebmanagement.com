How It Works

Three steps. No platform login.

There's no learning-management portal to sign up for and no subscription to manage. Buy a course, and everything you need lands in your inbox.

1. Browse and pick a course

Search or filter the catalog of 62 courses by category and level. Every listing shows exactly what you'll learn and how long it takes before you buy.

2. Book a quick appointment

Pick a time that works and tell us which course you want. No account to create, and it takes under a minute.

3. Get a payment link and access by email

We confirm your course on the call and email you a secure payment link, then your course materials and access details, usually within one business day.

What You Get

What's actually inside a course

Video lessons and written guides

Each course is broken into short, focused lessons you can move through at your own pace, with written notes and resources to go with them.

Lifetime access

Once you're enrolled, the course is yours. Revisit it whenever you want as you apply what you learned.

Direct support

Questions about the material go straight to info@ljwebmanagement.com, answered by a real person, not a ticket queue.

Certificate of completion

Finish a course and request a certificate of completion for your resume, LinkedIn, or portfolio.
