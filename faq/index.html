FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before enrolling. Hover any question to expand it. Can't find your answer? Contact us directly.

Courses & Pricing
How much do courses cost?

Every course is priced between $12 and $49 as a single, one-time payment. There are no subscriptions, monthly fees, or hidden charges. The price is shown on every course card before you buy.

What's included in a course?

Video lessons, written guides, and any resources (templates, code, cheat sheets) referenced in that course. Every course listing shows exactly what you'll learn before you buy.

Do courses ever go on sale?

Occasionally. Since every course is already priced between $12 and $49, discounts are the exception rather than the rule. Follow our LinkedIn or X for announcements.

Can I preview a course before buying?

Every course card shows the full description, level, duration, and a "What you'll learn" list before you enroll, so you know exactly what you're getting. If you'd like more detail on a specific course, ask us first.

How long does each course take to complete?

It varies by course, from about 2 hours for a short workshop-style course up to 12 to 14 hours for an in-depth, project-based one. The exact duration is listed on every course card.

Are the courses kept up to date?

Yes. Tech and AI tools move fast, so we review and refresh course content as tools and best practices change. If you already own a course, updates are included at no extra cost.

Can I suggest a course topic you don't offer yet?

Yes, and we mean it. Send us a note with what you'd like to learn. Course topics are picked based partly on what people actually ask for.

Enrollment & Payment
How do I enroll and pay?

Open any course and click Book an Appointment. Pick a time and tell us which course you want, no account or card required upfront. On the call we confirm your course and send a secure payment link to your email.

What happens right after I enroll?

We confirm your course on the call and follow up by email with a secure payment link. Your course access and materials go out once payment is complete, usually within one business day.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment links are processed through Stripe, which accepts all major credit and debit cards, and Apple Pay or Google Pay where available.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. Payment is handled entirely by Stripe, a payment processor used by millions of businesses. We never see or store your full card details. See our Privacy Policy for more.

Can I split a purchase into installments?

Since every course is priced at $49 or less, we don't offer installment plans. If cost is a barrier for a specific course, reach out and we'll see what we can do.

Do you offer discounts for buying multiple courses?

If you're enrolling in several courses at once, or enrolling a team, contact us before you pay. We can often work out a bundle rate.

Can a business or team purchase courses?

Yes. Mention the number of seats and which courses you're interested in when you contact us, and we'll set up enrollment and billing for the whole group.

Can I get an invoice or receipt?

Yes. Stripe automatically emails a receipt once payment goes through. If you need a formatted invoice for expensing or accounting, email info@ljwebmanagement.com and we'll send one.

Access & Learning
Is there a learning platform I need to log into?

No. There's no separate account or portal to manage. Course materials, videos, and resources are delivered straight to your email so you can start right away.

Do I need any prior experience?

It depends on the course. Every course in the catalog is labeled Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced so you can find the right starting point. Most Beginner courses assume no prior experience at all.

How long do I have access to a course?

Access is yours to keep once you've enrolled. Revisit lessons and materials at any time as you apply what you've learned.

Can I access courses on my phone or tablet?

Yes. Materials are sent by email, so you can watch, read, and download them on any device, phone, tablet, or computer.

Do you provide downloadable materials?

Most courses include downloadable notes, templates, or resource files alongside the video lessons, noted in the course description.

What if I fall behind or need to pause?

No problem. Every course is self-paced with lifetime access, so there's no deadline, no streak to keep, and no penalty for pausing and picking it back up later.

Are the courses live or self-paced?

Fully self-paced. There are no scheduled live sessions to attend, so you can move through the material whenever it fits your schedule.

What if a video or link doesn't work?

Email info@ljwebmanagement.com with the course name and what's broken. We fix access issues quickly, usually the same day.

Refunds & Cancellations
Can I get a refund?

Yes. If a course isn't right for you, email info@ljwebmanagement.com within 14 days of purchase and we'll issue a full refund, no forms or hoops. See our Terms of Service for the complete policy.

What if I already started the course?

The 14-day refund window applies whether or not you've started. We'd rather you get your money back than sit on a course that isn't a fit.

Can I cancel an enrollment before I pay?

Yes. Booking an appointment isn't a payment, it's just the first step. If you never complete the payment link we send, you're never charged. Just let us know if you'd like to cancel and we'll close it out.

Certificates & Career
Do you offer certificates of completion?

Yes. Once you finish a course, email us and we'll send a certificate of completion you can add to your resume, LinkedIn profile, or portfolio.

Will a certificate help me get a job?

A certificate shows initiative, but it's the skill itself that matters most in an interview or on the job. Our Career & AI Productivity courses cover resumes, portfolios, and interview prep if that's your goal.

Can I list a completed course on LinkedIn?

Yes, plenty of people add their certificate of completion to LinkedIn's "Licenses & Certifications" section. We include everything you need (course name, completion date) on the certificate.

Gifting & Teams
Can I buy a course for someone else?

Yes. Email info@ljwebmanagement.com with the course you'd like to gift and the recipient's email address, and we'll take care of the rest.

Do you offer bulk pricing for teams or schools?

Yes, for groups enrolling in the same course. Contact us with the group size and course, and we'll put together a rate.

Support & Other
What if I have a question about the material?

Email info@ljwebmanagement.com any time. A real person reads and responds to every message, we don't route support through a bot or ticket queue.

How fast do you respond to emails?

Usually within one business day. Enrollment and access emails typically go out even faster.

Do you offer live coaching or custom training?

Not as a standard offering today, but if your team needs something tailored, tell us what you're looking for and we'll let you know if we can help.

Is my personal data safe with you?

Yes. We collect only what's needed to enroll you and deliver a course, and we never sell your information. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

What browsers or devices are supported?

Any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) on any device works fine, since everything is delivered by email rather than a dedicated app or platform.

Still have a question?

Reach out and we'll get back to you personally, usually within one business day.
