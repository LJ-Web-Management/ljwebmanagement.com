Overview

## What Marketplace & Multi-Vendor Platform includes

A multi-vendor marketplace platform for businesses connecting multiple sellers, providers, or contractors with customers through a single storefront: a local services marketplace, a multi-brand e-commerce platform, or a vendor network. The platform handles vendor onboarding and profile management, order or booking routing to the correct vendor, commission calculation, and split payouts, all without manual reconciliation. Vendors get their own dashboard for managing listings, orders, and payouts, and platform admins get oversight tools for approving vendors, resolving disputes, and monitoring marketplace-wide performance from one place.

## Marketplace & Multi-Vendor Platform questions

**How do split payouts work?**

Using a payments platform like Stripe Connect, each customer payment is divided automatically between the vendor and the marketplace commission, and vendors are paid out on schedule.

**Should we build a marketplace from scratch?**

Not always. Platforms like Sharetribe can launch a marketplace quickly, and custom development makes sense when your workflows or scale require it.

**Who handles vendor taxes and verification?**

Payment platforms collect tax information and verify vendor identity as part of onboarding, which we configure for your marketplace.
