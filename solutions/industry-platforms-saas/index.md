## Industry Platforms, Apps & SaaS questions

**Does the POS process card payments itself?**

No. It pairs with your existing card processor or terminal and records how each guest paid, which keeps payment processing flexible and bookkeeping clean.

**How do split payouts work?**

Using a payments platform like Stripe Connect, each customer payment is divided automatically between the vendor and the marketplace commission, and vendors are paid out on schedule.

**Should we use AppFolio or Buildium or a custom system?**

AppFolio and Buildium fit most residential portfolios. We implement and automate them, and build custom tools for unique workflows or integrations they do not cover.

**How do you keep the first version from getting too big?**

We define the smallest product that solves the core problem for a specific customer, launch it, and expand based on real usage rather than assumptions.

**Should we build a mobile app or a mobile website?**

A mobile-friendly website covers most needs. An app makes sense when customers use it often, or when you need push notifications, offline access, or device features like scanning.

**What does managed SaaS include?**

Hosting, monitoring, security patching, backups, support, and ongoing improvements for your custom software or automations, for a predictable monthly fee.
