## Mobile App Development questions

**Should we build a mobile app or a mobile website?**

A mobile-friendly website covers most needs. An app makes sense when customers use it often, or when you need push notifications, offline access, or device features like scanning.

**Do you build for both iPhone and Android?**

Yes. We typically use a cross-platform framework so one codebase serves both, reducing cost and keeping features in sync.

**Who publishes the app to the app stores?**

We handle submission under your developer accounts so you own the listing and the app.
