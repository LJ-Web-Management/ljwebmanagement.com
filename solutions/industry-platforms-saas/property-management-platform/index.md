Overview

## What Property Management Platform includes

A property management system for residential, commercial, and short-term rental operators that connects listings, applications, leases, rent collection, maintenance, and owner reporting. Applicants apply and screen online, leases are generated and e-signed, rent is collected by ACH with automatic late fees and reminders, and maintenance requests are triaged and dispatched to vendors with photos and updates sent back to tenants. Owners get monthly statements and a portal, and accounting stays reconciled by property.

### Who it's for

## Property Management Platform questions

**Should we use AppFolio or Buildium or a custom system?**

AppFolio and Buildium fit most residential portfolios. We implement and automate them, and build custom tools for unique workflows or integrations they do not cover.

**Can tenants pay rent online automatically?**

Yes. Tenants can set up autopay by ACH or card, and reminders and late fees are applied according to the lease.

**How are maintenance vendors managed?**

Requests are categorized and dispatched to approved vendors, who update status and upload photos and invoices for owner approval and billing.
