Overview

## What Restaurant Point of Sale (POS) System includes

A complete point-of-sale platform built for restaurants, cafes, and quick-service operations, covering order entry, table and tab management, and kitchen display integration in one connected system. Checkout closes out tabs and records how each guest paid (cash, card, or other) for clean bookkeeping, and it pairs with your existing card processor or terminal rather than handling card charges itself. The platform also includes menu management, role-based staff logins, and real-time reporting dashboards, giving owners and managers accurate visibility into top-selling items, average tab size, and daily revenue without reconciling numbers across separate tools. This offering includes hardware setup, menu configuration, and staff training to get your team live fast.

## Restaurant Point of Sale (POS) System questions

**Does the POS process card payments itself?**

No. It pairs with your existing card processor or terminal and records how each guest paid, which keeps payment processing flexible and bookkeeping clean.

**Can it connect to delivery apps?**

Yes. Orders from DoorDash, Uber Eats, and online ordering can flow into the same kitchen queue and reporting.

**How long does it take to go live?**

Timing depends on menu size and hardware, and includes menu configuration, hardware setup, and staff training before your first service.
