Overview

## What Managed SaaS Delivery & Support includes

A subscription-based service where a business's custom software, internal tools, or automation stack is hosted, monitored, secured, and maintained on the client's behalf. This includes uptime monitoring, security patching, feature updates, technical support, and scaling infrastructure as usage grows, allowing clients to treat their internal software the same way they'd treat any other reliable SaaS product, without needing an in-house DevOps or IT team.

### Who it's for

- Businesses with custom software but no IT team
- Companies whose original developer has moved on
- Teams running critical automations that cannot fail quietly

## Managed SaaS Delivery & Support questions

**What does managed SaaS include?**

Hosting, monitoring, security patching, backups, support, and ongoing improvements for your custom software or automations, for a predictable monthly fee.

**Can you take over software another developer built?**

Yes. We start with a technical review, document the system, fix urgent risks, and then bring it under ongoing management.

**Do we keep ownership of our software and data?**

Yes. Code, infrastructure, and data remain in accounts you own.
