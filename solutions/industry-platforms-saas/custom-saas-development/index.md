Overview

## What Custom SaaS Product Development includes

End-to-end development of a software-as-a-service product, from validating the idea and defining a focused first version through design, development, launch, and growth. We build secure multi-tenant applications with user accounts, roles, subscription billing, onboarding, and admin tools, on modern cloud infrastructure that scales as customers sign up. Analytics show how customers use the product, and an iterative roadmap keeps development focused on what drives retention and revenue.

## Custom SaaS Product Development questions

**How do you keep the first version from getting too big?**

We define the smallest product that solves the core problem for a specific customer, launch it, and expand based on real usage rather than assumptions.

**Who owns the code?**

You do. We deliver the source code and infrastructure in accounts you control.

**Can you run the product after launch?**

Yes. Our managed SaaS service covers hosting, monitoring, security, and ongoing development.
