Overview

## What Knowledge Base & Self-Service Help Center includes

A searchable help center that answers customer questions before they become tickets and gives your staff one trusted source for procedures and policies. We structure the content, write or migrate articles from your most common support requests, and connect the knowledge base to your help desk and AI chat so suggested answers appear as customers type. Article analytics show what people search for and cannot find, review reminders keep content accurate, and an internal section holds SOPs and training material for your team.

## Knowledge Base & Self-Service Help Center questions

**How much can a knowledge base reduce support tickets?**

A well-organized knowledge base connected to your ticket form and chat can deflect a meaningful share of simple questions. The exact reduction depends on how repetitive your requests are.

**Should internal and customer documentation live together?**

They can share one platform with separate permissions, which keeps answers consistent while protecting internal procedures.

**Who keeps the articles up to date?**

Each article gets an owner and review date, and the system reminds owners when content is due for review or when related tickets suggest it is wrong.
