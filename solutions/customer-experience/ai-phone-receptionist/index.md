Overview

## What AI Phone Receptionist & Call Routing includes

An AI-powered phone system that answers every call in a natural voice, handles common questions, books appointments, takes detailed messages, and routes urgent or high-value calls to the right person. Missed calls trigger an instant text back so callers are never left hanging, call summaries and recordings are logged to your CRM, and smart routing sends callers to the right department, location, or on-call technician. Reporting shows call volume, missed call rate, and how many calls turned into booked work.

## AI Phone Receptionist & Call Routing questions

**Do callers know they are speaking with AI?**

We recommend being transparent. A short disclosure is standard, and modern voice AI is natural enough that most callers simply get their question answered or their appointment booked.

**What happens with calls the AI cannot handle?**

They are transferred to a person or, after hours, the AI takes a detailed message and alerts the right staff member immediately.

**Can we keep our current phone number?**

Yes. Calls can be forwarded from your existing number, or the number can be ported, so nothing changes for your customers.
