Overview

## What Customer Portal & Self-Service Platform includes

A branded customer portal where clients can log in to view order history, account status, invoices, project progress, or service history without calling or emailing your team for updates they could see themselves. Self-service actions (updating payment methods, downloading documents, submitting requests) are handled directly in the portal, cutting down the volume of routine inbound contact your team fields every week. The platform integrates with your existing billing, CRM, or project management system so the data shown is always current, and role-based access supports multiple contacts per account for larger clients.

## Customer Portal & Self-Service Platform questions

**What can customers do in a portal?**

Typical portals let customers check order or project status, view and pay invoices, download documents, submit requests, and manage account contacts.

**Is a customer portal secure?**

Yes. We use encrypted connections, role-based permissions, and secure authentication, and sensitive files are stored with access controls rather than sent by email.

**Does the portal replace our existing software?**

No. It sits on top of your billing, CRM, and project tools and shows customers the right slice of that data.
