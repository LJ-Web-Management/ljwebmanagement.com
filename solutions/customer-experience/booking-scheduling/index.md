Overview

## What Booking & Scheduling Platform includes

A custom booking and scheduling system built for businesses that run on appointments, reservations, or service calls: real estate showings, salon bookings, consultations, rentals, or facility reservations. Clients see live availability and book directly online, automated confirmations and reminders cut down on no-shows, and staff get a centralized calendar with buffer times, resource assignment, and conflict prevention built in. The platform integrates with your existing calendar (Google, Outlook) and payment processor for deposits or full prepayment, and reporting dashboards show booking volume, cancellation rates, and staff utilization so managers can plan capacity instead of guessing.

### Who it's for

- Appointment-based businesses booking by phone
- Rental and facility operators managing resources
- Teams with frequent no-shows and last-minute cancellations

FAQ

## Booking & Scheduling Platform questions

**How much do reminders reduce no-shows?**

Automated text and email reminders with easy confirm and reschedule options typically reduce no-shows significantly, and deposits reduce them further for high-value appointments.

**Can customers book specific staff or rooms?**

Yes. The system handles staff preferences, skills, rooms, equipment, and travel time so bookings are always feasible.

**Will it sync with our existing calendars?**

Yes. Bookings sync two-way with Google and Outlook calendars so personal commitments block availability automatically.
