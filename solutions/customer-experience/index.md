## Customer Experience & Support questions

**How much do reminders reduce no-shows?**

Automated text and email reminders with easy confirm and reschedule options typically reduce no-shows significantly, and deposits reduce them further for high-value appointments.

**When should a business move from email to a help desk?**

As soon as more than one person answers customer emails, or when requests start slipping. A help desk prevents duplicates, shows ownership, and measures response times.

**What can customers do in a portal?**

Typical portals let customers check order or project status, view and pay invoices, download documents, submit requests, and manage account contacts.

**What is an omnichannel inbox?**

It is one workspace where messages from email, text, chat, and social platforms arrive together, so the team can assign, answer, and track every conversation in one place.

**Do callers know they are speaking with AI?**

We recommend being transparent. A short disclosure is standard, and modern voice AI is natural enough that most callers simply get their question answered or their appointment booked.

**How much can a knowledge base reduce support tickets?**

A well-organized knowledge base connected to your ticket form and chat can deflect a meaningful share of simple questions. The exact reduction depends on how repetitive your requests are.
