Overview

## What Omnichannel Shared Inbox includes

A shared inbox that brings email, SMS, website chat, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Google Business messages into one place your whole team can work from. Conversations are assigned to the right person, internal notes keep context in the thread, and every customer's history across channels appears in one timeline. Automations handle after-hours replies, tag and route conversations by topic, and push leads into your CRM, while reporting shows response time by channel and team member.

## Omnichannel Shared Inbox questions

**What is an omnichannel inbox?**

It is one workspace where messages from email, text, chat, and social platforms arrive together, so the team can assign, answer, and track every conversation in one place.

**Can we keep our existing business phone number for texting?**

In most cases yes. Landlines and VoIP numbers can usually be text-enabled so customers keep reaching you at the number they know.

**Does it work with our CRM?**

Yes. Conversations and new contacts sync to your CRM so sales and support share the same history.
