Overview

## What Learning Management System (LMS) Deployment includes

A fully configured learning management platform used to onboard new employees, deliver ongoing compliance training, or run certification programs, complete with course-building tools, progress tracking, automated reminders for incomplete training, and reporting dashboards for managers and HR. This offering includes initial setup, custom branding, content migration or creation, and integration with existing HR systems so training completion data flows automatically into employee records.

### Who it's for

- Companies with mandatory compliance training
- Franchises and multi-location brands standardizing training
- Organizations selling courses or certifications

## Learning Management System (LMS) Deployment questions

**What is an LMS?**

A learning management system delivers online courses, tracks who completed what, and reports on training across your organization.

**Can we sell courses to customers with an LMS?**

Yes. Many LMS platforms support e-commerce, so you can sell courses, memberships, or certifications to external learners.

**Can you create the course content?**

Yes. We can convert existing manuals and slide decks into online courses, or produce new content with your subject matter experts.
