Overview

## What Applicant Tracking & Recruiting Platform includes

An applicant tracking system that posts jobs to your careers page and major job boards, collects applications in one pipeline, and moves candidates forward automatically. Knockout questions and AI-assisted screening surface qualified applicants, interview scheduling happens through self-booking links, and candidates get timely status updates so none are left wondering. Offer letters are generated and signed electronically, and hired candidates flow straight into onboarding and payroll.

## Applicant Tracking & Recruiting Platform questions

**What is an applicant tracking system?**

An ATS collects job applications from every source into one pipeline and helps the hiring team screen, interview, communicate with, and hire candidates efficiently.

**Is AI resume screening fair?**

AI should assist, not decide. We configure screening around job-related criteria, keep humans in charge of decisions, and review outcomes for bias.

**Can candidates apply by text?**

Yes. Text-to-apply and short mobile applications are especially effective for hourly roles.
