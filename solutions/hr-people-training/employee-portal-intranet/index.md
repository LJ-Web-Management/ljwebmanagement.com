Overview

## What Employee Portal & Intranet includes

A branded employee portal that gives every team member one place to find announcements, policies, procedures, forms, benefits information, and links to the tools they use. Requests for IT help, equipment, time off, or reimbursements are submitted through simple forms that route to the right approver, onboarding checklists guide new hires through their first weeks, and a searchable directory helps people find who does what. Mobile access means frontline staff stay informed too.

## Employee Portal & Intranet questions

**What is the difference between an intranet and an employee portal?**

The terms overlap. An intranet usually focuses on news and documents, while a portal adds self-service requests, onboarding, and links into HR and IT systems. We typically build both together.

**Can frontline employees without email use it?**

Yes. Mobile apps and SMS login let deskless workers access the portal on their phones.

**Can we build it on Microsoft 365?**

Yes. SharePoint and Teams are a strong foundation for companies already using Microsoft 365.
