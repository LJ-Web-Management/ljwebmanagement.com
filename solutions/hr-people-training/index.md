## HR, People & Training questions

**Should HR and payroll be in the same system?**

Usually yes. A single system removes duplicate data entry between HR records and payroll, which is where many payroll errors begin.

**What is an applicant tracking system?**

An ATS collects job applications from every source into one pipeline and helps the hiring team screen, interview, communicate with, and hire candidates efficiently.

**How does geofenced clock-in work?**

Employees can only clock in on their phone when they are within a set distance of the work location, which reduces time theft without extra hardware.

**What is the difference between an intranet and an employee portal?**

The terms overlap. An intranet usually focuses on news and documents, while a portal adds self-service requests, onboarding, and links into HR and IT systems. We typically build both together.

**What is an LMS?**

A learning management system delivers online courses, tracks who completed what, and reports on training across your organization.
