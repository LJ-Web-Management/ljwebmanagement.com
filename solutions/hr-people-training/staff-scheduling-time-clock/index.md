Overview

## What Staff Scheduling & Time Clock System includes

A scheduling and time tracking system for hourly teams that builds schedules from templates and forecasted demand, publishes them to employees' phones, and lets staff swap shifts and pick up open ones with manager approval. Mobile clock-in with geofencing or kiosk photo verification prevents buddy punching, overtime and break rules are enforced as the schedule is built, and approved timesheets flow straight into payroll.

## Staff Scheduling & Time Clock System questions

**How does geofenced clock-in work?**

Employees can only clock in on their phone when they are within a set distance of the work location, which reduces time theft without extra hardware.

**Can the system help control overtime?**

Yes. Managers see projected overtime while building the schedule and get alerts before an employee crosses the threshold.

**Does it work for multiple locations?**

Yes. Each location has its own schedule, with staff able to work across locations and combined labor reporting.
