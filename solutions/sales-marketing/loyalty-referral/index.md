Overview

## What Loyalty & Referral Program Platform includes

A loyalty and referral platform that rewards customers for repeat purchases, visits, and introductions, without punch cards or manual tracking. Points, tiers, or perks are earned automatically from your POS, booking, or e-commerce data, referral links track who sent whom, and rewards are issued the moment they are earned. Automated reminders tell customers about unused rewards and approaching tier upgrades, and reporting shows how much revenue the program drives compared with its cost.

### Who it's for

## Loyalty & Referral Program Platform questions

**Do loyalty programs actually increase revenue?**

When rewards are tied to behavior you want (more visits, higher spend, referrals) and members are reminded of their progress, programs typically increase visit frequency. We set up reporting so you can see the real effect.

**Can customers join without downloading an app?**

Yes. Members can sign up and track rewards by phone number, email, or a web wallet, with no app required.

**How are referrals tracked?**

Each customer gets a unique link or code. When a new customer uses it, both are credited automatically and the referral is recorded in your CRM.
