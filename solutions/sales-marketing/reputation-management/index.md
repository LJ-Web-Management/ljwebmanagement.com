## What Review & Reputation Management Platform includes

A reputation system that asks every happy customer for a review at the right moment, routes unhappy feedback to a manager before it goes public, and monitors reviews across Google, Facebook, Yelp, and industry sites in one inbox. AI-drafted replies help your team respond quickly in your voice, sentiment reporting surfaces recurring complaints by location or staff member, and review widgets put fresh social proof on your website automatically.

FAQ

## Review & Reputation Management Platform questions

**Is it allowed to filter who gets asked for a review?**

Platforms like Google prohibit selectively soliciting only positive reviews. Our setup asks every customer, while giving anyone with a problem an easy way to reach a manager first.

**How quickly should we respond to reviews?**

Within a day or two is a good standard. Prompt, specific replies show prospects you care and can soften the impact of a negative review.

**Can this work across multiple locations?**

Yes. Each location gets its own request flow and reporting, with a combined view for leadership.
