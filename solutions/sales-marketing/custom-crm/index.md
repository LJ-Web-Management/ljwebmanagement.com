Overview

## What Custom CRM Platform includes

A fully custom-built customer relationship management platform designed around your actual sales process instead of forcing it into someone else's rigid workflow. This includes pipeline and deal-stage tracking, contact and account management, activity logging, and role-based access for sales, support, and leadership teams. The platform integrates with your existing email, calendar, phone system, and marketing tools so every interaction is captured automatically instead of relying on manual data entry. Custom reporting dashboards give leadership real visibility into pipeline health and rep performance, and the system scales with the business instead of charging per-seat fees that grow faster than your team does.

## Custom CRM Platform questions

**Should we build a custom CRM or customize HubSpot or Salesforce?**

Most businesses are best served by configuring and automating an established CRM. A fully custom CRM makes sense when your process is unusual, you need deep integration with operational systems, or per-seat costs have become hard to justify. We help you decide on a free call.

**Can you migrate our existing contacts and deals?**

Yes. We clean, deduplicate, and migrate contacts, companies, deals, and activity history so your team starts with trustworthy data.

**How do you get reps to actually use the CRM?**

By removing the busywork. When emails, calls, and meetings log themselves and tasks are created automatically, reps only enter what only they know.
