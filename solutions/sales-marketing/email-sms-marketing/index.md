A complete email and text messaging program set up for deliverability, compliance, and repeat revenue. We authenticate your sending domain, design branded templates, build welcome, reminder, re-engagement, and post-purchase flows, and set up two-way SMS with proper consent capture and opt-out handling. Your list is segmented so each campaign reaches the right customers, and reporting shows revenue per message, not just opens and clicks.

## Email & SMS Marketing System questions

**Is SMS marketing legal for my business?**

Yes, with proper consent. We set up clear opt-in capture, keep consent records, honor STOP replies automatically, and follow quiet-hours rules to keep your program compliant.

**Why do my emails go to spam?**

Usually missing domain authentication, poor list hygiene, or sudden volume spikes. We fix authentication, clean the list, and warm up sending so inbox placement improves.

**How often should we email customers?**

Enough to stay top of mind without fatigue, typically weekly or every other week for campaigns, plus automated flows triggered by each customer's actions.
