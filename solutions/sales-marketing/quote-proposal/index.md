Overview

## What Quoting & Proposal Software includes

A configure-price-quote system that turns your pricing rules, product options, and templates into fast, accurate quotes and professional proposals. Sales staff pick options and the system calculates pricing, discounts, and margins, then generates a branded proposal the customer can review, sign, and pay a deposit on online. Approval rules catch discounts outside policy, accepted quotes convert into jobs, orders, or invoices automatically, and follow-up reminders go out for proposals that have not been opened or signed.

### Who it's for

- Manufacturers and distributors with complex pricing
- Agencies and consultants sending custom proposals

## Quoting & Proposal Software (CPQ) questions

**What does CPQ stand for?**

Configure, price, quote. CPQ software lets sales staff select products or services, applies your pricing rules automatically, and produces a finished quote or proposal.

**Can customers accept and pay online?**

Yes. Proposals include e-signature and an optional deposit or full payment through your processor, so jobs can be scheduled the moment they are accepted.

**Will this work for custom or project-based pricing?**

Yes. We model labor rates, materials, markups, and optional line items so even custom projects are priced consistently.
