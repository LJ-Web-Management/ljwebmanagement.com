Overview

## What Marketing Automation Platform includes

A marketing automation system that captures leads from every channel, segments them by interest and behavior, and nurtures them with timely email, SMS, and retargeting until they are ready to buy. We design the lifecycle stages, build the nurture journeys, set up lead scoring so sales only talks to warm prospects, and connect everything to your CRM so marketing and sales share one view of each contact. Reporting ties campaigns to pipeline and revenue, so budget goes where it works.

### Who it's for

## Marketing Automation Platform questions

**What is the difference between marketing automation and email marketing?**

Email marketing sends messages to lists. Marketing automation reacts to each person's behavior across channels, scores their interest, and coordinates with sales, so the right message goes out at the right time without manual effort.

**Do we need a big list for marketing automation to work?**

No. Even a few hundred leads a month benefit from fast follow-up, nurture sequences, and scoring. The system scales as the list grows.

**Which platform do you recommend?**

HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, and Klaviyo cover most needs depending on whether you sell B2B, services, or e-commerce. We recommend based on your channels, CRM, and budget.
