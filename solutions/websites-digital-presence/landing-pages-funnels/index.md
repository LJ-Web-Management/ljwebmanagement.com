## What Landing Pages & Conversion Funnels includes

High-converting landing pages and multi-step funnels built for specific campaigns, offers, and audiences. Each page is designed around one action, tested for speed and clarity, and wired to automated follow-up so every lead gets a text and email within minutes, is tagged by campaign in your CRM, and is nudged toward booking until they convert or opt out. Conversion tracking is set up for Google Ads, Meta, and your analytics, so you can see exactly which campaigns produce revenue, not just clicks.

## Landing Pages & Conversion Funnels questions

**Why not send ads to my homepage?**

Homepages serve many audiences, so they convert poorly for any one campaign. A landing page matches the ad's promise and asks for one action, which usually lifts conversion rates noticeably.

**How fast should leads be contacted?**

As fast as possible. Response speed is one of the strongest predictors of whether a lead converts, which is why every funnel we build sends an automatic first touch within minutes.

**Can you track leads all the way to revenue?**

Yes. We capture campaign data on every lead and pass it into your CRM, so closed deals can be reported back by campaign and even sent to ad platforms as offline conversions.
