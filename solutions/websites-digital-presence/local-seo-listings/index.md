Overview

## What Local SEO & Listings Management includes

A system for ranking in local search and Google Maps across one or many locations. We clean up and sync your business listings across Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp, and industry directories, build optimized location and service-area pages, and automate review requests after every job so your rating and review count keep climbing. Google Business Profile posts, Q&A, and photo updates are scheduled, and a monthly report shows rankings, calls, direction requests, and leads by location.

### Who it's for

- Contractors and home service companies that live on map leads

## Local SEO & Listings Management questions

**What affects local search rankings most?**

Relevance, distance, and prominence. In practice that means a complete and accurate Google Business Profile, consistent listings, strong on-site location pages, and a steady flow of genuine reviews.

**Can you manage listings for many locations?**

Yes. The system is designed for multi-location brands, with one dashboard for listings, reviews, and reporting across every location.

**Do you write fake or incentivized reviews?**

No. We automate honest review requests to real customers after real jobs, which is what platforms allow and what builds lasting trust.
