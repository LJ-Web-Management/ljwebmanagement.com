Overview

## What E-Commerce Store Development includes

A complete online store built on Shopify, WooCommerce, or a headless platform, designed to sell and to run without manual order handling. We set up your catalog, variants, shipping and tax rules, and payment options, then connect the store to your inventory, fulfillment, accounting, and email marketing so stock levels stay accurate, orders flow to the warehouse automatically, and every sale lands in your books. Abandoned cart recovery, post-purchase sequences, and review requests are configured from the start, so the store grows revenue on its own.

## E-Commerce Store Development questions

**Which e-commerce platform should I use?**

Shopify suits most retailers that want reliability and a large app ecosystem. WooCommerce fits businesses already on WordPress that want more control. Headless builds make sense for complex catalogs or custom buying experiences. We recommend based on your catalog, channels, and team.

**Can you migrate my existing store?**

Yes. We migrate products, customers, and order history, and set up redirects so you keep the search rankings your old URLs earned.

**Do you connect the store to my point of sale?**

Yes. We sync inventory and customers between your POS and online store so in-store and online sales draw from the same stock.
