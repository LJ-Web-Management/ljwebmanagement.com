## What Custom Website Design & Development includes

A fully custom-built website designed and developed around how your business actually operates, not a template with your logo swapped in. This offering covers UX and visual design, responsive development, and on-page and technical SEO (structured data, page speed optimization, keyword-mapped content, and search console setup) so the site is built to rank, not just to look good. For businesses that sell in person as well as online, we integrate the site with your POS system, booking software, CRM, and payment processor so orders, appointments, and customer records stay in sync across every channel. Hosting, security updates, analytics, and ongoing maintenance are included, so launch day is the starting point, not the finish line.

## Custom Website Design & Development questions

**How is a custom website different from a template?**

A template forces your content into someone else's layout. A custom site starts from your services, customers, and sales process, so every page is structured to answer buyer questions and move them toward booking or buying.

**Will my new website rank on Google?**

We build in the technical foundations search engines reward (speed, structured data, crawlable architecture, and keyword-mapped pages). Rankings also depend on competition and content over time, which is why ongoing care is part of the plan.

**Can I edit the site myself after launch?**

Yes. We build on a content management system you can update without code, and we train your team on it. Larger changes can be handled under the care plan.
