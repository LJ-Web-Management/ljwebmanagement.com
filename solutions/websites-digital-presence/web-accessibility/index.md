Overview

## What Website Accessibility (ADA & WCAG) Remediation includes

A hands-on accessibility program that audits your website against WCAG 2.1 AA, fixes the issues in code and content, and keeps the site compliant as it changes. We combine automated scanning with manual keyboard and screen reader testing, remediate navigation, forms, color contrast, images, video captions, and documents, and publish an accessibility statement. Ongoing monitoring flags new issues introduced by content updates before they become a problem, and training helps your team publish accessible content by default.

## Website Accessibility (ADA & WCAG) Remediation questions

**Do accessibility overlay widgets make a site compliant?**

No. Overlays do not fix the underlying code and can interfere with assistive technology. Real compliance comes from fixing templates, content, and components directly, which is what we do.

**What standard should my website meet?**

WCAG 2.1 Level AA is the widely used benchmark referenced in many legal settlements and procurement requirements, and it is what we build and test to.

**How do we stay compliant after remediation?**

Ongoing monitoring scans new and changed pages, and we train your content editors on headings, alt text, link text, and accessible documents.
