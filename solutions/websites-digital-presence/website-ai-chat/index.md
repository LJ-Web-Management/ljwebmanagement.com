Overview

## What AI Website Chat & Lead Capture Assistant includes

An AI chat assistant trained on your services, pricing guidelines, policies, and FAQs that answers website visitors instantly, day or night. It qualifies leads with the questions your team would ask, books appointments into your calendar, collects contact details into your CRM, and hands off to a human with the full conversation when a question needs one. Guardrails keep answers within approved information, and every conversation is logged so you can see what customers ask and improve the site and the assistant over time.

## AI Website Chat & Lead Capture Assistant questions

**Will an AI assistant give customers wrong answers?**

We ground the assistant in your approved content and set guardrails so it declines rather than guesses on topics like pricing exceptions, legal, or medical advice. Anything outside scope goes to a person.

**Can it book appointments?**

Yes. It checks real availability in your calendar or booking system and confirms the appointment in the chat, with reminders sent automatically.

**How is this different from a regular chatbot?**

Older chatbots follow rigid scripts. An AI assistant understands free-form questions, answers in natural language, and still follows the rules and handoff paths you set.
