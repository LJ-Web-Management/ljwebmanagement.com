Overview

## What Data Warehouse & Pipeline Setup includes

A modern data stack that extracts data from your business systems, loads it into a cloud warehouse, and transforms it into clean, documented tables ready for reporting, analysis, and AI. Pipelines refresh on a schedule or in near real time, history is preserved even when source systems overwrite records, and data quality tests catch problems before they reach a dashboard. Your team gets a single source of truth that every report, model, and integration can rely on.

## Data Warehouse & Pipeline Setup questions

**What is a data warehouse?**

A central database optimized for analysis that stores cleaned, combined data from your business systems, including history, so reporting and analytics run from one trusted source.

**Is a data warehouse expensive to run?**

For most small and midsize businesses, cloud warehouse costs are modest because you pay for storage and queries used. We design to keep costs predictable.

**How often is the data refreshed?**

Anywhere from daily to every few minutes, depending on how current the reports need to be and what the source systems allow.
