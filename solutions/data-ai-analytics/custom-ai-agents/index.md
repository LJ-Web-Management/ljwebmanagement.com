## What Custom AI Agents & Assistants includes

Custom AI agents built to handle multi-step work that used to require a person: researching leads, triaging inboxes, drafting replies and proposals, updating records, answering staff questions from internal documents, and coordinating tasks across your tools. Each agent is connected to the systems it needs through secure, permissioned integrations, grounded in your data, and given clear limits, with human approval on actions that carry risk. Logs and evaluations show what each agent did and how well, so you can expand its responsibilities with confidence.

## Custom AI Agents & Assistants questions

**What is an AI agent?**

An AI agent is a system that uses a language model to plan and complete multi-step tasks, using tools like your CRM, email, or databases, rather than only answering questions in a chat window.

**Is it safe to let AI update our systems?**

With the right design, yes. Agents get only the permissions they need, risky actions require human approval, and every action is logged.

**Will AI agents replace our staff?**

In practice, agents remove repetitive work so people can focus on customers, judgment calls, and growth. Most clients redeploy time rather than reduce headcount.
