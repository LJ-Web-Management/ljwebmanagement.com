Overview

## What Business Intelligence & Reporting Dashboards includes

A centralized reporting and business intelligence platform that pulls data from your CRM, ERP, POS, marketing tools, and financial systems into live dashboards instead of a monthly spreadsheet someone has to manually rebuild. Custom dashboards are built around the metrics that actually matter to your business (revenue, margin, customer acquisition cost, pipeline velocity, whatever leadership needs to see) with drill-down detail available on demand. Automated report delivery pushes key numbers to stakeholders' inboxes on a schedule, and role-based access means every team sees the data relevant to them without wading through everyone else's.

## Business Intelligence & Reporting Dashboards questions

**Which BI tool should we use?**

Power BI is a strong default for Microsoft shops, Looker Studio suits Google-centric and budget-conscious teams, and Tableau excels at complex analysis. We recommend based on your data sources and users.

**Do we need a data warehouse for dashboards?**

Not always. Simple dashboards can connect directly to source systems. A warehouse becomes worthwhile when you combine many sources or need historical trends.

**How do we make sure the numbers are right?**

We document KPI definitions with your leadership, reconcile dashboard totals to source systems, and add data quality checks.
