## What Forecasting & Predictive Analytics includes

Forecasting and predictive models built on your historical data to answer the questions that drive planning: how much will we sell, how much stock and staff will we need, which customers are likely to leave, and which leads are most likely to close. Models are trained and validated on your data, refreshed automatically, and delivered inside the tools your team already uses, such as reorder suggestions in your inventory system or churn-risk flags in your CRM, with plain-language explanations of what drives each prediction.

## Forecasting & Predictive Analytics questions

**How much data do we need for forecasting?**

Generally at least one to two years of consistent history for seasonal forecasting. Simpler models can work with less, and we assess your data before committing to an approach.

**How accurate will the forecasts be?**

Accuracy depends on your data and how predictable your business is. We measure accuracy on held-out history and report it honestly before you rely on the model.

**Do we need a data scientist to use the results?**

No. Predictions are delivered as simple numbers, flags, and suggestions inside the tools your team already uses.
