## Data, AI & Analytics questions

**Which BI tool should we use?**

Power BI is a strong default for Microsoft shops, Looker Studio suits Google-centric and budget-conscious teams, and Tableau excels at complex analysis. We recommend based on your data sources and users.

**What is a data warehouse?**

A central database optimized for analysis that stores cleaned, combined data from your business systems, including history, so reporting and analytics run from one trusted source.

**What is an AI agent?**

An AI agent is a system that uses a language model to plan and complete multi-step tasks, using tools like your CRM, email, or databases, rather than only answering questions in a chat window.

**How accurate is AI document extraction?**

Accuracy is high on typed documents and improves with consistent layouts. Every field gets a confidence score, and anything below the threshold goes to a person, so errors are caught before posting.

**How much data do we need for forecasting?**

Generally at least one to two years of consistent history for seasonal forecasting. Simpler models can work with less, and we assess your data before committing to an approach.
