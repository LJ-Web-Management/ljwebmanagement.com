Overview

## What AI Document Processing & Data Extraction includes

An AI document processing pipeline that reads incoming documents (invoices, purchase orders, applications, intake forms, bills of lading, contracts, and scanned paperwork), extracts the fields you need, validates them against your rules and records, and posts the results into your ERP, CRM, or database. Documents arrive from email, upload portals, or scanners, low-confidence fields are sent to a quick human review queue, and every document is filed with its extracted data for audit.

## AI Document Processing & Data Extraction questions

**How accurate is AI document extraction?**

Accuracy is high on typed documents and improves with consistent layouts. Every field gets a confidence score, and anything below the threshold goes to a person, so errors are caught before posting.

**Can it read handwriting?**

Modern models handle clear handwriting reasonably well. For critical handwritten fields we route to human review.

**What happens to our documents and data?**

We use enterprise AI services with data protection terms, store documents in your environment where possible, and restrict access to those who need it.
