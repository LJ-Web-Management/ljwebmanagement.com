Overview

## What Procurement & Vendor Management Platform includes

A procurement system that standardizes how your organization requests, approves, orders, and pays for goods and services. Employees submit purchase requests from a catalog or form, approvals route by amount and department, purchase orders go to vendors automatically, and receipts are matched against invoices before payment. Vendor records hold contracts, insurance certificates, W-9s, and performance history with expiration alerts, and spend reporting reveals where to consolidate suppliers and negotiate better terms.

### Who it's for

- Growing companies without a formal purchasing process
- Multi-location operators buying from many suppliers
- Manufacturers and contractors managing subcontractors

## Procurement & Vendor Management Platform questions

**What is three-way matching?**

It compares the purchase order, the receiving record, and the vendor invoice before payment, so you only pay for what was ordered and delivered at the agreed price.

**Is a procurement system only for large companies?**

No. Even a 20-person company benefits from simple request and approval rules, especially when several people can spend company money.

**Can vendors submit documents themselves?**

Yes. A vendor portal lets suppliers upload W-9s, insurance certificates, and invoices directly.
