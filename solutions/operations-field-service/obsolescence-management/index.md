Overview

## What Obsolescence Management Platform includes

A centralized system that tracks the lifecycle status of every component, part, or piece of equipment a business relies on, flagging items approaching end-of-life or end-of-support long before they become a supply chain emergency. The platform continuously cross-references bill-of-materials data against manufacturer lifecycle notices, alerts procurement and engineering teams to at-risk parts, and recommends qualified alternatives, giving manufacturing, defense, aerospace, and industrial clients a proactive way to avoid costly last-time-buy scrambles and unplanned production line stoppages.

### Who it's for

- Electronics, aerospace, and defense manufacturers
- Industrial operators maintaining long-lived equipment
- IT teams managing hardware and software lifecycles

FAQ

## Obsolescence Management Platform questions

**What is obsolescence management?**

It is the practice of tracking when components, equipment, and software will be discontinued or lose support, and planning replacements or last-time buys before shortages disrupt operations.

**Which industries need obsolescence management?**

Any business with long product lifecycles or critical equipment, especially electronics, aerospace, defense, medical devices, industrial automation, and utilities.

**Can it monitor software end of support too?**

Yes. Operating systems, firmware, and business software can be tracked alongside physical components.
