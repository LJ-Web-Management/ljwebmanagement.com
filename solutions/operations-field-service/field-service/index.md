Overview

## What Field Service Management Platform includes

A dispatch and work-order management platform for businesses that send technicians, crews, or inspectors into the field: home services, pest control, HVAC, landscaping, or property maintenance. Jobs are created, assigned, and routed automatically based on technician location and availability, field staff get a mobile-friendly view of job details, photos, and digital sign-off, and customers receive automatic on-my-way and completion notifications. The platform tracks parts and labor for accurate job costing, and real-time dashboards give dispatchers full visibility into every crew's status without a single phone call.

## Field Service Management Platform questions

**What is field service management software?**

It is software that schedules, dispatches, and tracks work done at customer locations, combining job scheduling, mobile work orders, customer notifications, and invoicing in one system.

**Can technicians use it offline?**

Yes. Mobile apps cache job details and sync photos, notes, and signatures once a connection returns.

**Should we use ServiceTitan or Jobber or a custom system?**

Off-the-shelf platforms fit most trades. We implement and automate them, and build custom systems only when your workflow, compliance, or integrations require it.
