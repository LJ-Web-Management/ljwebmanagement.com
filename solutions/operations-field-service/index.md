## Operations & Field Service questions

**What is field service management software?**

It is software that schedules, dispatches, and tracks work done at customer locations, combining job scheduling, mobile work orders, customer notifications, and invoicing in one system.

**When do we need an inventory system instead of spreadsheets?**

When you sell across more than one channel, hold stock in more than one location, or regularly oversell or run out. At that point spreadsheets cannot keep up with real-time changes.

**Which project management tool should we use?**

It depends on the work. Asana, Monday.com, and ClickUp suit services teams, while Procore and Buildertrend fit construction. We configure the right tool and connect it to sales and billing.

**Can we track equipment that does not have an engine?**

Yes. Battery-powered GPS tags and Bluetooth beacons track trailers, tools, and attachments.

**What is three-way matching?**

It compares the purchase order, the receiving record, and the vendor invoice before payment, so you only pay for what was ordered and delivered at the agreed price.

**What is obsolescence management?**

It is the practice of tracking when components, equipment, and software will be discontinued or lose support, and planning replacements or last-time buys before shortages disrupt operations.
