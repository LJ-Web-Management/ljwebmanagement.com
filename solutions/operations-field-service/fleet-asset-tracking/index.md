Overview

## What Fleet & Asset Tracking System includes

A fleet and asset tracking system that combines GPS telematics, equipment tags, and maintenance scheduling in one view. Vehicles and high-value equipment are tracked in real time, preventive maintenance is scheduled automatically by mileage, engine hours, or date, and driver inspections are completed digitally. Alerts flag speeding, idling, after-hours use, and equipment leaving a job site, and reports show utilization so you can right-size the fleet and stop renting equipment you already own.

## Fleet & Asset Tracking System questions

**Can we track equipment that does not have an engine?**

Yes. Battery-powered GPS tags and Bluetooth beacons track trailers, tools, and attachments.

**Does fleet tracking help with insurance?**

Many insurers offer discounts for telematics, and dashcam footage and driving data can help resolve claims.

**How does preventive maintenance scheduling work?**

Mileage and engine hours come from telematics automatically, and work orders are created when service thresholds are reached.
