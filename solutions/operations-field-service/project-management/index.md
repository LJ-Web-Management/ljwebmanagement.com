Overview

## What Project & Job Management Platform includes

A project and job management system configured around how your team delivers work, whether that is construction jobs, client engagements, or production runs. Projects are created from signed quotes with templates for tasks, milestones, and budgets, team members see what is due and blocked, and time and expenses are tracked against the budget in real time. Clients receive automatic progress updates and approvals, change orders are captured and billed, and dashboards show utilization, profitability, and at-risk projects across the portfolio.

FAQ

## Project & Job Management Platform questions

**Which project management tool should we use?**

It depends on the work. Asana, Monday.com, and ClickUp suit services teams, while Procore and Buildertrend fit construction. We configure the right tool and connect it to sales and billing.

**Can projects be created automatically from sales?**

Yes. When a deal closes or a quote is signed, the project, tasks, budget, and client portal access can be created automatically.

**How do we track project profitability?**

By logging time, materials, and expenses against each project's budget and comparing them with what was billed, which the system does in real time.
