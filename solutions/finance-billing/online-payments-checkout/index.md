## What Online Payments & Checkout Integration includes

A payments setup that lets customers pay the way they prefer, online, in person, by text link, or on recurring autopay, with every transaction flowing cleanly into your accounting and CRM. We select and configure the right processor, build secure checkout into your website, invoices, and booking flows, set up text-to-pay and saved payment methods, and automate payout reconciliation so fees, refunds, and deposits match your bank statements without manual work.

## Online Payments & Checkout Integration questions

**Is it safe to take payments on our website?**

Yes, when card data is handled by a PCI-compliant processor through hosted fields or checkout pages, so sensitive card numbers never touch your servers.

**What is text-to-pay?**

A secure payment link sent by SMS. Customers tap it, pay with a card or wallet, and the payment is recorded against their invoice automatically.

**Can you help lower our processing fees?**

We review your volume and mix of transactions and recommend processor and pricing options, including ACH for larger invoices.
