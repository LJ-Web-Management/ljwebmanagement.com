## Finance, Billing & Payments questions

**What is dunning?**

Dunning is the process of recovering failed recurring payments through automatic retries, card updater services, and reminder messages asking the customer to update their payment details.

**How much faster do customers pay with automated reminders?**

Most businesses see invoices paid sooner once invoices go out immediately with a payment link and reminders run on a consistent schedule, because nothing depends on someone remembering to follow up.

**Can AI read our vendor invoices accurately?**

Modern document AI extracts vendor, dates, amounts, and line items with high accuracy, and anything below a confidence threshold is flagged for a quick human check before posting.

**Is it safe to take payments on our website?**

Yes, when card data is handled by a PCI-compliant processor through hosted fields or checkout pages, so sensitive card numbers never touch your servers.

**How long should a month-end close take?**

Many small and midsize businesses aim for five to ten business days. Automating reconciliations and recurring entries is usually the biggest lever for getting there.
