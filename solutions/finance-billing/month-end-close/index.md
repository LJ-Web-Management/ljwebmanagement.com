Overview

## What Month-End Close & Reconciliation Automation includes

A close management system that turns month-end from a scramble into a checklist. Bank and card transactions are reconciled automatically against your ledger, recurring journal entries and accruals are posted on schedule, intercompany and clearing accounts are matched, and exceptions are routed to the right person with supporting documents attached. A close checklist tracks every task, owner, and due date, and management reports are generated and delivered as soon as the books are final.

## Month-End Close & Reconciliation Automation questions

**How long should a month-end close take?**

Many small and midsize businesses aim for five to ten business days. Automating reconciliations and recurring entries is usually the biggest lever for getting there.

**Can reconciliations be fully automated?**

Most matching can be automated with rules and AI. A person still reviews exceptions and signs off, which keeps controls intact.

**Does this work with our accountant or bookkeeper?**

Yes. External accountants can work inside the same checklist and reconciliation tools, which usually speeds up their work too.
