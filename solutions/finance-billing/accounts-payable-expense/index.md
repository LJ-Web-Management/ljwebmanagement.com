Overview

## What Accounts Payable & Expense Management includes

A payables and expense system that captures vendor bills from email, reads them with AI document extraction, codes them to the right accounts, and routes them for approval before scheduling payment. Employee expenses are submitted from a phone with receipt photos and checked against policy, corporate card transactions are reconciled automatically, and every approval is logged for audit. Finance gets a clear view of upcoming obligations, and vendors are paid on time without late fees or duplicate payments.

### Who it's for

- Companies processing dozens to thousands of bills a month
- Teams with employees spending on company cards
- Businesses that need audit-ready approval trails

## Accounts Payable & Expense Management questions

**Can AI read our vendor invoices accurately?**

Modern document AI extracts vendor, dates, amounts, and line items with high accuracy, and anything below a confidence threshold is flagged for a quick human check before posting.

**How do approvals work?**

Rules route each bill to the right approver based on amount, vendor, department, or project, with reminders and escalation if it sits too long.

**Does this help prevent payment fraud?**

Yes. Duplicate invoice detection, vendor bank detail change alerts, and separation of duties reduce common payables fraud risks.
