    Custom Website Design & Development

    A fully custom-built website designed and developed around how your business actually operates, not a template with your logo swapped in. This offering covers UX and visual design, responsive development, and on-page and technical SEO (structured data, page speed optimization, keyword-mapped content, and search console setup) so the site is built to rank, not just to look good. For businesses that sell in person as well as online, we integrate the site with your POS system, booking software, CRM, and payment processor so orders, appointments, and customer records stay in sync across every channel. Hosting, security updates, analytics, and ongoing maintenance are included, so launch day is the starting point, not the finish line.

    Obsolescence Management Platform

    A centralized system that tracks the lifecycle status of every component, part, or piece of equipment a business relies on, flagging items approaching end-of-life or end-of-support long before they become a supply chain emergency. The platform continuously cross-references bill-of-materials data against manufacturer lifecycle notices, alerts procurement and engineering teams to at-risk parts, and recommends qualified alternatives, giving manufacturing, defense, aerospace, and industrial clients a proactive way to avoid costly last-time-buy scrambles and unplanned production line stoppages.

    ERP Implementation & Integration Solutions

    End-to-end enterprise resource planning services covering selection, implementation, customization, and integration of ERP systems (such as NetSuite, SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics, or Odoo) into a client's existing operations. This offering unifies finance, inventory, procurement, HR, and reporting into a single source of truth, and includes data migration from legacy systems, workflow configuration, staff training, and ongoing support so growing businesses can replace disconnected spreadsheets and standalone tools with one integrated backbone.

    Talk to us about this

    Managed SaaS Delivery & Support Service

    A subscription-based service where a business's custom software, internal tools, or automation stack is hosted, monitored, secured, and maintained on the client's behalf. This includes uptime monitoring, security patching, feature updates, technical support, and scaling infrastructure as usage grows, allowing clients to treat their internal software the same way they'd treat any other reliable SaaS product, without needing an in-house DevOps or IT team.

    Learning Management System (LMS) Deployment

    A fully configured learning management platform used to onboard new employees, deliver ongoing compliance training, or run certification programs, complete with course-building tools, progress tracking, automated reminders for incomplete training, and reporting dashboards for managers and HR. This offering includes initial setup, custom branding, content migration or creation, and integration with existing HR systems so training completion data flows automatically into employee records.

    Restaurant Point of Sale (POS) System

    A complete point-of-sale platform built for restaurants, cafes, and quick-service operations, covering order entry, table and tab management, and kitchen display integration in one connected system. Checkout closes out tabs and records how each guest paid (cash, card, or other) for clean bookkeeping, it pairs with your existing card processor or terminal rather than handling card charges itself. The platform also includes menu management, role-based staff logins, and real-time reporting dashboards, giving owners and managers accurate visibility into top-selling items, average tab size, and daily revenue without reconciling numbers across separate tools. This offering includes hardware setup, menu configuration, and staff training to get your team live fast.

    Custom CRM Platform

    A fully custom-built customer relationship management platform designed around your actual sales process instead of forcing it into someone else's rigid workflow. This includes pipeline and deal-stage tracking, contact and account management, activity logging, and role-based access for sales, support, and leadership teams. The platform integrates with your existing email, calendar, phone system, and marketing tools so every interaction is captured automatically instead of relying on manual data entry. Custom reporting dashboards give leadership real visibility into pipeline health and rep performance, and the system scales with the business instead of charging per-seat fees that grow faster than your team does.

    Talk to us about this

    Booking & Scheduling Platform

    A custom booking and scheduling system built for businesses that run on appointments, reservations, or service calls: real estate showings, salon bookings, consultations, rentals, or facility reservations. Clients see live availability and book directly online, automated confirmations and reminders cut down on no-shows, and staff get a centralized calendar with buffer times, resource assignment, and conflict prevention built in. The platform integrates with your existing calendar (Google, Outlook) and payment processor for deposits or full prepayment, and reporting dashboards show booking volume, cancellation rates, and staff utilization so managers can plan capacity instead of guessing.

    Field Service Management Platform

    A dispatch and work-order management platform for businesses that send technicians, crews, or inspectors into the field: home services, pest control, HVAC, landscaping, or property maintenance. Jobs are created, assigned, and routed automatically based on technician location and availability, field staff get a mobile-friendly view of job details, photos, and digital sign-off, and customers receive automatic on-my-way and completion notifications. The platform tracks parts and labor for accurate job costing, and real-time dashboards give dispatchers full visibility into every crew's status without a single phone call.

    Membership & Subscription Billing Platform

    A recurring billing and membership management platform for businesses that run on subscriptions, memberships, or recurring service plans: gyms, subscription boxes, SaaS products, or membership-based clubs. The platform handles plan tiers, automated recurring billing, failed-payment retry logic, and self-service upgrade, downgrade, and cancel flows so members manage their own accounts without generating support tickets. Dunning sequences recover failed payments automatically, usage-based billing is supported for metered plans, and revenue dashboards break down MRR, churn, and lifetime value so leadership always knows where recurring revenue stands.

    Help Desk & Support Ticketing Platform

    A branded support and ticketing platform that gives customers one place to submit issues, track status, and get answers, and gives your support team a shared queue instead of a flooded inbox. Tickets route automatically by category or priority, canned responses and a searchable knowledge base cut down repetitive replies, and SLA timers flag tickets at risk of breaching response commitments before they slip. Built-in reporting shows ticket volume, resolution time, and agent performance, giving support leads the data to staff appropriately instead of reacting to whatever inbox looks worst that day.

    Talk to us about this

    Inventory & Warehouse Management Platform

    A real-time inventory and warehouse management platform that tracks stock levels, locations, and movement across one or multiple warehouses instead of relying on spreadsheets that are out of date the moment they're saved. Barcode and QR scanning speeds receiving and picking, low-stock and reorder-point alerts trigger purchase orders automatically, and the platform integrates with your e-commerce storefront, POS, or ERP so stock counts stay accurate across every sales channel. Batch and lot tracking is available for businesses with expiration dates or compliance requirements, and reporting dashboards flag slow-moving stock before it ties up working capital.

    Talk to us about this

    HR & Payroll Management Platform

    A centralized HR and payroll platform that consolidates employee records, time tracking, benefits administration, and payroll processing into one system instead of five disconnected tools. Employee self-service lets staff request time off, view pay stubs, and update their own information, automated payroll runs handle tax calculations and direct deposit, and compliance tracking flags expiring certifications or required documentation before it becomes a liability. The platform integrates with your existing time clock or scheduling system, and reporting dashboards give HR and leadership visibility into headcount, labor cost, and turnover without exporting data from four different logins.

    Talk to us about this

    Business Intelligence & Reporting Dashboard Platform

    A centralized reporting and business intelligence platform that pulls data from your CRM, ERP, POS, marketing tools, and financial systems into live dashboards instead of a monthly spreadsheet someone has to manually rebuild. Custom dashboards are built around the metrics that actually matter to your business (revenue, margin, customer acquisition cost, pipeline velocity, whatever leadership needs to see) with drill-down detail available on demand. Automated report delivery pushes key numbers to stakeholders' inboxes on a schedule, and role-based access means every team sees the data relevant to them without wading through everyone else's.

    Customer Portal & Self-Service Platform

    A branded customer portal where clients can log in to view order history, account status, invoices, project progress, or service history without calling or emailing your team for updates they could see themselves. Self-service actions (updating payment methods, downloading documents, submitting requests) are handled directly in the portal, cutting down the volume of routine inbound contact your team fields every week. The platform integrates with your existing billing, CRM, or project management system so the data shown is always current, and role-based access supports multiple contacts per account for larger clients.

    Talk to us about this

    Marketplace & Multi-Vendor Platform

    A multi-vendor marketplace platform for businesses connecting multiple sellers, providers, or contractors with customers through a single storefront, think a local services marketplace, a multi-brand e-commerce platform, or a vendor network. The platform handles vendor onboarding and profile management, order or booking routing to the correct vendor, commission calculation, and split payouts, all without manual reconciliation. Vendors get their own dashboard for managing listings, orders, and payouts, and platform admins get oversight tools for approving vendors, resolving disputes, and monitoring marketplace-wide performance from one place.
