Overview

## What Legacy System Modernization & Data Migration includes

A structured program for replacing outdated software, Access databases, on-premise servers, and spreadsheet-based systems with modern cloud platforms. We document what the old system does (including the undocumented workarounds), choose or build the replacement, clean and migrate the data, and run old and new in parallel until results match. Cutover is planned around your busy periods, users are trained before go-live, and historical data stays accessible for reporting and compliance.

FAQ

## Legacy System Modernization & Data Migration questions

**How do you avoid losing data during migration?**

We profile and clean the data first, run trial migrations, reconcile record counts and totals, and keep the legacy system available until the new one is verified.

**Can we migrate in phases?**

Yes. Phased migrations by module, location, or team reduce risk and let users adapt gradually.

**What happens to our old reports?**

We rebuild the reports people rely on in the new system or a BI tool, and archive historical data so it stays accessible.
