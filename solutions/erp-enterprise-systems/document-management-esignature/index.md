Overview

## What Document Management & E-Signature Platform includes

A document system that generates contracts, agreements, and forms from templates using data already in your CRM or ERP, sends them for e-signature, and files the signed copy in the right folder with consistent naming. Documents are searchable by content, permissions control who sees what, retention rules archive or delete records on schedule, and reminders chase unsigned documents and upcoming renewals so nothing expires unnoticed.

FAQ

## Document Management & E-Signature Platform questions

**Are electronic signatures legally binding?**

In the United States, electronic signatures are generally valid under the ESIGN Act and UETA for most business documents. Some documents have special requirements, which we account for.

**Can documents be generated automatically from our CRM?**

Yes. Templates pull names, pricing, terms, and other details directly from CRM or ERP records.

**How do retention policies work?**

Each document type gets a retention period. The system archives or flags documents for deletion when the period ends, keeping you compliant without manual cleanup.
