## What ERP Implementation & Integration includes

End-to-end enterprise resource planning services covering selection, implementation, customization, and integration of ERP systems (such as NetSuite, SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics, or Odoo) into a client's existing operations. This offering unifies finance, inventory, procurement, HR, and reporting into a single source of truth, and includes data migration from legacy systems, workflow configuration, staff training, and ongoing support so growing businesses can replace disconnected spreadsheets and standalone tools with one integrated backbone.

## ERP Implementation & Integration questions

**How do I know if my business needs an ERP?**

Common signs are re-keying data between systems, slow month-end close, inventory inaccuracies, multiple entities or locations, and leadership unable to get timely reports.

**Which ERP is best for a small or midsize business?**

NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, Acumatica, SAP Business One, and Odoo all serve this market. The right choice depends on industry, complexity, budget, and integrations.

**What makes ERP projects fail?**

Unclear requirements, poor data migration, and low user adoption. We address all three with structured discovery, careful data cleanup, and hands-on training.
