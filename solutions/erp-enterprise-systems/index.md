## ERP & Enterprise Systems questions

**How do I know if my business needs an ERP?**

Common signs are re-keying data between systems, slow month-end close, inventory inaccuracies, multiple entities or locations, and leadership unable to get timely reports.

**What is an API integration?**

An API integration connects two software systems through their programming interfaces so they can exchange data automatically, such as sending new orders from a store to an ERP.

**How do you avoid losing data during migration?**

We profile and clean the data first, run trial migrations, reconcile record counts and totals, and keep the legacy system available until the new one is verified.

**Are electronic signatures legally binding?**

In the United States, electronic signatures are generally valid under the ESIGN Act and UETA for most business documents. Some documents have special requirements, which we account for.

**What does a compliance management system do?**

It tracks regulatory requirements, deadlines, licenses, inspections, policies, and incidents, assigns owners, sends reminders, and stores evidence so you can prove compliance.
