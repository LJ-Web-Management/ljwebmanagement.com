Overview

## What Compliance & Risk Management Platform includes

A compliance system that keeps every regulatory obligation, license, certification, inspection, and policy acknowledgment on a schedule with an owner. Deadlines trigger reminders and escalations, evidence is collected and stored with each task, incidents and corrective actions are tracked to closure, and dashboards show compliance status by location, team, or regulation. Audit packages can be generated in minutes because the documentation is captured as the work happens.

FAQ

## Compliance & Risk Management Platform questions

**What does a compliance management system do?**

It tracks regulatory requirements, deadlines, licenses, inspections, policies, and incidents, assigns owners, sends reminders, and stores evidence so you can prove compliance.

**Can it track employee certifications?**

Yes. Each employee's licenses and training are tracked with expiration reminders to them and their manager.

**Does this replace legal advice?**

No. It operationalizes the requirements your advisors identify, making sure the work gets done and documented.
