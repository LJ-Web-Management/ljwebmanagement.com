# LJ Web Management

> LJ Web Management builds custom, done-for-you AI automation systems that remove repetitive tasks and bottlenecks, connecting the business tools a team already uses so people can save time and focus on growth.

LJ Web Management is based in Lockport, Illinois, and works with businesses across 57+ industries. Rather than selling off-the-shelf software, the company scopes and builds automations tailored to a client's specific role, tools, and workflow, then delivers a flat, upfront quote before any work begins. The catalog covers 1000+ pre-scoped, done-for-you automations organized by industry and job role (real estate, healthcare, legal, home services, restaurants, e-commerce, and dozens more), alongside larger platform-scale solutions for businesses that need more than a single automation.

## Who this is for

- **Business owners and executives** evaluating how to implement AI in their business: LJ Web Management scopes, builds, and supports the automation, so leadership gets the outcome (time saved, costs cut, faster response) without needing an in-house AI or engineering team.
- **Employees and individual contributors** looking to supplement their own work with AI: the automations remove the repetitive parts of a role so the person keeps doing the judgment-based work that AI can't, which is increasingly the work that keeps a role valuable as more of a job becomes automatable.
