# Privacy Policy

> How LJ Web Management collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information through its website and automation services.

**Effective Date: October 25, 2026** · Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LJ Web Management, doing business as LJ Web Management ("LJ Web Management," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you visit [https://www.ljwebmanagement.com](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/) or our blog at [https://blog.ljwebmanagement.com](https://blog.ljwebmanagement.com/), contact us, chat with us, subscribe to our newsletter, schedule an appointment, or use our services.

**The short version**

- We collect what you give us (for example, through forms, chat, email, or calls) and basic technical information when you visit.
- We use analytics (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity) to understand how the site is used. You can opt out at any time from our cookie banner or [Your Privacy Choices](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/privacy-choices/).
- We do not sell or share personal information, use advertising cookies, or engage in targeted advertising, and we honor Global Privacy Control signals.
- Any U.S. resident can ask to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of their personal information, and appeal if we say no (see Section 32).

If you need this Policy in another format because of a disability, contact us using the details in Section 51 and we will provide it in a format that works for you.

## 20. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

A cookie is a small text file or identifier stored by or through a browser. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts, and server-side identifiers.

Essential cookies may be used to deliver website functions, maintain sessions, route traffic, protect forms, prevent abuse, remember security choices, or support content requested by the visitor.

Preference cookies may remember language, region, time zone, or other choices so that the website can provide a more consistent experience.

Analytics technologies may measure visits, page views, referrals, general location, device characteristics, navigation, and interactions so that we can understand and improve website performance.

Third-party content, embedded media, social links, or integrations may allow the applicable third party to receive technical information when the content loads or when a visitor interacts with it.

When you first visit, a banner explains that we use analytics cookies and lets you opt out. "OK" and "Opt out" are equally easy to choose, and "Settings" explains each category. You can change your choice at any time from [Your Privacy Choices](https://www.ljwebmanagement.com/privacy-choices/); if you opt out, we stop loading analytics and delete the analytics cookies our site can access.

| Category | What it is | Examples | When it is used |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly necessary | Remembers your privacy choices and on-device preferences | `lj_consent` cookie (12 months); local storage for course progress, tool entries, and the blog newsletter pop-up | Always; cannot be switched off on our site |
| Analytics | Measures traffic and how pages are used | Google Analytics cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_*`; Microsoft Clarity cookies such as `_clck` and `_clsk` | Unless you opt out, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal |
| Live chat | Provides the Tawk.to chat window | Cookies and storage set by Tawk.to, for example to keep a conversation open between pages | After you first interact with a page (for example, by scrolling or moving the pointer) |

| Advertising | Ad targeting or retargeting | None | Never; we do not use advertising cookies or pixels |

Cookie names and durations are set by each provider and may change. Each provider's privacy materials describe the cookies it uses.

You can use browser settings to block, delete, or receive warnings about cookies. Blocking essential cookies may prevent portions of the website from operating correctly.

Where required, optional cookies will be subject to the choices presented through an available consent mechanism. A visitor may be able to revise a cookie choice through that mechanism or by clearing stored cookies.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising for that browser, and we keep optional analytics off. We treat a Do Not Track signal the same way. You can still turn analytics on yourself in the privacy settings if you choose. Because the choice is stored in your browser, it applies to that browser and device.

## 21. ANALYTICS

We use website analytics to understand general traffic, visitor behavior, page performance, and interactions with inquiry or appointment pathways.

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity on our website and blog unless you opt out. Analytics scripts load after you first interact with a page. Google Analytics measures visits, page views, referrals, devices, approximate location, and similar usage information. Microsoft Clarity records how visitors interact with pages, including clicks, scrolling, mouse movement, and page content, to produce heatmaps and session recordings that help us find usability problems.

Our contact, appointment, and newsletter forms are embedded from Typeform in a separate frame, so the information you type into those forms is collected by Typeform rather than captured in Clarity session recordings.

Analytics information may include technical, usage, referral, device, approximate-location, and event information. It may be aggregated or associated with online identifiers.

Analytics providers may set their own cookies and receive information under their own privacy terms. We configure Google Analytics with Google signals and ad personalization turned off, and we signal to Microsoft Clarity that advertising storage is not permitted.

We use analytics only for measurement and improvement, not for advertising. Analytics data is kept according to each provider's retention settings and is not used to identify you by name.
