Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LJ Web Management, LLC ("LJ Web Management," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit our website or purchase a course.

1. Information we collect

We collect information in these ways:

  • Enrollment information: When you book an appointment to enroll in a course, we collect the information you provide through our Typeform booking form, such as your name, email, and the course you're interested in. This is used to confirm your appointment, send you a payment link, and, after payment, your course access.
  • Payment information: Once we send you a payment link, our payment processor, Stripe, collects your payment details directly. We receive confirmation of payment and the amount paid, not your full card number.
  • Contact form submissions: When you contact us through the site, we collect the name, email, and message you provide through our Typeform contact form.
  • Website analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, including pages viewed and general location and device information, via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Live chat: If you use our Tawk.to chat widget, the messages you send and basic browser information are collected by Tawk.to on our behalf.

2. How we use your information

  • To follow up on an enrollment with a payment link;
  • To deliver course access and materials after payment;
  • To respond to support requests and contact form messages;
  • To send order confirmations and, where relevant, course-related updates;
  • To understand and improve how visitors use our website;
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment processing

Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. via a payment link we send after you enroll. Stripe's collection and use of your payment information is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy. We receive confirmation of your payment, not your full card details.

4. Third-party services

Our website uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy practices:

  • Typeform for our appointment booking and contact forms;
  • Stripe for payment processing;
  • Google Analytics for website traffic analytics;
  • Tawk.to for live chat support.

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and to keep the site functioning correctly. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended without them.

6. Data retention

We retain purchase and contact records for as long as necessary to provide support, honor refund requests, and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@ljwebmanagement.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children's privacy

Our courses and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect as of the effective date shown at the top of this page.
